Sale Dossier

Everything built for the sale of the JRS / DRR asset, what each piece contains, and its state as of 13 August 2026. Assembled from the files on disk, not from memory. The live tracker is research/IP_SALE_TRACKER.md, which is revised every turn.

The one thing to know

Ten sale artifacts are finished. None has a recorded recipient. No buyer has ever been approached. That is not an execution failure: the plan written on 1 August set three prerequisites before buyer outreach (a publication accepted, marks filed, one paying pilot) and a later recommendation to hold the trademark filings made the second unreachable. That gate was withdrawn on 13 August. None of the three is a real prerequisite for talking to a buyer. There is now no gate at all.

What is actually being sold

Not "a standard". A bundle:
1. A coined, ownable problem category, Decision Reconstruction Risk, plus the five conditions, the three-read scale and the seven AI failure modes.
2. A labelled, blind-verified 24-record dataset with an independently reproduced answer key. A benchmark asset.
3. A self-updating reproducibility engine running nightly cross-vendor.
4. An international credentialed reviewer panel: 36 completers (all completers, both arms) across 16 countries.
5. Methodology authored with a Big-Four model-validation director.
6. A finished Investigator Field Guide product line (EEO, Fair Housing, International), mentioned in two verified Dewey Publications places.
7. Papers in progress plus one accepted for November.
8. Live product surface: training and certification, review-engine API, dashboards.

Value grades, stated honestly

Research credibility: strong and improving. Reproducibility, reliability, verified key, pre-registration.
Commercial proof: weak. Zero paying or piloting organizations. This is the binding constraint.
Acquisition readiness: middling. The assets are real but scattered.

Seven conveyance blindspots, from the transfer map

1. Marks are unregistered. JRS is used as ™ only.
2. Co-authored papers carry shared copyright. Position them as evidence, not conveyed assets.
3. Reviewer and pilot PII lack the transfer-consent tier. Only part of the list has it. Do not represent the full panel as transferable.
4. AI features depend on third-party keys that do NOT convey. The buyer brings their own Anthropic, Supabase, Vercel and analytics accounts. Document it so the demo does not look broken at handoff.
5. Public "free" framing can depress willingness to pay. Keep the community layer free; never say the core is free forever.
6. Founder dependency. Goodwill and volunteers do not convey. Sell owned entities: marks, copyrights, data, code, domain.
7. Third-party assessment entanglement. An unsolicited external "assessment" under a self-styled framework was declined on 2026-07-27 and is NOT part of the validation. Do not cite it as validation in diligence or marketing.

Buyer archetypes, and the guardrails that protect the price

Targets: GRC and compliance software vendors · e-discovery and legal-tech · HR-tech and people-analytics · AI-governance platforms · standards bodies and foundations. No named target list exists yet.

Guardrails: NDA before specifics · the five-condition scoring and the gold answer key never enter a data room · protect the blind, nothing reveals the Arm B method or the arm split · no "for sale" signal on the public site or LinkedIn · hold all claims to the completer sample and the pre-registered figures · no urgency signalling.
