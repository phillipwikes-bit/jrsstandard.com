What am I agreeing to?

Open Initiatives & International Registry

Ticking the box means three things and no more: contact me about the JRS project, store my details securely, and allow them to transfer with the project if it is ever acquired. It does not publish your name. Being listed in the public Registry is a separate, optional choice offered after you have what you came for.

Joining also opens the free, private JRS diagnostic check on your own records. Your record text is never stored: each record is assessed in memory and discarded.

You can leave the Registry at any time by emailing info@jrsstandard.com. The standard, the research, and the site stay free to read without registering.

Your details are stored securely and used only for the JRS project. Request access or deletion any time at info@jrsstandard.com. See the Privacy Policy.
