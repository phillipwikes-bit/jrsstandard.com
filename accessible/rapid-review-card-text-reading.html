JRS OPERATIONAL REVIEW                                                                                                         JRS-F1

REVIEWER QUICK REFERENCE

JRS Rapid Review Card Apply before any record enters an official system of record. Each item is pass/fail at the record level.

BEFORE FINALIZATION, ASK:

1     Can the conclusion be reconstructed from the record alone?
      Would a later reviewer with no prior knowledge understand the basis without calling the original author?
2     Is the basis for each conclusion identifiable in the file?
      Dates, logs, documented interactions, or referenced records. Verify attachments are on file.
3     Can the chronology be followed from the record?
      Dates and intervals for any stated pattern. Missing dates are the most common single gap.
4     Can the decision process be traced from evidence to conclusion?
      The path is visible in the document itself, not in the author's memory.
5     Is the evidence sufficient to support the conclusion?
      Characterizations verified against source material. Human reviewer confirms before finalization.

RAPID REVIEW / Minimum Standard

1 Confirm the conclusion can be reconstructed from the record alone 2 Confirm each conclusion has an identifiable basis in the file 3 Confirm timeline anchors are present for any stated pattern of conduct 4 Confirm the decision path from evidence to conclusion is visible 5 Confirm the evidence is sufficient to support the conclusion

SUBMISSION READINESS

STOP           Return to Drafter. Evaluative language without anchors. Timeline or policy basis absent.
REVIEW         Clarification Required. Conclusion may be accurate but basis not visible. Additional anchoring needed.
READY          Self-Contained. Basis reconstructable, chronology clear, evidence sufficient without explanation from author.

JRS OPERATIONAL REVIEW                                                                                                   JRS-F1

SECONDARY REVIEW REQUIRED WHEN:

-- Evaluative language without a specific behavioral anchor -- Pattern conduct claim without specific dated instances -- Escalation without a documented prior counseling trail -- Termination without referenced supporting documentation -- AI-assisted wording not reviewed against source notes -- Accommodation record missing interactive process documentation

REVIEWER NOTE Review occurs before the record enters the official system. That is the only point where a gap can be resolved without consequence. If the supporting record cannot be identified quickly, stop before submission.
