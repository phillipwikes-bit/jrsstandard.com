# Brief for an outside record author

Date: 24 November 2026. Purpose: every test record so far was written by the developer, which a buyer will rightly question. This brief lets someone with no connection to the tool write fresh fictional records, so the tool can be tested on material it was not shaped around.

## Who should write them

Someone who writes or reviews decision records at work (an investigator, auditor, compliance officer, records manager or AI governance practitioner) and who has **not** seen the tool's checks, test records or study answers. They must not be shown this repository.

## What to write

Twelve fictional records, each a short decision record (one paragraph, 60 to 150 words) plus two to four source documents (each two to five sentences).

| Record type | Number |
|---|---|
| Supplier or third-party access exception | 3 |
| Authorization for an AI system to take an action | 3 |
| Workplace investigation summary (findings only, no employment decision) | 3 |
| Discovery or document-production decision | 3 |

For each type, write:

- **one sound record**: everything it states is supported by its documents;
- **two flawed records**, each with one or two realistic gaps of the writer's own choosing, the kind they have seen in real work.

## Rules

- Entirely invented people, companies, systems and identifiers. No real names, no email addresses, phone numbers or ID numbers.
- Plain text only. Each document starts with `Title:` and `Version:`, then a blank line, then the text. The record file is `record.txt`.
- Do not mention the tool, and do not try to write records that test it.
- In a separate, private file, list for each record whether it is sound or flawed, and describe each gap in one sentence. This answer key is sealed before the records are run.

## How the results will be used

The records are run through the extractor and the review exactly once, with the output recorded and fingerprinted before the answer key is opened. Results are reported with denominators and with any misses stated plainly. No check is changed using these records; any improvement is tested on further new records.

## Rights

The writer is asked to confirm in writing that the records are their own invented work and may be used, without payment unless agreed, to test and demonstrate Decision Spellcheck, including by a future licensee or buyer. Credit is offered only with the writer's approval of the exact wording.
