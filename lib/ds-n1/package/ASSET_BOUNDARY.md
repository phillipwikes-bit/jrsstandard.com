# DS-N1 technical package: asset boundary and disclosures

Status: a proposed technical boundary for discussion. **Not** a legal opinion, licence, assignment, warranty of title or authority to transact.

## Included

Only the files listed with SHA-256 hashes in the generated `ASSET_MANIFEST.json`: the engine, lenses, local reviewer and author pages, unit tests, developer-constructed fictional fixtures, these documents and the packaging scripts. The tests also include text from **published public decisions** (U.S. EEOC appellate decisions and public-records determinations, each with its source address) used as test material; these are public documents, not customer or private records. Works of the U.S. federal government are generally not subject to copyright in the United States (17 U.S.C. section 105); reuse elsewhere should be checked. (Corrected 2026-11-26 after an external audit found the earlier wording said real records were excluded.)

## Excluded

- The sealed evaluation sets (HOLDOUT-2, isolation, stress), stored results and labelling packets.
- Git history and every other branch and file of the source repository.
- The Justification Review Standard (JRS), its name, marks, website, publications, training and other materials.
- Any separate product the owner offers for sale.
- Credentials, accounts, deployments, and any private, customer, employer or participant record.
- All rights not expressly conveyed by a signed written agreement.

## Before any transaction

Owner-level steps, not engineering ones: confirm ownership and any third-party or employer interests; decide whether to keep developing in a private repository; obtain counsel review of any licensing or regulatory claim; record owner release authorization. Building or verifying this package completes none of them.
