Data handling

Data Isolation Guarantee

All customer files and sample records submitted for diagnostic evaluation are processed in ephemeral working memory and deleted upon delivery of the diagnostic report. Customer records are never stored, logged to public research sets, or used for model training.

Processed in ephemeral memory and purged immediately upon report generation.

The mechanism, not just the promise. There is no upload form anywhere on this site. No page accepts a file, and no intake page has a form of any kind. Records arrive by whatever channel your organization already approves for material of that sensitivity, and de-identification is agreed in writing before the first one is sent.

  • De-identification. Records are supplied de-identified by you, to a standard agreed at scoping. If a record arrives carrying an identifier, it is returned rather than redacted, so the decision about your data stays with you.
  • Retention. Records are held only for the duration of the read and destroyed on delivery. The finding is retained; the records are not.
  • No sub-processing. Records are read by one person. They are not sent to a third-party service, and they are not put through the public review engine or any model for training.
  • Confidentiality. An NDA on your paper is welcome and is signed before scoping if you prefer that order.

The deliverable

You own it outright. The written finding is your work product on delivery: no licence back, no reuse of your material in research or marketing, no case study without your separate written agreement.

The finding is structured in four parts: an executive summary and defensibility assessment, the failure modes observed with the passage that shows each one, findings against each of the five review conditions, and a prioritised list of documentation controls.

What this is not

Is this legal advice?

No. It is a structured read of documentation quality. It does not assess whether a decision was correct, lawful, or defensible in law, and it is not a substitute for counsel.

Does it establish regulatory compliance?

No. JRS is designed to support organizational governance, auditability, documentation traceability, and review processes that may be relevant to frameworks and regulatory requirements such as the EU AI Act and NIST AI RMF. It does not establish compliance with any of them, and no framework requires it.

Is the method proven?

Not yet, and the evidence is published rather than summarised. Reproducibility and reliability are reported. Accuracy on the detection set is 83.9% across 16 independent experts and 384 graded reads, 95% confidence interval 72.7 to 95.1, scored against a key fixed before any scoring took place under a pre-registered analysis plan. Controlled comparison against unaided review is deployed and has not reached its enrollment target, so no claim of improvement over careful reading is made.
