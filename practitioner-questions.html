Practical Questions for Reviewing AI-Assisted Records

Choose the specific documentation question that arises in your existing workflow. These guides demonstrate human review procedures without requiring a new platform or record upload.

Record-level questions

How do I check evidence support in an investigation report?Trace findings to identifiable, accessible source material and route unresolved gaps for human review. How do I verify sources in an AI-drafted decision record?Examine model-generated claims, quotations, citations and assumptions against original evidence. Can a later reviewer reconstruct the decision chronology?Separate events, source dates, knowledge, approvals and later explanations. What should a supplier-access exception note preserve?Examine approval, policy basis, dated request, duration and review disposition in a fictional compliance example.

How to use these guides

  1. Review an authorized record using local organizational procedures.
  2. Use the relevant questions to identify gaps, not to infer legal merits.
  3. Locate primary source evidence and document unresolved questions.
  4. Refer required corrections or escalations to the authorized human reviewer.

No automatic decision or score. These guides support human examination. The public website does not accept identifiable case records, produce automated determinations or certify organizational compliance.

Primary reference routes

Start with the seven-point record defensibility check or the five-condition reference; examine the specific publication edition you intend to use. The research page explains what has and has not been measured.
