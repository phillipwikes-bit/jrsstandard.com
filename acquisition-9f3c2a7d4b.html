01 · The Opportunity

A standard for decision defensibility.

Organizations are asked to defend decisions, not documents. When a consequential decision is challenged long after it is made, the record becomes the only witness, and if the record cannot explain why the decision was made, exposure follows.

JRS identifies that gap before it becomes permanent. It is a pre-finalization review standard that evaluates whether a record can still explain the basis, reasoning, evidence, and chronology behind a consequential decision. The risk it names, Decision Reconstruction Risk, sits upstream of audits, appeals, investigations, regulatory review, and litigation, across HR, public records, compliance, internal audit, investigations, and AI governance.

02 · What JRS Is

A method, an instrument, and a brand.

JRS evaluates a record against five conditions, then resolves them into a single calibrated judgment, the Decision Defensibility Score (1 to 5). It judges whether the record can explain the decision, not whether the decision was correct or lawful. That scoping is deliberate and is what keeps the claims defensible.

The five conditions
Reconstructability, Basis Identification, Chronology, Decision-Process Traceability, Evidentiary Sufficiency.
The Score
A 1 to 5 rating of how well a record alone explains a decision. The teachable, licensable instrument.

04 · Validation Status (stated plainly)

Operational validation, in progress.

JRS is in an operational validation phase. No proven-effectiveness claim is made. The evidence is built on staged rungs, each a separate, honestly reported claim:

  • R1Reproducibility: do independent AI models from different vendors apply JRS the same way. Reported: 84% cross-vendor agreement across 15 constructed records.
  • R2Reliability: do independent human reviewers reach the same judgment, measured with chance-corrected agreement. Reported: Gwet's AC1 0.74 (experts) and 0.62 (trained reviewers) on 10 records, clearing the pre-registered point floor of 0.61. Interim: intervals are wide and the secondary lower-bound criterion is not yet met.
  • R3Accuracy: does a read match a held-out, independently verified answer key. Reported: 83.9% panel accuracy across the 16 independent experts of the detection panel and 384 graded reads (95% CI 72.7 to 95.1; sensitivity 87.0%, specificity 80.7%), clearing the pre-registered threshold on both criteria. Manuscript in preparation.
  • R4Outcome association: does the read track what happened when the record was tested. Reported: in an employment-law corpus of 22 adjudicated matters across three jurisdictional systems, records read Ready before the outcome was known were sustained in 6 of 8 resolved cases against 1 of 8 for records read as incomplete (Fisher's exact p = 0.041, odds ratio 21.0). In a public-records corpus of 32 cases the same test is null, because published appellate decisions are selected for contested legal questions rather than for thin files; in that corpus the read instead agreed with independent government auditors in 5 of 5 cases where both assessments existed. Two manuscripts in preparation.
  • R5Instrument effect, the earliest and least mature signal on this ladder. Observed: on a shared 10-record corpus, 22 reviewers using the five conditions returned an unreconstructable determination on 68.1% of 113 labels (95% CI 59.1 to 76.0), against 6.2% of 16 labels from 3 reviewers working without them (95% CI 1.1 to 28.3). Fisher's exact p = 2.4e-06, a rate ratio of 10.9 (95% CI 1.6 to 73.0). Stated precisely because it matters: reviewers were not randomly assigned to a method, the unstructured group is 3 people and 16 labels, and its interval is wide. This is a signal worth a randomized test, not a trial result, and it is not published as one.
  • R4Criterion validity: do JRS reads correspond to documented real-world outcomes in public cases, scored blind to the outcome. Cases accruing.
Statistics are pre-registered and gated. Reproducibility and reliability are now reported with their bounds; accuracy and criterion-validity figures remain withheld until thresholds are met, and the default is to show nothing rather than a thin number. Methodology is advised by Ubayet Hossain, FRM, a model-validation professional (KPMG). The integrity controls on the dataset (gated writes, attribution, de-identification screening) are in place so the evidence is defensible in diligence.
05 · The Reviewer Panel

An independent, international bench.

The validation program is carried by a panel of credentialed independent experts recruited across professional networks, not by the founder alone. That independent bench is one of the hardest parts of this asset to copy: it is the source of the reliability and accuracy evidence, and it travels with the sale.

Two domain case sets sit alongside the panel and are separately owned research assets: 32 adjudicated public-records determinations, advisory opinions and compliance audits across four document classes and two states, and 22 adjudicated employment and labour matters across three jurisdictional systems. Both are fully cited, both were read blind to outcome, and both are the basis of manuscripts under preparation with the domain leads as first authors.

Scale
48 credentialed reviewers registered and 36 completed a full 24-record set (all completers, both arms) as of 11 August 2026. The 16 completers on the named detection panel span 11 countries and 5 continents, across AI governance, compliance, internal audit, investigations, law, human resources, and records management. Reviewers take part in a personal capacity, unpaid.
Progress
As of 5 August 2026, 16 independent experts on the detection panel have finished the full 24-record blind read against a held-out answer key that is independently verified 24 of 24, producing 384 graded reads. The study closes on or about 14 August 2026; invited reviewers may still complete, so these counts are provisional and are recomputed at close. A manuscript reporting the result is in preparation.
Acknowledgment is not endorsement. Naming a reviewer reflects participation in the validation study only. Reviewers are credited as independent reviewers, never as endorsers or validators of JRS. Attribution is opt-in, each reviewer approves the exact public wording before it appears, and may withdraw before publication.
06 · The Investigator Field Guides

The standard, in a working guide.

Three finished, domain-specific Investigator Field Guides translate the five conditions into the day-to-day language of a working investigator: Employment/EEO, Fair Housing, and International. They are a near-term, packageable product line that sits alongside the Academy, and they widen the standard's reach beyond a training seat into the investigator's desk reference.

The Investigator Field Guides and the standard drew two favorable mentions from Dewey Publications, a federal civil-service law publisher. Its News and Case Alert (Issue #18-07, July 10, 2026) told readers involved in agency investigations that the site "offers good guidance on proper assembly and quality controls for investigations and investigators" and pointed them to "the Investigator Field Guide and Justification Review Standard." On The Dewey Publications Podcast (July 6, 2026 episode), host Peter Broida described the Investigator Field Guide favorably and encouraged listeners involved in investigations to look at the site, while stating expressly that neither he nor Dewey was endorsing it; the episode notes list the site under "Resources for investigators." Both are favorable third-party mentions, not an endorsement or certification of JRS, and are represented here only as mentions.
07 · The Academy (nearest-revenue, licensable now)

Education revenue before full validation.

Professional associations do not endorse methodologies, but they routinely approve educational courses for continuing-education credit. The Academy is built for that path: domain courses (HR, public records, internal audit, compliance, AI governance, and more) that can pursue CE/CPE approval through bodies such as ARMA, SCCE, IIA, IAPP, ACFE, AIIM, and SHRM. It is a recurring-revenue layer that does not require the standard to be fully validated first.

10 · Honest Positioning

JRS is research-informed and in active validation. It evaluates whether a record can explain a decision; it does not guarantee that any decision is correct, lawful, or will survive challenge. The value today is a credible standard, a working tool, a turnkey education asset, and a disciplined evidence program, with the upside that the validation data continues to accrue. Every claim here is scoped to what the evidence supports.
