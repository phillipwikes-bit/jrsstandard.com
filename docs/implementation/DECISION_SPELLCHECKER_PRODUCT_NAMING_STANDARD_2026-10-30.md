# Decision Spellchecker: Product Naming and Representation Rule

**Controlled decision date:** 2026-10-30  
**Status:** Owner-directed product-name correction, applicable to the current website implementation and future product-facing work.  
**Scope:** Public website copy, inquiry fields, reports, implementation records, software UX, product evidence and commercial materials. Does not rename historical research or alter ownership rights.

## 1. Canonical product name

**Decision Spellchecker** is the exact active product name. Use both words, in this order and capitalization, on first reference and preferably throughout public-facing materials.

Do not market the product as **Spellchecker** alone, **JRS Spellchecker** or **JRS Decision Spellchecker**. Existing historical project files and archived planning documents using earlier labels remain historical evidence, not the active naming authority.

The five review conditions and other source methods remain attributable to the **Justification Review Standard (JRS)**. Product naming does not erase JRS authorship, study limitations, provenance, rights or the historical Review Engine and Decision Reconstruction Manifest. The current JRS website continues to offer the free public method and resources.

## 3. Audience-facing wording

Preferred concise distinction:

> **JRS provides the public review methodology. Decision Spellchecker is the separately developed software candidate.**

Current capability boundary:

> **Decision Spellchecker remains in controlled development and is not available for public case-record submission, production evaluation or licensing.**

Product concept statement (a hypothesis, not a performance claim):

> **Decision Spellchecker is designed to help reviewers identify where a decision record's stated reasons lack traceable supporting evidence before the record is finalized.**

Any present-tense feature statements must be supported by version-specific evidence. Proposed features such as Justification Underline, Cognitive Controls, Report Card and evidence lookup must be clearly qualified as designed, implemented in a specific prototype, or independently tested, as applicable.

## 4. Implementation rules

1. Use **Decision Spellchecker** on the JRS homepage, Contact & Inquiries page, privacy notice, organizational inquiry categories, owner dashboard labels, implementation programme and release materials.
2. Retain existing `commercial` source/category values, API paths, original repository branch identifiers and historical document filenames when changing them would break data compatibility or provenance. **Display labels and schema values are separate concerns.**
3. Existing plan titles that contain the shorter historic name remain accurate citations to those dated documents. Add a current-name explanation rather than silently rewriting the past.
4. Preserve explicit separation of research collaboration, training, organizational method inquiry and **prospective Decision Spellchecker interest**. Those inquiries do not automatically establish sales leads or willingness to pay.
5. Do not add **™**, **®**, trademark registration, exclusivity or ownership assertions unless supported by reviewed rights evidence. This naming correction is not a trademark clearance search or legal opinion.
6. Source UI and content checks must fail if active public product-facing pages revert to the unqualified standalone name.

## 5. Status

A naming decision and implementation-copy change, **not** an approval to merge, deploy, file a trademark or issue a commercial offer. Production conditions for the JRS site and the Decision Spellchecker validation/release gates remain independently controlled.
