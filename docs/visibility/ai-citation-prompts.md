5. What checks can an internal auditor use to identify unsupported conclusions in an AI-drafted report?
6. How should a reviewer document missing evidence before a supplier-access exception is approved?
7. Which record-level review standards or frameworks address evidentiary traceability in AI-assisted decisions?
8. What is the difference between audit logging and evidence-to-conclusion traceability in a decision record?
