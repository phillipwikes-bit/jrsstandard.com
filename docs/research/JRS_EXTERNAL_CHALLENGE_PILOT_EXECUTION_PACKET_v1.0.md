## Standard first-pass instruction
Review only the supplied masked corpus and the frozen Codebook. Do not use outside facts to repair the record. For each JRS condition select PASS, REVIEW, or GAP; identify the evidence location; give a concise rationale; flag ambiguity; and record confidence. Where relevant, distinguish MISSING from UNSUPPORTED under the Adjudication Protocol. Submit independently before seeing another reviewer's response.
