# JRS Public-Interest Narrative: Transmission Review and Editorial Controls

**Date:** 3 November 2026  
**Status:** Candidate source-copy analysis; no production release authorization.  
**Purpose:** Preserve JRS's original narrative while making the user path, free access and DRR definition easier for individual practitioners to understand.

## Non-negotiable primary message

> Organizations do not need to purchase or replace a software platform to begin examining whether their decision records preserve sufficient evidence, chronology and reasoning for later independent review.

The exact sentence remains part of the prominent homepage editorial treatment and organizational information page. It is **not** a claim that free resources equal software features, replace case-management systems, or have demonstrated superior real-world effectiveness.

## Preserve the JRS voice and identity

- **Original question, unchanged:** "Can the record explain the decision after its author is gone?"
- **Real problem:** Decision Reconstruction Risk (DRR) occurs where a later reviewer cannot independently reconstruct the record's supporting evidence, sequence and reasoning.
- **Intended user:** The practitioner writing, investigating, checking or approving records within an existing professional process.
- **No new spending required to begin:** A public method, six training modules, direct record check, investigator guides, simulations and a searchable 16-item library.
- **Actual next step:** Open an existing draft inside an authorized process; locate its cited basis, chronology and reasoning; document gaps, correction or escalation under local procedures.
- **Boundaries:** JRS is not a purchase pathway, litigation decision, automatic score, data upload service, enterprise case system or validated claim of field effectiveness. Source editions and PDF-accessibility cautions remain in place.

## Implemented page roles

1. **Homepage:** The familiar question and original restrained voice. DRR explained immediately. Exact non-purchase message prominent after the first free action. Specific routes to the check, guides, training and 16-resource directory. Fictional demonstration retains explicit source verification and no invented support.
2. **Resources:** DRR plus free practice entry; immediate record check, training and rapid review card. Searchable 16-entry directory remains ahead of detailed explanation. Exact document edition and accessible-format disclosures remain.
3. **Training:** Six free self-paced modules tied to source, chronology and reasoning gaps. No compulsory registration or certification claim.
4. **Investigator Guides:** Contextualize investigative documentation gaps and direct access to three edition-specific resources. Preserve the differences between the older supplement editions and published Standard PDF.
5. **About:** Preserve the origin in civil-rights documentation review. Explain risk without claiming AI caused the problem or that operational effectiveness is established.
6. **Organizational Use:** Present the exact no-purchase proposition. Organizational practitioner use is possible within terms, but no case intake, hosted engine, public software license or commercial pathway is offered.

## Conversion and effectiveness guardrails

The desired outcome is higher *qualified practitioner use*: finding the correct aid, opening it, understanding the five conditions and making an appropriate review disposition within the practitioner's existing system. A stronger sentence alone cannot establish demand growth, cost savings or effectiveness.

Measure only through an approved, non-sensitive analytics plan, if appropriate: visits to practitioner entry pages, approved aggregate tool-open/training-entry events, resource-directory usage and voluntary user feedback. Do not send resource search terms, case narratives, names or sensitive review content into third-party analytics; do not infer completed field reviews or legal outcomes from clicks.

## Release acceptance

- Original question and exact non-purchase statement retained in public copy.
- No new sales path, commercial CTA, Decision Spellchecker cross-sell, additional global navigation or case-upload route.
- Primary record check still opens directly; published edition identities, 16 catalog entries and six training modules retained.
- Source-level regression, keyboard and accessibility scanner tests must pass on the exact final candidate; hosted preview and manual assistive-technology/PDF accessibility remain separate conditions.
- A passing source-level editorial or WCAG-tagged automated check does **not** certify increased demand or full accessibility.
