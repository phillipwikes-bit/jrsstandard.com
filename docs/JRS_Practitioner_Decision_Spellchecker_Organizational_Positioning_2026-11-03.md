# JRS for Practitioners; Decision Spellchecker for Organizations

**Decision record:** 3 November 2026  
**Status:** Proposed public positioning and separate organizational product architecture. No website release, software deployment, pricing promise, license offer, or public engine authorization is created by this record.

## Positioning decision

**JRS is the free public-interest documentation-review standard for practitioners. Decision Spellchecker is a distinct organizational software initiative with structured evidentiary and cognitive review controls.** These are **separate products and audiences**, not free and paid tiers of one product.

JRS remains independently useful. It helps HR practitioners, investigators, civil-rights reviewers, compliance officers, auditors and legal-review professionals assess whether records preserve enough evidence, chronology, identifiable basis, reasoning and sufficiency for later independent reconstruction.

Decision Spellchecker is intended to support organizational workflows where records, source inventories, reviewer dispositions, revisions, and learning from recurring documentation gaps may need a structured interface. It is **not presently validated or approved as a production enterprise service**. Its experimental build is not a live LLM service.

Employees of organizations can still use JRS public resources within their published terms. The organizational-product distinction does not prohibit practitioners at larger institutions from using the free Standard. Free access is not automatically a commercial reproduction, embedding, sublicensing, integration or derivative-work license.

## 2. Decision Spellchecker cognitive controls

The current separately hosted experimental Decision Spellchecker interface identifies three **human-directed response controls**:

- **Reflect:** Record a review concern or possible gap in the justification or source-supported reasoning. This is an observation requiring professional interpretation, not a determination of employee motive or ability.
- **Correct:** Request clarification or correction of the record and supporting explanation, under the organization's applicable approval and document-retention process. Selecting the control is *not evidence that the record was corrected*.
- **Learn:** Identify a possible template, instruction, or review-process improvement where documentation gaps recur. The suggestion is not automatically implemented or independently validated.

These controls are **cognitive in the sense of supporting deliberate examination of reasoning and documented decision processes**. They do not diagnose cognitive biases, identify personalities, infer mental states, rate staff intelligence, or evaluate the underlying merits of a decision. They do not themselves prove whether a conclusion is true, lawful or defensible.

**Evidence distinction:** JRS's five documentary review conditions define the questions a reviewer asks of a record. The three Decision Spellchecker cognitive controls structure **how a reviewer responds** to an observed documentary concern. Neither set of controls authorizes a model to finalize a record.

### Intended workflow (proposed, not production-validated)

1. **Document and evidence:** Load only constructed/authorized material; identify record ID and version, evidence inventory, source availability and cited spans.
2. **Human initial assessment:** The reviewer records an initial condition-by-condition judgment, including uncertainty, before viewing proposed findings.
3. **Structured findings:** The software presents candidate support gaps, conflicts, source comparisons and explicit missing-source limitations.
4. **Cognitive-control disposition:** Human reviewer may **Reflect** (record concern), **Correct** (seek source or explanation revision), or **Learn** (suggest a template/process change).
5. **Review and follow-up:** Export a pending or human-recorded review, retain the source/edition identity, and compare revised records with earlier unresolved requests.
6. **Organizational controls:** Authorized supervisor determines acceptance, escalation and process adoption. No automatic approval, diagnosis, or disciplinary consequence.

The experimental interface describes a version 0.7 research candidate using constructed examples, while preserving an earlier compiler version. It states that it makes **no live AI calls** and that review processing is in browser memory. These facts must be treated as current candidate behavior, not as a guaranteed enterprise security assessment or feature roadmap commitment.

## 3. Competitive language and approximate costs

**Defensible public claim:** "The JRS documentation-review method and practitioner materials are available without purchase, subscription or ordinary-use registration." JRS currently includes a free browser check, a searchable 16-resource directory, six self-paced training modules, investigator editions and reference material. State edition and accessibility limits; do not call training completion certification.

**Avoid:** "JRS is more effective than commercial platforms," "better than enterprise case-management software," "equivalent enterprise functionality for $0," or a precise dollar-savings figure without a same-function independent experiment.

A number of enterprise software providers offer **quote-based** rather than directly comparable posted prices. Verify their specific official pricing page, edition, users, integrations, implementation and annual term before publicizing any price. In the absence of published apples-to-apples quotes, prefer:

| Procurement item | Free JRS practitioner site | Typical enterprise software procurement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Review method and free field aids | $0 to access the published public materials | May include free resources, with paid software separately |
| Mandatory subscription to start an individual JRS review | None | Depends on the platform and product |
| Enterprise case-management, SSO, controlled document repository and integrations | **Not provided** | May be offered via quoted subscription/implementation |
| Automated organizational review workflow and service obligations | **Not provided** | Requires separate product selection/contract |
| Proven savings or performance advantage | **Not established** | Must be evaluated against the actual alternative |

Vendor sources to check before any claim: Case IQ https://www.caseiq.com/pricing ; NAVEX https://www.navex.com/en-us/request-pricing/ ; OneTrust https://www.onetrust.com/pricing/ ; Relativity https://www.relativity.com/pricing/ . Pricing page presence alone is not a verified dollar quote. Some competitors also provide free training and resources.

**Positioning distinction:** JRS offers *specialized, practical review of record defensibility at no cost to access*. Decision Spellchecker, if separately validated, addresses *the organizational systems problem* of structured review, human dispositions, source comparison, cognitive correction/learning loops and possible workflow integration.

## 4. Public website narrative and controls

**JRS hero concept:**
> A free documentation review standard for practitioners.
>
> Before a consequential record is finalized, check whether someone without firsthand knowledge can identify the supporting evidence, follow the chronology and reconstruct the reasoning. Use the method, guides, training and record-check tools at no cost.

Supporting links should lead to existing free record check, resource directory, published editions and training. No prices for competitors without matched procurement evidence, no "better than paid software" claim, no fabricated return on investment, and no sales or acquisition call to action.

**JRS visitor proposition:** "No subscription needed to start. No case-record upload. No mandatory training registration. No claim of legal certification. No need to replace the organization's existing systems."

**No public JRS commerce path:** No enterprise demo, software trial, license quote, investment or IP-acquisition portal; no Decision Spellchecker cross-sell funnel or auto-forwarding of contact information. The existing non-sensitive practitioner/methodology contact route may remain for correspondence; it is not a purchase offer. An unsolicited institutional approach may be assessed privately and separately, after verifying ownership, rights and the actual product's status.

**Decision Spellchecker product language (future, conditional):**
> Organizational documentation review with structured source comparisons, explicit review dispositions and human-directed Reflect, Correct and Learn controls.
>
> Research candidate. Constructed examples only. Not validated for live organizational records or released as an enterprise service.

Do not call it "AI-powered" until a real, authorized AI implementation and independent operational testing exist. The organizational brand must have its own terms, permissions, data-processing approval, customer evidence, and release gate; the public-interest JRS mark and free publication commitments remain separately identifiable.

## 5. Governance, commercial demand and separation

Large agencies may seek rights to reproduce materials, implement the standard internally, procure a software system, sponsor research or negotiate an intellectual property transaction. These are **different requests**. An inbound offer does not automatically grant a right or trigger a public commercial pathway. Review a request privately for:

1. Actual asset requested: JRS publication license, education/use permission, research collaboration, Decision Spellchecker software license, or separate IP assignment.
2. Authorship, code/source provenance, ownership of each artifact, earlier grants, third-party components, collaborators' claims and mark status.
3. Effect on the free public-interest commitments, practitioner access, published edition identity and independent research integrity.
4. Confidentiality, data-protection, security, procurement and legal obligations.
5. Independent valuation and written counsel review before exclusivity, sublicensing or assignment.

A transaction involving Decision Spellchecker should **not** silently convey the JRS Standard, publication rights, original datasets or all future methodological developments. Conversely, making the Standard freely available to practitioners should not be represented as a perpetual unrestricted commercial software right.

**Do not build a hidden commercial funnel in the public site.** Separate branding, domain or distribution, analytics/consent, intellectual-property schedules and contractual terms if and when a commercial product is offered. Keep practitioner research participant information separate from product marketing absent valid separate permission.
