## A. Shared definitions, boundaries and audience
**JRS:** Justification Review Standard, a human-applied documentation-review instrument designed to help practitioners assess the independent reviewability, evidentiary traceability, chronology and recorded reasoning of decision records.
**DRR:** Decision Reconstruction Risk, the risk that a retained decision record does not enable a person without firsthand knowledge to reconstruct the evidence, chronology or reasoning underlying a conclusion.
**Evidence boundary:** JRS remains in operational validation. A documentation gap is not proof that a decision was unlawful, incorrect, discriminatory or unfair. JRS does not create disclosure rights, audit model performance or certify compliance. No agency endorses JRS by virtue of the founder's career history.
**No required commitment:** Reading and linking to free JRS resources require no payment, account, mandatory registration, response, campaign support or new software. Reuse/rebranding rights are distinct from simple link sharing. Optional support is a separate, explicit POST action and is not evidence of adoption.

## E. Cialdini: fully mapped, truthful application
The contemporary framework consists of **reciprocity, scarcity, authority, commitment and consistency, liking, social proof, and unity**. Applying all seven means assessing the relevance of each and refusing artificial use of those without a genuine factual basis. This is an editorial design specification, not a claim that JRS communications have been experimentally validated.
| Principle | Authorized website implementation | Authorized email implementation | Explicit exclusion |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reciprocity | Give useful free publications immediately | One directly relevant free link before any request | No obligation to reciprocate |
| Authority | Accurate independent founder and former Lead Civil Rights Officer history | Relevant first-person experience, bounded to documentation review | No Maryland government endorsement |
| Liking | Human, respectful, easy-to-understand information | Polite, audience-specific language without forced familiarity | No fabricated connection, flattery or unverified prior contact |
| Unity | Identify real shared responsibilities (accountable records; proper review) | Name the recipient's evident professional responsibility | No invented membership or institutional alliance |
| Commitment and consistency | Support is optional, explicit and separate; read/share choice is voluntary | Invite independent consideration or original-link sharing, no compelled response | Never count navigation, draft or clicks as verified adoption |
| Social proof | Use only verified attributable peer use if ever authorized and supported | No unsupported adoption claim | No synthetic testimonials or misleading counts |
| Scarcity | The materials are free; make no scarcity claims | No false expiry, urgency, deadline or limited access | No fabricated restriction |

**Primary sources:** Robert B.
