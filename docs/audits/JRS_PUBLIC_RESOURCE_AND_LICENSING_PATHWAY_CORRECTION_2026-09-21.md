# JRS Public Resource and Licensing Pathway Correction Record

**Date:** 2026-09-21  
**Scope:** Public website navigation, resource access, and commercial-stage language  
**Change type:** Surgical correction  

## Problem corrected

The revised homepage elevated enterprise licensing while leaving the free practitioner resources inside an old homepage state panel. Links from secondary pages opened the top of that panel rather than the actual resources, and two buttons labeled as downloads only changed homepage state. The site also contained a direct contradiction between the Record Defensibility Check, which said nothing would be sold until research was complete, and the enterprise page, which invited licensing discussions.

## Corrections executed

1. Created `resources.html` as the canonical, indexable public-resource hub.
2. Restored a visible free-resource pathway in the homepage hero, decision path, and principal action block.
3. Changed the homepage Practitioner Resources navigation control from an in-page state button to a normal link.
4. Repointed legacy `index.html#section-tools` links across public HTML pages to `/resources.html`.
5. Repaired the two homepage controls that appeared to download resources but did not navigate or download.
6. Preserved direct access to the Seven-Point Record Defensibility Check, Review Controls PDF, Rapid Review Card, Investigator Guides, Reviewer Training, Simulations, Reference Library, and Research Record.
7. Clarified that training remains open without registration and that contact details are requested only for a named certificate and consented project updates.
8. Replaced the categorical statement that nothing is sold until research is finished with a precise separation between unavailable individual record-review services and the enterprise evaluation, integration, licensing, and strategic-inquiry pathway.
9. Reframed the enterprise page from implied production availability to operational validation, controlled evaluation, licensing readiness, and potential licensing.
10. Reframed Review Engine token requests as controlled evaluation access.
11. Relabeled the repeated footer pathway so research participation and free resources match their destinations.
12. Added `/resources` and `/free-resources` routes and added the canonical resources page to the sitemap.

## Boundaries preserved

- No claim of validated effectiveness was added.
- No compliance, certification, legal-sufficiency, or admissibility claim was added.
- The public standard remains usable without the Review Engine.
- The Review Engine remains described as being in operational validation.
- Research participation remains distinct from enterprise evaluation and licensing inquiries.
- No pricing, production authorization, product-market-fit claim, or completed transaction is represented.

## Resulting public architecture

The website now presents three distinct pathways:

1. **Public use:** free standard, tools, training, simulations, and references.
2. **Evidence review:** published research, results, limitations, and operational boundaries.
3. **Enterprise pathway:** controlled evaluation, integration, licensing, or acquisition inquiry without representing production maturity.

This correction restores the public-resource strategy while preserving the owner's stated goal of pursuing an asset purchase agreement or exclusive licensing arrangement with an established platform.
