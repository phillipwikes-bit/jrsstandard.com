# JRS Public Experience Modernization

**Version:** 2026-10-05 public-experience release  
**Purpose:** Improve navigation, visual hierarchy, accessibility, responsive behavior, and public-boundary clarity without changing the JRS methodology or exposing the controlled Review Engine.

## Design direction

JRS uses an editorial research-institution visual language: graphite surfaces, restrained gold accents, high-contrast typography, hairline rules, and concise operational labels. The experience should make the public method easy to understand and use without resembling a public AI software product.

## Implemented surfaces

1. **Homepage:** stronger typography, visual hierarchy, responsive cards, status utility bar, refined actions, and motion with reduced-motion support.
2. **Resources:** clearer visual hierarchy, improved resource cards and starting paths, accessible focus treatment, and mobile-friendly layout.
3. **Review Engine status:** rebuilt as a consistent, accessible status page that makes the public/controlled boundary and all four release gates clear.

## Non-negotiable boundaries

- No public record intake, sandbox, token-gated API, or public evaluation output.
- No visual control or call to action that implies the Engine is available for production use.
- Research about JRS remains distinct from validation of the controlled Engine.
- Public methodology and practitioner tools remain usable without purchase or account creation.
- Any future external controlled use remains subject to the four documented release gates.

## Next safe modernization increment

Apply this design system to remaining high-traffic methodology, research, training, and reference pages. Keep Engine, evaluation, benchmark, implementation-protocol, and non-public evidence materials outside the public experience.
