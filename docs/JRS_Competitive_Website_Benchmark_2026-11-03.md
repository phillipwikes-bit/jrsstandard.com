# JRS competitor website benchmark and controlled changes

**Review date:** 3 November 2026  
**Scope:** Public-facing web pages of ten direct or adjacent providers in investigations, audit, case management and AI governance.  
**Assessment type:** Content and information-architecture review, not hands-on product evaluation, penetration test, accessibility certification, conversion-rate study or user research. Vendor claims are described as vendor claims, not independently verified operating outcomes.

## Decision summary

**Market positioning:** JRS must not claim exclusive ownership of evidence traceability, defensibility or AI-assisted documentation review. Relativity, Case IQ, NAVEX, Optro, OneTrust and Diligent already offer source-linked or reviewable evidence capabilities within substantial platforms. The defensible distinction is a **human-applied, record-level, pre-finalization diagnostic in existing workflows**, using the five JRS review conditions and trained reviewer judgment. JRS does **not** offer SaaS case intake, automated AI risk monitoring, deployed decisions, live case-record upload, software replacement or a certified assessment result.

**Highest-value website change:** Replace an abstract explanation of "workflow fit" with a practical, three-part first-use procedure. Establish (1) the existing approval checkpoint, (2) the five-condition review and source verification, (3) the owner's record-local gap, correction and escalation disposition. Name the artifact the practitioner retains in the organization's existing system. Do not present this as software, a legal conclusion or a measured outcome.

**Second change:** Link the procedure from the homepage task chooser without expanding the seven-link global navigation or inventing a resource count.

**Hold:** The nine current published PDFs have no detected tag structures and remain edition locked. A page-link to request an alternative format is useful but does not cure PDF accessibility. Exact-commit hosted preview, screen-reader signoff and approved tagged reissues are independent release requirements.

## Competitive landscape and website practices

### 1. Case IQ: direct investigation-platform alternative

- **Positioning:** One system from intake to investigation closure for HR, ethics, fraud, Title IX and security cases.
- **Features described:** Custom forms, configurable case routing, investigator-specific AI assistance, dashboards, audit trails, and department-specific workflows.
- **Website experience:** Homepage/product navigation by investigator task and use case; product explainer, high-visibility demo CTA and quantified customer case study. A search-and-filter template directory, downloadable investigation plan template and packaged investigator resources provide task-focused entry.
- **Content strategy:** 2025 case-management benchmark and field templates establish a repeatable discovery route and reason to revisit.
- **Relevance:** Strong illustration of role-to-tool navigation and practice-oriented reference content. Its reported customer gains belong to Case IQ and must not be transferred to JRS. Some templates require a form, whereas JRS public materials are deliberately ungated.
- **JRS response:** Keep existing free tools visible, add a simple first-use procedure, and clarify JRS is not an intake-to-closure system.
- **Primary sources:** https://www.caseiq.com/product/case-management-software ; https://www.caseiq.com/resources/templates ; https://www.caseiq.com/resources/templates/investigation-plan-template ; https://www.caseiq.com/resources/research-reports/investigative-case-management-benchmark-2025

### 2. NAVEX: direct case-documentation and evidentiary adjacency

- **Positioning:** Speak-up, whistleblowing, incident management and case handling through an enterprise platform.
- **Features described:** Secure intake, investigators' assignments, AI-assisted case summaries, traceable case documentation, case timelines and reporting.
- **Website experience:** Tiered product pathways by organization need and region, clear capability sections, feature releases and demo links.
- **Content strategy:** Dated releases and large incident-benchmark publications create a credible product history. The October 8, 2026 product update expressly claims traceability of facts to case details, attachments, notes and follow-ups.
- **Relevance:** Refutes an exclusive claim that JRS alone addresses defensible, traceable AI-assisted investigation records.
- **JRS response:** Position JRS as a separate reviewer question applied to any case platform's retained narrative; do not assert that NAVEX lacks traceability.
- **Primary sources:** https://www.navex.com/en-us/platform/whistleblowing-software-solutions/ ; https://www.navex.com/en-us/platform/product-updates/ ; https://www.navex.com/en-us/platform/analytics-benchmarking/

### 3. Relativity: strong evidence-to-narrative competitor

- **Positioning:** End-to-end investigative data review with AI-assisted search, chronologies, evidence-linked findings and case strategy.
- **Features described:** Preservation/collection, review, AI-assisted fact discovery, timelines, case narrative, source citations, legal hold.
- **Website experience:** Clear stages from assess through preserve, review and build the case, with examples, client stories and a repeated demo CTA.
- **Content strategy:** Use-case content for compliance, finance and cybersecurity plus documented customer examples. A July 2026 release expanded AI-based case strategy.
- **Relevance:** Its evidence linkage and case narrative overlap with JRS language, but its product is a hosted investigative review platform.
- **JRS response:** Present JRS as an independent human review condition at record finalization, not as an alternative evidence-management database.
- **Primary sources:** https://relativity.com/platform/investigations/ ; https://relativity.com/data-solutions/investigations/ ; https://relativity.com/news-events/relativity-announces-generative-ai-powered-solution-for-case-intelligence-now-standard-in-relativityone/

### 4. Ethico: operational clarity and proof cues

- **Positioning:** Ethics hotline, investigator case management, screening, conflict disclosure and compliance analytics.
- **Features described:** Configurable investigator routing, immutable audit trail, integrations, board reporting and benchmark comparison.
- **Website experience:** Concrete capability categories and short outcome-oriented blocks tied to a product-specific next action.
- **Content strategy:** Vendor-stated service and usage figures, support-response and intake benchmarks.
- **Relevance:** Practitioners value records and reviewability, but these figures are Ethico claims, not JRS benchmarks.
- **JRS response:** Show what a JRS reviewer actually checks and records, while avoiding invented adoption/download outcome metrics.
- **Primary sources:** https://www.ethico.com/ ; https://www.ethico.com/hotline-case-management

### 5. Optro (formerly AuditBoard): audit documentation overlap

- **Positioning:** Agentic GRC platform across audit, compliance, risk and infosec. AuditBoard announced its rebranding as Optro in March 2026.
- **Features described:** Autonomous evidence testing, source-annotated workpapers, controls workflows, human review and audit history.
- **Website experience:** High-level paths by practitioner group; succinct product-specific "discover" actions, enterprise proof cues, resource library and demo CTA.
- **Content strategy:** Analyst acknowledgments, use-case resources, operator outcomes, and a direct description of evidence-to-workpaper annotations. Treat claims as vendor reported.
- **Relevance:** Directly competes in claims around AI and traceable evidence workpapers, although it is software infrastructure.
- **JRS response:** Keep JRS's pre-finalization **human documentary sufficiency** check distinguishable from automated workpaper generation and control testing.
- **Primary sources:** https://optro.ai/ ; https://optro.ai/ai-info-for-llms ; https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/meet-optro-auditboard-unveils-new-identity-as-ai-transforms-grc-302707325.html

### 6. Diligent: instructional documentation and workpapers

- **Positioning:** Enterprise GRC and audit software, audit planning through fieldwork and findings.
- **Features described:** Workplan templates, evidence attachments, documented observations, linked testing workpapers, reviewer notes, review-stage reporting and assurance processes.
- **Website experience:** Main site speaks to specific executive audiences. Product documentation provides concrete chronological steps, expected outputs and instructions, rather than only high-level product claims.
- **Content strategy:** Practitioner-oriented documentation exists alongside platform marketing, increasing clarity for new operators.
- **Relevance:** JRS should make its reviewer action just as explicit while not borrowing their claim of automated enterprise audit assurance.
- **JRS response:** Provide three documented first-use steps and a defined local disposition.
- **Primary sources:** https://www.diligent.com/solutions/audit-professionals ; https://help.diligentoneplatform.com/helpdocs/d1p/en-us/Content/get_started/solution_guides/audit_management/executing_your_audit.htm

### 7. OneTrust: structured governance workflow, different control scope

- **Positioning:** AI governance from discovery and inventory to risk assessment, monitoring, runtime enforcement and evidence reporting.
- **Features described:** Control and policy mapping, AI inventories, approvals, source-linked assessments, revalidation, workflow history and enforcement.
- **Website experience:** Product-tour CTA, capability-specific pages and stages that pair purpose, process and expected result. Some interactive experiences are form gated.
- **Content strategy:** Continuously updated feature notes, educational frameworks, risk and governance maturity resources.
- **Relevance:** Evidence and reviewable process are not exclusively JRS capabilities. OneTrust governs the AI estate rather than asking whether a particular retained decision memo stands on its own.
- **JRS response:** Make that unit-of-review distinction explicit. Do not imply JRS performs continuous runtime governance.
- **Primary sources:** https://www.onetrust.com/solutions/ai-governance/ ; https://docs.onetrust.com/en/ai-governance/getting-started/ai-governance-overview ; https://www.onetrust.com/resources/ai-governance-maturity-framework/

### 8. Credo AI: focused control-plane messaging

- **Positioning:** Enterprise AI governance across model and agent lifecycle, marketed as purpose-built.
- **Features described:** Registry, assessment, policy control, monitoring, reporting and enterprise-level governance integrations.
- **Website experience:** Very clear platform headline, short repeated differentiators and expert consultation CTA.
- **Content strategy:** Framework resources, research, analyst acknowledgement and defined application scenarios.
- **Relevance:** Good example of position clarity but not a claim that JRS needs to become another AI platform.
- **JRS response:** Keep the first screen grounded in record reconstructability rather than expanding into AI system inventories.
- **Primary source:** https://www.credo.ai/product

### 9. Holistic AI: three-pillar narrative

- **Positioning:** Continuous enterprise AI discovery, risk testing and policy enforcement.
- **Features described:** Identify, Protect and Enforce; inventory and agents, model testing, policy controls and monitoring.
- **Website experience:** A compact three-stage product story with repeated "explore" destinations, capability sections and clear demo CTA.
- **Content strategy:** Product releases, regulatory content, framework mapping and resource publication.
- **Relevance:** The three-step information pattern is adaptable, but JRS cannot claim these automated capabilities or vendor risk-reduction figures.
- **JRS response:** State practitioner action in three stages using only published JRS conditions.
- **Primary source:** https://www.holisticai.com/

### 10. Workiva: connected evidence and audit documentation

- **Positioning:** Linked governance, financial reporting, audit management and control documentation.
- **Features described:** Permissioned workpapers, embedded audit evidence, collaborative review and documentation trails.
- **Website experience assessed:** Official public corporate documentation, not a full hands-on website usability test.
- **Relevance:** Reinforces that enterprise buyers recognize traceability and audit trails as established software functions.
- **JRS response:** Do not market JRS as a database, evidence repository or audit-management replacement.
- **Source:** https://workiva.gcs-web.com/node/11326/html (Workiva public SEC filing description)

## Prioritized corrective backlog

1. **P0, implement now:** Explain a three-step manual reviewer procedure near the top of existing Workflow Fit page. Link the current record check and five-condition method. Include what remains in the organization's records and what is not transmitted to JRS.
2. **P0, implement now:** Add a contextual link from homepage task chooser to the existing workflow guidance, without adding another global nav item or more cards.
3. **P0, implement now:** Qualify overbroad claims such as "there is no practical intervention point after [record filing]" to acknowledge documented correction after entry, even though earlier review is preferable.
4. **P1:** In future versioned resources, create a controlled role-specific "review note" example. Do not issue a new publication edition without content review, sourcing and approval.
5. **P1:** Improve accessible PDF supply through validated, edition-aware reissues, preserving the original nine locked identities. Independent reading-order and PDF/UA testing remain mandatory.
6. **P1:** Resolve exact-commit protected preview and production release parity, before certifying or merging.
7. **P1:** Acquire documented VoiceOver, desktop screen-reader, zoom and keyboard review outcomes. Automated axe-core success is supporting evidence, not final conformance.
8. **P2:** Track task-entry usage and return-to-resource events only through the existing approved, non-sensitive analytics scheme, without collection of search terms, case content or invented conversion rates.
9. **P2:** Add real approved participant workflows or editorial examples only when their provenance and permissible use are documented. Do not attribute customer results to JRS without evidence.

## Measures and decision rules

- Existing 40 static WCAG-tagged Chromium scans and 12 dynamic-state scans must remain zero-violation at the final exact commit. Expand only without weakening pass thresholds.
- Preserve the 16-resource directory, six existing linked PDF downloads, 10 web/interactive routes, source edition distinctions and the public engine closure.
- First-use guidance must offer distinct steps for input, reviewer action and disposition; remain accessible without JS; avoid case-record submission and proprietary-data capture.
- Public web release remains **HOLD** until protected exact-head preview and final commit/alias verification. Full accessibility certification remains **NOT ESTABLISHED** until accessible PDF editions and independent assistive-technology acceptance.
- Evaluate whether new guidance improves real task completion only after observing consented practitioner use; without those data, report it as an implemented information-architecture improvement, not a conversion lift.

## Method and limitations

All findings are from public vendor pages accessed through web retrieval on 3 November 2026. Product marketing may be aspirational, may differ from deployments, and may change after review. No third-party analytics, A/B results, Lighthouse runs, login-gated flows, pricing negotiations or product trials were obtained. Scoring one vendor against another on conversion rate, realized ROI or accessibility is not supportable here. JRS design/source comparisons use the exact GitHub candidate examined during this review, not an independently inspected live production build.

**Responsible publishing rule:** Do not reproduce competitor copy or logo artwork on the public JRS site. This is a private decision-support research record with citations to primary vendor URLs. It does not authorize commercial claims, release, or certification.
