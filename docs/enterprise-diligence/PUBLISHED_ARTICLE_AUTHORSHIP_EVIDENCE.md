## The DRR definition, as published

P-3 defines Decision Reconstruction Risk as:

> "the condition in which a record cannot explain, on its own terms, why a consequential
> decision was made"

and states that it measures whether an independent reviewer could rebuild the reasoning
from the record alone, separate from whether the decision itself was correct.

**This is materially the same construct the Codebook and the detection study use.**
