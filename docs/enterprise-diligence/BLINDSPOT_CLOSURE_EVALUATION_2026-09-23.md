**Date:** 2026-09-23 · **Subject:** JRS Buyer Readiness Decision Report and Comprehensive
Checklist, revised · **Authority:** analysis only.

## Verdict

**All twelve are addressed. None is closed, and the document says so itself.**

That is the correct outcome, and the revision states it in the strongest line it contains:

> *Status at this revision: These are added decisions and tests. No blind spot is marked
> resolved by writing it into this document.*

test*, to its own response to criticism. It would have been easy to tick twelve boxes.

## Two things the revision does that were not asked for and are correct

**It refuses to take my findings on trust.** The front matter records that the estate files
I cited *were not supplied here*, and instructs that they be treated as *reported estate
findings requiring source verification, not fresh independent findings*. AG-01 makes that a
check. **That is the right response to an assessment arriving without its sources**, and it is
the same discipline the checklist applies to everything else.

**It corrects an overstatement in my own input.** It notes the 15 to 25 percent licensing
figure is *an internal estimate, not a measured probability*. That hedge is in the estate and
I passed the number along without it.

## Four residual gaps

**R1. KP-03 has the supply problem EV-18 was created to solve.** EV-18 exists because
independent reviewers must be found, costed and committed. **KP-03 requires a *competent
permitted outsider* to perform witnessed handoffs and no equivalent item asks where that
person comes from.** The same gap, one section later.

**R2. The hold case has no reconsideration trigger of its own.** AG-05 records the hold case.
The decision rule requires each *activation* to record a reconsideration date, and FR-01
applies to *activated items*. **A hold is neither.** If hold is chosen, nothing schedules the
next look.

**R3. KP-02 prioritises seven functions against a reported twelve.** It names build/deploy,
model change, Codebook revision, research reproduction, rights provenance, publication and
data deletion. **The remaining five material functions are not addressed or explicitly
deferred.** A scope reduction that is not stated as one.

**R4. No item asks what happens to the public layer after a sale.** AG-02 asks what a buyer
gets beyond the public materials and BR-06 records seller-retained rights. **Neither asks
whether the public materials remain public.** A buyer may well want that controlled, and
§II holds that the asset should remain useful and credible with or without a transaction.
This is a term, not a diligence item, and it belongs in BR-01's offer note.

## Smallest meaningful next action

Unchanged, and the revision already names it: **AG-01 and AG-02.** Source-verify the
commercial baseline, then finish the incremental-rights paragraph. The provisional version is
already drafted and is closer to sufficient than anything else in the document.

R1 to R4 are amendments to a working document, not obstacles to using it.

**No blindspot is certified closed by this evaluation, and none of the underlying estate
claims has been independently re-verified here.**
