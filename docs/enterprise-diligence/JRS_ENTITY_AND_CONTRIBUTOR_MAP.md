   reliability raters; `RR-` are comparison-study completers. Conflating them has
   previously produced wrong conclusions and the distinction is preserved here.
2. **Two git identities, one person.** `phillipwikes-bit` and `Phillip Wikes` share one
   email. A reader counting identities would wrongly infer two contributors.
3. **`Claude <noreply@anthropic.com>` is a tool identity, not a person**, and is
   treated in `AI_ASSISTED_CREATION_PROVENANCE_RECORD.md` rather than here.
4. **Metadata names are not contribution evidence.** Billups and Anholzer appear only
   in DOCX authoring metadata, which commonly records a template origin or a machine,
   and no located evidence connects either to the substance of any asset.
