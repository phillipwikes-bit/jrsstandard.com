## The distinction that matters most here

**Consent to be named is not an assignment of rights.** Fifty-eight people contributed
across three studies and thirty-two elected to be named. Those elections are recorded
carefully and honoured. **None of them conveys ownership of anything**, and nothing in
this package should be read as suggesting otherwise.

The same distinction applies to the contributed argument in Section 2.1 of the
detection manuscript. It is credited with permission. Credit is not conveyance.

## A sequencing point already recorded internally

`research/IP_SALE_TRACKER.md` (revision 20, 2026-08-23) records that filing the marks
personally and assigning them afterwards **creates a USPTO recordation gap in the very
chain the filing exists to make airtight**, and that the marks should be filed in the
entity's name instead. That analysis is carried forward here because it is a
chain-of-title finding, not only a cost finding.

## Determination

**The directive's Phase I cannot exit on repository evidence, and that is the
correct outcome rather than a shortfall.** Four matters are with counsel and two
require the owner personally. Every other known matter is documented or stated as
a transaction condition.

**What this does NOT mean.** It does not mean rights are clear, that the estate is
transferable, or that an acquirer would accept these conditions. It means the
**open matters are bounded and named** instead of being discovered during
diligence by someone else.

**No rights were created, resolved or inferred by writing this table.**
