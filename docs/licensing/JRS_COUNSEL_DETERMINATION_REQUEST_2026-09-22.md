# JRS Counsel Determination Request

**Status:** OPEN, COUNSEL DETERMINATION REQUIRED

**Purpose:** Establish the rights that may be offered in a licence, exclusive licence, integration agreement, or asset purchase agreement.

## Controlling boundary

This record does not determine ownership, chain of title, trademark rights, or transfer authority. It presents the factual record and the questions requiring competent legal review. No public statement, technical implementation, repository history, authorship record, contributor consent, or publication event is treated as a substitute for an executed rights instrument.

Until written determinations and any required instruments are complete, JRS may receive and evaluate non-binding licensing, integration, and acquisition inquiries. It must not execute an exclusive licence or asset purchase agreement that conveys rights not established as available.

## Required determinations

### 1. Ownership and chain of title

Identify which JRS assets Phillip Wikes presently owns, controls, or may transfer. Identify any asset for which ownership or transfer authority is unresolved. State what additional executed instruments are required.

### 2. Contributor and co-author permissions

For each contributor and co-author category, determine whether the existing record supports publication, research use, commercial use, licensing, derivative use, successor transfer, and acquisition transfer. Silence must not be treated as permission.

### 3. Licensing and successor-transfer rights

Determine which rights may be granted through:

- an evaluation licence;
- a non-exclusive commercial licence;
- an exclusive licence;
- an OEM or embedded-platform licence;
- a successor-transfer clause;
- an asset purchase agreement.

### 4. Source-code and methodology rights

Determine separately the rights available in the public methodology, Codebook, condition definitions, training materials, Review Engine source code, API implementation, Manifest schema, Manifest implementation, test infrastructure, private datasets, and technical documentation.

### 5. Publication and research-use rights

Determine whether existing permissions support the continued publication, commercial diligence use, licensing support, transfer, and successor maintenance of research materials and participant-derived evidence.

### 6. Trademark language

Determine what statements may properly be made concerning JRS, Justification Review Standard, Decision Reconstruction Risk, DRR, JRS Review Engine, and related names. Address ownership, use, registration status, specimens, goods and services, jurisdiction, transfer, and licence quality-control requirements.

### 7. Exclusive licence and asset-sale package

Identify the assets and rights that may be included, excluded, conditioned, or separately assigned in an exclusive licence or asset purchase agreement. Address derivative rights, sublicensing, territory, duration, source-code access, publication rights, continued creator use, support, liability, confidentiality, representations, warranties, and transferability.

## Required written outputs

## Closure rule

This gate closes only when the Master Register identifies the counsel determination, supporting evidence, affected assets, any executed cure instruments, and the exact rights established as available. A website edit, repository entry, owner instruction, absence of objection, or commercial inquiry does not close the gate.
