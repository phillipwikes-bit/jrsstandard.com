05 / CONTROLS

Owner controls

This is a single-user prototype, not a bank, broker, marketplace, or trading service.

iPhone alerts and response

IncomeOS Priority Study Alerts currently checks for individual, worth-your-time studies on an hourly ChatGPT schedule. Alerts are delivered by ChatGPT when supported and enabled, not by this website.

  1. On iPhone, open Settings → Notifications → ChatGPT.
  2. Enable Allow Notifications and confirm ChatGPT task notifications are enabled.
  3. Open an alert's direct recruiter link, or come here to compare research leads.

This page does not subscribe to browser push and does not automatically synchronize with ChatGPT task results yet.

Security and data boundaries

The preview branch is intended for Vercel-authenticated access. Hidden URLs and noindex do not make a page private. Do not merge this route into the publicly accessible JRS domain until server-side owner authentication, authorization tests and path-specific no-store controls are in place.

Local study status, bookmarks and ledger entries remain on this device only. Clearing browser storage removes them. Do not input account credentials.

Scope

Single-owner research and creative-income discovery, direct recruiter applications, manually verified payments, and realistic investment underwriting. No selling, client acquisition, commercial subscriptions, background financial transactions or performance guarantees.

IncomeOS remains entirely separate from JRS evaluation tooling and protected records.
