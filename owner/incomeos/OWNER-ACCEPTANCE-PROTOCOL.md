# IncomeOS Owner Desk, controlled acceptance protocol
**Status:** pre-commercial engineering, NOT a production acceptance certificate. **Applicable branch:** `verification/incomeos-accounting-ci-20261108`, draft PR #180. **Date:** 2026-11-08.

This protocol converts source-tested claims into reproducible owner acceptance evidence. It does not create accounts, migrate databases, enable revenue, run transactions, accept Decision Spellchecker, or approve publication. The immutable reviewed Git commit and a protected deployment identifier must be entered in the acceptance record before any upgrade of status.

## Required sequence and evidence

1. **Review deployment and authority, before using real data.** Verify the protected Vercel IncomeOS project is SSO restricted to an authorized owner, not an unrestricted preview. Verify Supabase RLS policies and the owner's allowlist identity separately. Reject access as an anonymous and unrelated authenticated account. Capture time, immutable commit, deployment ID, policy proof, and redacted status only. Never collect passwords, bearer tokens, encryption passphrases, bank documents or raw owner records in tickets, screenshots or GitHub.
2. **Confirm append-only hosted encrypted storage.** On a physical iPhone, sign in as the approved owner. Use a **synthetic** workspace with a distinguishable synthetic income, an unpaid entry and an expense. Enter a new passphrase of at least 16 characters privately. Save the encrypted workspace. Confirm the reported save and independently verify that only the authenticated owner's encrypted envelope can be retrieved. A client-side HTTP success message alone is insufficient.
3. **Recover rather than assume persistence.** Reload the page, confirm authentication is cleared and no plaintext is stored in local/session storage, then sign in afresh. Open the encrypted vault copy using the original passphrase. Confirm exact synthetic record identifiers, amounts, receipt statuses, history and Decision Spellchecker programme remain intact. Repeat recovery in a separate authorized browser/device session. Confirm wrong passphrases, tampered ciphertext, and oversized envelopes fail closed without changing a loaded workspace.
4. **Verify revocation and local privacy.** Sign out before an encrypted save, during simulated AES encryption and during decrypted restore. Verify no encrypted upload or restored workspace is accepted after revocation. Confirm the passphrase field is cleared. **Important limitation:** signing out of the vault does not erase an already loaded workspace from screen memory. The user must securely save necessary edits, then close or reload the page to clear the tab.
5. **Separate staged revision testing from real migration.** Apply and independently audit the staged PostgreSQL/RPC migrations on an approved non-production Supabase target before considering any production migration. Confirm owner-scoped CAS append, denied unauthenticated writes, unique ordered revisions, high-water rollback detection, and checksum validation. Document rollback/migration and disaster recovery procedures. Source tests and the disposable PostgreSQL workflow alone do not authorize activation.

   The preparation and non-destructive rollback sequence is in `engineering/incomeos/REVISION-MIGRATION-AND-ROLLBACK.md`. The legacy vault and staged revision service use distinct allowlists. A valid legacy login is not evidence of staged authorization. A stalled vault upload requires an integrity-verified fresh recovery read before another save in the same page session; the hold is not durable across reloads. The staged server revision protocol remains necessary for cross-device conflict protection.
6. **Test editing conflicts without overwriting records.** Open the same synthetic revision in two authorized sessions. Save different edits explicitly. One must win; the stale edit must be quarantined and offered as a **locally encrypted draft**, not silently merged or overwritten. Verify original change history survives, then reopen and confirm the newer server revision before saving an intentionally reconciled ledger. Repeat a timeout after the server commits; neither retry nor automatic background action may duplicate a row.
7. **Assess Decision Spellchecker source identity.** Verify DS-A original archive, version (including original 0.6.11 / mobile 0.7.0 if applicable), checksum, owner/licence rights, executable reproducibility, original Codebook and exact SP-01 through SP-18 source contract. Log **each** SP acceptance decision and evidence against the authentic source. The synthetic DS-N1 reviewer, the read-only Owner Desk panel, and the JRS method are different assets. An absent original requirement is NOT_ACCEPTED.
8. **Physical iPhone and routine operation.** Perform normal navigation, form entry, edits, deletion, restore, encrypted backup, Decision Spellchecker inspection and owner sign-out on the actual iPhone. Check accessibility, touch targets, scrollability, network interruption, page reload, failed authentication and recovery from a lost response. For a declared observation window, record scheduled check outcomes, owner interventions, failures, stale-source alerts, unresolved conflicts, and manual correction effort. **No percentage of unattended operation may be reported without observed executions and denominators.** Accounting edits and transfers remain owner-directed: do not turn on automatic transaction execution.
9. **Verify operational boundaries.** Review key rotation and database availability, backup and restore custody, the limitations of the Supabase free plan (including possible inactivity pause and absent automated backups), alert ownership, support/escalation triggers, incident logging, reconciliation procedures, and recoverability without the original device. Verify that a working local encrypted backup can be restored, not merely downloaded.
10. **Owner release decision.** Upgrade each of the 16 acceptance items only on independent, immutable and dated evidence. A14 live owner storage/recovery and A16 explicit approval remain BLOCKED until their actual conditions have been met. Merge, production promotion, customer onboarding, billing and financial automation remain prohibited until **all** items are PRODUCTION VERIFIED and the owner issues a separate explicit commercial authorization.

## Minimal required acceptance log per observation
- Test identifier, date/time and observer role
- Version/commit, deployment identity, protocol and environment (synthetic/staging/owner production)
- Scenario, expected result, actual result and pass/fail
- Redacted evidence location and independent reviewer (if required)
- Defect reference, corrective commit, retest result and rollback plan
- Owner approval reference, only if approval has actually been issued

**No source CI pass, browser emulation, disposable database result, automation cadence or reported income constitutes release approval.** No real financial records should be introduced into an unaccepted test environment.
