## 1. The reframe, and why

Both editors read the instrument, not the cases, as the paper's centre. Nothing in the
science needs changing. What needs changing is which category an editor drops it into in
the ninety seconds they spend deciding.

**Move the protocol out of the title and out of the first sentence of the abstract.**
Lead with the corpus and with the one result that does not depend on the instrument or on
the authors' own words: the five-of-five convergence with independent Comptroller audits.

### Title options, for the first author to choose

1. **Documentation Quality in 32 Public-Records Determinations: A Two-State Case Study**
2. **When the Record Cannot Explain the Decision: 32 Public-Records Determinations in New York and Connecticut**
3. **Can the Basis Be Rebuilt? A Case Study of 32 Public-Records Determinations**

Option 1 is the most case-study-forward and the least likely to be filed as an instrument
paper. Option 3 keeps closest to the current title.

### Reframed abstract

> This study examines 32 publicly available public-records determinations and audits from
> 32 distinct sources, issued between 2005 and 2026 across four document classes and two
> United States jurisdictions. Each case was assessed for whether an independent reviewer
> could reconstruct the documented basis of the determination from the record alone, using
> a three-level read recorded before the documented outcome was consulted.
>
> Five of the cases were programmes that a State or City Comptroller had independently
> audited. In all five, the record-level assessment and the auditor's own finding agreed
> that the programme could not adequately evidence its public-records administration. The
> reads also differed across document classes according to how far each source exposed the
> underlying basis, and were not associated with appellate outcome, which is consistent
> with reconstructability and appellate disposition being different properties.
>
> The study is a pilot. The corpus is small, non-random and limited to two jurisdictions;
> 22 of the 32 cases carry a single reviewer, and a blind second read of 10 cases returned
> 70 percent agreement. It does not establish that applying the review improves
> documentation or reduces adverse findings.

**Word count of the reframed abstract: within normal journal limits. Verify against the
chosen venue before sending.**

## 4. Zenodo

**What it is.** A repository operated by **CERN and OpenAIRE** on a non-profit basis, free
to use, meeting the requirements of major research funders. It assigns a **DOI**
automatically, and supports a **concept DOI** covering all versions alongside a **version
DOI** for each deposit.

**What it is not.** It is not peer review and it is not a journal. A Zenodo deposit is a
preprint. It confers a citable identifier and a timestamp. It confers no editorial
validation, and the manuscript should not be described as published because it is there.

### Does it prejudice the journal route?

**Generally no, with two qualifications.** Under ICMJE recommendations and most major
publisher policies, posting a preprint does not count as prior publication provided it is
disclosed at submission, labelled as not peer reviewed, and linked to the eventual version
of record. **But it can affect an individual journal's editorial priority, a minority of
journals apply narrower rules, and archives and records is a field where preprinting is
less established than in the sciences.**

**So the inquiry above asks the question directly.** Four editors answering it costs
nothing and removes the guess.

### What matters for this estate specifically

- **A deposit is effectively permanent.** Files cannot be added to a published record;
  corrections are made by publishing a new version that links to the original. Treat a
  deposit as irreversible disclosure, because in practice it is.
- **The default licence is CC-BY 4.0**, which permits commercial reuse and derivatives
  with attribution. The estate's commercial rights position is **unresolved and with
  counsel (D-6)**. Choosing the licence is therefore a decision to take with counsel, not
  a default to accept. **This is a factor to raise, not a legal conclusion.**
- **An embargo is available**, and it is the option that fits this situation best: the
  record is deposited and timestamped now, metadata visible, **files withheld until the
  embargo lifts**. That establishes chronology for the estate without public disclosure
  while journals are still considering it.
- **Publication is a Section 23 act.** A public deposit requires the publication review and
  human approval, whatever the licence.
- **Both authors decide.** A preprint under both names is a publication decision belonging
  to the first author as much as to the owner.

### Recommendation on Zenodo

**Do not post publicly yet.** Ask the four editors the preprint question first; it is one
sentence and it is already in the inquiry. If a chosen venue permits preprints, post then,
with the licence decided rather than defaulted.

**If a timestamped record is wanted now**, deposit under **embargo** with restricted files.
That captures the date without making the disclosure, and the embargo can be lifted
deliberately once the venue question is settled.
