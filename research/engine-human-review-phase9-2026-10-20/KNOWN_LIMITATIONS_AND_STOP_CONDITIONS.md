- Engine predictions were made by reading source. One prediction was wrong (R9-CON-04); others could have been right for reasons the author did not intend.

- Stability transforms are presentation changes on synthetic records (44 transforms); they are not a robustness result.
- Every reliability measure is not measurable locally.
