Decision model

Seven outcomes. The package never selects, infers or fills one in.

OutcomeMinimum basisProhibited reasoningEscalation
supports_findingAt least one verified quotation whose actual words, not an inferred meaning, carry the support, with the reviewer naming those words.Support inferred from the presence of a quotation, from the Engine status, from the outcome of the decision, or from what the author probably meant.None, unless a conflict indicator or missing information remains unresolved; then escalate instead.
does_not_support_findingThe reviewer states what the quoted words actually say and why that does not support the finding.Rejecting support because the decision seems wrong, because of the outcome, or because of a guessed motive.Escalate if other record text might carry the support but is not cited.
insufficient_contextThe reviewer names the specific missing information.Filling the gap with assumptions or with knowledge outside the record.Always escalates.
ambiguousThe reviewer states at least two readings and why the record does not decide between them.Choosing the reading that fits the outcome.Always escalates.
record_conflictThe reviewer cites the conflicting passages.Deciding which passage is true.Always escalates.
outside_authorized_scopeThe reviewer names the scope boundary that applies.Reviewing the material anyway, or deciding the excluded question.Always escalates; the item is not reviewed further.
abstain_and_escalateThe reviewer states why no other outcome can be reached.Abstaining to avoid recording a disagreement with the Engine.Always escalates to the adjudicator.

Reviewers are never asked to decide: legal liability; discrimination; policy compliance; whether an employment decision was correct.

Cognitive Controls

Structured review safeguards. Each names a review risk and asks a neutral question; none directs a result. No clinical or diagnostic claim is made.

CC-INTENT · Intent inference

Review riskInferring why someone acted from the outcome alone.

Which words in the record state a reason, and which reasons are being supplied from the outcome rather than from the record?

CC-PLAUSIBLE · Plausible explanation is not evidence

Review riskTreating a reasonable-sounding explanation as if the record established it.

Is this explanation stated in the record, or is it one possible reading that the record does not state?

CC-NEGATION · Negation, qualification and condition

Review riskMissing "not", "unless", "only if" or similar words that change what the text says.

Does the quoted text contain a negation, qualification or condition, and how does it change what the text says?

CC-DATES · Event, document and decision dates

Review riskMerging the date something happened, the date it was written and the date it was decided.

For each date in the quotation, is it an event date, a document date or a decision date, and does the sequence hold?

CC-WORDS · Actual words versus assumed meaning

Review riskReading meaning into a quotation that its words do not carry.

What does the quotation literally say, without adding meaning that is not in its words?

CC-MISSING · Missing information

Review riskCompleting gaps in the record with assumptions.

What information would be needed to answer the reviewer question that the record does not contain?

CC-BASIS · Stated basis versus conclusion

Review riskTreating a stated conclusion as if it were its own basis.

Which text is the conclusion, and which text, if any, is the stated basis for it?

CC-ESCALATE · Escalation when reconstruction is not possible

Review riskCompleting a review that the record cannot support instead of escalating.

Can this condition be reviewed from the record as it stands, or does the review need to be escalated?

Limitations and escalation

  • Escalation triggers come from the Engine output only: ET1 No verified quotation is cited for this condition. ET2 A conflict indicator applies to this condition. ET3 The condition was not assessed or the record was refused.
  • The rehearsal set is synthetic and engineering-authored by the team that built the layer. It is holdout-ineligible. Expectations are authoring judgments, not labels.
  • First evaluator run: 49 of 50 packets matched the seal. One differed (escalation_as_sealed): A low-versus-unresolved risk conflict can reach the reviewer without an Engine conflict indicator. Detection of this conflict then rests on the human reviewer and the CC-WORDS control.
  • Every reliability measure is defined and marked not measurable locally. Nothing in this workbench is a performance figure.
  • The dual-review protocol is execution-disabled. No reviewer, label or outcome exists.
