Subject: Enquiry: would Clearinghouse consider a study of public-records documentation quality?

Dear NAGARA,

I am writing to ask whether Clearinghouse would consider a contribution, and if so what length and form you prefer.

We have a 32-case study of United States public-records determinations, drawn from 32 distinct public sources across four document classes and two states, covering decisions issued between 2005 and 2026. It asks whether the documented basis of a determination can be independently reconstructed from the record itself after the original decision-maker is gone.

In the five cases where a State or City Comptroller had independently audited the same programme, our record-level assessment and the auditor's own finding agreed in all five that the programme could not adequately evidence its public-records administration.

The audience we had in mind is exactly yours: government records administrators who will be asked, eventually, to explain a determination they did not make.

I am a Deputy Records Access Officer and my co-author developed the review protocol. We would be glad to write to whatever length suits the newsletter.

Would this be of interest?

Sincerely,

Stacyann Young and Phillip Wikes
