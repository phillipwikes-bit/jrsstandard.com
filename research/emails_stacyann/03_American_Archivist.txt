Subject: Presubmission enquiry ahead of the January window

Dear Professor Cary,

I am writing ahead of the January submission window to ask whether the following would fall within the scope of The American Archivist.

We have a 32-case study of United States public-records determinations, drawn from 32 distinct public sources across four document classes and two jurisdictions, covering decisions issued between 2005 and 2026. It asks whether the documented basis of a determination remains independently reconstructable from the record itself, which we frame through Duranti on reliability and authenticity and Yeo on records as persistent representations of activities.

In the five cases where a State or City Comptroller had independently audited the same programme, our record-level assessment and the auditor's own finding agreed in all five. The assessment also differentiated document classes by how far each exposed the underlying basis, and was not associated with appellate outcome, which separates evidentiary sufficiency from legal result.

The manuscript is approximately 5,600 words and an anonymised version is prepared for double-blind review.

Would this be appropriate to submit when the window opens on 1 January?

Sincerely,
