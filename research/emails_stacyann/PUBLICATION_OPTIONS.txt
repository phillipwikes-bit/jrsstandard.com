EVERY PUBLICATION OPTION FOR THE 32-CASE PUBLIC-RECORDS STUDY
Stacyann Young and Phillip Wikes. 2026-09-23.
================================================================================

Read the tiers as a trade. Acceptance likelihood and credential value move in
opposite directions. Nothing on this list is both certain and prestigious. Pick
which one you are buying.

Addresses marked VERIFIED were read off the organisation's own page. Anything
not verified says so. No address on this list was guessed.


================================================================================
TIER 1. GUARANTEED. NO GATEKEEPER. NOT PEER REVIEW.
================================================================================

ZENODO                                                    zenodo.org
  What it is: a repository run by CERN and OpenAIRE, non-profit, free. You
  upload, it issues a permanent DOI, the work is citable forever.
  Acceptance: certain. Nobody reviews it. There is no decision.
  Cost: none.
  Worth: a citable, timestamped, public record with your names on it. It is a
  preprint. It is not a peer-reviewed credential and must never be described as
  published in a journal.
  Notable: it offers an EMBARGO. You can deposit today, keep the files private,
  and lift it later. That captures the date without spending the disclosure
  while journals are still considering it.
  Caution: a deposit is effectively permanent. You cannot delete a published
  record, only add a new version. Default licence is CC-BY 4.0, which permits
  commercial reuse, and your commercial rights position is still with counsel.

SSRN                                                      ssrn.com
  What it is: the Social Science Research Network, owned by Elsevier. The
  standard preprint venue for law, public policy and public administration.
  Acceptance: effectively certain. There is a light editorial screen for
  obvious spam, not a review.
  Cost: none.
  Worth: more than Zenodo for this paper, because law and public policy people
  genuinely read and cite SSRN. Still a preprint, not peer review.

OSF PREPRINTS                                             osf.io/preprints
  What it is: the Open Science Framework's preprint service.
  Acceptance: certain. Cost: none.
  Worth: similar to Zenodo. Choose one preprint home, not three.

YOUR OWN SITE, LINKEDIN, MEDIUM
  Acceptance: certain. Cost: none.
  Worth: zero as a credential. Useful for reach, nothing else. Posting the full
  paper here can complicate a later journal submission, so do not do it while
  any venue is considering the work.

TIER 4. MODERATE. ACADEMIC PEER REVIEW. REAL CREDENTIAL VALUE.
================================================================================

STATE AND LOCAL GOVERNMENT REVIEW        mscicc@polisci.ufl.edu     VERIFIED
  What it is: a Sage journal on state and local government, edited by Michael
  Scicchitano at the University of Florida. Submission via SAGEtrack.
  Why it is the best academic fit found: the corpus IS state and local
  government. New York and Connecticut determinations, New York State and New
  York City Comptroller audits.
  Acceptance: moderate. This is real academic peer review.

JOURNAL OF BEHAVIORAL PUBLIC ADMINISTRATION        journal-bpa.org portal
  What it is: an open access journal on behavioural approaches to public
  administration. No author fees, stated as a deliberate design choice.
  Length: Research Article up to 4,000 words. Research Letter up to 2,000.
  APA 7th edition. Anonymised PDF plus author-identified Word.
  Why it fits unusually well: it runs an active symposium on NULL RESULTS and
  explicitly encourages exploratory work below conventional significance
  thresholds. This paper reports a null, a wide interval and an unmet criterion
  and refuses to soften them. No other venue on this list has said it wants that.

GOVERNMENT INFORMATION QUARTERLY          Elsevier Editorial Manager
  What it is: an Elsevier journal on the intersection of policy, information
  technology, government and the public. Draws on public administration,
  information science and law.
  Acceptance: lower. Well regarded, high volume of submissions.
  No presubmission address is published.

INFORMATION POLITY                        Sage submission system
  What it is: a journal on the impact of information and communication
  technologies on governance and public administration, now published by Sage.
  Length: academic articles should not exceed 6,000 words including footnotes.
  Author instructions: journals.sagepub.com/author-instructions/ipo

THE AMERICAN ARCHIVIST         americanarchivist@archivists.org      VERIFIED
  What it is: the flagship journal of the Society of American Archivists.
  Editor Amy Cooper Cary, also amy.cary@marquette.edu.
  Review: double blind.
  TIMING IS A HARD GATE: submission windows are 1 January to 15 February and
  1 June to 15 July. Both are currently shut. Manuscripts arriving outside a
  window are not sent for review until the next one opens. An enquiry can go now.
  Fit: the paper already cites Duranti in Archivaria and Yeo in this journal, so
  it is in conversation with its literature.
