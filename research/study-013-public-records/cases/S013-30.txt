Investigative record summary (background section of a published federal-sector EEO appellate decision; outcome text removed).

At the time of events giving rise to this complaint, Complainant worked as an Intermittent Food Inspector (GS-7) at the Agency’s Smithfield Food facility in Red Springs, North Carolina. On or about March 25, 2022, Complainant reported an incident of alleged sexual harassment by one of her coworkers (“Coworker”). Complainant complained when the Coworker stated that she “could take two fingers and stick them up [Complainant’s] ass.” Complainant averred that she could not report the incident to her first-line supervisor (“Supervisor 1”), who was related to the Coworker, so Complainant reported the matter to another supervisor (“Supervisor 2”). Complainant noted that the Coworker was moved to a different facility, and she had not seen her since the incident. Report of Investigation (ROI) at 238, 61. Complainant initiated the instant complaint on May 3, 2022, and on June 29, 2022, she filed an EEO complaint alleging that the Agency discriminated against her based on her age (60), and in reprisal for her prior protected EEO activity of opposition to discrimination and harassment, when: 1. on May 2, 2022, Complainant learned that she was not selected for a GS-1863-7, Food Inspector position, advertised under vacancy announcement number FSIS- 22-MCE-11426010; and 2. on May 2, 2022, Complainant learned that she was not selected for a GS-1863- 5/7, Food Inspector position, advertised under vacancy announcement number FSIS-22-MCE-11416244. At the conclusion of the investigation, the Agency provided Complainant with a copy of the ROI and notice of her right to request a hearing before an EEOC Administrative Judge. When the Agency did not receive notice of a request for a hearing or a final decision, 2 it issued a final decision pursuant to 29 C.F.R. § 1614.110(b), concluding that Complainant failed to prove that she was subjected to discrimination as alleged.
