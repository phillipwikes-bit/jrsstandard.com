Investigative record summary (background section of a published federal-sector EEO appellate decision; outcome text removed).

At the time of events giving rise to this complaint, Complainant worked as a Claims Technical Expert (CTE), GS-0105-12, at the Agency’s Great Lakes Program Service Center in Chicago, Illinois. Complainant received a reprimand on July 20, 2021, for conduct unbecoming a federal employee. The reprimand cited two specifications: on June 16, 2021, Complainant refused to provide a status update on assigned cases, raised her voice, and continued to refuse during a subsequent meeting; and on June 23, 2021, she refused assistance with a mainframe password issue, leading to a two-hour work delay. Complainant’s first-line supervisor, Module Manager (S1), asserted that requesting status updates and assisting with password issues were common management practices. Complainant also claimed workplace harassment, alleging that S1 assigned duties preventing her from completing primary CTE tasks and treated her differently from other CTEs. She complained of accusatory, condescending, and rude communication. Complainant alleged that the reprimand itself constituted harassment, tarnishing her character, and that the issuance of a 3.0 Performance Assessment Communications Systems (PACS) rating for Fiscal Year 2021, lower than a prior 4.5 PAC rating in Fiscal Year 2019, was harassment. Complainant reported these concerns to the union and management. S1 denied the allegations explaining that work assignments aligned with the week's plan and that management routinely assisted employees with password issues. S1 denied claims of harassment in assigning duties and asserted that the reprimand and rating were issued in line with standard practices. On November 11, 2021, Complainant filed an EEO complaint alleging that the Agency discriminated against her and subjected her to a hostile work environment on the bases of race (African-American), sex (female), color (Black), age (63), and in reprisal for prior protected EEO activity when: 1. On July 20, 2021, she received a reprimand; and 2. Beginning June 23, 2021 and present, with regard to work assignments, assignment of duties, condescending comments, spoken to in a loud voice, lowered PACS, reprimand, and questioning time to work on inquiries. 2 At the conclusion of the investigation, the Agency provided Complainant with a copy of the report of investigation and notice of her right to request a hearing before an Equal Employment Opportunity Commission Administrative Judge (AJ). When Complainant did not request a hearing within the time frame provided in 29 C.F.R. § 1614.108(f), the Agency issued a final decision pursuant to 29 C.F.R. § 1614.110(b). In the decision, the Agency determined that Complainant was not subjected to discrimination or reprisal as alleged. The Agency dismissed an additional allegation as untimely raised with an EEO Counselor. The Commission will, however, consider the claim as background evidence in support of Complainant’s hostile work environment claim.
