2015) (explaining that the de novo standard of review “requires that the Commission
examine the record without regard to the factual and legal determinations of the

To prevail in a disparate treatment claim, a complainant must satisfy the three-part
evidentiary scheme fashioned by the Supreme Court in McDonnell Douglas Corp.
v. Green, 411 U.S. 792 (1973). A complainant must initially establish a prima facie
case by demonstrating that he was subjected to an adverse employment action under
circumstances that would support an inference of discrimination. Furnco Constr.
Co. v. Waters, 438 U.S. 567, 576 (1978). Proof of a prima facie case will vary
depending on the facts of the particular case. McDonnell Douglas, 441 U.S. at 804
n.14.    The burden then shifts to the agency to articulate a legitimate,
nondiscriminatory reason for its actions. Texas Dep’t of Cmty. Affairs v. Burdine,
450 U.S. 248, 253 (1981). To ultimately prevail, a complainant must prove, by a
preponderance of the evidence, that the agency’s explanation is a pretext for
discrimination. Reeves v. Sanderson Plumbing Products, Inc., 530 U.S. 133, 143
(2000); St. Mary’s Honor Ctr v. Hicks, 509 U.S. 502, 519 (1993).

The Agency articulated legitimate, nondiscriminatory reasons for placing
Complainant in a temporary light-duty status. The record contains a declaration
under penalty of perjury from S1. Therein, S1 asserts that Complainant was placed
in a temporary light-duty status based on a pending investigation regarding
allegations that Complainant used a government vehicle outside of his scheduled
duty hours. 3 Complaint File at 178. The record contains a memorandum for the
record dated October 11, 2024, from a Supervisory Police Officer setting forth that
Complainant requested use of a government vehicle in August 2024.4 Complaint
File at 184. The record also contains a declaration from S2 corroborating S1’s

Complainant failed to establish, by a preponderance of evidence, that the Agency’s
articulated reason was pretext for retaliation. While Complainant, in his declaration,
asserts that he believes S1 and S2 took the action at issue based on him filing an
EEO complaint against a named Agency official, we find that this mere assertion is
insufficient to establish pretext. The record also contains a declaration from
Complainant’s co-worker. Therein, the co-worker stated that he had conversations
with S2, who set forth that a named Agency official 5 instructed S2 to place
Complainant on light duty. Complaint File at 210. This assertion also does not
establish that the Agency’s motivation for placing Complainant in a light-duty status
was based on his prior protected activity (rather than the articulated reason of
allegations that Complainant used a government vehicle while in off-duty status).
Complainant did not request a hearing before an EEOC AJ in this matter, and as a
result we do not have the benefit of an AJ’s credibility determinations. We find that
the preponderance of the evidence in the record does not establish that the Agency’s
legitimate, nondiscriminatory reason is pretextual.

We AFFIRM the Agency’s final decision finding no discrimination.

The Commission may, in its discretion, reconsider this appellate decision if
Complainant or the Agency submits a written request that contains arguments or

      1. The appellate decision involved a clearly erroneous interpretation of

      2. The appellate decision will have a substantial impact on the policies,
         practices, or operations of the agency.

                    RIGHT TO REQUEST COUNSEL (Z0815)

If you want to file a civil action but cannot pay the fees, costs, or security to do so,

paying these fees or costs. Similarly, if you cannot afford an attorney to represent

must submit the requests for waiver of court costs or appointment of an
attorney directly to the court, not the Commission. The court has the sole
discretion to grant or deny these types of requests. Such requests do not alter the
time limits for filing a civil action (please read the paragraph titled Complainant’s
Right to File a Civil Action for the specific time limits).
