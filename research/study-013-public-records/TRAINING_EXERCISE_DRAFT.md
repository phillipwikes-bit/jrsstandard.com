# "Spot the gap": a free training exercise (DRAFT)

**Draft 2026-10-02 for the IAOHRA and NFHTA route (COMPANION_PLAN.md, target 5).** Not for distribution until the owner approves and the publication review is done. Free, under D8.

**Source:** six published EEOC federal-sector appellate decisions, with the outcome text removed (`cases/`). These are employment cases. For fair-housing audiences, say so at the start.

## Session plan (60 to 90 minutes)
1. **10 minutes. Framing.** An agency's final decision must rest on an "impartial and appropriate factual record". When the record falls short, the EEOC sends the case back for more investigation. Today's question is not *who should win*, but *is this record complete enough to decide?*
2. **35 minutes. Individual review.** Each participant reads three cases and fills in the worksheet.
3. **25 minutes. Reveal and discuss.** For each case, what did the EEOC do, and what did it order the agency to obtain? Compare with the worksheets.
4. **10 minutes. Close.** The pre-closing checklist (D1 to D10, below).

**Facilitator note:** in this set the sent-back records are shorter than the upheld ones. Tell participants not to judge by length. A short record can be complete, and a long one can miss the key witness.

## Worksheet (one per case)
1. Who made the decision being challenged? Is their own account in the record, under oath?
2. Which witnesses are named? Were all of them interviewed? If not, does the record say why?
3. Are comparators identified, with the information needed to compare them?
4. Which documents does the decision rely on? Are they in the record?
5. Is management's reason explained, or only stated?
6. Is every accepted claim or incident investigated?
7. Are dates clear, including when management learned of the problem?
8. **Your call:** complete enough to decide / needs a closer look / gaps to fill first. Name the first three items you would request.

The questions follow the JRS conditions in plain words: 1 and 2 are RC5; 4 is RC2; 5 is RC4; 7 is RC3; 6 is RC1 and RC5.

**Checklist to close the session (D1 to D10, `COMPANION_PLAN.md` section 2):**
- the decision-maker's sworn account;
- every named witness, or a documented attempt;
- comparators;
- the documents relied on;
- reasons explained, not just stated;
- every accepted claim investigated;
- the accommodation process, where relevant;
- dates of notice;
- failed attempts documented.
