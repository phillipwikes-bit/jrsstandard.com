# Evaluation Measurement Dictionary

**LOCAL ENGINEERING MATERIAL ONLY. INDEPENDENT EVALUATION NOT AUTHORIZED OR CONDUCTED. NO_GO_NOT_VERIFIED REMAINS IN EFFECT.**

Definitions only. No measure has been calculated, estimated or observed, and no figure here describes the Engine. Measures are calculated only at Gate E of an authorized evaluation, on sealed evaluation material, after adjudication.

| Id | Measure | Definition | Unit | Calculated |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | quote-verification precision | Of quotations the candidate marks as verified, the share that labelers confirm as present at the recorded offsets. | proportion | no |
| M2 | quote-verification recall | Of quotations labelers identify as present and relevant, the share the candidate marks as verified. | proportion | no |
| M3 | routing agreement | Share of conditions where the candidate route (supported, review_required, not_assessed, refusal) matches the adjudicated label. | proportion | no |
| M4 | false-refusal rate | Share of in-scope records the candidate refuses. | proportion | no |
| M5 | missed-conflict rate | Share of labeled chronology or control conflicts the candidate does not report. | proportion | no |
| M6 | inter-rater agreement | Chance-corrected agreement between independent labelers before adjudication; statistic named at freeze. | coefficient | no |
| M7 | adjudication rate | Share of labeled items that required adjudication. | proportion | no |
| M8 | abstention or review-required rate | Share of conditions the candidate routes to review_required or not_assessed. | proportion | no |

## Conventions to fix at protocol freeze (Gate B)

- Unit of analysis for each measure (quotation, condition, record).
- The chance-corrected agreement statistic for M6.
- How abstention (review_required, not_assessed) enters M3 and M4.
- Confidence-interval method and minimum sample sizes.
- Treatment of records refused at intake in each denominator.
