# Positioning lines: slides, LinkedIn, decks

**SCOPE RULE, applies to every line below.** A line citing **16** describes the detection study alone. A line citing **36** describes both review arms. A line citing **58** describes every independent expert who has graded records for this programme, across all three studies. Never use a detection-only figure where the subject is the programme: it under-credits 42 people who did the work. The verified figures at lock are `reviewers_all` 58, `completers_all` 36, `completers_detection` 16, `completers_comparison` 20, `countries_all` 16, `countries_detection` 11.

**These are marketing copy. They do not go in the manuscript.** The paper makes no efficacy claim and clears a detection threshold only; a line like "bulletproof standard" would contradict Section 4.8 and hand a reviewer an easy objection. Two lines from the supplied set are already in the paper at academic register: the epigraph, and the local-versus-global contrast in Section 2.1.

Each line below is marked for what the evidence actually supports at data lock 2026-08-15.

| Line | Attach |

| **If it hasn't been stress-tested across borders, it isn't ready.** | Pair with "83.9% detection accuracy against a key fixed before scoring, 16 experts, 11 countries." Without a number this reads as a claim about competitors. |
| **A framework isn't proven until it's globally challenged.** | Same. "Proven" is a strong word; anchor it to the pre-registered threshold that was actually cleared. |

## DO NOT USE

| Line | Why |
|---|---|
| **"One bulletproof standard"** | The paper makes no efficacy claim. JRS is in a validation phase. "Bulletproof" asserts something no study has tested and one study has explicitly declined to claim. |
| **"Tested globally. Trusted everywhere."** | "Trusted everywhere" is an adoption claim. Adoption is zero. This is the line most likely to be quoted back at you. |
| **"Pressure-tested worldwide, built to stand anywhere."** | "Built to stand anywhere" is a durability claim with no supporting study. |
| **"battle-test"** | Fine in a deck, wrong next to a p-value. Keep it out of anything a journal reviewer or a buyer's counsel will read. |
| **Any line implying the method beats unaided judgment** | The comparison study closed on 2026-08-15 and **did not meet its pre-registered Floor 3**: B1 75.00% versus B2 67.63%, difference +7.37 points, 95% CI -15.25 to +29.99, p = 0.503. Claiming superiority contradicts your own registered analysis. |

## The executive one-liner, corrected

Supplied:

> "If it's not globally stress-tested, it isn't resilient. We brought together diverse, multi-lingual reviewers worldwide to battle-test this framework, not just evaluate it in a single jurisdiction."

Usable version, same force, nothing unsupported:

> **If it hasn't been stress-tested across borders, it hasn't been tested. We put this framework in front of credentialed reviewers in 11 countries across 5 continents, working in multiple first languages, blind to a verified answer key. They identified unreconstructable records at 83.9 percent accuracy against a threshold set before anyone saw the data.**

That version carries the number, names the blind, names the pre-registration, and makes no claim the study did not test.
