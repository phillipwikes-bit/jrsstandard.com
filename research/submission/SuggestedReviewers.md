# Suggested and Opposed Reviewers

*Editorial Manager usually asks for 3 to 5 suggested reviewers (optional but recommended; it speeds handling). The editor is not obliged to use them. HONESTY CONSTRAINT: do not invent reviewer names or email addresses. Fabricated reviewer contacts are a serious integrity breach and a known desk-reject and retraction trigger. Fill this from people you can actually verify. The profiles below define who to look for; you supply real names and institutional emails.*

## Rules before you list anyone
- No one at your or the co-author's institution, no collaborators, co-authors, or anyone who has seen the work (that excludes every JRS reviewer, mentor, or LinkedIn contact who engaged with the standard, including the panel).
- Use an institutional email you can verify (a faculty page), never a personal address.
- Spread across countries and institutions.

## Profiles to fill (supply real name + verified institutional email + one-line reason)

1. **Inter-rater reliability / agreement statistics.** A methodologist who publishes on chance-corrected agreement (Gwet's AC1, kappa paradox). Reason: can assess the statistical core.
2. **Responsible AI / AI governance.** A researcher in AI accountability or algorithmic documentation. Reason: fits the journal's scope and the DRR framing.
3. **Records / evidence in a domain (HR, public administration, audit, or law).** An academic who studies documentation quality or administrative evidence. Reason: can judge construct relevance.
4. **Measurement / psychometrics (optional 4th).** Someone on construct validity of rating instruments. Reason: speaks to the staged-validation logic.
5. **Human-centred computing / STS (optional 5th).** Aligns with the journal's editorial board interests.
