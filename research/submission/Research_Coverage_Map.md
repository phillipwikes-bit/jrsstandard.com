## Why it is not one giant paper (this is the correct strategy)

1. **Different co-authors and venues.** The FOIL paper is co-authored with Stacy Young; the HR paper with the Business Ethics contributors; the legal article with Gabriela Bar. Merging them would break earned-authorship boundaries.
2. **Different maturity.** The real-case pilots (n=7, n=5) are below their pre-registered targets and gated. Bolting immature data onto the flagship would drag its credibility down, not lift it.
3. **Focus wins in peer review.** Reviewers reject sprawling papers that try to prove everything at once. Four focused papers that each earn one claim are worth more, individually and to a buyer, than one unfocused mega-paper.
4. **IP value.** A *program* of related papers (detection, criterion validity, governance) reads as a substantial, defensible body of work in diligence. That is the stronger asset.
