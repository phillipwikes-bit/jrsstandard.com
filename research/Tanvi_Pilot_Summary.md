# HR / Employment Pilot: Program Summary

*Lead: Tanvi Pokhriyal (reviewer ID V-HR-01), HR/employment practitioner, UAE. Prepared 2026-07-27. Figures are as verified in the study database; self-reported additions are labeled as such. JRS is in an operational validation phase; no proven-effectiveness claim is made.*

## Mission
The HR/Employment pilot is the human-resources leg of the Justification Review Standard (JRS) real-case validity study (Rung 3, criterion validity). Its purpose is to test JRS against reality: does a JRS review of a real HR record predict how that record actually holds up when it is later challenged, appealed, or litigated? This moves the evidence beyond constructed test records toward correspondence with real-world outcomes.

## Goals
- Assemble real HR/employment records paired with their documented outcomes (challenged, appealed, upheld, overturned).
- Provide the real-case evidence base for the journal paper "Documentation Governance in AI-Assisted Decision-Making" (target: Journal of Business Ethics).
- Reach the pre-registered target of 20 to 30 cases with a spread of outcomes.
- Capture practitioner observations for the paper's discussion section.

## Progress (as of 2026-08-01, verified live in the study database)
