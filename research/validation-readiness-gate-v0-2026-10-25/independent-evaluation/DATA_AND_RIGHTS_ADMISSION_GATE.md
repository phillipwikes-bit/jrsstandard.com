# Data and Rights Admission Gate (draft)

**Gate V0, 2026-10-25. Draft; no material has been admitted.** This document makes no legal determination. Rights and legal questions require human authority (CLAUDE.md Rules 4, 5, 8). B-018 OPEN. NO_GO_NOT_VERIFIED in effect.

A record may enter any evaluation only if every item is recorded **YES** with evidence:

| # | Item | Evidence required | Authority |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Provenance is known: who created the record, when, and how | Construction log entry | Corpus constructor |
| 2 | The right to use the record for this evaluation is documented. Authorship, possession and publication are not rights (Rules 4 to 6) | Rights record; counsel review where the source is not owner-created | Owner; counsel where applicable |
| 3 | No personal data, or a documented lawful basis and minimization reviewed by counsel | Data review record | Counsel |
| 4 | Not employment, housing, lending, insurance, medical, legal-outcome or other excluded-domain material, unless a separate scope decision exists | Scope review | Owner; counsel |
| 5 | Not one of the three controlling source files and not derived from them | Hash and derivation check | Custodian |
| 6 | Not engineering material (V0 corpus, Phase 6 to 9 scenarios, Stage A records) | Hash check against package manifests | Custodian |
| 7 | Retention, access and deletion terms set | Custody record | Owner |
| 8 | If sent to a live model, the provider route is authorized and no credential appears in any record | Authorization; secret scan | Owner |

Any **NO** or **NOT ESTABLISHED** blocks admission. Nothing is admitted conditionally.

**B-018 remains OPEN. NO_GO_NOT_VERIFIED remains in effect.**
