## Notes for sending

**The link to use is `https://jrsstandard.com/reviewer`.** It is the landing page: it explains the programme, then opens the same free training that has always been there. Do not send `/train` for this; it drops people straight into Module 1 with no context.

**Add a source tag when you send in volume** so the channel is measurable: `https://jrsstandard.com/reviewer?src=linkedin`, `?src=email`, `?src=signature`. Untagged traffic is invisible in the funnel.

**Use `?owner=1` if you are just checking the link yourself.** Anything else you click counts as engagement, and the per-person link metrics feed the acquisition record.

**Do not describe the certificate as an accreditation or a licence.** It records that someone completed six modules and submitted an evaluation, issued by this research programme. That description holds up under questioning; anything stronger does not, and the person most likely to test it is the one you most want to impress.

**What to expect.** Most people will not finish six modules. The evaluation is worth sending on its own to anyone who will not: `https://jrsstandard.com/reviewer/evaluation.html`. Four minutes of a compliance officer's time answering nine questions is a better outcome than three hours they never start.
