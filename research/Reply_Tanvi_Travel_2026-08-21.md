## Notes for Phillip, not part of the message

**Why the facts rather than the reassurance.** "I already have what I need" reads as
kindness and she will discount it. "The set closed on 29 July and the article is written"
is checkable and cannot be argued with. Give her the fact.

**Why first authorship goes in this message.** She thinks she failed to deliver. The single
most effective thing against that is learning she is first author on a finished paper. It
reframes the whole exchange from a shortfall into a completed contribution.

**The loose end your draft had.** "We'll leave everything else for another time" would have
left her still believing she owes cases, and left the submission unable to proceed. The
three asks are named so nothing is hanging, and explicitly deferred so nothing is demanded.

**Do NOT attach the certificate or the acknowledgment to this message.** They deserve their
own send when she is home and can enjoy them. Landing recognition inside a reply to an
apology dilutes both.

**Do not attach the article again either.** She already has it and it would read as a nudge.
