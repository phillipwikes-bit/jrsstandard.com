## What changed from the preprint (for the editor's comparison)

1. **Restructured to the journal's required blocks:** Introduction, Literature Review, Methodology, Results, Discussion, Conclusions. The preprint's stray "2. Introduction" heading after Section 1 is gone.
2. **Socioeconomic framing added,** as the editor requested. The new introduction and the Discussion subsection "Socioeconomic Implications" cover:
   - who bears the cost when a record cannot be reconstructed;
   - transparency and public trust;
   - the governance of automated and AI-assisted drafting.
   These are framed as arguments. No new cost data is claimed.
3. **Literature review expanded from 7 to 20 references.** The additions cover accountability (Bovens), transparency and trust (Grimmelikhuijsen and Meijer; OECD), freedom of information (Roberts; Worthy), administrative justice (Mashaw; Tyler), automated decisions (Citron; Pasquale), records practice (Shepherd and Yeo) and agreement statistics (Cohen; Landis and Koch; Wilson). **Every DOI was resolved against Crossref on 2026-10-21; the books were checked against Open Library.**
4. **Citation corrected:** the preprint cites "Duranti and Makhlouf Shabou (2015)". Crossref lists *Records Management Journal* 25(1), 56–77 as sole-authored by **Basma Makhlouf Shabou**. Corrected here. The SSRN preprint still carries the error and can be revised there.
5. **More transparent on known reviewer points (no numbers changed):**
   - the audit read was taken from the audit reports themselves, and no audit in the corpus found records adequate;
   - the 6.4 comparison shares five cases with the audit comparison, and restricted to case-level sources it is 6 of 7 against 0 of 2, p = 0.0833;
   - a sensitivity check is reported: under a stricter coding of one in-camera note the result is 5 of 7 against 0 of 17, p = 0.000494.
   All p-values were recomputed exactly on 2026-10-21 and match the preprint: 0.0000520, 0.00466, 0.0000050, 1.000.
6. **Statements required by the journal added:**
   - JEL codes (D73, H11, H83, K23, M42) and keywords (alphabetical, none repeating title words);
   - CRediT author contributions, Acknowledgements, AI statement, Funding, Conflict of interest, Data availability, Informed consent, and a Preprint note.
7. **Conflict of interest strengthened** to match the AI and Ethics declaration of 5 October 2026: P.W. "would benefit professionally and commercially from its adoption".
8. **Wording:**
   - gender-neutral references to the second reader, as the journal requires;
   - the promotional byline "Developer of the Justification Review Standard" removed from the author line;
   - the dangling "That portability" sentence rewritten.
9. **Unchanged:** the data, the counts, the figures, the order and names of the authors, and Stacyann Young's own Disclosure text, kept verbatim.
