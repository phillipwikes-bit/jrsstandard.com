# Correspondence archive

**PRIVATE. `research/` is excluded from the deploy, which is why correspondence lives here
and nowhere else.**

## Why this exists

Conversations with contributors have been arriving as screenshots and pasted text, acted on
in the moment, and then lost. That has already cost real accuracy twice:

- The claim that Ubayet Hossain had been "promised co-authorship in writing" rested on an
  unsent draft, and the owner had to correct it (`MASTER_TRACKER.md`, 2026-08-21).
- Tanvi Pokhriyal spent two weeks believing she was behind on a corpus that had closed on
  29 July, because the closing message was never sent.

**A contributor's own words are the authority on what they agreed to.** Reconstructing them
from a tracker summary is exactly the Decision Reconstruction Risk this programme is about.

## Convention

One file per person: `<Surname>_<Firstname>.md`.

Inside, newest exchange at the bottom, each entry stamped and attributed:

```
## 2026-07-14, from Ubayet
> verbatim text or a faithful transcription of the screenshot

## 2026-07-14, to Ubayet
> verbatim text
```

**Transcribe verbatim. Do not summarise.** A paraphrase of a consent decision is not a
consent decision. Where a screenshot is the only source, say so in the entry.

## Standing rules

- **Never deployed.** These files carry personal correspondence and stay in `research/`.
- **Never quoted outside this workspace** without the person's agreement.
- **Emails and phone numbers are not transcribed**, even when visible in a screenshot. The
  name is the key; the contact route is in the roster.
