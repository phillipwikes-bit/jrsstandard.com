## Standing interpretation

**This is a deferral, not a refusal.** He has not declined anything, asked for a change, or
raised an objection. He has asked for time on a message that carried two separate things at
once: an honor to accept and a manuscript to sign off.

**A defect in the letter he was sent, and it needs correcting in the next message.** The
letter's fallback sentence says that on silence "the paper uses what is on file for you."
What is on file for him is **co-author** (`api/_contributor-roster.js:75`, `kind:'author'`).
So the sentence as written would deem an authorship sign-off from silence on 5 September.
**That is the one thing the earned-authorship policy he set forbids**
(`MASTER_TRACKER.md:19`: authorship is legitimate only if he reviews and signs off on that
specific manuscript). The fallback is a naming-election rule for contributors; it was never
meant to carry an author's sign-off, and it must not be allowed to.

**Consequence:** the 5 September date does not bind him and should be withdrawn as to
authorship in the reply. If he has not signed off, the paper does not go out with his name
on it, whatever the date says.
