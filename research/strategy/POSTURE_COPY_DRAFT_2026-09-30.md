# POSTURE STATEMENT
### Proposed copy for `licensing-acquisition.html`. Sharpening, not building.

**DRAFT 1, 2026-09-30. NOT APPLIED TO ANY PAGE. Owner approval required.**

---

## WHAT THE PAGE ALREADY DOES WELL

`licensing-acquisition.html` already carries a **Transaction status** block that is
honest and unusually careful:

> *"JRS is available for licensing and acquisition discussions. No public statement
> represents that all potential assets are presently conveyable, that an exclusive
> licence is ready for signature, or that an Asset Purchase Agreement can be executed
> without rights review, technical scoping, diligence, professional advice, and
> negotiated terms."*

It also already tables the potential transaction asset by category: method and
construct, technology, evidence and adoption. **Nothing there needs changing.**

---

## THE GAP

The page states what is *available*. It does not state **how this operates**, and that
absence is doing quiet damage with the one audience that matters.

A senior legal or risk professional evaluating an unfamiliar framework is scanning for
the sales mechanism. When they cannot find it, the default reading is not *"there isn't
one"*. The default reading is *"it is further down the page"*. **Unstated, a passive
posture reads as an incomplete funnel. Stated once, plainly, it reads as deliberate**,
and it is the clearest available signal to a skeptical reader that they are not being
worked.

There is a second gap, and it is commercial rather than psychological. **The page does
not draw the line between what is given away and what is licensable.** That line is
drawn in the Exit Strategy and it is the exact line a poorly designed marketing plan
would cross by proposing to license the standard or to sell certification.

---

## PROPOSED BLOCK

To sit directly beneath the existing Transaction status block.

---

### How this operates

**The standard is free and stays free.** The Justification Review Standard, the
practitioner guides, the reference, the rapid review card and the training are published
without registration, without a paywall and without a lead form. That is not an
introductory offer and there is no gated tier behind it.

**There is no sales process.** No demo funnel, no discovery call, no pursuit, no
follow-up sequence. Enquiries sent to info@jrsstandard.com are answered substantively.
Whether a conversation follows is the enquirer's decision, not a step in a process.

**What is available for licence or transfer is narrower than the name.** It is the
Review Engine implementation, the versioned API contract, the Manifest specification and
generator, the Codebook and evaluation logic, the training curriculum and the
implementation knowledge. **It is not the standard, which is given away, and it is not
certification, which JRS does not offer.** JRS is not certification, not accreditation
and not a credential, and it does not establish compliance with the EU AI Act, the NIST
AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001 or any other framework.

**Evaluation comes before any of it.** The defined first step is a bounded,
non-production examination under a written Evaluation License, with the environment,
versions, test rights, data boundaries and confidentiality set out in advance.

---

## WHAT THIS BLOCK DELIBERATELY DOES NOT SAY

- No price, no range, no indication of value.
- No claim that a transaction is expected, likely or in progress.
- No named counterparty, no pipeline, no interest level.
- No urgency of any kind.

**Any of those would convert the page from a statement of posture into a solicitation**,
which is the opposite of its purpose and would additionally be an unsupported claim
about demand that no evidence in this estate supports.
