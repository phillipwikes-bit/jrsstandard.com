# NETWORKING EXECUTION PLAN
## Converting a list of job titles into an operating system

**Date:** 2026-09-30
**Status:** PROPOSAL. Nothing in this plan has been executed.
**Constraint set:** passive commercial posture (Exit Strategy §2.3); external
communication is approval-gated (`CLAUDE.md` §16, §26); no unsupported claims (§24).

---

## 0. THE REFRAME THAT MAKES THE REST WORK

The pasted list grades A-minus because the titles are right. It is not yet a plan,
because a list of titles implies outreach, and **outreach is the one thing this estate
has decided not to do.**

Under a passive posture, networking is not a campaign. It has three jobs:

1. **Be present where they already read.**
2. **Be reachable without a pitch.**
3. **Be forwardable** once they have found you.

Everything below serves one of those three. Anything that does not is out of scope,
including every form of cold outreach to a named executive.

---

## 1. RE-TIER THE TITLES BY DISTANCE TO THE LIABILITY

The pasted list treats the four groups as equal. They are not. Ranking should follow
**proximity to the exact failure the published article describes**, not seniority.

### TIER 1. BULLSEYE: The article is about their Tuesday.

| Title | Why this tier |
|---|---|
| Employment and labor attorneys, defense side | McDonnell Douglas burden-shifting is their daily vocabulary. The article's core argument needs no translation for them. |
| Employee Relations Directors | They own the file that fails. They are the person who wrote the memo. |
| VP Human Resources | Approves the decision, inherits the exposure, rarely reads the evidence trail. |
| Internal Audit Directors covering HR and investigations | Professionally trained to ask *"can you show me the support for that conclusion."* |

### TIER 2. OWNS THE LIABILITY, ONE STEP REMOVED.

| Title | Why this tier |
|---|---|
| **General Counsel, 500 to 5,000 employees** | See the deliberate exclusion below. This is the single highest-value segment in the plan. |
| Regulatory Compliance Directors | Reads CEP. Buys frameworks. Has budget authority for exactly this class of tool. |
| AI Governance leads | Conceptually aligned, but often advisory rather than accountable. Influence, not decision. |

### TIER 3. STRATEGIC: Credibility and referral, not conversion.

Chief Risk Officers, Chief Audit Executives, Privacy Officers, Data and Analytics
Governance leads. Their world is financial, operational and model risk. The employment
record is adjacent to their remit, not inside it. **Do not build assets for this tier.**
Let them find the ones built for Tiers 1 and 2.

### THE DELIBERATE EXCLUSION

**Fortune 500 General Counsel are not the target and should be dropped from the
priority list.** A GC at that scale has an in-house employment team, a panel of outside
firms, and a records function. You would be solving a problem they have already staffed,
against incumbents, through a procurement process built to exclude single-founder assets.

**The GC of a 500 to 5,000 person organization is a generalist who is personally
exposed, has no employment specialist down the hall, reads the same publications, and
answers their own messages.** That is the segment where recognition converts.

## 2. FIVE MECHANISMS, RANKED BY EFFORT TO VALUE

### M1. Re-contact the sixteen. Highest value, lowest cost, available this week.

Sixteen named experts completed a full 24-record set. They span eleven countries. Most
hold Tier 1 or Tier 2 titles themselves. **They are already the network, and they have
not been told the article exists.**

**The note is a courtesy, not an ask.** They graded records for the study the article
draws on. Telling them it published is ordinary professional respect. It contains no
request to share, no request to comment, no request of any kind.

**Hard guardrails, and these are not optional:**

- Consent E-001 covers **panel participation**. It does not make anyone an endorser, a
  reference, a referral source or a testimonial. Treating the roster as a distribution
  list would convert a research consent into a marketing asset, which is exactly the
  conversion Rule 5 forbids.
- **Never publish the roster as social proof.** The blind-study guardrail already
  recorded in the tracker still applies.
- Never describe any of them as a user, adopter, advocate or supporter of JRS.
- One message. No follow-up if there is no reply.

**Expected outcome:** some proportion reshare on their own initiative. Each reshare puts
the article in front of a governance audience that no amount of outbound could reach.
**That outcome is a side effect and must not be the stated purpose of the message.**

**Owner action:** approve and send. Drafting can be prepared here.

### M2. The comment layer, not the post layer.

Twenty minutes, three days a week. Not posting. **Commenting substantively under other
people's posts** on AI in hiring and termination, Mobley v. Workday, EEOC guidance on
automated decision tools, and EU AI Act employment scope.

One useful observation per comment. No link. No pitch. No product. The profile is the
only thing that sells, and it sells to the people who click it.

**Why this beats posting:** a post reaches your followers. A good comment on someone
else's post reaches theirs, and it arrives as peer commentary rather than as
promotion. It is the single cheapest way to appear in a Tier 1 feed without entering it
as a vendor.

**Rule:** if a comment could be mistaken for marketing, do not post it.

### M3. Quarterly placement, one venue at a time, each co-authored for the credential the piece needs.

| Quarter | Venue | Why | Co-author supplies |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q4 2026 | **CEP Magazine (SCCE)** | Read by Chief Compliance Officers and corporate counsel. Structured around operational risk and accountability. | Compliance operations credibility |
| Q1 2027 | **Risk & Compliance (Financier Worldwide)** | Boardroom and GC audience. Governance and liability framing. | Board or governance seat |
| Q2 2027 | **Risk.net** | Model validators and risk architects. Harder audience, higher authority. | Quantitative or model-risk credential |

**Verify before counting: Lexology and JD Supra generally publish through law firms and
member organizations rather than independent authors.** Confirm eligibility before
either appears in a plan. **Check "Journal of Legal, Ethical and Regulatory Issues"
against a recognised index before submission**, given how carefully every other venue in
this estate has been vetted.

**Standing rule already established and preserved: co-authorship is earned by writing.**

### M4. Search presence at the moment of need.

A GC who has just read an AI-drafted termination memo and felt uneasy will search before
they ask anyone. The phrases they will use come from the article itself.

Target queries, all derived from published language rather than invented:

- AI drafted termination memo evidence
- can AI generated performance review be used in court
- pretext AI documentation employment
- defensible decision record standard

**Action:** confirm the CCI article and the relevant site pages are reachable for these.
This is measurement and adjustment, not content production.

### M5. The inbound protocol. A passive posture fails at the inbox, not at the top of the funnel.

Everything above is wasted if an inbound enquiry is handled like a sales lead. The
standing response:

1. **Answer the actual question.** Fully, in writing, with no reciprocity implied.
2. **Send the free material** that addresses it. The standard, the guides, the reference.
3. **Do not propose a call.** The call is theirs to propose. Proposing it converts you
   from peer to vendor in one sentence and forfeits the entire locus-of-control advantage.
4. **Say the commercial posture once, plainly, if asked.** Licensing, integration,
   acquisition. Then stop.

## 4. WHAT NOT TO DO

Explicit, because each of these is tempting and each destroys the posture.

- **No cold outreach to the named executives in the buyer list.** That list is a
  transaction map, not a networking list. Contacting a CEO of a target acquirer before
  the asset is transaction-ready spends the introduction at the worst possible moment.
- **No connection request carrying a pitch.**
- **No "quick fifteen minutes."**
- **No publishing the contributor roster as social proof.**
- **No naming any individual or organization as a JRS user.**
- **No paid promotion.** It contradicts the posture and signals the opposite of authority
  to this audience specifically.

## 5. MEASUREMENT

**Count these:**

- Inbound enquiries, **by title and organization size**
- Referral source on article-driven arrivals
- Resource downloads by page
- Placement acceptances

**Do not count these, and do not report them:**

- Impressions, reach, connection count, likes, follower growth

Those are vanity metrics in every market and they are actively misleading in this one,
where the entire audience is five thousand people who do not behave like a feed.

## 7. HONEST LIMITATION

**This is craft, not measurement.** No part of this plan has been tested against this
audience and no conversion rate is predicted. The tiering rests on the published
article's own argument and on the estate's recorded posture, both of which are
documented. The behavioral claims in the companion plan rest on published research,
cited there. **Nothing here should be represented to a buyer as evidence of market
demand.**
