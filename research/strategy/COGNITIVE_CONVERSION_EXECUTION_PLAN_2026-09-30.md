## 0. WHY THIS FRAMEWORK GRADED A, AND WHAT IT STILL LACKS

The framework is correct and it is correctly matched to this estate. Loss aversion over
gain creation is the right frame for an audience whose upside is invisible and whose
downside is career-ending. The locus-of-control point explains why the passive posture
is an asset rather than a limitation. **Upward cover is the sharpest idea in the whole
package and nobody has built it.**

What it lacks is artifacts. A psychological framework with no object to hand someone is
a theory of conversion with nothing to convert. **Each of the four mechanisms below is
turned into one thing that exists, with a test that says whether it works.**

One correction carried forward: the framework's own logic **rules out** the
self-diagnostic proposed in the marketing plan. Asking a General Counsel to upload a
real case file is the opposite of respecting autonomy, and it creates a privilege and
data-protection problem besides. Artifact 2 replaces it with something stronger on the
framework's own terms.

## ARTIFACT 1. THE UPWARD COVER BRIEF

**Mechanism served:** upward cover. **Priority: build this first.**

### The insight being operationalized

An interested executive rarely stalls because they disagree. They stall because **they
cannot explain it upward without looking like they have been sold something.** Give them
a document that survives being forwarded to a CEO, a board or a regulator, and you have
solved the actual blocker.

### Specification

**Length:** two pages. Not three.
**Real audience:** not your reader. **The person your reader has to convince.** Write for
the CEO who receives the forward, not the GC who sends it.

**Structure:**

1. The failure mode, one paragraph, clinical, no adjectives.
2. Why routine review does not catch it. This is the paragraph that gets forwarded.
3. The cross-file pattern. The strongest single argument available and the one no
   reader has thought of.
4. What a defensible record contains. Positive, checkable, short.
5. **What this brief does not claim.** Explicit limitations section.

### The design rule that makes it work

**It contains no ask.** No call to action. No "contact us." No product description. No
pricing. No demo. A footer URL and nothing else.

The moment it asks for anything, forwarding it makes the forwarder look like a vendor's
mouthpiece, and the forward does not happen. **The absence of the ask is not modesty. It
is the mechanism.**

### Acceptance tests

- **Test 1, the forward test.** A General Counsel can send this to their CEO with a
  one-line covering note and nothing in it embarrasses them.
- **Test 2, the claim register test.** Every figure carries a source and an as-of date
  in an accompanying register, per the `WC-10` discipline.
- **Test 3, the hostile read test.** Opposing counsel reading it finds nothing
  overstated.

### Claim discipline, non-negotiable

- If the 83.9 percent detection figure appears, it appears **with its denominators**:
  a constructed 24-record corpus, 16 reviewers, 384 graded reads, and its interval.
- **If any reliability figure appears, the unmet pre-registered criterion appears in the
  same breath.** A brief that reports the coefficient and omits the failure is the exact
  document this estate exists to argue against.
- No certification language. The live site says **"Not certification, not accreditation,
  not a credential"** and the brief must not contradict it.
- No compliance claim for any framework. Alignment is not compliance.
- No prevention claim. Historical analysis does not establish prevention.

### Effort

Two to three days including the claim register. **This is the highest-return single
document available to the estate right now.**

## ARTIFACT 2. THE OFFLINE SELF-AUDIT

**Mechanism served:** loss aversion plus locus of control.
**This replaces the web self-diagnostic, and it is strictly better.**

### Why the proposed version had to be killed

The marketing plan invited a GC to run *"a closed historical case file from their own
organization"* through the diagnostic. `api/review.js` posts submitted text to
`api.anthropic.com`. The Master Register records **Anthropic's terms as not located**
and the **DPA as not located**. For a lawyer, there is also a privilege question that is
counsel's to answer.

### Why the replacement is stronger, not merely safer

Three gains, and the third is the one that matters:

1. The privilege question disappears. Nothing leaves their building.
2. The processor dependency disappears. No DPA is needed for a piece of paper.
3. **It is a better expression of the framework.** You are not asking them to touch your
   server, create an account or trust your infrastructure. **You are handing them an
   instrument and walking away.** That is the locus-of-control mechanism executed
   properly rather than described.

### What already exists, and what the gap actually is

`JRS_Practitioner_Self_Review_Final.pdf` exists and is good. **It is a worked example,
not an instrument.** It shows the creator applying the five conditions to a constructed
performance improvement plan in 34 minutes. A reader watches someone else do it.

**The gap is an instrument the reader runs on their own file.** That is a different
document and it does not exist.

### Specification

**Length:** one page. **Medium:** PDF, printable, no account, no upload, no telemetry.
**Instruction at the top:** *use a real record, do not send it to anyone, including us.*

**Structure:** the five review conditions as five questions, each with a binary answer
and a one-line evidence prompt. A scoring box at the foot. A short "what this does and
does not tell you" panel.

### The conversion moment

**It is the finding, not a form submission.** The GC who cannot answer question three on
their own file has just discovered something about their organization on their own
authority, in private, with no vendor present. That is the recognition event the entire
framework is built around, and you are not in the room for it.

### Acceptance tests

- A GC completes it in under 20 minutes on one real file, alone, telling nobody.
- They finish holding a written finding they could hand to their own team.
- Nothing in it requires contacting the author to understand the result.

### Effort

One to two days. Reuses the five conditions already published.

## ARTIFACT 3. THE RESONANCE LIBRARY

**Mechanism served:** cognitive resonance.

### Specification

Not marketing copy. A growing set of **clinically described failure modes**, one page
each, published on the site. The test of each one is whether a reader thinks *"that
happened here."*

**Candidate set, all drawn from evidence already held:**

1. The record that reads well and supports nothing.
2. **The phrase that recurs across files.** Strongest. Already published in CCI.
3. The reviewer who approved without the underlying material.
4. The AI summary that became the source of truth.
5. The decision whose explanation was written after it.
6. The audit trail that records who clicked and not what they knew.

### The rule that makes it resonate

**Describe the failure. Never the product.** Not one sentence about JRS in the body of
any of them. A single footer line pointing to the standard is the entire commercial
content.

**Product mention breaks resonance instantly**, because the reader stops recognizing
themselves and starts evaluating a vendor. The two mental states are mutually exclusive.

### Cadence

One per month. Twelve pages a year is a body of work.

## ARTIFACT 4. THE POSTURE STATEMENT

**Mechanism served:** locus of control.

### Specification

**Verify first whether `licensing-acquisition.html` already does this.** If it does, the
action is to sharpen rather than to build.

One short page stating the commercial posture plainly:

- The standard, the guides, the training and the reference are **free and ungated**, and
  will stay that way.
- There is **no sales process**, no demo funnel and no pursuit.
- What is licensable is named precisely: **the Engine, the Manifest, the training
  curriculum and the implementation knowledge.** Not the standard, which is given away.
  Not certification, which is disclaimed.
- Enquiries are answered. Calls are the enquirer's to propose.

### Why it converts

The refusal to chase already **is** the positioning. Unstated, it reads as absence.
Stated once, plainly, it reads as deliberate, and it is the single clearest signal to a
skeptical senior professional that they are not being worked.

## KILL CRITERIA

State these now, so the decision later is not made under sunk cost.

- **Upward Cover Brief:** if it cannot be written in two pages without a claim that needs
  qualifying, the argument is not ready and the brief should not ship.
- **Offline self-audit:** if a competent reader cannot finish it in 20 minutes, it is a
  questionnaire rather than an instrument. Cut it.
- **Resonance library:** if a page cannot be written without naming the product, that
  failure mode is not one you have evidence for. Drop it.
- **Whole plan:** if six months produce no inbound from a Tier 1 or Tier 2 title, the
  problem is reach, not craft, and the venue strategy is the thing to change.

## HONEST LIMITATION

The behavioral premise is well supported: loss aversion is established in the
Kahneman and Tversky prospect-theory literature, and the commonly cited coefficient is
roughly two to one. **The application of it to this audience, these artifacts and this
posture is craft and has not been measured.** No conversion rate is predicted and none
should be represented to a buyer. **Nothing in this plan constitutes evidence of market
demand, and `RS-02` continues to govern what any published analysis may claim.**
