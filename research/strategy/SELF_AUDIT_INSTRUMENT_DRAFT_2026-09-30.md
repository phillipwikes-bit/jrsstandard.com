### THE SECOND TEST, AND IT IS THE HARDER ONE

Pull **ten** records of the same type from the same period. Read only the evaluative
language.

**Does the same subjective phrase appear across multiple files?**
Cultural fit. Executive presence. Struggles with change. Not a team player.

☐ No recurring phrases
☐ Recurring phrases, no apparent clustering
☐ **Recurring phrases clustering among employees who share a characteristic**

The third box is the one that changes the nature of the exposure, and it is invisible
to anyone reviewing one file at a time.

### WHAT THIS TELLS YOU, AND WHAT IT DOES NOT

**It tells you** whether one record can explain itself to a reader who was not there.
That is the question asked in litigation, in an agency investigation and in an audit.

**It does not tell you** whether a decision was correct, whether it was lawful, or
whether it is defensible. **Defensibility is a question for counsel.** This test does not
establish compliance with any statute, regulation or standard, and it is not
certification, accreditation or a credential.

**A record can fail every question here and still describe a decision that was entirely
right.** The gap this test finds is in the record, not in the judgment.

## NOTES FOR PHILLIP. NOT PART OF THE INSTRUMENT.

**The five questions are RC1 to RC5 verbatim in substance**, drawn from `codebook.html`,
rewritten in the second person and shortened. **All five remain at Experimental
maturity**, which the published codebook states and which this page does not contradict.

**Why the second test is on the page.** It is the strongest argument in the estate and
it cannot be run on a single record. It also converts the instrument from a checklist
into a discovery, which is the difference between a form and a finding.

**Why there is no score.** A number invites the reader to argue with the scale. A column
of "no" answers in their own handwriting, on their own file, argues with nobody.

**Design constraints, all binding.** No upload path. No form action. No telemetry. No
account. If this ever becomes a web page rather than a PDF, **it must not POST anything**,
because `api/review.js` transmits submitted text to a third party whose terms and DPA
are both recorded in the Master Register as not located.

**Acceptance tests.** Completed in under 20 minutes on one real file, alone. Finishes
with a written finding the reader could hand to their own team. Requires no contact with
the author to interpret.
