## D. THE DELIBERATE DECISION ABOUT STATISTICS

**The brief uses almost none of Section A, and that is a strategic choice rather than an
oversight.**

The brief's job is to survive being forwarded to a chief executive by someone who must
not look credulous. A founder's own study invites a methodology argument, and a
methodology argument is a fight the forwarder loses in front of their boss even when the
study is sound.

**The argument does not need the statistics.** It is verifiable by the reader against
their own files in ten minutes, which is a stronger form of proof than a cited interval
from a corpus of constructed records.

Section A therefore exists to answer the second question, from the reader who comes back
and asks what evidence exists. **Holding the numbers in reserve, fully qualified, is
worth more than spending them on page one.**
