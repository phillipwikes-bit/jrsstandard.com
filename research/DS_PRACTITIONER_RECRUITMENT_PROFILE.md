# Decision Spellcheck: practitioner recruitment profile

Date: 2026-11-19. Purpose: decide, before anyone answers, who counts as a practitioner reviewer and what is recorded about them, so the results can be defended to a buyer, a journal or counsel later.

## Who qualifies
1. Works, or has worked, in a role that reviews or relies on decision records: investigations, HR or employee relations, civil rights, EEO or fair housing enforcement, audit, risk or compliance, legal or eDiscovery, AI governance, fraud examination, or public-records administration.
2. At least two years in that kind of work, self-reported.
3. Answers in a personal, professional capacity, not on behalf of an employer.

**Researchers (added 2026-11-19).** Organizational psychologists and other researchers who study decisions, judgment or workplace processes may take part. Their answers are reported as a separate group, not counted as practitioner answers, unless they also do the record-review work listed in item 1.

## Who does not count in the results
- Anyone who has seen Decision Spellcheck's output, answer key or development discussion for the record they review.
- Anyone who helped build or design the tool.
- A second answer from the same person on the same record (the first answer counts).
- Answers that contain real case details or personal data (removed, not scored).
Potential buyers or licensees may take part; their answers are flagged so they can be reported separately.

## What is asked
| Item | Required? | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Their answer to the one question | Yes | It is the data. |
| Field (from a short list) | Optional | Lets results be reported by field. |
| Name | Optional | Only needed for credit. |
| Email | Only if they tick a box | To send results, confirm credit wording, or follow up on a pilot or licence. |
| Job title and workplace | Not asked | Not needed to score answers. Asked only later, and only if the person wants to be credited, in the exact form they choose. |

## Consent rules
- Taking part is not consent to be named, contacted for sales, or quoted. Each is a separate opt-in tick-box.
- Credit wording is shown to the person before anything is published, and changed or removed if the tool's validation claims change.
- People in government or regulated roles take part only in a personal capacity; no request is made of their organisation.

## What is recorded per answer
Date, record ID, field, the answer, opt-in flags, country (from the connection), and whether the person is flagged as a potential buyer. Kept in the private `pilot_contacts` table under source `practitioner-review`.

## How results are reported
Counts with denominators: issues raised, matched, partly matched and missed by the tool, the tool's output recorded before any review, and the number of reviewers and fields. Fictional records only. Not an accuracy claim until reviewers are numerous and independent.
