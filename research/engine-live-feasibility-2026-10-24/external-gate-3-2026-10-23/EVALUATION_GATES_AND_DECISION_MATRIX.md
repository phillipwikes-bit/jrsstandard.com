# Evaluation Gates and Decision Matrix

**NON-OPERATIVE. Prepared 2026-10-23. Independent evaluation is not authorized. B-018
remains OPEN. Release status remains NO_GO_NOT_VERIFIED.**

Every gate fails closed: a missing, deferred, withdrawn or ambiguous decision results in no
evaluation activity of any kind. Gates are sequential where a later gate depends on an
earlier one. Mapping to the existing sealed protocol (JRS-IEP-0.1-draft gates A to F) is
shown in brackets.

| Gate | Required Decision | Required Evidence Before Start | Responsible Authority | What the Gate Authorizes | What It Does Not Authorize | Failure or Deferral Result |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| G1 B-018 dependency review | Whether any open B-018 item must be resolved before evaluation, and which representations remain prohibited while it is open | Current B-018 record; reconciliation record; a whole-worktree composition result with declared conditions only | Owner | Proceeding to later gates with a written statement of B-018 limits | Any claim based on source documents; closing B-018 | No later gate opens |
| G2 Candidate version freeze [B] | Which candidate is frozen, and the evaluation boundary | Package hash, rule-pack hashes, environment record, clock rule, measure list and adjudication rule, all recorded and sealed; clean composition result | Owner | Execution of exactly the frozen candidate in a later gate | Any change to code, rules or prompts; any other version | No execution; any later change voids the freeze |
| G3 Data classification and handling review [A] | Which material classes are permitted, from which sources, with which retention and deletion rules | Written material-class definitions; custody plan; retention schedule; deletion procedure | Owner and counsel | Use of the approved classes only, under the approved custody | Real records unless separately and expressly authorized; any other class | No material is created, collected or released |
| G4 Counsel review [A, F] | Whether proposed data flows, rights, consent, privacy and disclosure are acceptable | Data-flow description for the actual proposal; rights and consent records; disclosure plan | Counsel | Proceeding on the reviewed flows only | Any legal conclusion beyond the written review; any flow not reviewed | No material handling or appointment proceeds |
| G5 Independent-role appointment [C] | Who fills each role, and confirmation of independence | Written independence declarations; conflict check record; role access list | Owner, with independence checks recorded by the evaluation coordinator | The named people acting in their roles only | Any person holding two conflicting roles; access beyond the role | No labeling, adjudication or execution |

| G7 Label and adjudication protocol approval [C, E] | The labeling form, adjudication rule, abstention rule and conflict treatment | Written protocol recorded before labeling; calibration plan on separate material | Owner, with the adjudicator's acknowledgement | Labeling and adjudication under the written rule | Changing the rule after labeling begins without a new version | No labeling |

| G10 Results review and claims control [F] | How results are reviewed and what, if anything, may be stated | Complete results with limitations; adjudication records; deviation log | Owner and counsel, with the results reviewer | Internal recording of reviewed results | Any public, commercial, release or licensing statement unless separately authorized | Results remain internal and unrepresented |
