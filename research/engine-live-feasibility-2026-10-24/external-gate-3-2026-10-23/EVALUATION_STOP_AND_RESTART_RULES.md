# Evaluation Stop and Restart Rules

**NON-OPERATIVE. Prepared 2026-10-23. These rules would apply only to a future, formally
authorized evaluation.** Independent evaluation is not authorized. B-018 remains OPEN.
Release status remains NO_GO_NOT_VERIFIED.

On any stop, all evaluation activity ceases, nothing is deleted, and the evaluation
coordinator records the time, the trigger and the people informed.

| ID | Stop condition | Immediate action | Evidence to preserve | Who may decide restart | Re-freeze or re-review condition |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| SR-01 | B-018 changes or becomes disputed | Stop all gates after G1 | B-018 record versions; the dispute record | Owner | New G1 decision; re-review of any representation limits |
| SR-02 | Candidate package changes after freeze | Stop execution; quarantine outputs | Freeze record; observed package hash; execution log | Owner | New G2 freeze; re-execution of all affected items |
| SR-03 | Data authorization absent, withdrawn or ambiguous | Stop handling; secure material in custody | Authorization records; custody log | Owner and counsel | New G3 and G4 decisions before any further handling |
| SR-04 | Rights, privacy, retention or consent concern | Stop handling; restrict access to the custodian | Concern record; affected item list; custody log | Counsel, then owner | Counsel disposition; G3 re-review; deletion if required |
| SR-05 | Role separation fails | Stop labeling, adjudication and execution | Access log; role list; independence records | Owner | New G5 appointment; re-labeling of exposed items |
| SR-06 | Labeler conflict or contamination concern | Stop affected labeling; withdraw the labeler from affected items | Exposure record; affected labels (preserved, marked) | Owner, with the coordinator's record | Replacement labels for affected items; G6 review |
| SR-07 | Provider or execution environment differs from the approved plan | Stop execution | Environment record at freeze and at execution | Owner and counsel | New G9 approval and new G2 freeze |
| SR-08 | Unexpected safety, scope or claim-control failure | Stop all activity | Incident record; affected outputs | Owner, with counsel where scope or claims are involved | Incident review; re-freeze if the candidate or protocol changes |
| SR-09 | Evaluation materials exposed outside approved custody | Stop all activity; contain the exposure | Exposure record; custody chain; access log | Owner and counsel | Exposed material retired from evaluation; G6 re-review; replacement material only after G3 |
| SR-10 | Any result used in a public, commercial, release or licensing claim before review | Stop all activity; record the use | The statement; the results it relied on; authorization records | Owner and counsel | G10 review; correction of the statement under the publication policy |

Restart is never automatic. A restart decision is recorded in writing by the named
authority, states which gates are re-opened, and is logged before any activity resumes.
