| Option | Scope | Benefits | Limits | Required approvals |

## 2. Option A: Do not authorize evaluation at this time

- **Activity permitted:** none beyond what is already permitted locally.
- **Effect on records:** the Phase 8 protocol and handoff freeze stay execution-disabled;
  Phase 9 protocols stay execution-disabled; all readiness material is preserved.
- **Reversibility:** fully reversible. The owner may revisit B or C later; readiness
  material may need refreshing if the candidate or records change.
- **What remains open:** P8-OD-04 (whether evaluation is authorized) and P9-OD-01 (whether
  a dual review may run) stay OPEN unless the owner records them as declined.

## 3. Option B: Synthetic-material independent methods rehearsal

- **Object of the rehearsal:** the evaluation method (protocol administration, blinding,
  role separation, labeling and adjudication flow), not the candidate's performance.
- **Material:** synthetic only. Existing Phase 9 rehearsal packets are engineering-authored
  and recorded as holdout-ineligible; using them for calibration requires P9-OD-04. Any
  new synthetic material would need its own authoring and custody record.
- **Permitted outputs:** process findings (for example, where the protocol was unclear,
  where blinding failed, time per item). No accuracy, agreement or performance figure may
  be reported as evidence about the candidate.
- **Prohibited:** real records; holdout data; personal data; provider or network use; any
  public, commercial, release or licensing statement.
- **Open questions for the owner:** whether giving external people access to JRS material
  needs a confidentiality or disclosure review (gap RTS-02), and whether that access is
  "external use" for Gate 1 (gap PRV-03).

## 4. Option C: Controlled independent evaluation on separately approved materials

- **Object of the evaluation:** the frozen candidate, on material approved under G3, by
  independent labelers and an adjudicator, measured under an approved plan.
- **Material:** only classes approved at G3 with counsel. Real records remain prohibited
  unless separately and expressly authorized in writing with counsel review.
- **Permitted outputs:** internally recorded results with limitations, after G10 review.
- **Prohibited:** any statement outside G10 authority; any change to the candidate after
  freeze; any provider use unless G9 is approved.
- **Additional dependencies:** counsel engagement (none exists); material-class definitions,
  custody, retention and deletion rules; holdout custody; measurement plan and thresholds.

## 5. Statements that apply to every option

- No option is a release, deployment, licensing, commercial or production decision.
- No option closes B-018 or changes NO_GO_NOT_VERIFIED.
- Every gate fails closed: a missing, deferred, withdrawn or ambiguous approval means no
  activity.
- Any change to the candidate package, rule packs, protocol, environment or scope after a
  freeze voids that freeze.
