## 4. Questions for counsel

These are the questions the records show must be answered before any ownership,
licensing, acquisition, sale or publication claim could be considered. They are listed
without any suggested answer.

1. For each contributor category C-01 to C-07, what instrument, if any, would be needed for
   the material each contributed to be held, licensed or transferred, and does any
   located record already serve that purpose?
2. What is the legal significance, if any, of participant consents that cover study
   publication and successor transfer but are silent on licensing and commercial use, and
   of the absence of a stored terms version for each consent?
3. What allocation between co-authors would be needed for the research instruments and
   co-authored manuscripts, and what does the absence of a written allocation mean for
   any later transfer or licence?
4. What publisher rights, if any, attach to each accepted manuscript, and what
   documentation would establish them?
5. On what terms is the repository and asset suite currently held in the absence of a
   LICENSE file, and what licence, if any, should be adopted before any disclosure or
   transfer?
6. What effect, if any, does AI-assisted creation recorded in commit metadata have on the
   ownership position of the affected implementation material?
7. What filing and recordation sequence, if any, is appropriate for the names in use, given
   the internally recorded concern about filing personally and assigning later?
8. What would counsel require to treat the source documents' rights propositions as
   applicable, given that hash verification establishes identity only?
9. Which of these questions must be answered before an independent evaluation, a
   partnership discussion, licensing or acquisition diligence, or a limited pilot could
   be considered?
