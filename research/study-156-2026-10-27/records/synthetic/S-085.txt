Control test record, backup restoration. Section 7 of the Internal Audit Methodology rates a control effective with exceptions when at least 90% of sampled items meet the target and every failure is remediated. Fourteen of 15 sampled Q2 restores (93%) met the 4-hour target, and the one failure, backup BK-2207, was re-run successfully on 3 July (working paper WP-14). The control was rated effective with one exception under section 7.
