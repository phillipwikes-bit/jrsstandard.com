Change approval record. Change Policy CP-7, section 3 allows a production data-store upgrade when a rollback rehearsal passes and post-upgrade headroom is at least 25%. For change request CR-2291 the rollback rehearsal on 4 March passed (test report TR-118) and the 5 March capacity review measured 38% headroom. The Change Advisory Board approved CR-2291 on 6 March under that section.
