Work-order priority record. Work order WO-3381 reported a leaking roof drain above the archive store on 8 May. The building survey of 9 May measured water ingress at 2 litres per hour and photographed staining on three archive boxes (survey BS-204). The facilities manager raised the order to priority 1 on 9 May. The priority decision is supported by survey BS-204 and the 8 May report.
