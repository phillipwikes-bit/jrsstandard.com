Investigation summary, scheduling concern. The complainant raised concerns about weekend-shift distribution. We interviewed the complainant (May 12) and two coworkers (May 13 and 14) and reviewed schedule records for January through April. Records show the complainant was assigned three weekend shifts during the period against a team average of 1.5. The manager attributed the difference to a documented coverage gap. Email exchanges between the complainant and manager (April 2 and April 9) are on file. The finding that scheduling was uneven, with a documented operational explanation, rests on the schedule data and interviews cited.
