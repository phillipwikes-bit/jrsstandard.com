## 4. Where to publish it (recommendation)

**Two-step, do both:**

### Step A (now, days): preprint for an immediate citable DOI
Post to a preprint server so the work is timestamped, citable, and linkable in outreach and the data room while peer review runs.
- **Primary: SSRN** or **OSF Preprints.** Best fit: applied, methods-and-governance audience; no format friction; SSRN carries weight with a legal/compliance/buyer audience, which matches JRS's market. OSF pairs naturally with a pre-registration record, which strengthens the "pre-registered thresholds" claim.
- Alternative: **arXiv** (cs.CY / stat.AP) if you want the AI-research audience; needs endorsement for a first arXiv submission, so SSRN/OSF is the faster path.

### Step B (parallel, months): peer-reviewed journal for the credential of record
Rank order for this specific paper (a reliability/reproducibility methods result, not an effectiveness study):
1. **Journal of Responsible Technology** (Elsevier, open access) — scope fits "is this instrument applied consistently"; receptive to staged validation of a governance tool.
2. **AI and Ethics** (Springer) — strong topical fit (AI-assisted documentation, governance). Note: the detection/Arm B paper is already targeted here, so use this for one paper and JRT for the other to avoid stacking two submissions in one venue.
3. **AI & Society** (Springer) — good fit if a more sociotechnical/qualitative framing is emphasized.
- Higher-prestige stretch: **FAccT** or **AIES** (conference proceedings). Prestigious and buyer-legible, but deadline-bound and selective; treat as optional, not the primary path.

**Match to the other papers so venues do not collide:** Detection/Arm B -> AI and Ethics; Business Ethics/HR pilot -> Journal of Business Ethics; **Rungs 1 & 2 (this paper) -> Journal of Responsible Technology**, with the SSRN/OSF preprint up first.
