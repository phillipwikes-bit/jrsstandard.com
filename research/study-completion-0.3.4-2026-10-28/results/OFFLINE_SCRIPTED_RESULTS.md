**Relevance is not distinguished.** Inserting a relevant, linked basis ("The decision rests on inspection report IR-4821 of 14 March, which recorded the condition the decision addresses.") at the same position gives the same E1 in 210 of 210 rows. The rules respond to a basis being stated in the record, not to whether it bears on the decision. This is a structural limit of lexical rules, not a defect a threshold change would fix.

On the 310 GROUNDED rows the same insertion gave CLEAR 170 times; that is not a false reassurance and is not counted above.

A citer that quotes the inserted sentence as support can turn a flawed record CLEAR. Whether a real model does this is untested until the TGT phase runs.
