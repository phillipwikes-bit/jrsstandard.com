**Answer: the Records Management Journal.** Reasoning and the ranked alternatives below.

## What actually determines acceptance here

Four properties of this specific article drive the answer. Scope match is only the first of them, and the JOCI rejection was purely about that one.

**1. The object of study is the individual record.** The question is whether an independent reviewer can rebuild the basis for a decision from the record alone. That is a recordkeeping question. Public records supply the corpus; they are not the subject.

**2. The evidence is applied and modest in scale.** 32 cases, two states, one instrument, a blind second read at AC1 0.582 with a pre-registered criterion that was not met. This is credible professional research. It is not a large-sample study and will not survive a venue that expects one.

**3. Neither author holds an institutional affiliation, and there is no IRB review.** Stated plainly in the manuscript. Venues that weight institutional signal will discount it; practitioner-facing venues will not.

**4. The single most persuasive result for a records audience is the five of five audit convergence.** An independent government auditor, using its own methodology with no knowledge of the study, reached the same judgment on every case where both assessments existed. That is the finding that makes a records-management editor take it seriously.

## What raises the probability at the Records Management Journal specifically

**Emerald requires a structured abstract.** The current abstract is continuous prose and must be rebuilt into labelled fields, typically Purpose, Design/methodology/approach, Findings, Research limitations/implications, Practical implications, Originality/value. **This is a rewrite, not a trim.** `[REQUIRED_ENV_PARAM]` the exact field list and word limit, which must be read from Emerald's author guidelines; the publisher site returns HTTP 403 to automated requests from this environment, so paste them or supply a copy and the abstract is built against the text.

**Lead the Originality/value field with the corpus, not the construct.** The defensible originality claim is a completed, citable, publicly sourced 32-case applied set with contemporaneous basis notes recorded blind to outcome. No comparable applied set exists in this literature. That claim rests on the work rather than on the instrument.

**Lead the Findings field with the five of five convergence.** It is the result least dependent on accepting the instrument's premises.

**Keep the failed reliability criterion and the interim framing.** They are why the paper reads as honest rather than promotional, and a records-management reviewer will not punish a stated limitation.

**On the title.** The proposed retitle leads with Decision Reconstruction Risk. For this venue that is the weaker choice: it foregrounds an unvalidated construct to an audience that responds to records evidence, and it inverts the byline emphasis away from the first author who built the corpus. A title leading with documentation quality and the 32-case evidence is the stronger submission.
