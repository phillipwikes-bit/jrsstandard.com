## 1. What the rejection is, and what it is not

**It is a desk rejection on scope.** The editor's stated reason is complete: "I reviewed the manuscript and determined it is not a good fit for our journal." He wishes the author well "in finding a suitable journal for the research."

**Nothing was said about the research.** No reviewer was assigned. No methodological, statistical or evidentiary criticism exists. The 32-case corpus, the convergent audit agreement, the construct and discriminant analyses and the blind second read were never evaluated.

**The operational consequence is the one that matters: do not repair anything.** A desk rejection on fit carries no information about quality, so any change made in response to it is a change made without evidence. The temptation after a same-day rejection is to revise; there is nothing here to revise against.

**Turnaround supports that reading.** Two and a half hours is a scope screen, not an assessment.

---

## 2. The blocking issue the target analysis omits

**Stacyann Young is the first author.** She designed the public-records case protocol, selected and screened all 32 determinations, recorded every read and its contemporaneous basis note blind to the outcome, recorded the outcomes and citations, and leads the public-records framing.

**Therefore:**

- the choice of the next journal is **hers as much as his**;
- the proposed retitle, which moves the paper from "public-records determinations" to "Decision Reconstruction Risk in Public Records", **reframes her contribution around his construct** and cannot be made unilaterally;
- the proposed framing shift, from public records to "documentation quality, record reconstructability, and information-governance risk", moves the paper toward the JRS programme and away from the public-records scholarship she leads.

The Master Tracker already carries **"FOIL awaiting Stacy's approval"** as an open item from before this rejection. That approval was outstanding for the packet as submitted; it is now also outstanding for where the paper goes next.

**Recommendation: no resubmission decision is executable until she has been asked.** This is not a formality. A first author learning by inference that her paper has been retitled around a co-author's construct is a collaboration problem that no journal fit fixes.

`[REQUIRED_ENV_PARAM]` **Information Polity**, **Archives and Records**, **Government Information Quarterly**: scope and submission format unverified.

**This matters because the last verification pass overturned two assumptions.** On 2026-09-01 the AI and Ethics page was fetched successfully and it contradicted the plan on two points: the abstract limit was 150 to 250 words rather than the assumed 300 to 350, and the reference style was numbered rather than the assumed author-date. **Journal requirements have not been reliable when assumed, in this programme, twice, on the same day.** Ranking four journals on unverified scope statements would repeat exactly that error.

**What can be said without verification:** the manuscript's own object is the individual record and whether its basis can be rebuilt from it. That is a records and information governance question demonstrated through a public-records corpus. The reasoning behind ranking a records-management venue first is sound on the manuscript's content. **The ranking is not the risk; the unverified submission requirements are.**

## 4. What the rejection does say about targeting

The editor saw a documentation-quality instrument tested through public-records cases and judged the instrument, not the cases, to be the paper's centre. **That is evidence about how the paper reads, and it is the only evidence this rejection produced.** It supports moving toward a venue whose object is records and information governance rather than civic information, which is the direction the analysis proposes.

## 6. On the proposed title

The recommended title, "Decision Reconstruction Risk in Public Records: A Documentation Quality Review of 32 Cases", leads with the construct.

**Two observations, for her and him to weigh, not for me to settle.**

It puts the first author's empirical contribution second and the second author's construct first, which inverts the current byline order.

It also raises a substantive point the manuscript itself is careful about: the detection paper states that this study does not validate the construct. A title that leads with Decision Reconstruction Risk in a paper whose companion explicitly disclaims construct validation invites a reviewer to ask what the construct's standing is. The current title claims less and is defensible on its own evidence.
