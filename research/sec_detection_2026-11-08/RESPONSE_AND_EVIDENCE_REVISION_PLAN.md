## Affirmative, evidence-bounded research position
**Supported:** A defined panel of 16 reviewers each assessed 24 constructed records, totaling 384 graded determinations. Reported mean reviewer-level concordance with a reference constructed and fixed by the first author was 83.9% (95% reviewer-level Student-t CI 72.7%–95.1%). The originally specified detection criterion was met under that reference and sampling design. [Controlling evidence ledger E-037](https://github.com/phillipwikes-bit/jrsstandard.com/blob/main/docs/enterprise-diligence/EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md); [archived aggregate](https://github.com/phillipwikes-bit/jrsstandard.com/blob/main/research/closed_aggregates_2026-08-15.json).

**Equally supported boundary:** Reference labels were reproduced unanimously by **three LLM instances**, not independently adjudicated human experts. It would overstate the evidence to call this externally established ground truth. [Verified_Key.md](https://github.com/phillipwikes-bit/jrsstandard.com/blob/main/research/Verified_Key.md); [Reference classification audit](https://github.com/phillipwikes-bit/jrsstandard.com/blob/main/research/REFERENCE_CLASSIFICATION_SOURCE_REPORT_2026-08-18.md).

**Supported, separately measured:** The separate reliability sample returned AC1 = 0.739 for invited raters and 0.623 for open enrollment. The predeclared two-part reliability criterion was not met because the specified analytic confidence-bound thresholds were not cleared. This is an informative negative result, not an absence of measurement. [E-038](https://github.com/phillipwikes-bit/jrsstandard.com/blob/main/docs/enterprise-diligence/EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md); [reliability output](https://github.com/phillipwikes-bit/jrsstandard.com/blob/main/research/current_reliability_2026-08-18.json).

## Proposed manuscript framing
**Candidate title, subject to author/editor approval:** *Human Review of Decision Records Drafted with Generative Artificial Intelligence: Classification Performance, Reviewer Variation and SocioEconomic Implications*.

**Candidate contribution paragraph:** “In a controlled study using 24 intentionally clear-cut, constructed records, 16 expert reviewers completed 384 blind classifications. Their mean agreement with a predefined, author-derived operational reference was 83.9%, above the study’s prespecified detection threshold. Individual performance varied considerably. A separate assessment of agreement under a five-condition review instrument did not satisfy its prespecified confidence-bound criterion. These findings document both the feasibility of studying detection under an explicit reference rule and the need for further independent reference validation, more heterogeneous records and larger reliability samples. Costs, appeals, equity effects and live-workflow effectiveness were not measured.”

**Candidate socioeconomic framing:** “Where organizations rely on human review to identify unsupported conclusions, variation in reviewer judgments is a relevant governance consideration. The present study does not estimate the monetary, legal or distributive consequences of that variation. Those consequences are proposed directions for future measurement, not observed outcomes.” This is a proposed interpretation grounded in the measured reviewer variation, not a claim that economic effects occurred.
