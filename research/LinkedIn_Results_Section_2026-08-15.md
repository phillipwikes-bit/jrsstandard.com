# LinkedIn results section

Three lengths. All figures locked 2026-08-15 and verified against the study database. None makes an efficacy or adoption claim.

---

## A. THE SECTION (about 130 words)

**Research results**

Two studies closed on 15 August 2026.

**Can independent experts spot a record that cannot explain its own decision?**
Yes. 16 experts in 11 countries across 5 continents read 24 constructed records cold, blind to an answer key fixed before any scoring and independently reproduced 24 of 24. They were right **83.9%** of the time (95% CI 72.7–95.1), on 384 graded reads. That cleared the threshold set in advance.

**Does using the five conditions beat unaided professional judgment?**
Not proven. 75.0% with the conditions against 67.6% without, a 7.4-point gap whose confidence interval spans zero (−15.3 to +30.0). The study was never powered to answer it: 205 people per arm were needed and it had 20.

58 independent experts across three studies. 36 completed a full 24-record set, in 16 countries. Nobody was paid.

## B. SHORTER (about 70 words)

**Research results, locked 15 August 2026**

16 independent experts in 11 countries read 24 records blind, against a key fixed before scoring and verified 24 of 24. They identified unreconstructable records **83.9%** of the time (95% CI 72.7–95.1, 384 graded reads), clearing a pre-registered threshold.

Whether the method beats unaided expert judgment is still open. That comparison came out null and underpowered, and is reported as such.

58 experts. Three studies. 16 countries. Nobody paid.

## C. SHORTEST, for a headline or banner (about 30 words)

**83.9% accuracy against an answer key fixed before scoring.** 16 independent experts, 11 countries, 384 blind reads, pre-registered threshold cleared. 58 experts across three studies. Nobody paid.

## What was cut, and why

| Cut | Reason |
|---|---|
| The spread, 37.5% to 100% | Real and important to a practitioner, but it needs a paragraph to land and reads as a weakness without one. Keep it for the paper and the deck. |
| Reliability, AC1 0.739 / 0.623 | Interim on 10 records. A coefficient with a wide interval is not a LinkedIn fact. |
| Per-condition separation, p < 1.5e-07 | Meaningless without the table under it. |
| Cross-vendor agreement, 87.2% mean over 41 runs | Invites "so the AI agrees with itself", which is the wrong reading. It measures consistency, not correctness, and that distinction does not survive a LinkedIn skim. |
| Any superiority claim | The comparison did not meet its pre-registered bar. |

## What was kept and must stay

- **The null.** It is two sentences in version A and one in B. Publishing only the win, when the same unpaid people produced both results, is the version that gets quoted back at you.
- **58 experts, three studies, nobody paid.** Under-crediting to 16 leaves out 42 people.
- **"Key fixed before scoring."** It is the single strongest fact here and costs six words.
