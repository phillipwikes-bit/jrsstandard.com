# Case File Summary

## Complainant and Claims
Complainant was a Counseling Psychologist/Licensed Marriage and Family Therapist at the Agency's Health Clinic in Baumholder, Germany. She alleged discrimination and a hostile work environment based on the following:

a) From September 2014 to the present, management denied her training. She also alleged derogatory comments, unjustified interrogations, nitpicking of her work, negative facial expressions, undermined authority, coworkers encouraged to circumvent the chain-of-command, overruled decisions, a need to prove negatives, disparagement and isolation, no reward for her work, and a failure by the Agency to investigate her reports to her supervisory chain.
b) On unspecified dates, other employees rescheduled her clients with other providers, and clients who asked for her by name were told they could not meet with her.
c) On October 5, 2016, in a staff meeting, the Captain made decisions about her clients without her input.
d) On October 12, 2016, in a meeting with the Lieutenant Colonel and the Lieutenant about her request to be removed from the Baumholder Team, she felt attacked, citing their tone of voice, which she considered disrespectful.
e) On or about November 12, 2016, she received a performance evaluation that did not accurately characterize her work.
f) On returning to work on December 21, 2016, after being out sick December 19-20, 2016, two clients were removed from her schedule and redirected.
g) During a holiday provider shortage, staff refused to engage her to help cover cases.
h) The NCOIC introduced a new staff member to everyone in the office except her, even though they were talking outside her office.
i) She contacted the Regional Health Command-Europe Inspector General (IG) in October 2016 about discrimination and harassment. On December 6, 2016, the Sergeant First Class contacted her about her complaint of discrimination and reprisal. She said her legal counsel would be in Germany the week of January 9, 2017, and wanted to coordinate a meeting. Counsel emailed the Sergeant First Class asking about the nature of the meeting. The Sergeant First Class did not respond, and the meeting did not occur.
j) On January 27, 2017, she received a developmental counseling regarding her temporary reassignment.

**Note on titles.** The Lieutenant Colonel stated in his affidavit that he was Deputy Chief of Clinical Services and Chief Nursing Officer (ROI1 at 431). The Lieutenant stated that he was Commander for Baumholder and Kaiserslautern Clinics (ROI1 at 444). The decision follows Complainant's usage for both. Complainant also referred to another official as the Commander, who is a different individual (ROI2 at 96).

## Evidence in the Record

**Prior grievance.** On November 28, 2014, Complainant filed an Administrative Grievance raising many of the same claims (ROI1 at 203). On January 30, 2015, she received a notice of decision granting her requested remedy, formal mediation to improve the work environment, and the grievance was closed (ROI1 at 209).

**Training (claim a).**
- The Commander for Baumholder, Landstuhl and Kaiserslautern Clinics said Complainant wanted a non-military professional development session on suicide and military personnel. He told her the Agency could not fund personal research but could give her time, not pay, to attend. He asserted that he did not deny her training (ROI2 at 98).
- The Lieutenant said he approved her request to attend Suicide Prevention training in Vicenza, Italy, but there were no funds, and the training was ultimately cancelled (ROI1 at 447).

**Client reassignment (claim b).**
- On September 20, 2016, Complainant emailed the Supervisory Psychologist/Chief of Behavioral Health (Chief) alleging that the Nurse Practitioner told two of her patients they were transferring to another provider, and that another patient was told she no longer worked in the clinic (ROI1 at 232).
- The Social Services Assistant said Complainant had previously stated she worked only with Marriage and Family counseling patients, which led to some transfers and limited the patients she could see (ROI2 at 113). The Nurse Practitioner reiterated this (ROI2 at 143).
- The Lieutenant Colonel stated that he investigated and resolved the matter. In one situation the transfer was unintentional and the client was switched back (cited in the record as ROI2 at 436).

**October 5, 2016 meeting (claim c).**
- On October 5 and 7, 2016, two patients of Complainant submitted Interactive Customer Evaluation (ICE) complaints to the IG about her standard of care (ROI1 at 217).
- On October 5, 2016, in a standard High-Risk meeting, the Captain discussed concerns about two of her patients (ROI2 at 6).
- The Captain asserted that, after the meeting, Complainant was aggressive, recorded her without consent, and spoke loudly about the patients in an open space within earshot of other patients (ROI2 at 6).
- The Captain denied making any changes and said the meeting discussed care plans, which occurs with every provider (ROI2 at 11).
- Complainant told the Lieutenant Colonel she no longer wanted to attend the meetings and wanted reassignment (ROI1 at 437).
- The Social Services Assistant said the meetings let providers coordinate care, that everyone gives a perspective, and that no one was telling anyone how to do their job (ROI2 at 110).

**October 12, 2016 meeting (claim d).**
- The Lieutenant Colonel and the Lieutenant said Complainant repeatedly alleged a hostile environment but gave no specifics when asked for examples (ROI1 at 437; 448).
- Complainant asserted that the Lieutenant Colonel and "Colonel" were hostile (ROI1 at 447).
- Both denied being aggressive, said they did not want to continue if she was uncomfortable, and offered to have a neutral party present (ROI1 at 455).
- On October 13, 2016, Complainant submitted a complaint to the IG alleging discrimination, harassment and abuse of authority (ROI1 at 219).

**Performance evaluation (claim e).**
- On or about November 12, 2016, the Lieutenant Colonel issued the evaluation, based on 15 metrics (ROI1 at 438). He stated Complainant received eleven "Exceed" and four "Met" ratings, with the "Met" ratings in collaboration with staff (ROI1 at 438).
- He said he gave her examples supporting the ratings, including that for most of May 2016 through October 31, 2016 there was no collaboration with staff (ROI1 at 439).
- Her overall rating was Excellent. The Lieutenant, as second-line rater, agreed (ROI1 at 456).

**IG report.**
- On November 15, 2016, the IG issued its report on the October 5, 7 and 13, 2016 complaints (ROI1 at 217-26). It found no evidence supporting the harassment or discrimination allegations.
- It found that Complainant created a disruptive environment in the Baumholder Behavioral Health Clinic. It recommended moving her out of the clinic, stating that if she remained there would continue to be conflict that "demonstrably impacts patient care and significantly decreases moral throughout the clinic" (ROI1 at 217).
- The report also found she violated German law by voice recording a conversation without the other party's consent (ROI1 at 225).

**IG contact (claim i).**
- On December 6, 2016, the Sergeant First Class contacted Complainant about her IG complaint. Complainant said he responded, but her Counsel was no longer in Germany by then (ROI1 at 400).
- The EEO Investigator said she contacted the Sergeant First Class for an interview. The IG Office, including the Sergeant First Class, said internal IG policy is to abstain when the Agency is taking action. The IG report is nonetheless part of the record (ROI2 at 173; ROI1 at 217).

**December 21, 2016 (claim f).** The Lieutenant Colonel, the Colonel and the Chief each attested they were unaware of any issues on that date.

**Introduction of new staff member (claim h).**
- Complainant said she did not know who the new member was, acknowledged she did not leave her office to introduce herself, and did not tell the NCOIC or anyone else (ROI1 at 398-99).
- The EEO Investigator said she contacted the NCOIC, who had been reassigned and did not respond (ROI2 at 173).

**Temporary reassignment and counseling (claim j).** On January 4, 2017, Complainant was temporarily assigned to Landstuhl Regional Medical Center (ROI1 at 459). The Chief said the January 27, 2017 counseling was standard and nothing negative (ROI1 at 459).

The record does not include specific evidence on claim g (holiday coverage).

## Procedural History and Decision
- After the investigation, the Agency gave Complainant the report of investigation and notice of her right to request a hearing before an EEOC Administrative Judge (AJ). She timely requested a hearing.
- On October 18, 2021, the Agency moved for a decision without a hearing. Complainant responded.
- On January 31, 2022, the AJ issued his decision granting the Agency's Motion.

**Decision-maker:** the AJ.

**Criteria applied and findings:**
1. **Record and material facts.** The AJ found the investigative record adequately developed. Making all reasonable inferences in Complainant's favor, he found no material facts in dispute.
2. **Disparate treatment.** The Agency articulated non-discriminatory reasons for the actions, and Complainant failed to show they were pretext for discrimination.
3. **Hostile work environment.** Complainant failed to show the conduct met the severe and pervasive elements.
4. **Overall characterization.** The AJ found the complaint essentially reflected her "utter dissatisfaction and personal grievances against her coworkers and management."

**Final action:** The Agency did not issue a final action, so the AJ's decision became the Agency's final action by operation of law. See 29 C.F.R. § 1614.110.

## Items to Verify Against the Source
- The record cites the Lieutenant Colonel's investigation of the transfers to "ROI2 at 436", although his other affidavit citations are to ROI1 (ROI1 at 431, 437-39).
- The "Colonel" referenced in the October 12 meeting and December 21 attestations is not separately identified in the record.
- The record does not state the protected bases of the discrimination alleged.
