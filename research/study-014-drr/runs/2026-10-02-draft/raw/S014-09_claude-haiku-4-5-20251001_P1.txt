# CASE FILE SUMMARY

**Complainants:** Two Emergency Management Specialists (Regional and National Tribal Government Liaisons) employed at the Agency's First Responder Network Authority since October 2015, working remotely from California locations.

**Complaints Filed:** June 13, 2019 (EEO complaints by each complainant)

**Bases of Discrimination Alleged:** Race (Native American), national origin (Native American), and retaliation for prior protected EEO activity. (Sex discrimination claims were withdrawn during hearing.)

**Primary Allegations:**

1. **Discriminatory Pay:** Complainants hired at Pay Band IV while performing identical work as peers at Pay Band V; initial hiring negotiations reportedly subjected only Native American applicants to 70% wage reductions; April 2018 wage disparity review commissioned but ignored by leadership.

2. **Career Advancement Barriers:** Exclusion from senior leadership positions due to lower pay band assignment.

3. **Organizational Retaliation:** June 2018 reorganization allegedly designed to isolate complainants and provoke resignations; instruction to "tamper" HR escalations.

4. **Unequal Work Conditions:** Separate strategic planning requirements; restricted resource access; mandatory coordination through gatekeepers and contractor representatives; limited meeting participation.

5. **Contractor Harassment:** Ongoing hostile treatment by contractor officials, with management condoning such conduct.

6. **Failure to Acknowledge:** Exclusion from May 2019 Agency social media recognition of worker contributions.

**Procedural Status:** Two separate Administrative Judges issued summary judgment findings of no discrimination or retaliation. Complainants have appealed both decisions.
