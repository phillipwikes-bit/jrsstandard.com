**Investigation Record Summary**

**Background**

During the period at issue, Complainant worked for the Agency as a Taxpayer Advocate in Laguna Niguel, California. On November 4, 2021, she filed a formal EEO complaint alleging that the Agency discriminated against her based on race (Hispanic) and national origin (Mexican). By letter dated November 16, 2021, the Agency accepted the complaint for investigation and framed the claim as follows:

Whether a co-worker who served as both an On-the-Job Instructor (OJI) and a Lead subjected Complainant to harassment based on race/national origin (Hispanic/Mexican) on multiple occasions from June 2020 through July 2021, when, among other things, the OJI/Lead:

1. Made fun of Complainant's accent and said she disliked speaking to Complainant.
2. Tried to sabotage Complainant's work by creating a list of inventory late actions and expired casework, and purposely gave Complainant misinformation about how to process cases.
3. Filled Complainant's calendar with unrealistic appointments that not even experienced employees could complete.
4. Advised Complainant's manager to have Complainant process a fraudulent tax return.

**Procedural History**

After the investigation, the Agency gave Complainant a copy of the report of investigation and notice of her right to request a hearing before an EEOC Administrative Judge. Complainant did not request a hearing within the time frame set out in 29 C.F.R. § 1614.108(f). The Agency therefore issued a final decision under 29 C.F.R. § 1614.110(b), finding no discrimination.

In that decision, the Agency determined that "assuming [Complainant's initial supervisor, S1] made these and other similar comments [as set forth in incident (1)], the statements would be unwelcome verbal conduct based on Complainant's protected categories of race/national origin/ethnicity." Final Agency Decision (FAD) at 12. The Agency nevertheless went on to conclude:

"Complainant has produced no persuasive evidence that management's actions described in [alleged incidents] 2, 3, and 4 were harassment based on her statutorily protected classes. Complainant's uncorroborated statements that management was motivated by race/ethnicity and/or national origin to take the actions complained of are not sufficient to sustain her burden of showing harassment…Finally, we note that Complainant's allegations of harassment were addressed through an internal anti-harassment inquiry, which did not substantiate Complainant's claims." FAD at 13.

Complainant then filed the present appeal.

**Complainant's Contentions on Appeal**

Complainant argues that the final decision failed to state properly that the alleged harasser, S1, was originally her manager, before later serving as her Lead and OJI. She repeats that S1 made derogatory comments about her accent beginning on her first day of employment. She also asserts that she notified S2 of S1's conduct and asked for a change of supervisor, but that S2 ignored the harassment and allowed it to continue.

**Agency's Response**

The Agency asks that the final decision finding no discrimination be affirmed. It maintains that Complainant failed to establish that she was subjected to harassment.

As to S1's alleged comments about Complainant's accent, the Agency states: "[a]ssuming the truth of Complainant's allegations, nothing in S1's comments indicate she mocked Complainant's accent or that the comments were based on her being of Mexican descent. At best, [S1] indicated she expressed difficulty in communicating with Complainant. At worst, S1's comments were the type of insensitive, hurtful comments that the Commission has declined to find as discriminatory." Agency Response Brief at 11.

The Agency further contends that Complainant's allegations fall short of the severity and pervasiveness required to establish a hostile work environment. It also states that management promptly separated Complainant and S1. According to the Agency, after Complainant raised the harassment issue in late July 2021, S4 separated the two, and Complainant stated that she had no further contact with S1 afterward.

Finally, regarding Complainant's report of the alleged harassment to S2, the Agency states that it "appears" Complainant only expressed a "lack of support." Agency Response Brief at 17-18.
