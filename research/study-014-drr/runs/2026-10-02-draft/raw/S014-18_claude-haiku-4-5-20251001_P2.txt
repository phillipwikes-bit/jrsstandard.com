# FINDINGS

## Background and Allegations

The Complainant, a CBP Officer (GS-1895-12) at the El Paso Port of Entry, filed an Equal Employment Opportunity complaint on April 13, 2020, alleging hostile work environment and discrimination based on sex (female), disability (cervical myelopathy with cervical fusion and limited neck movement), and age (56). The alleged incidents occurred between September 8, 2019, and February 6, 2020, during a period when Complainant was assigned to Light Duty status.

## Nature of Allegations

The complaint encompasses fifteen distinct allegations involving two supervisory officers. The allegations include:

- **Assignment of Inappropriate Duties**: Assignment of cashier and full-duty passport control work despite Light Duty restrictions
- **Verbal Conduct**: Multiple instances of yelling, raised voice communications, and derogatory statements
- **Hostile Comments**: Statements including that Complainant was a "liability," did not want to work, and was "nonproductive worthless"
- **Isolation and Surveillance**: Approximately 10-15 interruptions of Complainant's conversations with coworkers
- **Schedule Changes**: Unilateral modification of Light Duty shift assignments
- **Confrontational Incidents**: Threatening conduct, demands to perform prohibited duties, and directives to leave work areas
- **Formal Disciplinary Action**: Issuance of a Weingarten notice and formal accusations of insubordination and neglect of duties

## Investigative Conclusion

The Agency concluded that Complainant failed to establish that she was subjected to unlawful discrimination or disparate treatment on the bases of sex, disability, or age. The Agency found insufficient evidence to support the allegations as presented.
