# INVESTIGATION RECORD SUMMARY

**COMPLAINANT POSITION AND EMPLOYMENT:**
Complainant worked as a PSE Sales and Services Distribution Associate, Grade Level 06 P, at the Agency's Cape Central Post Office in Cape Coral, Florida.

**COMPLAINT FILING:**
On April 4, 2022, Complainant filed a formal EEO complaint claiming racial harassment based on her race ("White") when her first-line supervisor (Supervisor) (African American) made racial slurs and threatened to physically assault her, and when she informed management officials, they did nothing. (ROI, Affidavit A)

**ALLEGED INCIDENTS:**

Complainant stated during the investigation:
- **Late January/early February 2022:** Supervisor told her not to clock out for lunch due to overtime. When she asked if Supervisor would input her hours into the computer, he responded, "I'll slap the shit out of you." Later, when she asked if she could take her break, Supervisor responded, "people shouldn't be allowed breaks, Black people used to be slaves." (ROI, Affidavit A)

- **February 3, 2022:** When Complainant asked Supervisor for clarification of instructions, he responded, "I'll slap the shit out of you if you don't catch on." (ROI, Affidavit A)

- **February 5, 2022:** When Complainant told Supervisor a package was too heavy to lift, he responded she was "full of shit. You can't lift that?" (ROI, Affidavit A)

- **February 8, 2022:** After Complainant was instructed to bring the wagons around, Supervisor said, "You need to do it because you're White...." (ROI, Affidavit A)

- **February 24, 2022:** When Complainant said "huh?" after not hearing what Supervisor said, he responded, "What do you mean huh? I'll slap the shit out of you." (ROI, Affidavit A)

- **March 3, 2022:** While Complainant was walking out of the housekeeping closet after getting disposable gloves, Supervisor yelled, "White people don't steal, they buy their own shit." (ROI, Affidavit A)

Complainant stated she reported Supervisor's racial slurs and threats to the Manager, Customer Service (Manager) (Caucasian) and the union, but nothing was done. (ROI, Affidavit A)

**RESPONDENT SUPERVISOR'S STATEMENT:**
Supervisor unequivocally denied making any of the statements alleged by Complainant. He stated he believed they basically had a good working relationship until the complaint was filed. (ROI, Affidavit B)

**MANAGER'S STATEMENT:**
Manager affirmatively stated that Complainant never reported to her any of the comments allegedly made by Supervisor. (ROI, Affidavit C)

**SUPERVISOR 2'S STATEMENT:**
Another supervisor (Supervisor 2) (Caucasian) stated that Complainant once told him that Supervisor told her he would "slap her upside the head." He told her to report the matter to Manager. Supervisor 2 denied Complainant told him about any racial comments from Supervisor and asserted that Complainant told him she did not want to report the matter to Manager. (ROI, Affidavit D)

**CLAIM WITHDRAWAL:**
Complainant originally alleged unlawful retaliation but withdrew this claim during the investigation into her complaint, leaving only the racial harassment claim.

**AGENCY ACTION:**
Agency management has since apparently reassigned Complainant to a different supervisor. Complainant did not raise her reassignment as a claim.

**EEO INVESTIGATION AND NOTICE:**
After the EEO investigation, the Agency provided Complainant with a copy of the report of investigation and notice of her right to request a hearing before an EEOC Administrative Judge (AJ).

**ADMINISTRATIVE JUDGE HEARING:**
Complainant requested a hearing. However, Complainant did not respond to the AJ's October 24, 2022 order to submit preliminary case information and did not appear at the initial conference scheduled for November 24, 2022, as ordered by the AJ. On January 19, 2023, the AJ dismissed Complainant's hearing request and remanded the formal EEO complaint to the Agency for a final decision.

**FINAL DECISION:**
The Agency issued a final decision pursuant to 29 C.F.R. § 1614.110(b), finding no discrimination or unlawful retaliation was established as alleged.
