# CASE FILE RECORD - EEO COMPLAINT INVESTIGATION

**COMPLAINANT POSITION AND EMPLOYMENT:**
The complainant was employed as a PSE Sales and Services Distribution Associate, Grade Level 06 P, at the Agency's Cape Central Post Office in Cape Coral, Florida, during the relevant period of this investigation.

**FORMAL COMPLAINT FILING AND ALLEGATIONS:**
On April 4, 2022, the complainant filed a formal Equal Employment Opportunity (EEO) complaint alleging racial harassment based on her race as a White employee. She claimed that during February 2022, her first-line supervisor, an African American male, subjected her to racial slurs, made threatening statements regarding physical assault, and created a hostile work environment. The complainant further alleged that when she reported these incidents to management officials, no corrective action was taken.

**DETAILED ALLEGATIONS:**
During the investigation, the complainant provided specific accounts of alleged incidents. In late January or early February 2022, she reported that the supervisor told her not to clock out for lunch due to overtime. When she requested that he input her hours into the computer system, she claimed he responded with the threat, "I'll slap the shit out of you." She further alleged that when she asked permission to take her break, the supervisor made a racially charged remark: "people shouldn't be allowed breaks, Black people used to be slaves."

On February 3, 2022, the complainant stated that after asking for clarification of work instructions, the supervisor again threatened her with the statement, "I'll slap the shit out of you if you don't catch on." On February 5, 2022, regarding a package she deemed too heavy to lift, she reported the supervisor said she was "full of shit. You can't lift that?" 

The complainant alleged that on February 8, 2022, when instructed to move wagons, the supervisor stated, "You need to do it because you're White." On February 24, 2022, after she said "huh?" upon not hearing the supervisor clearly, he allegedly responded, "What do you mean huh? I'll slap the shit out of you." Finally, on March 3, 2022, while retrieving disposable gloves from the housekeeping closet, she reported the supervisor yelled, "White people don't steal, they buy their own shit."

**WITNESS STATEMENTS AND DENIALS:**
The supervisor categorically denied making any of the alleged statements, asserting that he and the complainant had maintained a positive working relationship until the complaint was filed. The Manager of Customer Service stated unequivocally that the complainant never reported any of the alleged comments to her office. A second supervisor recounted that the complainant mentioned the supervisor had threatened to "slap her upside the head" and advised her to report the matter to management. However, this supervisor denied receiving any reports of racial comments and stated the complainant declined to formally report the incident to the manager.

**COMPLAINT WITHDRAWAL AND REASSIGNMENT:**
The complainant originally alleged unlawful retaliation but withdrew this claim during the investigation, proceeding only with the racial harassment allegations. Subsequently, the Agency reassigned the complainant to a different supervisor, a change she did not contest or raise as a separate claim.

**ADMINISTRATIVE HEARING AND FINAL DETERMINATION:**
Following the investigation, the Agency provided the complainant with the investigative report and notice of her right to request an EEOC Administrative Judge hearing. The complainant exercised this right; however, she failed to respond to the Administrative Judge's October 24, 2022 order requesting preliminary case information and did not appear at the scheduled initial conference on November 24, 2022. Consequently, on January 19, 2023, the Administrative Judge dismissed the hearing request and remanded the complaint to the Agency for final disposition. The Agency subsequently issued a final decision determining that no discrimination or unlawful retaliation was established based on the allegations presented. This appeal followed.
