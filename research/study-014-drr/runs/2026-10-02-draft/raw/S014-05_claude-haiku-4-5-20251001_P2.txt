# FINDINGS

## Background and Jurisdiction

The Complainant, a PSE Sales and Services Distribution Associate, Grade Level 06 P, employed at the Agency's Cape Central Post Office in Cape Coral, Florida, filed a formal Equal Employment Opportunity (EEO) complaint on April 4, 2022. The complaint alleged that she was subjected to ongoing racial harassment during the period of late January through early March 2022. Specifically, Complainant claimed that her first-line supervisor, who is African American, made racial slurs, made offensive statements referencing slavery, and threatened to physically assault her. Complainant further alleged that when she reported these incidents to management officials, no action was taken. Complainant initially included a retaliation claim but withdrew it during the investigation. The Agency conducted an investigation and subsequently issued a final decision finding no discrimination or unlawful retaliation. Complainant requested a hearing before an EEOC Administrative Judge but failed to comply with procedural orders and was dismissed from the hearing process.

## Factual Findings

**Complainant's Account of Alleged Incidents:**

Complainant alleged that between late January and early March 2022, her supervisor made the following statements and threats:
- Late January/early February 2022: When asked about inputting hours into the computer, Supervisor allegedly responded, "I'll slap the shit out of you."
- When asked about taking a break, Supervisor allegedly stated, "people shouldn't be allowed breaks, Black people used to be slaves."
- February 3, 2022: When Complainant asked for clarification, Supervisor allegedly threatened, "I'll slap the shit out of you if you don't catch on."
- February 5, 2022: Regarding a heavy package, Supervisor allegedly responded, "full of shit. You can't lift that?"
- February 8, 2022: When instructed to bring wagons around, Supervisor allegedly stated, "You need to do it because you're White..."
- February 24, 2022: When Complainant said "huh?" after not hearing Supervisor, he allegedly responded, "What do you mean huh? I'll slap the shit out of you."
- March 3, 2022: Upon observing Complainant exit the housekeeping closet, Supervisor allegedly stated, "White people don't steal, they buy their own shit."

Complainant asserted that she reported these incidents to the Manager, Customer Service, and to the union, but no action was taken.

**Supervisor's Response:**

The Supervisor unequivocally denied making any of the statements attributed to him. He stated that he believed he and Complainant had a good working relationship until the formal complaint was filed.

**Manager's Statement:**

The Manager affirmatively stated that Complainant never reported to her any of the comments allegedly made by Supervisor.

**Corroborating Witness:**

Another supervisor (Supervisor 2) recalled that Complainant once mentioned that Supervisor told her he would "slap her upside the head." However, Supervisor 2 did not corroborate any of the alleged racial statements. He stated that he advised Complainant to report the matter to the Manager and that Complainant indicated she did not wish to file such a report.

**Post-Investigation Actions:**

Following the Agency's investigation and final decision, the Agency apparently reassigned Complainant to a different supervisor. Complainant did not raise her reassignment as a claim in her complaint.

**Procedural History:**

After receiving notice of her right to request a hearing before an EEOC Administrative Judge, Complainant requested a hearing. However, she failed to comply with the Administrative Judge's October 24, 2022 order to submit preliminary case information and did not appear at the initial conference scheduled for November 24, 2022. On January 19, 2023, the Administrative Judge dismissed Complainant's hearing request and remanded the complaint to the Agency for a final decision.

## Conclusion Regarding Credibility and Evidence

The record reflects material contradictions between Complainant's allegations and the testimony of those who would have observed or been informed of the alleged conduct. The Manager's denial that Complainant ever reported the alleged incidents to her is particularly significant, given Complainant's explicit claim of having done so. While Supervisor 2 provided limited corroboration regarding a threat to "slap," he did not corroborate any of the racial statements central to Complainant's claim and testified that Complainant declined to pursue a formal report with management. The Supervisor's categorical denial, coupled with his assertion of a positive working relationship prior to the complaint, further undercuts Complainant's account. The lack of documentary evidence, corroborating witnesses to the specific incidents alleged, and the contradictions between Complainant's account and management's account of whether the incidents were reported create substantial questions regarding the factual basis for the harassment allegations.
