# CASE FILE SUMMARY

**Complainant:** PSE Sales and Services Distribution Associate, Grade 06 P, Cape Central Post Office, Cape Coral, Florida

**Complaint Date:** April 4, 2022

**Allegation:** Racial harassment based on race (White) by African American first-line supervisor during February 2022, including alleged racial slurs, physical assault threats, and unresponsive management.

**Alleged Incidents:**
- Late January/Early February: Threat to "slap the shit out of you" and comment "Black people used to be slaves"
- February 3: Threat to assault if complainant didn't "catch on"
- February 5: Insulting comment about lifting ability
- February 8: Statement "You need to do it because you're White"
- February 24: Threat to assault in response to "huh?"
- March 3: Comment "White people don't steal, they buy their own shit"

Complainant reported incidents to Manager and union; claims no action was taken.

**Investigation Findings:**
- Supervisor denied all allegations
- Manager stated Complainant never reported incidents to her
- Supervisor 2 confirmed only one threat ("slap upside the head") and denied knowledge of racial comments
- Complainant withdrew retaliation claim during investigation
- Agency reassigned Complainant to different supervisor

**Hearing:** Complainant failed to respond to AJ's October 24, 2022 order and did not appear at November 24, 2022 initial conference. AJ dismissed hearing January 19, 2023, remanding to Agency.

**Final Decision:** Agency found no discrimination or unlawful retaliation established.

**Status:** Appeal pending.
