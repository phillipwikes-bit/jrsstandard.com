**Case File Summary**

**Parties and position.** Complainant, who identifies as White, was a PSE Sales and Services Distribution Associate (Grade Level 06 P) at the Agency's Cape Central Post Office in Cape Coral, Florida. Her first-line supervisor (Supervisor) is African American. The Manager, Customer Service (Manager) is Caucasian, as is a second supervisor (Supervisor 2).

**Complaint.** On April 4, 2022, Complainant filed a formal EEO complaint alleging ongoing racial harassment (race: White). She claimed that in and around February 2022, Supervisor made racial slurs and threatened to physically assault her, and that management did nothing when she told them. The Agency accepted the complaint and investigated. She also originally alleged retaliation but withdrew that claim during the investigation, leaving only the harassment claim.

**Complainant's statements (ROI, Affidavit A).** Complainant said the following occurred:
- **Late January/early February 2022:** Supervisor told her not to clock out for lunch because of overtime. When she asked whether he would input her hours, he said, "I'll slap the shit out of you." When she later asked to take her break, he said, "people shouldn't be allowed breaks, Black people used to be slaves."
- **February 3, 2022:** When she asked for clarification of instructions, Supervisor said, "I'll slap the shit out of you if you don't catch on."
- **February 5, 2022:** When she said a package was too heavy to lift, Supervisor said she was "full of shit. You can't lift that?"
- **February 8, 2022:** After being told to bring the wagons around, she said Supervisor told her, "You need to do it because you're White...".
- **February 24, 2022:** When she said "huh?" because she did not hear him, Supervisor said, "What do you mean huh? I'll slap the shit out of you."
- **March 3, 2022:** As she left the housekeeping closet after getting disposable gloves, Supervisor yelled, "White people don't steal, they buy their own shit."
- **Reporting:** She said she reported the slurs and threats to Manager and to the union, but nothing was done.

**Responses considered.**
- **Supervisor (ROI, Affidavit B):** He unequivocally denied making any of the alleged statements. He said he believed they had a good working relationship until the complaint was filed.
- **Manager (ROI, Affidavit C):** She affirmatively stated that Complainant never reported any of the alleged comments to her.
- **Supervisor 2 (ROI, Affidavit D):** He stated that Complainant once told him Supervisor said he would "slap her upside the head," and that he told her to report it to Manager. He denied that she told him of any racial comments. He asserted that she told him she did not want to report the matter to Manager.

**Other facts.** Agency management has apparently since reassigned Complainant to a different supervisor. She did not raise the reassignment as a claim.

**Procedural history.**
- After the investigation, the Agency gave Complainant the report of investigation and notice of her right to request a hearing before an EEOC Administrative Judge (AJ). She requested a hearing.
- She did not respond to the AJ's October 24, 2022 order to submit preliminary case information. She also did not appear at the initial conference scheduled for November 24, 2022, as the AJ had ordered.
- On January 19, 2023, the AJ dismissed the hearing request and remanded the complaint to the Agency for a final decision.
- The Agency then issued a final decision under 29 C.F.R. § 1614.110(b). It found that no discrimination or unlawful retaliation was established as alleged. The record does not explain why retaliation was addressed given that Complainant had withdrawn that claim.
- Complainant then filed the instant appeal.

**Decision-makers.** The AJ dismissed the hearing request, and the Agency issued the final decision.

**Criteria applied.** The record cites only 29 C.F.R. § 1614.110(b) as the authority for the Agency's final decision. It does not state the substantive standard the Agency applied to the harassment claim. It also does not state the outcome of the appeal.
