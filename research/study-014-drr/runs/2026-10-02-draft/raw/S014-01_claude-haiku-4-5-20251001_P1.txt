# CASE FILE SUMMARY

**Complainant:** GS-12 Senior Bankruptcy Specialist, Small Business/Self-Employed Division, Houston, Texas

**Complaint Date:** September 17, 2021

**Alleged Bases:** Disability (Major Depression, Somatic Symptoms Disorder, Generalized Anxiety, TIA, Myofascial Pain, Acute Stress Disorder) and reprisal for prior EEO activity

---

## ALLEGATIONS AND FINDINGS

**Claim 1 & 3 – FMLA Leave Denial:**
Complainant alleged denial of paid FMLA leave from February 2021 onward and specific denial of eight hours on August 3, 2021. Investigation revealed Complainant had exhausted available paid leave by March 2021, as documented in Agency's February 26, 2021 letter. Upon Complainant's August 5, 2021 audit request, a payroll review showed paid leave was available, and the Agency retroactively granted all requested paid leave. Complainant conceded she received all entitled leave. No comparators identified.

**Claim 2 – Case Reassignment:**
On June 2021, retired co-worker's cases were redistributed among three GS-12 Bankruptcy Specialists alphabetically. Supervisor documented appropriate grade-level work assignment and equal distribution. No evidence of disparate treatment; no similarly situated employees identified as treated more favorably.

**Claim 4 – Reasonable Accommodations:**
Complainant requested reduced workload and/or reassignment in May 2021; amended request to include reassignment on May 18, 2021. Agency approved a lightweight portable printer and interim mail assistance. Complainant cancelled the printer before delivery, citing accountability concerns and lifting limitations. Agency denied reduced workload (citing already-low 25-case inventory, medical documentation gaps, and mission impact) and reassignment (noting accommodation of last resort status and Complainant's demonstrated ability to perform essential duties). No medical evidence supported accommodation need.

**Claim 5 – Official Time Denial:**
Supervisor denied official time on October 26, 2021 after Complainant received approximately 18 hours to complete an affidavit, with only two questions remaining. Agency directed Complainant to request extension directly from investigator.

---

## ADMINISTRATIVE JUDGE DECISION

The Administrative Judge issued summary judgment in favor of the Agency, finding:
- No hostile work environment established; alleged conduct insufficiently severe or pervasive
- No discriminatory or retaliatory animus demonstrated
- All leave entitlements properly provided
- Case reassignment reasonable and equitable
- Reasonable accommodations offered and appropriate
- Official time grant was objectively reasonable

**Final Order:** Agency's final order adopted the Administrative Judge's findings. Complaint dismissed.
