# CASE FILE SUMMARY

**COMPLAINANT'S EMPLOYMENT AND COMPLAINT FILING**

Complainant worked as a Biologist, GS-0401-11, until promotion to GS-12 in May 2013, at the Agency's National Institute on Aging, Laboratory of Neurogenetics ("Lab"), Neuromuscular Disease Research Section ("Section") in Bethesda, Maryland. On November 8, 2017, Complainant filed an EEO complaint, as amended, alleging discrimination and reprisal.

**CLAIMED DISABILITIES**

Complainant stated she has been diagnosed with bipolar disorder (2009), Post Traumatic Stress Disorder (PTSD) (2017), generalized anxiety disorder, benign mitral valve prolapse, and fibroid tumors requiring surgical removal (December 18, 2013, with five-day hospital stay and six weeks home recovery).

**PROTECTED EEO ACTIVITY**

Complainant initiated EEO counseling on July 27, 2017, regarding the instant EEO complaint.

**ALLEGATIONS OF DISCRIMINATION BASED ON RACE/COLOR (AFRICAN AMERICAN/BLACK), NATIONAL ORIGIN (GUYANESE), AND SEX (FEMALE)**

1. **June 13, 2017**: Section Chief (Ireland, Caucasian, male), first-line supervisor, did not conduct a scheduled meeting with Complainant.

2. **June 15, 2017**: Section Chief changed Complainant's work assignment to making DNA plates, a role usually suitable for summer students, not a biologist with a Ph.D.

3. **2013**: Section Chief told a colleague Complainant had a heart problem and disclosed dissatisfaction with managing her medical issues. Complainant stated she never disclosed her heart condition to Section Chief or Lab Chief and believed Section Chief made this statement "in an effort to protect himself of [sic.] disclosing my medical information." Her colleague stated he had expressed concern about Complainant's well-being to Section Chief, who advised she had a heart condition.

4. **June 2015**: Section Chief told Complainant she had to accept a lower model computer than other employees in the laboratory.

5. **2015**: Section Chief acknowledged Complainant deserved a promotion but delayed processing the paperwork until 2017, during a hiring freeze. Complainant was notified of the hiring freeze in February 2017, and that it was indefinite.

6. **February 23, 2018**: Section Chief failed to promote Complainant.

**ALLEGATIONS OF DISABILITY DISCRIMINATION**

Same as above claims 3, 4, 5, and 6, plus:

7. **Late December 2013 or 2014**: Section Chief expressed surprise that Complainant wrote well.

8. **2016 and ongoing**: Section Chief was not present for Complainant's 1:1 standing meetings.

9. **2016 and ongoing**: Section Chief warned Complainant not to knock on his office door when closed.

**ALLEGATIONS OF HOSTILE WORK ENVIRONMENT BASED ON RACE/COLOR, NATIONAL ORIGIN, SEX, AND DISABILITY**

1. **2009**: Section Chief began subjecting Complainant to inappropriate comments and unfair treatment; directed lab employees to work on a project, made inappropriate comments, retracted them, and joked he should stop before an EEO complaint is filed against him.

2. **2010**: Section Chief told Complainant the Agency would submit findings to a top-tier journal without her writing because it had to be perfect; purposely distracted her during an experiment and called her an "idiot"; required her to work on Christmas Day; repeatedly told her she wrote slow and it took her forever to complete her Ph.D. program.

3. **March 2011**: Section Chief assigned Complainant to lead a task force; she had difficulties because he often excluded her from emails communicating assigned tasks to the group.

4. **April 2011**: Section Chief told Complainant no one would put up with her mental illness if she left.

5. **September 2011**: After Complainant told Section Chief she met with Career Services, he questioned why she would talk to anyone about her career and said she should stay and be comfortable where she is; on a research article, he put her name last.

6. **2011**: Section Chief told Complainant she would like the movie, "The Help."

7. **2012**: Section Chief joked to Complainant about being from Africa.

8. **2013**: Section Chief had an uncomfortable exchange with Complainant about her ancestry; advised post-baccalaureate students to check her work; called her a "Diva," causing colleagues not to approach her; precluded her from writing top-tier papers.

9. **2013 and ongoing**: Section Chief precluded Complainant from writing top-tier papers.

10. **May 5, 2017**: Section Chief excluded Complainant from a project after telling her he would include her.

11. **May 12, 2017**: Section Chief yelled at Complainant in front of colleagues "No wonder nothing you do ever works" when she answered his questions on protocols.

12. **Late May or June 2017**: Section Chief laughed with another colleague and a new trainee about the time he required Complainant to work on Christmas Day.

13. **Section Chief approached Complainant and pressured her to disclose her political affiliation** (no specific date provided).

**ALLEGATIONS OF REPRISAL FOR PROTECTED EEO ACTIVITY (INITIATING EEO COUNSELING ON JULY 27, 2017)**

1. After July 27, 2017: Agency management excluded Complainant from a major project in her office/lab.

2. After July 27, 2017: Section Chief did not communicate with her on work assignments.

3. Agency management has not assigned her any projects in retaliation for filing the informal complaint.

4. **2013 and 2017**: Section Chief notified Complainant multiple times to avoid working with a colleague because she had filed EEO complaints. Regarding two incidents, the second was in April 2017. (Per footnote 2: The Agency's definition did not include the month in 2017, but Complainant stated she recalled two incidents, the second in April 2017.)

5. **February 2018**: The Agency assigned Complainant to report to Lab Chief (British, Caucasian, male), second-line supervisor, due to her EEO complaint.

6. **March 2018**: Lab Chief re-positioned Complainant's photograph under a different lab group in the breakroom, although she remained a member of the Section.

7. **March 14, 2018**: Section Chief did not communicate with Complainant about an ongoing collaborative project, and because Section Chief did not speak directly to RMO-2 about the project, Complainant was unable to work on it.

8. **April 5, 2018**: Section Chief communicated to staff Complainant no longer reported to him.

9. **April 2018**: The Agency removed Complainant's name from the Lab staff listing on the National Institute on Aging website, causing harm because her job entailed working with outside agencies, individuals, and groups.

10. **April 23, 2018**: All employees and collaborators copied Lab Chief on every email sent to Complainant, including on minor issues.

**AGENCY INVESTIGATION AND DISMISSAL**

The Agency investigated Complainant's EEO complaint, as amended. An Administrative Judge (AJ) remanded the complaint to the Agency for a final decision. The Agency dismissed claims 6–11 for failure to timely initiate EEO counseling. The Agency found no discrimination on the remaining claims.

**APPEAL AND PROCEDURAL ISSUE**

Complainant filed an appeal before issuance of the Final Agency Decision (FAD). Complainant argues that more than 15 months elapsed after the AJ's 60-day time limit with still no FAD. Complainant submitted documentation showing that on May 20, 2020, she asked the EEO office about the status of the FAD. Citing caselaw, Complainant argues this extraordinary tardiness warrants a default judgment against the Agency as a sanction. The Agency did not reply to Complainant's appeal.
