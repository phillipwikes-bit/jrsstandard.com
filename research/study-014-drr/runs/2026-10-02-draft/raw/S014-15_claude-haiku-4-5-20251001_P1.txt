# CASE FILE SUMMARY

**Complainant:** City Carrier Technician, A-02/Q, Village Station, New York

**Complaint Date:** Post-October 4, 2016

**Allegations:**
1. Assignment to work outside medical restrictions and improper/late compensation (June 26, 2016 onward)
2. Deletion of clock rings (October 4, 2016 and other dates)
3. Denial of 204-B (acting supervisor) detail assignment

**Medical Background:**
Complainant suffered an on-the-job back injury (May 10, 2014) resulting in back sprain, lumbar disc displacement, and hernia. Documented restrictions: sitting 3 hours, walking 30 minutes maximum, standing 10 minutes, no reaching above shoulder, twisting, bending, stooping, pushing, pulling, lifting, squatting, kneeling, or climbing; 15-minute breaks required hourly.

**Key Facts:**
- Complainant accepted a modified limited-duty assignment (July 18, 2016) shuttling carriers via van and delivering express mail (up to 4 hours/30 minutes daily)
- Agency offered alternative lobby attendant position; complainant declined
- Clock rings deleted July 18-28, 2016; time coded as workers' compensation due to facility transfer from Village Station to Lenox Hill Station
- 204-B detail denied; Manager cited lack of operational need and concerns about complainant's punctuality and reliability

**Administrative Judge Decision:**
Ruled in favor of Agency. Found:
- Complainant provided reasonable accommodation (modified assignments within restrictions)
- Medical restrictions too severe for job performance with or without accommodation
- Agency articulated legitimate, nondiscriminatory reasons for all challenged actions
- No evidence of discriminatory or retaliatory intent
- Clock ring errors attributed to administrative procedures (manual timecards, facility transfer, system errors)

**Outcome:** Final Agency order adopting AJ decision; appeal filed.
