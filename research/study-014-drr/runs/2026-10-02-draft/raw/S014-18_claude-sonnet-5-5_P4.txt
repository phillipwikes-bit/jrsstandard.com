# Case File Summary

**Complainant and position:** At the time of the events, Complainant was a CBP Officer, GS-1895-12, at the Agency's El Paso Port of Entry, El Paso Field Office, Office of Field Operations, El Paso, Texas.

**Complaint filed:** April 13, 2020. Complainant alleged a hostile work environment and discrimination based on sex (female), disability (physical: cervical myelopathy, cervical fusion with limited neck movement) and age (56).

**Allegations.** All of the following are Complainant's allegations, as stated in the complaint. Quoted words are attributed by Complainant to the named supervisors.

1. From September 8, 2019, to January 2020, Supervisor 1 (Supervisory CBP Officer) assigned Complainant regular cashier duties in addition to issuing I-94s, although Complainant was on Light Duty.
2. In September 2019, Supervisor 1 told the Program Manager she did not want to work with Complainant in Passport Control Secondary because Complainant was on Light Duty and had no weapon.
3. On September 13, 2019, Supervisor 1 told Complainant she did not want to work with her and that Complainant was a liability due to her health issues.
4. From October 2019 to February 2020, on approximately 10 to 15 occasions, Supervisor 1 interrupted Complainant's conversations with other employees and asked them to stop talking.
5. On December 24, 2019, Supervisor 1 assigned Complainant passport control duties despite Light Duty. When Complainant said she could help but could not perform regular duties for a full eight hours, Supervisor 1 replied "it is what it is."
6. On December 24, 2019, in front of the public, Supervisor 1 yelled at Complainant to start issuing permits, said Complainant did not want to do her job, and told members of the public they could file a complaint against Complainant for not attending to them.
7. On January 1 and 3, 2020, Supervisor 1 yelled at Complainant. She accused Complainant of doing a pat down and processing a case without a weapon while on Light Duty, and of abusing her light duty schedule.
8. On January 2, 2020, Supervisor 1 accused Complainant of making false statements in a memorandum.
9. On January 3, 2020, Supervisor 1 commented that Complainant did not want to work.
10. On January 3, 2020, after Supervisor 1 called the Chief about Complainant's schedule, Complainant's Light Duty shift was changed from 10:00 p.m.–6:00 a.m. to midnight–8:00 a.m. for two days before she returned to full duty on her regular shift.
11. On January 6, 2020, Supervisor 1 yelled at Complainant over the phone, accusing her of working the wrong schedule and of not wanting to perform her duties.
12. On January 15 and 16, 2020, Supervisor 1 raised her tone and claimed Complainant did not want to do her job. She threatened a verbal warning and reprimand for not taking over another officer's duties, and threatened to write Complainant up for insubordination and disobeying a direct order.
13. On January 16, 2020, Supervisor 2 (another Supervisory CBP Officer) responded to Complainant's request to initial an assignment by saying "I'm not signing shit for you." Later, after Complainant refused to perform cashier duties, he said he did not need a nonproductive worthless person there and told her to go to the back of the office.
14. On January 31, 2020, Supervisor 1 approached Complainant in a threatening voice and demanded she perform cashier duties although three other officers were available. After Supervisor 1 called Supervisor 2, he reassigned Complainant to detention log officer duties, raised his voice, and demanded she leave the work area and work elsewhere. When she did not, he told her to go home on administrative leave. Complainant did not go home.
15. On February 6, 2020, Complainant received a Weingarten notice. She was informed that Supervisor 1 and Supervisor 2 accused her of not wanting to work, disobedience, neglecting duties and disobeying an order, and she was asked to provide a written statement to the Chief.

**Procedural history:** At the conclusion of the investigation, the Agency gave Complainant a copy of the report of investigation (ROI) and notice of her right to request a hearing before an EEOC Administrative Judge. At Complainant's request, the Agency instead issued a final decision under 29 C.F.R. § 1614.110(b).

**Agency decision:** The Agency concluded that Complainant failed to prove she was subjected to discrimination as alleged. It found she failed to show she was subjected to disparate treatment.
