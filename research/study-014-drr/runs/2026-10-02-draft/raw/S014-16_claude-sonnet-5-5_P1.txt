**Case Summary**

**Parties and background:** Complainant, a GS-12 Deportation Officer at the Agency's San Bernardina, California office, alleged discrimination and a hostile work environment based on national origin (Romanian), age (48; YOB 1971), and reprisal for prior EEO activity (HS-ICE-21417-2012 and HS-ICE-22663-2012). His first-line supervisor was a Supervisory Detention and Deportation Officer (Vietnamese; YOB 1977; no prior EEO activity). Complainant made EEO contact on October 12, 2018 and filed a formal complaint on June 6, 2019.

**Claims:**
1. On July 26, 2018, Supervisor denied Complainant's request to attend a concert with his daughter.
2. On July 27, 2018, Supervisor asked Complainant to return to work after he had already left for the day.
3. On October 1, 2018, Complainant became aware of pay discrepancies made by Supervisor.
4. On October 12, 2018, Supervisor admonished Complainant several times in his Performance Work Plan (PWP) and rated him in an area not in his official PWP.

**Supervisor's account (ROI):**
- **Claim 1:** Complainant asked by text to adjust his schedule to 0500–1300. Supervisor said the earliest start was 0600 or Complainant could take annual leave. Complainant said he would take leave but reported to work instead. Supervisor said DO 1 (YOB 1982; no prior EEO activity) was not treated differently.
- **Claim 2:** Supervisor said he asked Complainant to come to his office during duty hours. Complainant then worked the remaining hours and overtime until 7:00 p.m. The record shows Complainant claimed 0500–1900 that day (8 hours regular pay, 6 hours AUO).
- **Claim 3:** Complainant claimed AUO for 3:00–5:00 p.m. on August 28, 2018, but was in Defensive Tactics training until 4:00 p.m. Supervisor said the training hour was excludable from AUO and adjusted the pay. Complainant alleged Supervisor told DO 2 (YOB 1969; prior EEO activity) of his AUO discrepancy before correcting it. Supervisor said DO 2 was in the office when the discrepancy was found.
- **Claim 4:** Supervisor said he rated Complainant only on elements in his PWP and did not admonish him. He said Complainant became upset and walked away, which ended discussion. The record supports Supervisor's account of the rated elements.

**Procedural history:**
- Complainant requested an EEOC hearing after the investigation.
- He failed to submit a completed investigative affidavit despite repeated requests by the investigator and a May 11, 2021 AJ order. He also gave no good-cause response to the AJ's June 7, 2021 Order to Show Good Cause beyond an apology on June 21, 2021.
- On June 22, 2021, the AJ dismissed the matter from the hearing process as a sanction and remanded it to the Agency for a Final Agency Decision (FAD).

**Agency decision (FAD, August 10, 2021):** The Agency found no discrimination or hostile work environment.
- **Claims 1, 3, 4 (disparate treatment):** Under *McDonnell Douglas*, management articulated legitimate, nondiscriminatory reasons, and Complainant did not show pretext or that the actions were based on a protected class.
- **Claim 2 and the other claims (harassment):** The record showed no discriminatory motive or unwelcome conduct based on a protected class. The conduct was not severe or pervasive.

**Appeal status:** Complainant appealed, but neither party submitted an appeal statement.
