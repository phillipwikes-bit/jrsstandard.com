# CASE FILE SUMMARY

**Complainant:** Travel Specialist, GS-6, Office of Infectious Disease, National Center for HIV/AIDS, Viral Hepatitis, STD and TB Prevention, Atlanta, Georgia

**Filing Date:** April 8, 2014

**Protected Classes Alleged:** Race (Caucasian), National Origin (German, Persian), Sex (Female), Religion (Jehovah's Witness, Jewish), and Reprisal

**Primary Allegations:**

1. Supervisor (S1) told complainant to leave the Agency, claiming management disliked her and union membership would harm her career
2. Support Specialist made disparaging comments about complainant's religious beliefs (Jehovah's Witness)
3. Coworkers made derogatory racial comments about complainant
4. Deputy Director engaged in unwanted sexual harassment including chest staring, inappropriate leg touching during meetings, shoulder massaging, invasion of personal space, and social advances; subsequently retaliated with extra duties, training denial, and hostile treatment
5. Supervisor made discriminatory racial comments ("Stop trying to act Black")
6. Received unsatisfactory performance ratings and was placed on Performance Assistance Plan (PAP) and Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)

**Key Findings:**

The Agency's Final Agency Decision concluded that:
- Complainant failed to establish allegations 1-3 and 5 with corroborating evidence; alleged incidents lacked severity/pervasiveness for hostile work environment claim
- Sexual harassment claim (allegation 4) unproven; while coworkers confirmed complainant's distress and Deputy Director's angry behavior, no witnesses directly observed the alleged sexual conduct
- Performance management actions (allegations 6-11) were supported by legitimate, nondiscriminatory reasons; complainant did not demonstrate pretext or discriminatory motivation

**Outcome:** Complaint denied in its entirety
