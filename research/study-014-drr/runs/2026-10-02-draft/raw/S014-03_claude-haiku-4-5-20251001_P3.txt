# INVESTIGATION RECORD AND CASE FILE DOCUMENTATION

## COMPLAINANT INFORMATION AND EMPLOYMENT STATUS

The complainant in this matter was employed as a Full-time Mail Handler Equipment Operator at the Agency's Morgan Processing and Distribution Center facility located in New York, New York at the time of the events giving rise to this complaint. The complainant's employment status and job classification remained consistent throughout the relevant period under investigation.

## COMPLAINT FILING AND ALLEGATIONS

On November 18, 2021, the complainant filed a formal Equal Employment Opportunity (EEO) complaint with the Agency. The complaint alleged that the Agency discriminated against the complainant on multiple protected bases, specifically citing sex (female), disability (physical), and reprisal for prior protected EEO activity. The legal framework for these allegations encompasses protections under Title VII of the Civil Rights Act and the Rehabilitation Act.

The complaint encompassed four distinct categories of alleged discriminatory conduct occurring over a five-month period. First, beginning on June 25, 2021, and continuing thereafter, the complainant alleged that she was denied overtime work within her stated limitations. Second, on July 19, 2021, the complainant asserted that she was subjected to verbal abuse by a coworker, and that no remedial action was taken by management when the incident was reported. Third, beginning on August 1, 2021, and ongoing, the complainant alleged that she was subjected to micromanagement, was assigned to either the rewrap section or city manual section in a manner suggesting discriminatory intent, and was confronted regarding her job duties. Fourth, on August 31, 2021, the complainant alleged that her request for a temporary change of schedule was denied in a manner consistent with discriminatory treatment.

## AGENCY INVESTIGATION AND REPORT OF INVESTIGATION

Following completion of its investigation into the complaint, the Agency transmitted a Report of Investigation (ROI) and Notice of Right to Request a Hearing before an EEOC Administrative Judge (AJ) to the complainant and her non-attorney representative on April 5, 2022. Concurrent with this transmission, the Agency emailed both parties, indicating that an electronic copy of the final ROI would be available for download for a period of thirty days and requesting confirmation of receipt via email. Documentary evidence in the case file reflects that the message was relayed to the complainant and her representative; however, the destination server did not provide a delivery notification, creating ambiguity regarding actual receipt.

It is noteworthy that the Agency initially accepted a formulation of the coworker verbal abuse allegation that differed from the complainant's original statement. The Agency's initial framing dated the incident as July 20, 2021, and characterized the alleged abuser as a supervisor rather than a coworker. During the investigation, the complainant clarified that the incident occurred on July 19, 2021, that it was indeed a coworker (not a supervisor) who engaged in the verbal abuse, and that supervisory personnel failed to take corrective action after she reported the incident.

## ADDITIONAL DOCUMENTS AND AFFIDAVITS

On April 5, 2022, coinciding with transmission of the ROI, the Agency provided the complainant and her representative with additional documents that had been submitted after the completion of the investigative file, requesting that these materials be added to the complainant's copy of the file. These supplementary documents included an investigative affidavit from an Acting Supervisor, completed on March 28, 2022, and the Acting Supervisor's Litigation Hold Notice, signed on March 31, 2022. In this initial affidavit, the Acting Supervisor stated that he maintained no organizational relationship to the complainant during the relevant time period and that he was not present at the facility on July 20, 2021.

On May 17, 2022, the Agency again emailed the complainant and her representative, advising that an electronic copy of the ROI would be available for download for thirty days. Similar to the prior transmission, the Agency's system indicated that the message was relayed but that the destination server did not provide a delivery notification. The documentary evidence suggests that materials were ultimately provided via first-class mail.

## COMPLAINANT'S RESPONSE AND HEARING REQUEST

On May 31, 2022, the complainant mailed a formal hearing request to both the Agency and the Commission's New York District Office. The Agency's stamped records indicate receipt on June 6, 2022. Accompanying the hearing request was a letter dated May 28, 2022, in which the complainant expressed her intent to refute statements made by the Acting Supervisor in his affidavit, particularly his claim that he was not present on the date of the verbal abuse incident. The complainant requested that the Agency provide her with the Acting Supervisor's Employee Everything Report to corroborate his presence or absence on that date.

According to the complainant's account, on May 18, 2022, she contacted an EEO Services Analyst to discuss the Acting Supervisor's contradictory statements. The complainant alleged that the EEO Services Analyst informed her that her case had been closed on May 1, 2022. When the complainant questioned how her case could be closed without notification or provision of a copy of the ROI, she was reportedly instructed that she could file a hearing request but should attach a letter explaining that she discovered the case closure date of May 18, 2022.

## SUBSEQUENT AGENCY DECISION AND APPEAL

On June 2, 2022, the Agency issued a final decision, citing 29 C.F.R. § 1614.110(b) as the basis for the decision and asserting that the complainant failed to submit a hearing request within the timeframe specified in 29 C.F.R. § 1614.108(f). The Agency concluded in its decision that the complainant failed to establish that the Agency subjected her to discrimination as alleged in her complaint.

The EEOC's New York District Office received the complainant's hearing request on June 10, 2022, which was subsequently docketed as Hearing No. 520-2022-00326X. On June 15, 2022, the complainant filed an appeal of the Agency's final decision.

## UPDATED AGENCY AFFIDAVIT AND ADMINISTRATIVE ADJUDICATION

On July 6, 2022, the Agency submitted an updated affidavit from the Acting Supervisor, requesting that this document also be added to the complainant's file. In this revised affidavit dated June 7, 2022, the Acting Supervisor provided substantially different testimony. He stated that, during the timeframe of the complaint, his organizational relationship with the complainant was that of Acting Supervisor. The Acting Supervisor reiterated that he was not present when the coworker verbally abused the complainant on July 19, 2021, and stated that he did not know whether the complainant or anyone acting on her behalf reported the harassment incident.

On November 29, 2022, an EEOC Administrative Judge issued an order dismissing the complainant's hearing request, noting that the Agency had already issued a final decision on the matter, which the complainant had appealed.
