# CASE FILE SUMMARY

**Parties:** Complainant v. Agency

**Complainant's Position:** Contract Specialist-GS-11 at Agency facility in Gallup, New Mexico

**Complaint Filing:** August 25, 2015 - Formal EEO complaint alleging discrimination based on race (Native American), national origin (Native American), age, and reprisal for protected activity

**Agency Acceptance:** November 23, 2015 - Agency accepted formal complaint for investigation

**Procedural History:**
- Agency conducted investigation and provided Complainant with report of investigation and notice of right to request hearing before EEOC Administrative Judge (AJ)
- Complainant timely requested hearing
- Over Complainant's objections, AJ granted Agency's motion for decision without a hearing dated March 22, 2018
- AJ issued decision by summary judgment on September 23, 2020

**Background:** The FAC-C program established education, experience, and training standards for the federal government acquisition workforce.

**AJ's Findings:**

*Prima Facie Case Failures (Claims 3, 4, 7, 8, 11, 13, 14, and 16):*
- Claim 8: Complainant did not apply and submit an application for the position
- Claims 3, 4, 7, 11, 13, and 16: Complainant did not meet minimum qualifications because she did not have Level II and Level III FAC-C certifications
- Claim 14: Complainant did not establish prima facie case of discrimination/retaliation because:
  - Selectee was of same national origin and sex as Complainant
  - Selectee was older than Complainant
  - No evidence that selecting official was aware of Complainant's EEO activity

*Legitimate, Nondiscriminatory Reasons and Failure to Establish Pretext (Claims 1, 2, 5, 6, 8, 15, 17-20, and 19):*
- Agency articulated legitimate, nondiscriminatory reasons for its actions
- Complainant failed to establish pretext

*Claim 12 (Harassment by C1 - Hugging):*
- Complainant did not claim sexual aspects of the hug
- No evidence that Complainant's protected classes motivated the hug
- Single act not sufficiently severe or pervasive to create hostile work environment

**Agency's Final Order:** Adopted AJ's finding that Complainant failed to prove Agency subjected her to discrimination as alleged

**Complainant's Appeal Arguments:**

- Agency systemically denied promotions to qualified Native Americans
- C1 decided Complainant's 17-hour class on Analyzing Contract Costs was insufficient for Level II certification, even though another named Agency official approved it
- C1 decided, without OPM approval, that future contracting jobs would require Level II certification
- These actions were taken to reduce hiring of Native Americans
- Regarding Claim 10: Agency failed to articulate legitimate, nondiscrimination reasons for its action
- Regarding Claim 12: C1's hug could "support an inference that [C1] has at least implicit bias that Native American women are inferior, like children, for whom an embrace from her is a gift. This is quite a different attitude that the equal respect that is normal among professionals." (Complainant's Brief at 25)

**Agency's Response:** Requests affirmation of final order implementing AJ's decision without a hearing finding no discrimination
