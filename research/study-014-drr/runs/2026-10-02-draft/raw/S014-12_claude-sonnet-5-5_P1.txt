**Case Summary**

**Parties and Setting:** Complainant, an Aircraft Mechanic at the Aircraft Maintenance Squadron, Robins Air Force Base, Georgia, alleged a hostile work environment based on race (African-American), color (Black), and age (52). Supervisor was his first-level supervisor, and Team Lead was his second-level supervisor.

**Allegation:** In an EEO complaint filed May 25, 2021, Complainant alleged that on December 11, 2020, Supervisor refused to give him first aid for a bleeding head wound. He also alleged that Supervisor verbally assaulted him, yelling profane remarks that included "Get your ass back on the plane" and "I want you off my fucking team immediately."

**Procedural History:**
- After the investigation, Complainant requested a hearing before an EEOC Administrative Judge (AJ).
- On January 21, 2022, the AJ issued a Notice of Proposed Summary Judgment. The Agency did not respond by the deadline but did submit Supervisor's suspension paperwork.
- Complainant responded with stipulations of fact that raised new, unsupported allegations. He claimed Supervisor had a history of hatred toward him and of mistreating other African-Americans, made comments about President Obama, and displayed Confederate flag images in the hangar.
- On February 25, 2022, over Complainant's objections, the AJ issued a decision without a hearing in favor of the Agency.
- The Agency issued a final order adopting the AJ's decision. Complainant appealed.

**Key Facts and Findings:**
- Complainant was injured and bleeding from the head. While he looked for a band-aid, Supervisor saw him away from his work area and yelled at him. Supervisor did not deny yelling but cited numerous performance concerns.
- Complainant conceded he did not initially believe the incident was based on race or age. He said past, unspecified incidents showed differential treatment, but he never raised them with Supervisor.
- The Agency suspended Supervisor for 10 days without pay, reassigned him to day shift, and barred him from supervising anyone for 90 days. His supervision of Complainant ended immediately.
- The AJ found the conduct unprofessional and unacceptable but not harassment. It was a single incident of temper using facially neutral language. Even considering the background allegations, the totality of circumstances showed neither a hostile environment nor sufficient severity.
- The AJ noted that the background allegations were absent from the EEO Counselor Report, the formal complaint, and Complainant's affidavit.
- The AJ concluded Complainant did not prove discrimination.

**Appeal Arguments:**
- **Complainant:** He was humiliated, intimidated, and bullied, and exposed to racist comments. He says he gave investigators a list of racially motivated incidents and witnesses, and that he raised discrimination allegations several times before summary judgment. He also claims the Agency withheld "vital information" about Supervisor, and that summary judgment denied him the chance to obtain it.
- **Agency:** The AJ's summary judgment was proper and should stand.

**Status:** The appeal is pending review of whether summary judgment was appropriate and whether Complainant proved a discriminatory hostile work environment.
