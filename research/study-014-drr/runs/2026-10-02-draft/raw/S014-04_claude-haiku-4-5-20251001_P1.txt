# CASE FILE SUMMARY

**Case:** Age Discrimination and Harassment Complaint
**Complainant:** Level 1 City Carrier, Meadow Mesa Station, North Las Vegas, Nevada
**Complainant's DOB:** 1973
**Filing Date:** February 25, 2023
**Complaint Basis:** Age discrimination and harassment

---

## ALLEGATIONS

1. **August 30, 2022 – Emergency Placement (EP):** Complainant placed on EP after disputing supervisor's instruction to return to work regarding union time request.

2. **September 14, 2022 – 14-Day Suspension:** Complainant suspended for safety violations (not fastening seatbelt before engine start; exiting vehicle with engine running).

3. **September 20, 2022 – Harassment:** Supervisor S2 allegedly followed Complainant to bathroom twice, yelled at him, and threatened discipline.

4. **September 24, 2022 – Seatbelt Policy Dispute:** S2 corrected Complainant's seatbelt practice; dispute arose over policy interpretation.

5. **November 1, 2022 – Removal from Premises:** Complainant escorted from facility after refusing to continue route despite doctor's note restricting overtime due to shoulder pain.

---

## KEY FINDINGS

**Investigation Conclusion:** Agency established legitimate, nondiscriminatory reasons for all disciplinary actions, including time-wasting, insubordination, and safety violations.

**AJ Decision (January 4, 2024):** Complaint dismissed. Complainant failed to establish age discrimination; no evidence of age-biased comments or disparate treatment of younger workers. Supervisory actions supported by documented performance and safety concerns.

**Final Outcome:** Agency's final order fully adopted AJ's decision. Complaint denied.
