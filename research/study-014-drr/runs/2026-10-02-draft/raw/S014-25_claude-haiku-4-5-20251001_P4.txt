# CASE FILE SUMMARY

## Parties and Background
- **Complainant:** Library Director, NF-1410-IV, Joint Base Andrews, Prince George's County, Maryland
- **Complainant's Supervisor (S1):** Force Development Flight Chief, GS-0301-13
- **Decision-Maker:** EEOC Administrative Judge (AJ)

## Claims Alleged
Complainant originally included a reprisal claim for prior EEO activity but withdrew it during conference before the AJ.

## Factual Timeline and Events

1. **May 31, 2018; July 3, 2018; July 16, 2018:** S1 claimed to have given verbal counseling to Complainant; no documentation was generated in Complainant's personnel file

2. **October 18, 2018:** S1 issued a Letter of Reprimand to Complainant

3. **February 12, 2019; March 14, 2019; March 21, 2019; April 2, 2019; April 3, 2019:** S1 accepted incorrect statements from Complainant's subordinates

4. **April 25, 2019:** S1 signed a Notice of Proposed Removal letter against Complainant

5. **June 28, 2019:** Complainant was mailed a Decision to Remove letter dated June 28, 2019, signed by S1

6. **October 22, 2019:** Complainant learned her Common Access Card (CAC) was still active

## Investigation and Hearing
The Agency provided Complainant with a copy of the report of investigation and notice of her right to request a hearing before an EEOC Administrative Judge. Complainant requested a hearing. Over Complainant's objections, the AJ granted the Agency's motion for decision without a hearing and issued a decision by summary judgment in favor of the Agency. The Agency subsequently issued a final order fully implementing the AJ's finding that Complainant failed to prove that the Agency subjected her to discrimination as alleged.

## Administrative Judge's Findings

### General Finding
The AJ found that, taking the facts as presented in the report of investigation and viewing the evidence in the light most favorable to Complainant, she failed to create a genuine issue of material fact to establish discrimination based on race, sex, color, or age.

### Claim 6 (CAC Issue)
The AJ analyzed Claim 6 but Complainant does not list it in her appeal brief and does not dispute the AJ's finding of no discrimination regarding this claim.

### Claims 1-5: Disparate Treatment and Discrimination

**Finding:** The AJ found that Complainant could not prove that she had been subjected to disparate treatment or discriminatory harassment.

**Regarding Claims 4 and 5 (Proposed Removal and Removal):**
Assuming Complainant could establish a prima facie case of discrimination, the AJ found that S1 articulated legitimate, nondiscriminatory reasons for removal, as stated in the Notice of Proposed Removal letter:
- "[F]ailure to meet suspenses [i.e., deadlines] in a timely manner"
- "Cancelling scheduled events without permission from leadership"
- "Not stagger[ing] schedules to meet the needs of [her] events and patrons"
- "Not reliev[ing] staff members for lunches or breaks"
- Failure to conduct "staff planning meetings for events"
- Staff accusations that Complainant "gossip[s] and release[s] privileged information about employees with other staff members"
- "[S]taff members fear[ing] retaliation, bad feedbacks and appraisals if they speak up"
- "[L]ack of attention to detail with funds management"

**Comparator Analysis:**
The AJ determined that Complainant was unable to identify similarly situated employees who were treated more favorably than her. Complainant pointed to coworkers not disciplined for having a fight in front of patrons. The AJ found that "Complainant was terminated for an entirely different set of circumstances and reasons" and that "both women [coworkers] hold a different type of position than Complainant," so were therefore not adequately similar.

### Hostile Work Environment Claims

**Alleged Harassment Incidents:**
1. S1's indication in the Notice of Proposed Removal letter that she had given verbal counseling on three separate occasions but had not documented it in Complainant's personnel file
2. S1's issuance of a Letter of Reprimand for "leaving the library unattended to deal with a personal issue, thereby blocking [a summer] intern from the building"
3. S1's acceptance of allegedly inaccurate statements from Complainant's White subordinates about Complainant's conduct without further investigation
4. Complainant's proposed removal and ultimate termination

**Nexus to Protected Bases:**
The AJ found that Complainant could not link any harassment allegations to her protected bases.

**Regarding Sex and Age:** The AJ determined that "Complainant fails to offer any evidence tying her age or sex to any of the listed events other than her own conclusory statements."

**Regarding Race and Color:** The AJ found that Complainant's statements "speculat[ing] that because Complainant is Black and her supervisor and subordinates are not, there was some kind of conspiracy to get her fired . . . does not, by itself, show racial animus."

**Coworker Affidavit:** The AJ acknowledged an affidavit by one of Complainant's coworkers supporting racial hostility, which averred that:
- Unnamed staff members stated they disliked being supervised by an African American
- A subordinate coworker and S1 both complained that Complainant was not experienced enough and that Complainant's predecessor, who is Caucasian, did a better job

However, the AJ found the affidavit "very confusing" and noted that the coworker "appears to have learned of these alleged instances second or third-hand, as opposed to witnessing them himself." The AJ determined that "the uncorroborated statement of [the coworker], without more, does not establish racial animus on the part of [S1] or Complainant's subordinates" because Complainant provided no other evidence showing these statements had occurred as alleged.

**Severity and Pervasiveness:** The AJ found that "Complainant has failed to establish that she was subjected to severe and pervasive harassment in the form of unwelcome verbal or physical conduct sufficient to change her employment conditions into an abusive working environment."

**Personnel File Policy:** An Agency employee, Chief Human Resources Officer, GS-0201-12, stated in her affidavit that verbal counseling is not required to be added to personnel files.

**Nature of Alleged Conduct:** The AJ determined that these events were "no more than ordinary tribulations of the workplace which Complainant interpreted as harassing in nature, including disagreements with supervisors, supervisor attempts to manage the office, communication problems, and a supervisor's implementation of progressive discipline."

## Final Determination
The AJ concluded that Complainant had failed to prove beyond a preponderance of the evidence that she was subjected to a hostile work environment.

## Appeal
The instant appeal followed the Agency's final order implementing the AJ's decision.
