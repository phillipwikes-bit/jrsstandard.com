# CASE FILE SUMMARY

**Complainant:** Mail Processing Clerk, Atlanta Processing and Distribution Center

**Complaint Date:** May 13, 2022

**Bases of Discrimination Alleged:** Race (African-American/Native American/Cuban), sex (female), color (Brown), disability (physical and mental), and reprisal for prior protected EEO activity

**Key Allegations:**

1. **Failure to Accommodate (November 10, 2021–present):** Complainant requested accommodation for PTSD and physical restrictions (back, foot, knee issues) preventing extended standing, walking, stair use, bending, stooping, and lifting. Medical documentation stated she could no longer work at the same facility as a co-worker who sexually assaulted her in September 2019.

2. **Leave Without Pay (LWOP):** Charged LWOP from November 21, 2021, despite medical inability to work due to Agency's failure to accommodate restrictions.

3. **Bid Position Denial (January 24, 2022):** Denied a bid position marked "ineligible" without explanation; position awarded to less senior employee.

**Timeline of Events:**
- September 2019: Sexual assault by co-worker
- April 2020: Cleared to return to different facility; harasser still present
- June 2020: Workplace knee injury
- July 2021: Modified assignment to different location granted
- September 21, 2021: Cleared to return after hospitalization for sepsis
- November 21, 2021: Placed on LWOP
- January 24, 2022: Bid position denied

**Agency's Final Decision (December 6, 2022):** Dismissed all claims, finding Complainant failed to submit required medical documentation and did not prove discrimination or pretext.
