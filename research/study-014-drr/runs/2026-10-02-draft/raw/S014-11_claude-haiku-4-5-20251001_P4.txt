# INVESTIGATION SUMMARY FOR CASE FILE

## PARTIES AND EMPLOYMENT DETAILS

**Complainants:** Two Emergency Management Specialists (Regional and Tribal Government Liaisons) at the Agency's First Responder Network Authority, working remotely from Pauma Valley, California and Thousand Palms, California.

**Employment Start Date:** October 2015 at Pay Band IV level

**Initial EEO Complaint Filing Date:** June 13, 2019 (both Complainants)

## BASES OF COMPLAINT

Complainants alleged discrimination and harassment based on:
- Race (Native American)
- National origin (Native American)
- Sex (Complainants withdrew sex discrimination claims during the hearing process)
- Reprisal for prior protected EEO activity

## CLAIMS AND ALLEGATIONS

### Claim 1: Discrimination in Pay (subparts a-g accepted by Agency)

**a) Discriminatory Initial Hiring:**
- Complainant 1: During pre-hire salary negotiations, told the Agency would only offer 70% of previous wages with a "take it or leave it" ultimatum; alleged non-Natives were not subjected to same treatment
- Complainant 2: Agency failed to account for changes in prior salary or teaching wages; offered salary commensurate only with tribal salary

**b) Pay Band Disparity:**
- Complainants performed same work as peers hired at Pay Band V level but were hired at Pay Band IV at lowest level

**c) April 2018 Review - No Action Taken:**
- Executive Director, Public Safety Advocacy Directorate directed Supervisor 1 to conduct review addressing wage and pay band disparities
- Executive Director and Senior Director took no action after review completion and failed to acknowledge it occurred

**d) May 31, 2018 Telephone Conversation:**
- Senior Director claimed unawareness of April 2018 review
- Complainants later learned from Supervisor 1 that Senior Director was "very well aware" of review

**e) June 15, 2018 Inquiry:**
- Complainants inquired with Deputy Chief Administrative Officer about hiring and direct hiring authorities
- Told Agency "does not have personnel policies and practices"
- Deputy Chief Administrative Officer failed to apprise Complainants of right to pursue EEO process

**f) July 2018 Supervisor 2 Inquiry:**
- Supervisor 2 inquired with Executive Director and Senior Director about April 2018 review
- Both denied knowledge of committing to review

**g) January 2019 Executive Director Acknowledgment:**
- Executive Director acknowledged to Supervisor 2 receipt of review but asserted no obligation to act
- Executive Director instructed Supervisor 2 to "tamper any escalation of HR issues for the tribal team"
- Executive Director subsequently backpaddled via email, claiming Supervisor 2 misunderstood instructions

### Claim 2: Exclusion from Senior Leadership Positions
- Precluded from applying to senior leadership positions due to discriminatory Pay Band IV setting
- Senior leadership positions made available only to Pay Band V employees

### Claim 3: June 2018 Reorganization
- Complainants "buried deeper" in organization through reorganization process
- Alleged effort to provoke resignation and "limit any HR escalations from [the] tribal team"

### Claim 4: Separate Strategic Plans Requirement
- Since at least June 2018, Complainants tasked with creating separate strategic plans
- Non-Tribal team peers not required to do same

### Claim 5: Contractor Harassment and Management Inaction
- Between June 2018 and February 2019, Complainants and Agency officials repeatedly apprised senior leadership of hostile and discriminatory treatment by Agency contractor officials
- Senior leadership condoned contractor treatment and continued placing team in hostile situations

### Claim 6: Denied Equal Access to Resources (subparts a-d)

**a) Separate Resource Coordination Process:**
- Forced to coordinate all activities through separate, discriminatory process to access resources and non-federal shareholders

**b) "Gatekeeper" Requirement:**
- Forced to engage with contracting staff and Tribal shareholders through "gatekeeper"

**c) Meeting Participation Restrictions:**
- Agency limited types of meetings Complainants allowed to participate in

**d) Contracting Officer Representative Requirement:**
- Agency Contracting Officer's Representative required to participate in daily engagements between contractors and Complainants

### Claim 7: May 5, 2019 Social Media Acknowledgment Failure
- Agency failed to acknowledge Complainants and other Tribal team members' accomplishments in tweet
- Tweet thanked local, state, and federal workers but did not reference Tribal team

## PROCEDURAL HISTORY

**Investigation Conclusion:** Complainants provided copies of investigative files

**Hearing Requests:** Both Complainants requested hearings before EEOC Administrative Judges (AJs)

**Assignment:** Two complaints assigned to different AJs in Commission's Washington Field Office

**Consolidation Motions:** Complainants' shared attorney filed motions to consolidate complaints for joint processing before each AJ
- Agency opposed motions
- One AJ denied motion
- Other AJ did not address motion

**AJ Decisions:** Both AJs issued separate summary judgment decisions finding no discrimination or unlawful retaliation established

**Agency Implementation:** Agency's final actions implemented AJs' decisions

**Appeals:** Both Complainants appealed

## ADMINISTRATIVE NOTES

The Agency accepted Claim 1 with subparts (a) through (g). One AJ consolidated subparts in Case Management Order, noting that subparts 1(c) through 1(g) fell under allegation that management officials took no remedial action after reviewing wage and pay band disparities.
