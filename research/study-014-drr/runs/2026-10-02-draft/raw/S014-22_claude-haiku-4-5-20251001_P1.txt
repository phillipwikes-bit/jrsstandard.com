# CASE FILE SUMMARY

**Complainant:** Biologist, GS-0401-11 (promoted to GS-12 in May 2013)
**Agency:** National Institute on Aging, Laboratory of Neurogenetics, Bethesda, Maryland
**Complaint Filed:** November 8, 2017 (as amended)
**Respondent:** Section Chief (Ireland, Caucasian, male)

## ALLEGATIONS

Complainant alleges discrimination and reprisal based on race/color (African American/Black), national origin (Guyanese), sex (female), disability (bipolar disorder, PTSD, generalized anxiety disorder, benign mitral valve prolapse, fibroid tumors), and prior EEO activity.

**Primary Claims Include:**
- Work assignment changes to unsuitable tasks (June 2017)
- Exclusion from major projects following EEO counseling initiation (July 2017)
- Lack of work communication and project assignments post-EEO complaint
- Hostile work environment spanning 2009-2017, including: inappropriate comments, exclusion from publications, yelling at complainant, disparaging remarks about mental illness and ancestry, unauthorized medical disclosures, computer/promotion delays, and political affiliation inquiries
- Failed promotion (February 23, 2018)
- Reassignment to Lab Chief; removal from staff website; supervisory communications restrictions (March-April 2018)

## INVESTIGATION OUTCOME

The Agency dismissed claims 6-11 for untimely EEO counseling initiation and found no discrimination on remaining claims.

## APPEAL STATUS

Complainant appealed, arguing the Agency's failure to issue a Final Agency Decision within 15 months of the 60-day remand period warrants default judgment as sanction. Agency has not replied.
