# CASE FILE SUMMARY

**Complainant:** Library Director (NF-1410-IV), Joint Base Andrews, Maryland

**Allegations:** Discrimination based on race, sex, color, or age; hostile work environment

**Key Facts:**
- Complainant received verbal counselings (May-July 2018), Letter of Reprimand (October 2018), Notice of Proposed Removal (April 2019), and was terminated (June 2019) by supervisor S1
- Complainant originally claimed reprisal for prior EEO activity but withdrew this claim during EEOC proceedings
- Security concern: Complainant's CAC remained active after removal (October 2019)

**Agency's Stated Reasons for Termination:**
Failure to meet deadlines, canceling events without permission, poor scheduling, inadequate staff breaks/planning, staff allegations of gossip and information disclosure, staff fear of retaliation, and mismanagement of funds.

**Administrative Judge (AJ) Decision:**
- Granted Agency's motion for summary judgment without hearing (over Complainant's objection)
- Found Complainant failed to establish discrimination or hostile work environment
- Determined no prima facie case for disparate treatment; even if established, Agency provided legitimate, nondiscriminatory reasons
- Found Complainant could not identify similarly situated employees treated more favorably
- Rejected hostile work environment claim, finding incidents constituted ordinary workplace disputes, not severe/pervasive conduct linked to protected bases
- Found uncorroborated coworker affidavit regarding racial animus insufficient

**Outcome:** Final Agency order upheld AJ's decision. Complainant appealed.
