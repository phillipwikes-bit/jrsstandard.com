# FINDINGS

## I. Scope and Basis

These findings rest only on the record provided, with citations to the Complaint File (CF) where the record gives them. Facts documented in the file are separated from facts that rest only on Complainant's statements. Matters the record does not resolve are listed in Part V. The record contains no merits findings other than the Agency's own conclusion, and these findings make none.

## II. Background Facts

1. At the relevant time, Complainant was a full-time Mail Handler Equipment Operator at the Agency's Morgan Processing and Distribution Center in New York, New York.

2. On November 18, 2021, Complainant filed a formal EEO complaint alleging discrimination based on sex (female), disability (physical), and reprisal for prior protected EEO activity, under Title VII and the Rehabilitation Act.

3. The complaint raised four claims:
   - **Claim 1:** On June 25, 2021, and ongoing, she was denied overtime work within her limitations.
   - **Claim 2:** On July 19, 2021, a coworker verbally abused her and no action was taken.
   - **Claim 3:** Beginning August 1, 2021, and ongoing, she was micromanaged, assigned to either the rewrap section or the city manual section, and/or confronted about her job duties.
   - **Claim 4:** On August 31, 2021, her request for a temporary change of schedule was denied.

## III. Findings of Fact

### A. Framing of Claim 2

4. The Agency initially accepted Claim 2 for investigation as: "On July 20, 2021, you were verbally abused by a supervisor and no action was taken." (CF at 60).

5. During the investigation, Complainant clarified that the incident occurred on **July 19, 2021**, that the person who verbally abused her was a **coworker** and not a supervisor, and that supervisors took no action when she reported it. (CF at 205-08).

6. The record does not show that the Agency formally amended the accepted claim after this clarification.

### B. Transmission of the Report of Investigation (ROI)

7. The Agency states that on April 5, 2022, it sent Complainant and her non-attorney representative a copy of the ROI and a notice of the right to request a hearing before an EEOC Administrative Judge (AJ).

8. On April 5, 2022, the Agency emailed both of them that the final ROI was available for download for 30 days. The email asked them to confirm receipt. (CF at 98-103).

9. The record contains a April 5, 2022 message showing the email was relayed to both recipients but that the destination server sent no delivery notification. (CF at 97). The record contains no confirmation of receipt from Complainant or her representative.

10. On May 17, 2022, the Agency again emailed both of them that an electronic copy of the ROI would be available for download for 30 days. (CF at 82-83). The record again shows the message was relayed but the destination server sent no delivery notification. (CF at 84). The record contains no confirmation of receipt.

### C. Supplemental Documents

11. On April 5, 2022, the Agency provided Complainant and her representative with documents submitted after the investigative file was completed, with a request that they be added to Complainant's copy of the file. (CF at 85-96). The record indicates these were sent by first-class mail. (CF at 85).

12. The documents were:
    - an investigative affidavit from an Acting Supervisor, completed March 28, 2022; and
    - the Acting Supervisor's Litigation Hold Notice, signed March 31, 2022. (CF at 86-95).

13. In the March 28, 2022 affidavit, the Acting Supervisor stated that he had **no organizational relationship** to Complainant during the relevant period and that he was **not at the facility on July 20, 2021**. (CF at 86-93).

14. On July 6, 2022, the Agency sent Complainant and her representative an updated affidavit from the Acting Supervisor, dated June 7, 2022. (CF at 1-10). In it, the Acting Supervisor stated that:
    - during the relevant timeframe his organizational relationship with Complainant was **Acting Supervisor** (CF at 2); and
    - he was **not present** when the coworker verbally abused Complainant on July 19, 2021, and did not know whether Complainant or anyone on her behalf reported harassment (CF at 4-9).

### D. Complainant's Statements Regarding Notice and Closure of the Case

15. Complainant mailed a hearing request dated May 31, 2022, to the Agency and to the EEOC New York District Office. (CF at 40-42). It included a letter dated May 28, 2022. (CF at 41).

16. In the May 28 letter, Complainant stated that:
    - after receiving the Acting Supervisor's affidavit in April 2022, she wanted to refute his statement that he was absent on the date of the incident, and she requested his Employee Everything Report (CF at 41);
    - on May 18, 2022, she called an EEO Services Analyst to dispute that statement, and the Analyst told her the case had been **closed on May 1, 2022** (CF at 41);
    - she asked how the case could be closed when she and her representative had not been notified or received the ROI (CF at 41); and
    - the Analyst told her she could request a hearing but should attach a letter stating that she learned the case was closed on May 18, 2022 (CF at 41).

17. These statements about the telephone call appear only in Complainant's letter. The record contains no statement from the EEO Services Analyst and no Agency record of the call.

### E. Agency Final Decision and Subsequent Events

18. The Agency stamped the hearing request envelope and form as received on **June 6, 2022**. (CF at 40, 42).

19. On **June 2, 2022**, before the Agency's stamped receipt date, the Agency issued a final decision under 29 C.F.R. § 1614.110(b). It stated that Complainant had not requested a hearing within the time frame in 29 C.F.R. § 1614.108(f). The decision concluded that Complainant had not proved discrimination as alleged.

20. On **June 10, 2022**, the EEOC New York District Office received the hearing request and docketed it as Hearing No. 520-2022-00326X.

21. On **June 15, 2022**, Complainant filed the instant appeal.

22. On **November 29, 2022**, an EEOC AJ dismissed the hearing request, noting that the Agency had already issued a final decision, which Complainant had appealed.

## V. Discrepancies and Unresolved Matters

1. **Receipt of the ROI.** The record shows the Agency sent the ROI by email on April 5 and May 17, 2022, but no delivery notification or acknowledgment of receipt exists for either. Complainant states she and her representative were not notified of or given the ROI. The record does not establish when, or whether, Complainant actually received it.

2. **Timeliness of the hearing request.** Whether the request was timely depends on when Complainant received the ROI and hearing notice, which the record does not establish.
   - Measured from April 5, the 30-day period would have ended in early May, and the May 31 mailing would be late.
   - Measured from May 17, the May 31 mailing falls within 30 days.
   - Complainant mailed the request on May 31, before the Agency issued its June 2 decision, and the Agency stamped it received on June 6, four days after the decision.

3. **Reported May 1, 2022 closure.** The record has no document showing the case was closed on May 1, 2022, or explaining why the Analyst would have said so. The Agency's final decision issued June 2.

4. **Acting Supervisor's inconsistent statements.** The March 28 affidavit said he had no organizational relationship to Complainant. The June 7 affidavit says he was her Acting Supervisor. The record does not explain the change.

5. **Date and identity mismatch in Claim 2.** The March 28 affidavit addresses July 20, 2021, the date the Agency originally framed, while Complainant clarified the incident occurred on July 19. The affidavit may therefore not have addressed the correct date, though the June 7 affidavit does address July 19. Because the Agency accepted the claim as abuse by a "supervisor" and Complainant says the abuser was a coworker, it is unclear whether the investigation covered the claim as clarified.

6. **Complainant's request for the Employee Everything Report.** The record does not show that the Agency responded to this request, or whether the report was ever obtained.

7. **Evidentiary gaps.** The provided record has no testimony or documents on Claims 1, 3, and 4, and none from the coworker, the supervisors Complainant says she notified, or the EEO Services Analyst. It also does not include the Agency's analysis supporting its no-discrimination conclusion.

## VI. Summary of Findings

- The record establishes the sequence of Agency transmissions, the Agency's issuance of a final decision on June 2, 2022, and the later AJ dismissal of the hearing request.
- The record does not establish that Complainant or her representative actually received the ROI and hearing notice, and the Agency's own records show no delivery confirmation.
- The Agency's final decision was issued after Complainant mailed her hearing request but before the Agency stamped it received.
- Claim 2 was initially misstated by the Agency, and the Acting Supervisor's evidence on it is internally inconsistent across his two affidavits.
- The record does not allow any finding on whether the alleged discrimination occurred.
