**Background**

During the relevant period, Complainant worked as a PSE Sales and Services Distribution Associate, Grade Level 06 P, at the Agency's Cape Central Post Office in Cape Coral, Florida. On April 4, 2022, she filed a formal EEO complaint alleging ongoing harassment based on her race (White). She claimed that, in and around February 2022, her first-line supervisor (Supervisor), who is African American, made racial slurs and threatened to physically assault her, and that management officials took no action when she reported his conduct. The Agency accepted the complaint and investigated.

**Complainant's Account**

Complainant stated that in late January or early February 2022, Supervisor told her not to clock out for lunch because of overtime. When she asked whether he would enter her hours into the computer, he allegedly replied, "I'll slap the shit out of you." Later, when she asked to take a break, he reportedly said, "people shouldn't be allowed breaks, Black people used to be slaves."

She identified these further incidents:

- **February 3, 2022:** When she asked for clarification of instructions, Supervisor allegedly said, "I'll slap the shit out of you if you don't catch on."
- **February 5, 2022:** When she said a package was too heavy to lift, he allegedly responded that she was "full of shit" and asked whether she could not lift it.
- **February 8, 2022:** After being told to bring the wagons around, she says Supervisor stated, "You need to do it because you're White..."
- **February 24, 2022:** When she said "huh?" because she had not heard him, he allegedly answered, "What do you mean huh? I'll slap the shit out of you."
- **March 3, 2022:** As she left the housekeeping closet after getting disposable gloves, he allegedly yelled, "White people don't steal, they buy their own shit."

Complainant said she reported the slurs and threats to the Manager, Customer Service (Manager), who is Caucasian, and to the union, but nothing was done. Report of Investigation (ROI), Affidavit A.

**Statements of Agency Witnesses**

Supervisor unequivocally denied making any of the alleged statements. He said he believed he and Complainant had a good working relationship until she filed the complaint. ROI, Affidavit B.

Manager affirmatively stated that Complainant never reported any of the alleged comments to her. ROI, Affidavit C.

Another supervisor (Supervisor 2), who is Caucasian, stated that Complainant once told him Supervisor said he would "slap her upside the head." He advised her to report the matter to Manager. He denied that she told him of any racial comments by Supervisor, and he asserted that she told him she did not want to report the matter to Manager. ROI, Affidavit D.

**Procedural History**

Complainant originally also alleged unlawful retaliation but withdrew that claim during the investigation, leaving only the racial harassment claim. Agency management has apparently since reassigned Complainant to a different supervisor. She did not raise the reassignment as a claim.

After the investigation, the Agency gave Complainant a copy of the ROI and notice of her right to request a hearing before an EEOC Administrative Judge (AJ). She requested a hearing. However, she did not respond to the AJ's October 24, 2022 order to submit preliminary case information, and she did not appear at the initial conference the AJ had scheduled for November 24, 2022. On January 19, 2023, the AJ dismissed the hearing request and remanded the complaint to the Agency for a final decision.

The Agency then issued a final decision under 29 C.F.R. § 1614.110(b), finding that no discrimination or unlawful retaliation had been established as alleged. The instant appeal followed.
