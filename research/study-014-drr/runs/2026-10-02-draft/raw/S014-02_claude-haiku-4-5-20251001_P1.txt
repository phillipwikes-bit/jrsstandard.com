# CASE FILE SUMMARY

**Complainant:** Carrier Technician, Garland-South Station, Garland, Texas

**Complaint Date:** April 17, 2021

**Basis:** Disability discrimination (physical—neck and shoulder conditions) under the Rehabilitation Act

**Allegations:** Between November 2020 and April 2021, the Manager of Customer Service:
- Continually questioned work restrictions and denied work assistance
- Required complainant to perform walking duties despite medical restrictions
- Publicly yelled at and embarrassed complainant (December 23, 2020)
- Required new CA-17 form submission without compensation for time missed
- Reassigned complainant from Route 74 to Route 23 (December 23, 2020)
- Discussed medical documents with complainant (February 1, 2021)
- Harassed complainant and referred her to DRAC (March 23, 2021)
- Marked complainant AWOL despite prior leave approval (April 2, 2021)
- Provided modified assignment without mail cart assistance (April 15, 2021)

**Dismissed Claim:** Dog attack injury compensation claim—dismissed as collateral attack on Office of Workers' Compensation (OWCP) jurisdiction.

**Outcome:** Administrative Judge denied hearing request as sanction for complainant's failure to submit required documentation. Case remanded to Agency. Final decision concluded complainant failed to prove discrimination.
