# INVESTIGATION RECORD – CASE FILE DOCUMENTATION

## COMPLAINANT BACKGROUND AND INITIAL COMPLAINT

The complainant was employed by the Agency as a Taxpayer Advocate at the Laguna Niguel, California office during the period under review. On November 4, 2021, the complainant filed a formal Equal Employment Opportunity (EEO) complaint asserting that the Agency had discriminated against her on the basis of race (Hispanic) and national origin (Mexican). The Agency formally accepted this complaint for investigation by letter dated November 16, 2021.

## ALLEGATIONS AND FRAMING OF CLAIMS

The Agency framed the formal complaint as an investigation into whether a co-worker serving as an On-the-Job Instructor (OJI) and Lead subjected the complainant to harassment based on her race and national origin. The alleged harassment reportedly occurred on multiple occasions beginning in June 2020 and continuing through July 2021. The specific allegations included four categories of conduct:

First, the OJI/Lead made derogatory comments about the complainant's accent and stated a dislike for communicating with her. Second, the OJI/Lead allegedly attempted to sabotage the complainant's work by creating lists of inventory issues and expired casework, while providing intentionally false guidance regarding case processing procedures. Third, the OJI/Lead filled the complainant's calendar with unrealistic appointment schedules that would have been difficult for even experienced employees to complete. Fourth, the OJI/Lead advised the complainant's manager to have her process a fraudulent tax return.

## INVESTIGATION PROCESS AND AGENCY DECISION

Following completion of the investigation, the Agency provided the complainant with a copy of the investigative report and notice of her right to request a hearing before an EEOC Administrative Judge, as provided in 29 C.F.R. § 1614.108(f). The complainant did not timely request such a hearing. Consequently, the Agency issued a final decision pursuant to 29 C.F.R. § 1614.110(b), concluding that no discrimination had occurred.

The Agency's final decision acknowledged that if the initial supervisor (S1) had indeed made the comments described in the first allegation regarding the complainant's accent, such statements would constitute unwelcome verbal conduct based on the complainant's protected categories. However, regarding the remaining allegations, the Agency determined that the complainant had failed to produce persuasive evidence that management's actions were harassment based on statutorily protected classes. The Agency noted that the complainant's uncorroborated assertions that race, ethnicity, and national origin motivated management's conduct were insufficient to meet her burden of proof. The Agency also referenced an internal anti-harassment inquiry that did not substantiate the complainant's claims.

## COMPLAINANT'S APPEAL ARGUMENTS

On appeal, the complainant asserted that the Agency's final decision failed to adequately recognize that the alleged harasser was initially her former manager before later serving as her Lead and OJI. The complainant reiterated that S1 made derogatory comments regarding her accent beginning on her first day of employment. She further asserted that she had notified another supervisor (S2) of S1's harassing behavior and requested a supervisory change, but that S2 failed to address the harassment and permitted the conduct to continue.

## AGENCY'S RESPONSE

The Agency requested affirmation of its final decision. Regarding the accent-related comments, the Agency argued that even accepting the complainant's allegations as true, S1's comments did not indicate mockery based on Mexican descent but rather reflected difficulty in communication. The Agency characterized such comments as insensitive remarks that the Commission has traditionally declined to find discriminatory. The Agency asserted that the complainant failed to establish the severity and pervasiveness required for a hostile work environment finding. Additionally, the Agency noted that management promptly separated the complainant and S1 after the harassment was raised in late July 2021, with no subsequent contact occurring between them.
