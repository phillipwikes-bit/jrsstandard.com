**Case File Summary**

**Parties and positions**
- Complainant was an Aircraft Mechanic at the Agency's Aircraft Maintenance Squadron facility, Robins Air Force Base, Georgia.
- Aircraft Overhaul Supervisor (Supervisor) was his first-level supervisor at the time of the event. ROI at 161-62.
- Production Team Lead (Team Lead) was his second-level supervisor. ROI at 169.

**Complaint**
- On May 25, 2021, Complainant filed an EEO complaint alleging a hostile work environment based on race (African-American), color (Black), and age (52).
- The complaint concerns December 11, 2020. Complainant alleged that Supervisor refused to render first aid for a bleeding head wound. He also alleged that Supervisor verbally assaulted him, screaming: "Get your ass back on the plane, you do not do shit every day, I want you off my fucking team immediately, and I am tired of your shit."

**Procedural history**
- After the investigation, the Agency gave Complainant the report of investigation and notice of his right to request a hearing before an EEOC Administrative Judge (AJ). Complainant timely requested a hearing.
- **January 21, 2022:** The AJ issued a Notice of Proposed Summary Judgment (Notice) and gave both parties an opportunity to respond.
- **February 25, 2022:** Over Complainant's objections, the AJ issued a decision without a hearing, incorporating the Notice.
- The Agency then issued a final order adopting the AJ's finding that Complainant failed to prove discrimination as alleged. Complainant appealed.

**Facts as outlined by the AJ in the Notice** (the record cites ROI at 162 for this passage)
- Complainant had an accident that made his head bleed. While he was looking for a band-aid, Supervisor saw him away from his work area and yelled at him, including the statement quoted above.
- Complainant conceded that he did not believe the incident was based on age or race. He added that Supervisor treated him differently because of his protected classes, citing past unspecified incidents. He did not approach Supervisor about those incidents.
- Supervisor did not deny yelling but said he had numerous performance concerns about Complainant.
- Afterward, Complainant contacted Team Lead and another Agency official.
- Supervisor was suspended without pay for 10 days, reassigned to day shift, and barred from supervising anyone for 90 days. His role as Complainant's supervisor ended immediately after the incident.

**AJ's findings and criteria applied**
- Supervisor's behavior was unprofessional and unacceptable but did not rise to the level of harassment.
- It was a single incident in which Supervisor lost his temper and used facially neutral language.
- Considering the background allegations, the totality of the circumstances did not show a work environment permeated with hostility, and the cursing did not show sufficient severity.
- In the decision, the AJ also noted that Complainant initially reported he believed the incident was not due to race or age.
- The AJ noted that the background allegations were never included in the EEO Counselor Report, the formal complaint, or Complainant's affidavit.
- Given the totality of the circumstances, the AJ found that Complainant did not prove discrimination as alleged.

**Responses to the Notice considered**
- **Complainant:** He submitted stipulations of fact that, for the first time, added allegations. He claimed, without supporting evidence, that Supervisor has a history of hatred toward him and of mistreating other African-Americans at work. He also asserted for the first time that Supervisor made comments about President Obama and displayed confederate flag images in the hangar.
- **Agency:** It did not respond by the deadline but did give the AJ Supervisor's suspension paperwork.

**Positions on appeal**
- **Complainant:**
  - He contends he was "humiliated, intimidated, bullied and exposed to racist comments and treatment by [Supervisor]."
  - He alleges he gave investigators a list of racially motivated incidents and a list of witnesses.
  - He says allegations of racial, age, or color discrimination were made several times before summary judgment.
  - He claims the Agency withheld "vital information" about Supervisor. He says summary judgment denied him the chance to obtain it, and that it "will prove a history of racial discrimination [of Supervisor] towards African Americans."
- **Agency:** It argues that the AJ's summary judgment was proper and should stand.
