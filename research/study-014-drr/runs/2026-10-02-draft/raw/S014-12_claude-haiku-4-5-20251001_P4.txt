# CASE FILE SUMMARY

**Parties and Positions:**
- **Complainant:** Aircraft Mechanic at Agency's Aircraft Maintenance Squadron facility, Robins Air Force Base, Georgia
- **Respondent Agency:** Employer
- **Supervisor:** First-level supervisor (Aircraft Overhaul Supervisor)
- **Team Lead:** Second-level supervisor (Production Team Lead)

**Relevant Positions and Citations:**
- Supervisor was Complainant's first-level supervisor (ROI at 161-62)
- Team Lead was Complainant's second-level supervisor (ROI at 169)

**Dates and Events:**

*December 11, 2020:* Complainant suffered a bleeding head wound from an accident. While Complainant looked for a band-aid away from his work area, Supervisor observed him and yelled at Complainant, including the statement: "Get your ass back on the plane, you do not do shit every day, I want you off my fucking team immediately, and I am tired of your shit." Supervisor did not render first aid. Complainant subsequently contacted Team Lead and another Agency official about the incident.

*May 25, 2021:* Complainant filed an EEO complaint alleging hostile work environment based on race (African-American), color (Black), and age (52).

**Investigation:**
The Agency provided Complainant with a Report of Investigation (ROI) and notice of his right to request a hearing before an Equal Employment Opportunity Commission Administrative Judge.

**Administrative Judge (AJ) Proceedings:**

*January 21, 2022:* AJ issued a Notice of Proposed Summary Judgment. The AJ outlined relevant facts and findings:
- Complainant conceded he did not believe the December 11, 2020 incident was based on age or race
- Complainant alleged differential treatment based on protected classes due to past unspecified incidents, but did not approach Supervisor about them
- Supervisor did not deny yelling but cited numerous performance concerns with Complainant
- Supervisor's role as Complainant's supervisor ended immediately after the incident (ROI at 162)

**Disciplinary Action Against Supervisor:**
- Suspended without pay for 10 days
- Reassigned to day shift
- Precluded from supervising anyone for 90 days

**AJ's Initial Finding (Notice):**
The AJ found that Supervisor's behavior was unprofessional and unacceptable but did not rise to the level of harassment. The AJ noted this was a single incident where Supervisor lost his temper using facially neutral language. Even considering background allegations, the AJ found the totality of circumstances did not reveal a work environment permeated with hostility and that cursing did not demonstrate sufficient severity. The AJ indicated intent to issue a decision without a hearing in favor of the Agency. Both parties were offered the opportunity to respond.

**Complainant's Response to Notice:**
Complainant provided stipulations of fact, including for the first time additional allegations: that Supervisor had a history of hatred toward him and mistreatment of other African-Americans; that Supervisor made comments about President Obama; and that Supervisor displayed images of the confederate flag in the hangar. Complainant provided no supporting evidence for these allegations.

**Agency's Response to Notice:**
The Agency did not provide a response by the deadline but produced Supervisor's suspension paperwork to the AJ.

*February 25, 2022:* AJ issued a decision without a hearing, over Complainant's objections. The AJ incorporated the Notice and noted:
- Complainant initially reported belief that the incident was not due to his race or age
- Background allegations were never included in the EEO Counselor Report, formal complaint, or Complainant's affidavit
- The totality of circumstances did not support Complainant's claims

**AJ's Final Finding:**
Complainant did not prove he was discriminated against as alleged.

**Final Agency Order:**
The Agency issued a final order adopting the AJ's finding that Complainant failed to prove the Agency subjected him to discrimination as alleged.

**Appeal Arguments:**

*Complainant contends:*
- He was "humiliated, intimidated, bullied and exposed to racist comments and treatment by [Supervisor]"
- He provided investigators a list of incidents that were racially motivated and a list of witnesses
- Allegations of racial, age, or color discrimination were made several times prior to summary judgment
- The Agency withheld "vital information" about Supervisor and summary judgment denied him the opportunity to obtain information that "will prove a history of racial discrimination [of Supervisor] towards African Americans"

*Agency argues:*
The AJ's summary judgment was proper and should stand.
