# CASE FILE SUMMARY

**Employee:** Mail Handler, Incoming Mail Processing & Distribution Center, Linthicum, Maryland

**Incident Date:** August 19, 2015

**Complaint Filed:** September 4, 2015 (alleging improper conduct, zero-tolerance policy violation, and workplace violence policy violation)

**Facts:** Following a meeting regarding mislabeled containers, the complainant called his manager "sorry" and "a sorry ass," then failed to leave the work floor when instructed. After being escorted to a conference room, he called the manager "a Bitch" multiple times and punched a hole in a wall while using profanity as he was escorted from the building. Complainant denied making profane statements about the manager but acknowledged taking "a swipe" at a wall.

**Prior Discipline:** 10-day suspension for Failure of Obedience to Orders; 14-day suspension for Workplace Violence Zero Tolerance Policy violation.

**Agency Action:** Emergency Placement status imposed; removal recommended on August 31, 2015, based on profanity use, failure to follow instructions, and property damage.

**Hearing Decision:** Administrative Judge issued decision without hearing on August 21, 2020. Found Agency had legitimate, nondiscriminatory reasons for termination based on complainant's confrontational, disrespectful, and violent conduct. Complainant failed to prove discrimination based on race, gender, or genetic information (GINA).

**Final Outcome:** Removal upheld; discrimination allegations dismissed.
