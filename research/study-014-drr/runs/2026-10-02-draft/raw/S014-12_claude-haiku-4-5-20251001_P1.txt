# CASE FILE SUMMARY

**Case:** EEO Discrimination Complaint
**Complainant:** Aircraft Mechanic, Robins Air Force Base, Georgia
**Respondent:** Aircraft Overhaul Supervisor
**Filed:** May 25, 2021

**Alleged Violation:**
Hostile work environment based on race (African-American), color (Black), and age (52) arising from December 11, 2020 incident.

**Factual Summary:**
On December 11, 2020, Complainant sustained a bleeding head wound while working on an aircraft. While seeking first aid, the Supervisor verbally assaulted him with profanity and profane language, ordering him back to work and stating he was removing Complainant from his team. Complainant initially conceded the incident was not motivated by race or age.

**Administrative Action:**
Supervisor was suspended without pay for 10 days, reassigned to day shift, and prohibited from supervising for 90 days. Supervisor's role as Complainant's supervisor was terminated.

**AJ Decision (February 25, 2022):**
Summary judgment issued in favor of the Agency. The AJ found Supervisor's conduct unprofessional but constituted a single incident of lost temper using facially neutral language, insufficient to establish harassment or hostile work environment. Background allegations were deemed untimely, unsupported, and not included in original complaint documentation.

**Appeal Contentions:**
Complainant claims: (1) investigators received prior incident lists; (2) race/age discrimination allegations made multiple times; (3) Agency withheld information establishing Supervisor's history of racial discrimination. Agency maintains summary judgment was appropriate.

**Current Status:** Appeal pending.
