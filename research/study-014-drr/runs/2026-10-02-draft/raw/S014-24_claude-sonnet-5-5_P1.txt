**Case Summary**

**Complainant:** Travel Specialist, GS-6, Office of Infectious Disease, NCHHSTP (Atlanta, GA). First-level supervisor (S1) was a female, African American GS-12. The Management Officer and Deputy Director also supervised her.

**EEO Complaint (filed April 8, 2014):** Alleged discrimination and harassment based on race (Caucasian), national origin (German, Persian), sex (female), religion (Jehovah's Witness, Jewish), and reprisal. Eleven claims were raised.

**Claims 1–3, 5 (hostile work environment, S1 and coworkers):**
- Claim 1: On 10/28/2013, S1 allegedly told Complainant to get out of the Agency and the union.
- Claim 2: The Support Specialist allegedly berated her for skipping the Christmas party.
- Claim 3: On 1/9/2014, a coworker allegedly made a remark that she was "built like a Black girl."
- Claim 5: On 1/13/2014, S1 allegedly told her to get out of her office.
- Complainant also reported that S1 made race-related remarks such as "Stop trying to act Black."
- S1, the Support Specialist, C1, and C2 all denied the alleged incidents or comments, and no evidence corroborated Complainant's account.

**Claim 4 (sexual harassment by the Deputy Director, from 2006 on):**
- Complainant alleged staring, touching her leg at two meetings, shoulder rubbing, invading her personal space, and repeated invitations to meals.
- She alleged retaliation for rebuffing him: extra duties, denied training, out-of-scope duties, and yelling and cursing at her.
- She said she reported it to the Director, S1, the Management Officer, and a Consultant. S1 and the Management Officer denied receiving any report.
- Several coworkers said Complainant confided in them about the harassment and was distressed, crying, or shaking. Some had seen the Deputy Director yell or throw things. One Public Health Advisor said he had harassed her too but she feared coming forward.
- No witness directly observed the alleged sexual harassment. One witness saw him encroach on Complainant's personal space but nothing more.

**Claims 6–11 (performance-related actions by S1):**
- Mid-year review rating of 2.06 (7/28/2014).
- Performance Assistance Plan (May 2014).
- Performance Improvement Plan (9/22/2014).
- Counseling Memorandum (10/16/2014).
- Reassignment to another supervisor (12/12/2014). Complainant said this was beneficial.
- End-of-year rating of 2 (1/29/2015).
- S1 cited untimely customer responses, inaccurate travel documentation, customer complaints, a complaint from the Senior FATA, reliance on peers, and unprofessional conduct at the PIP meeting.
- A coworker observed Complainant in extreme distress over S1's treatment.

**Agency Final Decision (per Complainant's request under 29 C.F.R. § 1614.110(b)):** The Agency found no discrimination.
- Claims 1–3 and 5: Complainant did not establish the events occurred as alleged, and the alleged conduct was not severe or pervasive enough for a hostile work environment.
- Claim 4: Evidence was insufficient to show sexual harassment, since no witness observed it, despite credible accounts of Complainant's distress.
- Claims 6–11: Management gave legitimate, nondiscriminatory reasons, and Complainant did not show pretext or discriminatory animus.
