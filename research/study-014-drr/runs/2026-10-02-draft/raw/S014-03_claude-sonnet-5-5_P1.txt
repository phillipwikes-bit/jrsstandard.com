**Case Summary**

**Parties and Claims:** Complainant, a full-time Mail Handler Equipment Operator at the Agency's Morgan Processing and Distribution Center (New York, NY), filed an EEO complaint on November 18, 2021. She alleged discrimination based on sex (female), physical disability, and reprisal under Title VII and the Rehabilitation Act. The claims were:
1. Denial of overtime within her limitations (from June 25, 2021, ongoing).
2. Verbal abuse by a coworker on July 19, 2021, with no action taken. The Agency had initially framed this as abuse by a supervisor on July 20, and Complainant clarified the date and the coworker's identity during the investigation.
3. Micromanagement, assignment to the rewrap or city manual sections, and confrontation about her duties (from August 1, 2021, ongoing).
4. Denial of a temporary schedule change on August 31, 2021.

**Procedural Chronology:**
- **April 5, 2022:** The Agency states it sent the ROI and hearing-rights notice by email (download link good for 30 days). The record shows only that the message was relayed, with no delivery confirmation from the destination server. The Agency also mailed by first-class mail an Acting Supervisor's affidavit (March 28, 2022) and a litigation hold notice (March 31, 2022). In the affidavit, the Acting Supervisor said he had no organizational relationship to Complainant and was not at the facility on July 20, 2021.
- **May 17, 2022:** The Agency re-sent the ROI link by email. Again, there was no delivery confirmation.
- **May 18, 2022:** Complainant says she called an EEO Services Analyst to dispute the Acting Supervisor's statement. She was told her case had been closed on May 1, 2022. She says she and her representative had not received the ROI, and that the analyst told her to request a hearing and attach a letter explaining when she learned the case was closed.
- **May 28/31, 2022:** Complainant mailed a hearing request, with a May 28 letter, to the Agency and the EEOC New York District Office. She also requested the Acting Supervisor's Employee Everything Report. The Agency stamped it received June 6, 2022.
- **June 2, 2022:** The Agency issued a final decision under 29 C.F.R. § 1614.110(b), on the ground that no hearing was timely requested under § 1614.108(f). It found Complainant had not proven discrimination.
- **June 7/July 6, 2022:** The Acting Supervisor gave an updated affidavit, which the Agency provided to Complainant on July 6. He described himself as Acting Supervisor during the relevant period. He said he was not present for the July 19, 2021 incident and did not know whether any harassment was reported.
- **June 10, 2022:** The EEOC New York District Office received the hearing request (Hearing No. 520-2022-00326X).
- **June 15, 2022:** Complainant appealed the final decision.
- **November 29, 2022:** An EEOC AJ dismissed the hearing request because the Agency had already issued a final decision that Complainant had appealed.

**Key Issues Noted in the Record:**
- Whether Complainant and her representative actually received the ROI and hearing notice. The email records show no delivery confirmation, and there is no evidence of mailed delivery.
- Whether the hearing request was timely, given the claimed lack of notice, the May 18 notice of case closure, and the May 31 mailing. This bears on whether the Agency's final decision was premature.
- The Acting Supervisor's inconsistent statements about his relationship to Complainant and his presence at the facility.
