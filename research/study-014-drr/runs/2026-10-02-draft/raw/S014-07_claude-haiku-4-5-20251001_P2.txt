# FINDINGS

## A. Jurisdiction and Timeliness

The Agency properly accepted Complainant's formal Equal Employment Opportunity (EEO) complaint filed on November 4, 2021, and framed the allegations within the statutory framework of Title VII of the Civil Rights Act of 1964. The complaint alleged discrimination based on race (Hispanic) and national origin (Mexican). The Agency conducted an investigation and issued a Final Agency Decision (FAD) on an unspecified date. Complainant timely appealed the FAD to the Commission.

## B. Factual Summary

Complainant worked as a Taxpayer Advocate in Laguna Niguel, California, during the period at issue (June 2020 through July 2021). Complainant alleged that a co-worker who served as an On-the-Job Instructor (OJI) and Lead (hereafter "S1") subjected her to harassment based on her Hispanic race and Mexican national origin. The alleged harassing conduct included: (1) comments regarding Complainant's accent and statements that S1 disliked speaking with Complainant; (2) attempts to sabotage Complainant's work through misinformation and the creation of detrimental case lists; (3) scheduling unrealistic appointments incompletable by experienced employees; and (4) advice to Complainant's manager to have Complainant process fraudulent tax returns.

Complainant further alleged that she reported the harassment to her manager (S2) and requested a change of supervisor, but that S2 failed to address the conduct. In late July 2021, after Complainant raised the harassment issue, management separated Complainant and S1. Complainant confirmed that she had no further contact with S1 following this separation.

## C. Agency's Finding of No Discrimination

The Agency's investigation resulted in a finding of no discrimination. Regarding Complainant's accent-related allegations, the Agency concluded that assuming the truth of Complainant's allegations, the comments were, at worst, "insensitive, hurtful comments" that did not constitute discriminatory conduct. The Agency determined that Complainant failed to establish that S1's comments mocked her accent or were based on her Mexican descent; rather, the comments merely reflected S1's expressed difficulty in communicating with Complainant.

Regarding allegations 2, 3, and 4, the Agency found that Complainant produced no persuasive evidence that management's actions were harassment based on her protected classes. The Agency concluded that Complainant's uncorroborated assertions that S1 was motivated by race or national origin were insufficient to sustain her burden of proof. The Agency further noted that Complainant's allegations had been addressed through an internal anti-harassment inquiry that did not substantiate her claims.

The Agency emphasized that management promptly addressed the harassment complaint by separating Complainant and S1 in late July 2021.
