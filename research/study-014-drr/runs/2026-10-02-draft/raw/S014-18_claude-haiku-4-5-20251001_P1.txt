# CASE FILE SUMMARY

**Complainant:** CBP Officer, GS-1895-12, El Paso Port of Entry

**Complaint Filed:** April 13, 2020

**Bases of Discrimination Alleged:** Sex (female), disability (cervical myelopathy/cervical fusion with limited neck movement), and age (56)

**Nature of Complaint:** Hostile work environment and discrimination

**Key Allegations:** Between September 2019 and February 2020, Supervisor 1 and Supervisor 2 allegedly:
- Assigned cashier and full-duty passport control duties despite complainant's Light Duty status
- Made disparaging comments about complainant's health and disability
- Repeatedly interrupted complainant's conversations with colleagues
- Yelled at complainant in public and in front of customers
- Accused complainant of refusing to work and abusing accommodations
- Changed complainant's Light Duty shift without notice
- Made hostile remarks regarding complainant's productivity and value
- Threatened verbal warnings, reprimands, and disciplinary action
- Reassigned complainant to different duties in retaliatory manner
- Sent complainant home on administrative leave

**Investigation Outcome:** Agency concluded complainant failed to establish discrimination or disparate treatment based on sex, disability, or age. Complainant was provided the investigation report and notice of right to request EEOC Administrative Judge hearing.
