**Case Summary**

**Complainant:** CBP Officer (GS-1895-12), El Paso Port of Entry, Office of Field Operations, El Paso, Texas.

**Complaint:** On April 13, 2020, Complainant filed an EEO complaint alleging a hostile work environment and discrimination based on sex (female), physical disability (cervical myelopathy, cervical fusion with limited neck movement), and age (56).

**Allegations (15 incidents, September 2019 to February 2020):** The incidents involve mainly Supervisor 1 and, in some, Supervisor 2. Complainant alleged that:
- Supervisor 1 assigned her cashier and passport control duties despite her Light Duty status (Claims 1, 5).
- Supervisor 1 said she did not want to work with Complainant and called her a liability because of her health issues. She also allegedly told the Program Manager she did not want to work with Complainant because Complainant was on Light Duty and had no weapon (Claims 2, 3).
- Supervisor 1 repeatedly yelled at, accused, or threatened Complainant. The accusations included not wanting to work, abusing her light duty schedule, working the wrong schedule, making false statements in a memorandum, and insubordination. One incident occurred in front of the public (Claims 4, 6–9, 11, 12, 14).
- After Supervisor 1 called the Chief, Complainant's Light Duty shift was changed for two days before she returned to full duty (Claim 10).
- Supervisor 2 made demeaning remarks, including "I'm not signing shit for you" and calling her a "nonproductive worthless person." He also reassigned her, directed her to leave the work area, and told her to go home on administrative leave (Claims 13, 14).
- On February 6, 2020, Complainant received a Weingarten notice stating that the supervisors accused her of not wanting to work, disobedience, neglect of duties, and disobeying an order. She was asked to provide a written statement to the Chief (Claim 15).

**Procedural history:** After the investigation, the Agency gave Complainant the report of investigation and notice of her right to request a hearing before an EEOC Administrative Judge. At her request, the Agency instead issued a final decision under 29 C.F.R. § 1614.110(b).

**Agency decision:** The Agency found that Complainant did not prove she was subjected to discrimination as alleged. It specifically found that she failed to show disparate treatment.
