# FINDINGS

## Background and Complainant's Medical Conditions

At the time of the events at issue, Complainant was employed as a Mail Processing Clerk at the Agency's Atlanta Processing and Distribution Center. Complainant suffers from multiple documented medical conditions, including post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD) and depression resulting from sexual harassment and assault by a co-worker, as well as physical limitations affecting her back, feet, and knees that prevent extended standing or walking, stair navigation, and bending, stooping, or lifting activities.

## Prior Sexual Harassment and Delayed Accommodation

Complainant was sexually harassed and assaulted by a co-worker in September 2019. Following this incident, she was placed on FMLA leave. Medical documentation indicated that due to her PTSD, Complainant's physician stated she could no longer work in the same facility as her harasser. Although Complainant was released to return to work at a different facility in April 2020, her harasser remained at her original facility, preventing her requested accommodation.

## Workplace Injury and Lack of Response

In June 2020, Complainant sustained a workplace knee injury and submitted related documentation to the Agency in July 2020. The Agency's Reasonable Accommodation Committee requested additional documentation that Complainant had already provided and focused solely on her physical restrictions rather than addressing her documented medical need to be separated from her harasser.

## Delayed Accommodation and Subsequent Medical Events

Approximately one year after her initial injury, in July 2021, Complainant was assigned to a modified position at a different location accommodating her physical restrictions. No explanation or documentation exists in the record regarding the specifics of this new position or the justification for the year-long delay. Complainant went on COVID leave on August 16, 2021, and was subsequently hospitalized for sepsis, ultimately not cleared to return to work until September 21, 2021.

## Failure to Accommodate Post-Return

Upon medical clearance in September 2021, the Agency refused to return Complainant to work, stating no positions were available that accommodated her restrictions. No documented evidence exists of any Agency effort to accommodate Complainant's restrictions from September 2021 onwards, nor any evidence that such efforts were unsuccessful.

## Leave Without Pay and Bid Denial

Since November 21, 2021, Complainant has been placed on leave without pay (LWOP) despite being medically unable to work due to the Agency's failure to provide reasonable accommodation. In January 2022, Complainant was denied a bid position and marked "ineligible" with no explanation provided. The position was awarded to an employee with less seniority. Post-hoc, the Supervisor of Distribution Operations asserted the bid was denied because Complainant had not provided updated medical documentation within six months of the bid. However, no evidence exists that Complainant was actually requested to provide such documentation or notified of this requirement.

## Documentation Deficiencies

The record contains no explanation, supporting documentation, or statement from Agency officials regarding the new position provided in July 2021, the year-long delay in accommodation, or the basis for the bid denial as communicated to Complainant at the time of denial.
