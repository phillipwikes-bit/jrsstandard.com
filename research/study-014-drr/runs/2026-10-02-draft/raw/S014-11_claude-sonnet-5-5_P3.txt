# Investigation Record: Background and Procedural History

## Background

At the time of the events underlying these complaints, Complainants worked as Emergency Management Specialists (Regional and National Tribal Government Liaisons) for the Agency's First Responder Network Authority. They worked remotely, one from Pauma Valley, California, and the other from Thousand Palms, California. Both began their Agency employment in October 2015 at the Pay Band IV level.

## Complaints and Claims

Beginning June 13, 2019, each Complainant filed a formal EEO complaint alleging discrimination and harassment based on race (Native American), national origin (Native American), and sex, and in reprisal for prior protected EEO activity. During the hearing process, Complainants withdrew their sex discrimination claims. The complaints raised the following claims.

**Claim 1: Discrimination in pay since hire.**

a. Complainant 1 negotiated his salary before being hired and was told the Agency was obligated to offer only 70% of his previous wages and that he could "take it or leave it." He alleged non-Natives were not treated this way during hiring. Complainant 2 claimed the Agency failed to account for changes in her prior salary or teaching wages and offered only a salary commensurate with her current tribal salary.

b. Complainants performed the same work as peers hired at Pay Band V, but they were hired at Pay Band IV and paid at the lowest level.

c. Around April 2018, the Executive Director, Public Safety Advocacy Directorate ("Executive Director"), directed Complainants' supervisor ("Supervisor 1") to review wage and pay band disparities. After the review was completed, the Executive Director and a Senior Director took no action and failed to acknowledge that it had occurred.

d. In a May 31, 2018 telephone conversation, the Senior Director claimed he was unaware of the review. Complainants later learned from Supervisor 1 that he was "very well aware" of it.

e. On June 15, 2018, Complainants asked the Deputy Chief Administrative Officer about hiring and direct hiring authorities. They were told the Agency "does not have personnel policies and practices." The Deputy Chief Administrative Officer also failed to inform Complainants of their right to use the EEO process to address their allegations of discriminatory pay.

f. Around July 2018, Complainants' new supervisor ("Supervisor 2") asked the Executive Director and Senior Director about the April 2018 review. They denied any knowledge of having committed to it.

g. In January 2019, the Executive Director acknowledged to Supervisor 2 that he had received the review but said he felt no obligation to act on it. He then instructed Supervisor 2 to "tamper any escalation of [human resources (HR)] issues for the tribal team." In a later email he backpedaled, claiming Supervisor 2 had misunderstood his instructions.

**Claim 2: Exclusion from senior leadership positions.** Complainants were precluded from applying for senior leadership positions because their pay was discriminatorily set at Pay Band IV, while the Agency made those positions available only to Pay Band V employees.

**Claim 3: Reorganization.** In June 2018, Complainants were "buried deeper" in the organization through a reorganization. They alleged this was meant to provoke them to quit and to "limit any HR escalations from [the] tribal team."

**Claim 4: Strategic plans.** On an ongoing basis since at least June 2018, Complainants were tasked with creating separate strategic plans, while none of their non-Tribal peers were required to do so.

**Claim 5: Hostile treatment by a contractor.** On multiple occasions between June 2018 and February 2019, Complainants and other Agency officials told senior leadership that Tribal team members were being subjected to hostile and discriminatory treatment by officials of an Agency contractor. Senior leadership allegedly condoned this treatment and continued to place the team in hostile situations with contracting officials.

**Claim 6: Unequal access to resources.** On an ongoing basis, Complainants were denied equal access to the resources their peers had for performing their duties, in that:

a. They were forced to coordinate all their activities through a separate, discriminatory process to access resources and their non-federal shareholders.

b. They were forced to engage with contracting staff and Tribal shareholders through a "gatekeeper."

c. The Agency limited the types of meetings they could attend.

d. An Agency Contracting Officer's Representative was required to participate in daily engagements between the contractors and Complainants.

**Claim 7: Failure to acknowledge accomplishments.** On May 5, 2019, the Agency issued a tweet thanking local, state, and federal workers for their dedication and service. It did not mention the Tribal team, and Complainants alleged this failed to acknowledge their and the team's accomplishments.

## Note on the Framing of Claim 1

The Agency accepted claim 1 with subparts (a) through (g) as set out above. However, one of the Administrative Judges (AJs) did not list the subparts in the Case Management Order. That AJ instead noted a consolidation of the subparts and stated that claim 1 included the allegation that management officials took no remedial action after reviewing wage and pay band disparities. Subparts 1(c) through 1(g) therefore fall under that allegation.

## Procedural History

At the conclusion of the investigations, Complainants received copies of the investigative files and requested hearings before an EEOC AJ. The two complaints were assigned to different AJs in the Commission's Washington Field Office. Complainants' shared attorney moved to consolidate the complaints for joint processing before each AJ, and the Agency opposed the motions. One AJ denied the motion, and the other did not address it.

The AJs then issued separate summary judgment decisions, each finding that no discrimination or unlawful retaliation had been established. The Agency's final actions implemented the AJs' decisions. Both Complainants filed the instant appeals.
