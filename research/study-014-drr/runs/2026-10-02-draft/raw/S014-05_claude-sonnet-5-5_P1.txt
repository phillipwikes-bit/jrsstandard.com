**Case Summary**

**Parties and Position:** Complainant, a White PSE Sales and Services Distribution Associate (Grade 06 P) at the Agency's Cape Central Post Office in Cape Coral, Florida, filed a formal EEO complaint on April 4, 2022. She alleged ongoing racial harassment by her first-line supervisor (African American) in and around February 2022, and alleged that management took no action when she reported it.

**Alleged Incidents (per Complainant):**
- Late Jan./early Feb. 2022: Supervisor said "I'll slap the shit out of you" when she asked him to input her hours. He later said "people shouldn't be allowed breaks, Black people used to be slaves" when she asked to take a break.
- Feb. 3: Supervisor said "I'll slap the shit out of you if you don't catch on" when she asked for clarification of instructions.
- Feb. 5: Supervisor said she was "full of shit" when she said a package was too heavy to lift.
- Feb. 8: Supervisor said "You need to do it because you're White" when instructing her to bring the wagons around.
- Feb. 24: Supervisor said "I'll slap the shit out of you" after she said "huh?" when she did not hear him.
- Mar. 3: Supervisor yelled "White people don't steal, they buy their own shit" as she left the housekeeping closet with gloves.
- Complainant stated she reported the comments and threats to the Manager, Customer Service (Caucasian), and to the union, but nothing was done.

**Investigative Evidence (ROI):**
- **Supervisor:** Unequivocally denied making any of the statements. He believed they had a good working relationship until the complaint.
- **Manager:** Stated Complainant never reported any of the alleged comments to her.
- **Supervisor 2 (Caucasian):** Said Complainant once told him Supervisor said he would "slap her upside the head." He told her to report it to the Manager. He denied she mentioned any racial comments, and said she told him she did not want to report it to the Manager.

**Procedural History:**
- Complainant originally alleged retaliation but withdrew that claim during the investigation, leaving only racial harassment.
- Management apparently reassigned Complainant to a different supervisor. She did not raise the reassignment as a claim.
- After receiving the ROI, Complainant requested an EEOC hearing. She did not respond to the AJ's October 24, 2022 order for preliminary case information and did not appear at the November 24, 2022 initial conference.
- On January 19, 2023, the AJ dismissed the hearing request and remanded the complaint to the Agency for a final decision.
- The Agency issued a final decision under 29 C.F.R. § 1614.110(b), finding no discrimination or retaliation established. The matter is now before the EEOC on Complainant's appeal.

**Note for file:** The record contains direct conflicts between Complainant's account and the denials of Supervisor and Manager. Supervisor 2's statement partially corroborates a report of a physical threat, but not of racial remarks. The decision refers to retaliation, though that claim was withdrawn.
