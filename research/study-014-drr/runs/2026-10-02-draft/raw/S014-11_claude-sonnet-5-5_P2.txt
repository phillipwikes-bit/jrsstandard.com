# FINDINGS

## I. Basis and Limits of These Findings

These findings rest solely on the procedural and background summary in the record provided. That record contains no investigative file, witness statements, pay or classification data, reorganization documents, the Supervisor 1 review, communications with contractors, or the AJs' decisions themselves. It recites the allegations and the procedural history. Accordingly, this section separates (A) facts the record states as background or procedure from (B) allegations the record does not substantiate or refute. It makes no finding on whether discrimination, harassment, or reprisal occurred.

## II. Background and Procedural Facts

1. **Employment.** Complainants 1 and 2 were Emergency Management Specialists (Regional and National Tribal Government Liaisons) at the Agency's First Responder Network Authority. They worked remotely from Pauma Valley and Thousand Palms, California.
2. **Hire date and level.** Both began work in October 2015 at the Pay Band IV level.
3. **Complaints.** Beginning June 13, 2019, each Complainant filed a formal EEO complaint alleging discrimination and harassment based on race (Native American), national origin (Native American), and sex, and reprisal for prior protected EEO activity.
4. **Withdrawn basis.** Complainants withdrew their sex discrimination claims during the hearing process. Sex is therefore no longer an issue.
5. **Accepted claims.** The Agency accepted seven claims, summarized below. Claim 1 was accepted with subparts (a) through (g).
6. **Treatment of Claim 1 subparts.** One AJ did not carry the subparts into the Case Management Order. That AJ consolidated them and described Claim 1 as including the allegation that management officials took no remedial action after reviewing wage and pay band disparities. The reviewing authority treated subparts 1(c) through 1(g) as falling under that allegation.
7. **Hearing stage.** After the investigations, Complainants received the investigative files and requested EEOC hearings. The complaints were assigned to different AJs in the Washington Field Office.
8. **Consolidation.** Complainants' shared attorney moved to consolidate the complaints before each AJ. The Agency opposed. One AJ denied the motion, and the other did not address it.
9. **Decisions.** Each AJ issued a separate summary judgment decision finding that no discrimination or unlawful retaliation was established.
10. **Final actions and appeals.** The Agency's final actions implemented the AJs' decisions. Both Complainants appealed.

## IV. Findings

1. **Established by the record (procedural):** Items 1–10 in Section II.
2. **Not established either way on this record:** Every substantive allegation in Section III, including any discriminatory or retaliatory motive, pay disparities, comparator status, and the existence and handling of the April 2018 review.
3. **Prior determinations:** The AJs found no discrimination or unlawful retaliation established on summary judgment, and the Agency adopted those findings. This record does not show the legal standard applied, the evidence relied on, or any analysis of the individual claims. These determinations are not evidence of the underlying facts.
4. **Open procedural issue:** The consolidation motion was denied by one AJ and unaddressed by the other. This record does not show whether that affected the outcome, and it may be raised on appeal.

## V. Record Deficiencies and Recommended Follow-Up

- **Garbled text.** A sentence about the Deputy Chief Administrative Officer is interrupted by footnote text (the sex-claim withdrawal), and redaction placeholders ("[number removed]") appear. Verify against the source document before quoting.
- **Missing protected-activity detail.** The record does not identify the prior EEO activity underlying the reprisal basis or when it occurred.
- **Missing comparator and pay data.** Obtain pay band, duties, and hiring information for the Band V peers and for non-Native hires.
- **Documents to obtain:** the April 2018 review and related communications, the January 2019 emails, reorganization records, senior leadership vacancy announcements, strategic-plan assignments, contractor-complaint records, and the May 5, 2019 tweet.
- **Decisions to obtain:** the AJs' summary judgment decisions, the Case Management Orders, and the Agency's final actions.
- **Witnesses to interview:** the Executive Director, Senior Director, Supervisors 1 and 2, and the Deputy Chief Administrative Officer.
- **Timeliness.** The record does not address timeliness of EEO contact for the 2015 pay-setting and other early events. This should be checked.
