# FINDINGS

## Factual Background

The Complainant, a Supervisory Transportation Security Officer employed at Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta International Airport, filed an EEO complaint on February 2, 2021, alleging discrimination and hostile work environment based on sex (male) and reprisal for prior protected EEO activity. The allegations span the period from November 24, 2020, through December 30, 2020, and encompass four primary claims: (1) an incident on November 24, 2020, in which a coworker claimed to practice Voodoo and placed a flower on Complainant's body; (2) management's failure to address Complainant's concerns regarding this incident; (3) Complainant's exclusion from management and scheduling meetings; and (4) an encounter on December 30, 2020, in which a manager allegedly paced, stood over Complainant, blocked the door, and coerced answers to investigative questions.

## Investigation and Procedural History

The Agency appointed a manager within Complainant's chain of command to investigate the initial allegations on December 21, 2020. Complainant objected to this appointment, citing the conflict of interest, and subsequently requested that another individual be assigned. On January 5, 2021, a Transportation Security Inspector was appointed to continue the investigation. Following interviews with Complainant, the coworker, and several other employees, the Inspector issued a fact-finding report on January 22, 2021, concluding that Complainant's allegations could not be substantiated.

## Investigation Findings

The Inspector's investigation revealed the following:

- **Contested Narrative**: While coworkers confirmed that a contentious conversation between Complainant and the coworker occurred, they could not verify the substance of the exchange or the physical details alleged by Complainant.

- **Conflicting Accounts**: Complainant alleged that the coworker initiated a private conversation and backed him into a corner, while the coworker testified that Complainant initiated the conversation by questioning her about a personal relationship, which caused her distress.

- **Evidentiary Limitations**: The Inspector acknowledged that most evidence gathered was characterized as "conjecture," limiting the ability to reach definitive conclusions regarding the specific facts in dispute.

- **Unsubstantiated Claims**: The Inspector concluded that Complainant's allegations regarding discrimination, hostile work environment, and reprisal could not be substantiated based on available evidence.

## Agency Final Decision

The Agency initially dismissed the complaint for failure to state a claim. However, upon Commission reversal and remand in *Norman S. v. Dep't of Homeland Sec.*, EEOC [number removed] (July 22, 2021), the matter was processed to conclusion. The Agency issued a final decision finding that Complainant was not subjected to discrimination or reprisal as alleged.

## Complainant's Contentions on Appeal

Complainant challenges the investigation's adequacy and conclusions, asserting that:

- The investigation was biased and artificially conducted
- Security footage would have corroborated his account of events
- A discriminatory pattern exists in the Agency's treatment of male versus female employees
- The fact-finding inquiry failed to prevent further alleged discrimination and retaliation
- Systemic gender-based disparities in discipline demonstrate sex-based discrimination
