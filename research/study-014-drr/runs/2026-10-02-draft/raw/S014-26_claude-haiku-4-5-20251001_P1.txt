# CASE FILE SUMMARY

**Complainant:** Contract Specialist-GS-11, Agency facility in Gallup, New Mexico

**Complaint Filed:** August 25, 2015

**Protected Classes Alleged:** Race (Native American), National Origin (Native American), Age, and Reprisal for Protected Activity

**Decision Process:** Agency accepted complaint November 23, 2015. Following investigation, Administrative Judge (AJ) granted Agency's motion for summary judgment on September 23, 2020, over Complainant's objection to proceeding without hearing.

**Primary Issues:** Complainant alleged 20 claims of discrimination/reprisal, including:
- Denial of FAC-C Level II training and certification (May, June, and September 2015)
- Non-selection for multiple promotion opportunities (eight separate job announcements)
- Non-renewal of FAC-C certification (April 2016)
- Harassment by officer C1 (unwanted hug, May 2016)
- Denial of training attendance and delayed leave approvals (April 2017-May 2018)

**AJ Findings:** Complainant failed to establish prima facie case of discrimination for majority of claims. For promotion denials, Complainant lacked required FAC-C certifications. Single hug incident insufficient to establish hostile work environment. Agency articulated legitimate, nondiscriminatory reasons for actions; Complainant failed to prove pretext.

**Final Agency Order:** Adopted AJ's findings; no discrimination substantiated.

**Appeal Position:** Complainant alleges systemic discrimination against Native Americans and bias in certification requirements; Agency requests affirmation of final order.
