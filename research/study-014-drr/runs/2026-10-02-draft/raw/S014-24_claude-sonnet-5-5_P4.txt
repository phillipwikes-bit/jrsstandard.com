# Case File Summary

## 1. Background
- Complainant was a Travel Specialist, GS-6, at the Agency's Office of Infectious Disease (OID), National Center for HIV/AIDS, Viral Hepatitis, STD and TB Prevention (NCHHSTP), in Atlanta, Georgia. (ROI at 49.)
- Her supervisors were:
  - S1, the Supervisor Program Specialist, GS-12 (female, African American), her first-level supervisor.
  - The Management Officer (male, Caucasian).
  - The Deputy Director (male).
- ROI page numbers are the Bates numbers at the bottom center of each page.

## 2. Complainant's Statements and Responses

**Claim 1: October 28, 2013 meeting with S1** (ROI at 127)
- *Complainant:* S1 told her to get out of the Agency because upper management did not like her and the Management Officer was out to get her. S1 also said she needed to get out of the "god damn" Union because it would never let her advance.
- *S1:* Denied making the comments and denied that the meeting or incident took place.

**Claim 2: Christmas party** (ROI at 129-30)
- *Complainant:* The Support Specialist asked why she was not attending the Christmas party and whether she celebrated Christmas. Complainant apparently replied that she is a Jehovah's Witness and does not celebrate Christmas. She said the Support Specialist "looked at her as if she had a disease" and rolled her eyes.
- *Support Specialist:* Did not recall any interaction with Complainant that day and maintained the incident did not occur as alleged.

**Claim 3: January 9, 2014 overheard comment** (ROI at 131-32)
- *Complainant:* She overheard coworker C1 tell coworker C2 that Complainant thinks "she is cute because she is built like a Black girl" and that she "is bad news and not your friend." Complainant said similar comments were made multiple times in hallways and the elevator.
- *C1:* Had no idea who Complainant was and did not make the comment.
- *C2:* Did not recall C1 making the comment to her.

**Claim 4: Alleged sexual harassment by the Deputy Director**
- *Complainant's account:*
  - The harassment occurred on multiple occasions and continued to the present. (ROI at 133-35.)
  - He stared at her chest.
  - He put his hand on her left leg and "rolled his hand down" during two separate staff meetings. She "scooted over" the first time and left the meeting the second time. She told him to stop because it made her uncomfortable. (Id.)
  - He often came into her cubicle and put his hands on her shoulders as if massaging her. (Id.)
  - He leaned over her at her computer, invading her personal space. (Id.)
  - He asked her to lunch and/or dinner, and she rebuffed him. (Id.)
  - He punished her for rebuffing him by assigning extra duties, refusing training requests, assigning duties outside her position description, and yelling and cursing at her in his office about processing employees' travel preparations. (Id. at 53.)
  - He often called her into his office and would "vent in a loud, angry voice while throwing things such as pens and pencils across the room." (Id. at 154.)
- *Reports by Complainant:*
  - She discussed his behavior with several coworkers.
  - She reported it to the Director a few weeks after it first occurred. The Director "simply shook his head in disbelief," and nothing was done. (Id. at 134.)
  - She also reported it to S1, the Management Officer, and a Consultant, who she said ignored her complaints. (Id.)
- *S1 and the Management Officer:* Both denied that Complainant ever reported the Deputy Director's behavior to them and said they were unaware of the alleged harassment.
- *Coworker statements:*
  - **Senior Budget Analyst** (ROI at 208): Complainant came to her office almost weekly to vent about the Deputy Director violating her personal space, asking her out, and making inappropriate comments. She had seen him throw tantrums and yell as if Complainant were his "soundboard" on a few occasions. Complainant visited her office several times crying over his behavior.
  - **Team Lead** (ROI at 205): Complainant talked to him about the Deputy Director's inappropriate behavior and said he had touched her inappropriately during a meeting. She told him she scheduled a meeting with the Management Officer and later spoke of the Management Officer's lack of concern. He told her it seemed nothing would come of that meeting.
  - **Resource Management Team Lead** (ROI at 223): She was frequently a "comforting ear" for Complainant, who expressed extreme discomfort with the Deputy Director. She had seen him in Complainant's office many times and perceived an encroachment of personal space, which she felt was unusual. She never directly witnessed him touch or address Complainant inappropriately.
  - **Public Health Advisor Supervisor** (ROI at 199): Complainant mentioned that he touched her leg and asked her to dinner.
  - **Public Health Advisor** (ROI at 199): He had harassed her in the past as well. She did not complain because she would have been the first woman to come forward and was afraid, since he was highly regarded within the Agency.
  - **Another Team Lead and two more employees:** Each stated that Complainant had mentioned the Deputy Director was harassing her.

**Claims 6-8 and 11: Performance rating, PAP, PIP, and year-end rating**
- *Complainant:* S1 improperly gave her an unsatisfactory mid-year rating of 2. and placed her on a Performance Assistance Plan (PAP). (ROI at 157-58.) She considered the review especially improper because S1 reportedly made comments such as "Stop trying to act Black," "Are you sure you're not Black?" and "Don't worry, we all know you're Black." (Id. at 139.)
- *PAP dated May 4, 2014* (ROI at 335-40): S1 noted that Complainant failed to respond to customers about travel tickets in a timely manner and failed to edit documentation for accuracy and completeness of travel reports, among other things.
- *PIP, September 22, 2014* (ROI at 341-44): S1 recorded that Complainant was rated "Achieved Unsatisfactory Results" for Customer Service. S1 noted that Complainant:

  - did not complete tasks timely or keep customers updated on their travel;
  - received more than three customer complaints and a complaint from the Senior Federal Agency Travel Administrator (FATA).
  
  As an example, S1 noted that the Senior FATA asked Complainant to correct an employee's travel voucher and she needed constant peer assistance to do so.
- *End-of-year evaluation:* Complainant received an unsatisfactory rating of 2. from S1. (ROI at 148-49.)

**Claim 9: Counseling Memorandum, October 16, 2014** (ROI at 345-46)
- *S1's account in the Memorandum:*
  - When S1 presented the PIP on September 25, 2014, Complainant spoke in a loud and rude tone throughout the meeting, engaged in verbal abuse, could not control her temper, and said she was being retaliated against, among other things.
  - Complainant complained that S1 allowed peers to call her a "wigger," meaning "white [N-word]."
- *Support Services Specialist* (ROI at 202): Complainant was under extreme distress due to S1's behavior. Once, in the copy room, Complainant came in shaking, and the Specialist thought she would need to call an ambulance. Complainant then described the harassment she was experiencing from S1 and how often it occurred.

**Claim 10: Transfer, December 12, 2014** (ROI at 146-47)
- *Complainant:* On December 12, 2014, she was told she would be moved to another team away from S1's supervision so she could get a new start. She said the transfer was good for her and she noticed a more positive working environment.

## 4. Decision
**Decision-maker:** The Agency, through its final decision. The record names no individual.

**Outcome:** Complainant failed to prove that the Agency subjected her to discrimination as alleged.

**Findings and criteria applied:**
- **Claims 1-3 and 5:**
  - *Finding:* Complainant did not establish that the events occurred as alleged. There was no evidence corroborating her version of events on the allegation that S1 subjected her to a hostile work environment.
  - *Criterion:* The allegations were not severe or pervasive enough to establish a hostile work environment.
- **Claim 4 (sexual harassment):**
  - *Finding:* Complainant did not establish that she was sexually harassed.
  - *Evidence considered:*
    - Several witnesses reported that Complainant was devastated by the Deputy Director's behavior, frequently vented about it, and was seen crying and shaking in their offices.
    - No witness specifically observed the sexual harassment, although Complainant said the events occurred in the workplace.
    - Some witnesses described the Deputy Director becoming angry and even throwing things, but the evidence was insufficient to show he sexually harassed Complainant.
- **Claims 6-11 (disparate treatment):**
  - *Finding:* Management articulated legitimate, nondiscriminatory reasons for its actions, and Complainant did not show those reasons were pretextual based on her protected classes.
  - *Criterion:* She did not show the actions were motivated by discriminatory animus based on her protected classes, so she did not establish disparate treatment.
