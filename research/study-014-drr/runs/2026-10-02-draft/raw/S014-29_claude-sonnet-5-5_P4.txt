**Case File Summary**

**Background.** Complainant was a Mail Handler at the Agency's Incoming Mail Processing & Distribution Center in Linthicum, Maryland. The record text is incomplete at the start. It does not state what action was taken, but it refers to "September 4, 2015, for improper conduct, violation of the zero-tolerance policy, and violation of the joint statement on violence and behavior in the workplace." The Agency accepted the complaint for investigation.

**Events of August 19, 2015 (per the ROI).**
- After a meeting about mistaken labels on containers held by Supervisor (first-line) and Manager (second-line), Complainant called Manager "sorry" and/or "a sorry ass." (ROI at 19-24, 78, 85, 89-90)
- Complainant did not immediately leave the work floor when Manager told him to. (ROI at 21, 78)
- After Manager threatened to call the police, Complainant walked with Supervisor to the conference room. (ROI at 78)
- In the conference room, he said of Manager, "He's a Bitch! He's a Bitch!" (ROI at 78-79, 84, 90)
- On the way to the work floor to retrieve his things, he yelled, "Manager is a Bitch—Bitch Ass!" (ROI at 79, 84, 90)
- As he was escorted out of the building, he punched a hole in the wall and said, "[Manager] is a Bitch—Fuck Him!" (ROI at 80, 90)

**Complainant's account.** Complainant stated that on the way to the conference room with Supervisor he said, "This is bullshit." (ROI at 23) He did not remember exactly what he said to himself while being escorted out, but he acknowledged taking "a swipe" at a wall. (ROI at 23-24) Management then placed him on Emergency Placement status. (Id.)

**Pre-Disciplinary Interview and removal.**
- **August 24, 2015:** Complainant attended a Pre-Disciplinary Interview with his union representative, Supervisor, and Supervisor 2. (ROI at 25, 79) Supervisor said the Agency would recommend his removal. (ROI at 79)
- **August 31, 2015:** Supervisor recommended removal to the Labor Manager. (ROI at 83-86)
- Supervisor issued the removal notice, citing profanity, failure to follow instructions, and punching a hole in a wall. She also noted prior discipline: a 10-day suspension for Failure of Obedience to Orders and a 14-day suspension for violating the Workplace Violence Zero Tolerance Policy.

**Procedural history.** The Agency gave Complainant the ROI and notice of his right to request a hearing before an EEOC Administrative Judge (AJ). He timely requested a hearing. Complainant did not object to the Agency's April 24, 2017 motion for a decision without a hearing. The AJ granted it and issued a decision without a hearing on August 21, 2020.

**AJ's decision.**
- **Legitimate, nondiscriminatory reasons:** The AJ found the Agency's actions were based on Complainant's confrontational, disrespectful, and violent workplace conduct, which violated the Zero Tolerance Policy.
- **Race and/or gender:** Complainant offered no evidence to counter those reasons or to suggest that race or gender played a role.
- **Size / GINA:** The AJ found Complainant could not establish a prima facie case of genetic information discrimination. He did not show that he is entitled to protection under the statute, that he suffered an adverse employment action, or that the action gave rise to an inference of discrimination based on genetic information.
- **Standard and conclusion:** Viewing the evidence in the light most favorable to Complainant, the AJ held that he did not prove by a preponderance of the evidence that he was discriminated against based on race, gender, or genetic information when he was issued a Notice of Removal on September 11, 2015.

**Final action.** The Agency issued a final order adopting the AJ's finding that Complainant failed to prove discrimination as alleged.
