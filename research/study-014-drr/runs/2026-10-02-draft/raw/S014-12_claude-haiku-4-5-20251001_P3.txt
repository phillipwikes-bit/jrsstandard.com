# CASE FILE RECORD: EEO DISCRIMINATION COMPLAINT

## CASE OVERVIEW

This case involves an Equal Employment Opportunity (EEO) complaint filed by an Aircraft Mechanic employed at the Agency's Aircraft Maintenance Squadron facility located at Robins Air Force Base, Georgia. The complainant alleged that he was subjected to a hostile work environment based on his protected status characteristics including race (African-American), color (Black), and age (52 years old).

## PARTIES AND POSITIONS OF AUTHORITY

At the time of the alleged incident, the complainant worked under the direct supervision of an Aircraft Overhaul Supervisor, who served as his first-level supervisor. The complainant's second-level supervisor was the Production Team Lead. The hierarchical structure is documented in the original Report of Investigation.

## FACTUAL BACKGROUND OF THE INCIDENT

On December 11, 2020, the complainant sustained a work-related injury resulting in a bleeding head wound. The complainant left his work area in an attempt to locate a bandage to treat the wound. During this time, his immediate supervisor observed him away from his assigned work station. Upon discovering the complainant, the supervisor began verbally assaulting him, utilizing profane and demeaning language. Specifically, the supervisor shouted the following statement: "Get your ass back on the plane, you do not do shit every day, I want you off my fucking team immediately, and I am tired of your shit." Additionally, the complainant alleged that the supervisor refused to provide first aid assistance for his head injury.

Following this incident, the complainant reported the matter to his second-level supervisor and another Agency official. The complainant did not initially attribute the supervisor's conduct to discriminatory motives based on age or race.

## EEO COMPLAINT AND INVESTIGATION PROCEDURE

On May 25, 2021, the complainant formally filed an EEO complaint with the Agency alleging hostile work environment discrimination. The Agency conducted an investigation and subsequently provided the complainant with a copy of the investigation report along with notification of his right to request a hearing before an Equal Employment Opportunity Commission Administrative Judge (AJ).

## ADMINISTRATIVE JUDGE'S DECISION

The complainant timely requested a hearing. On January 21, 2022, the AJ issued a Notice of Proposed Summary Judgment, outlining the relevant case facts and preliminary findings. The AJ noted that while the supervisor's conduct was unprofessional and unacceptable, it did not constitute harassment under applicable law. The AJ characterized the incident as an isolated occurrence in which the supervisor lost his temper while using facially neutral language. The cursing, though inappropriate, was deemed insufficient to establish the severity required to support a hostile work environment claim.

The AJ offered both parties an opportunity to respond to the proposed summary judgment. In his response, the complainant introduced new allegations for the first time, asserting without supporting evidence that the supervisor harbored a history of hatred toward him and had mistreated other African-American employees. The complainant further alleged that the supervisor had made comments regarding President Obama and displayed Confederate flag imagery within the hangar. These new allegations had not been previously included in the EEO Counselor Report, the formal complaint, or the complainant's affidavit.

The Agency did not timely respond to the proposed summary judgment notice but did submit documentation of the supervisor's disciplinary suspension. On February 25, 2022, the AJ issued a final decision without holding a hearing, adopting the proposed summary judgment. The decision noted that the complainant had initially reported not believing the incident was motivated by race or age. Given the totality of circumstances presented, the AJ concluded that the complainant failed to establish discrimination as alleged. The Agency subsequently issued a final order adopting the AJ's finding.

## DISCIPLINARY ACTION TAKEN

Following investigation of the incident, the Agency imposed disciplinary measures against the supervisor, including a ten-day suspension without pay, reassignment to day shift operations, and a ninety-day restriction from supervisory duties. The supervisor's role as the complainant's supervisor terminated immediately following the incident.

## CURRENT APPEAL CONTENTIONS

The complainant has appealed the AJ's decision, contending that he was subjected to humiliation, intimidation, bullying, and racist treatment. He maintains that he provided investigators with a detailed list of racially motivated incidents and corresponding witnesses. The complainant further argues that discrimination allegations were raised multiple times prior to summary judgment and that the Agency has withheld vital information regarding the supervisor's history that would demonstrate a pattern of racial discrimination against African-American employees. The complainant asserts that the summary judgment decision denied him the opportunity to obtain such information.

The Agency maintains that the AJ's summary judgment decision was procedurally and substantively proper and should be upheld.
