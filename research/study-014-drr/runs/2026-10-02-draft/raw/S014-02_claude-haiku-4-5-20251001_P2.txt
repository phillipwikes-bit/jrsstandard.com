# FINDINGS

## A. Jurisdiction and Allegations

The complainant, employed as a Carrier Technician at the Agency's Garland-South Station in Garland, Texas, filed an Equal Employment Opportunity (EEO) complaint on April 17, 2021, alleging discrimination on the basis of disability (physical—neck and shoulder). The complaint encompassed nine distinct incidents occurring between November 7, 2020, and April 15, 2021, including allegations of harassment regarding work restrictions, required performance of duties outside medical limitations, public reprimand, denial of paid time to obtain required medical documentation, route reassignments, medical document review, referral to the District Reasonable Accommodation Committee (DRAC), denial of approved leave resulting in Absent Without Leave (AWOL) charges, and inadequate workplace accommodations.

## B. Jurisdictional Issues

The Agency dismissed one claim alleging that complainant was attacked by a dog while on duty and not properly compensated for missed time as a collateral attack on the Office of Workers' Compensation Programs (OWCP). This dismissal was affirmed as appropriate. The Commission has established that the EEO process cannot be used to challenge actions occurring during the OWCP adjudicatory process, as OWCP possesses exclusive jurisdiction over work-related injury compensation matters.

## C. Core Allegations

The remaining allegations constitute claims of discrimination and harassment based on disability under the Rehabilitation Act. Complainant alleged that the Manager of Customer Service subjected her to harassment based on her disability, required her to work outside her medical restrictions, charged her AWOL despite a previously approved leave request, and denied her necessary workplace accommodations in the form of a mail cart.

## D. Procedural Disposition

Following completion of the investigation and provision of the Report of Investigation to complainant, an administrative hearing was scheduled before an Equal Employment Opportunity Commission Administrative Judge. The Administrative Judge denied the hearing request as a sanction for complainant's failure to submit required Preliminary Case Information and failure to respond to an Order to Show Cause. The matter was remanded to the Agency, which issued a final decision concluding that complainant failed to prove the Agency subjected her to discrimination as alleged.
