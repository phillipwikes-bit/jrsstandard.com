# INVESTIGATION RECORD - CASE FILE DOCUMENTATION

## COMPLAINANT INFORMATION AND EMPLOYMENT STATUS

At the time of the events that precipitated this complaint, the Complainant was employed as a Supervisory Transportation Security Officer at the pay grade SV-1802-G Band at Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta International Airport in Atlanta, Georgia. On February 2, 2021, the Complainant filed a formal Equal Employment Opportunity (EEO) complaint with the Agency alleging unlawful discrimination and creation of a hostile work environment based on sex (male) and in retaliation for prior protected EEO activity.

## ALLEGATIONS AND FACTUAL BASIS

The complaint encompasses four primary allegations spanning a three-month period from November through December 2020:

**Allegation One:** On November 24, 2020, a coworker (hereinafter referred to as CW1) allegedly claimed to practice Voodoo and placed a flower on the Complainant's body without authorization or consent.

**Allegation Two:** Following the November 24, 2020 incident, Agency management allegedly failed to adequately address the Complainant's concerns regarding CW1's conduct, prevented him from attending certain management meetings, and restricted his participation in scheduling operational meetings.

**Allegation Three:** On December 30, 2020, a manager (hereinafter referred to as Manager) allegedly engaged in intimidating conduct by pacing back and forth, standing directly over the Complainant, and physically blocking the door when the Complainant attempted to leave the area.

In support of these allegations, the Complainant stated that he had requested CW1 to maintain distance and cease her behavior, but she refused and allegedly backed him into a corner. The Complainant reported that he submitted complaints to Agency management on November 24 and November 28, 2020, but received no corrective action.

## INVESTIGATION AND PROCEDURAL HISTORY

On December 21, 2020, the Interim Federal Security Director appointed Manager to investigate the Complainant's allegations. The Complainant subsequently objected to this appointment, contending that Manager was part of his direct management chain and therefore possessed an inherent conflict of interest. During the investigation process, the Complainant alleged that Manager refused to provide proper documentation of his investigative authority and deliberately prevented the Complainant from viewing the authorizing memorandum on Manager's computer.

In response to these concerns, on January 5, 2021, the Federal Security Director appointed a Transportation Security Inspector to assume investigative responsibility. The Complainant agreed to cooperate with the Inspector's investigation.

## INSPECTOR'S FINDINGS

On January 22, 2021, the Inspector issued a comprehensive fact-finding report concluding that the Complainant's allegations could not be substantiated. The investigation included interviews with the Complainant, CW1, and several coworkers who worked in the same operational area.

The Inspector acknowledged that a "contentious conversation" occurred between the Complainant and CW1, but the parties offered conflicting accounts regarding the substance and initiation of the exchange. The Complainant maintained that CW1 requested private conversation and subsequently cornered him. CW1 provided contradictory testimony, asserting that the Complainant initiated the conversation and questioned her regarding her relationship with another employee, which she found upsetting. Coworkers confirmed that a conversation transpired but could not substantiate the specific details or allegations made by either party.

The Inspector noted that much of the evidence gathered consisted of "conjecture" rather than verifiable facts. No physical evidence was recovered, and witness accounts remained inconclusive.

## COMPLAINANT'S POSITION AND EVIDENCE ASSESSMENT

The Complainant contended that the totality of circumstances revealed a discriminatory pattern, arguing that female employees were not subjected to comparable discipline while male employees experienced harsher treatment. He characterized the investigation as biased and insufficient, asserting that security footage would have corroborated his version of events. The Complainant expressed dissatisfaction with the investigation's scope and conclusions, stating it failed to address his concerns or mitigate the alleged discriminatory conduct.

CW1 submitted an affidavit reaffirming her original statements and denying the Complainant's characterization of events. Coworker statements remained consistent with their investigative interviews, confirming a contentious interaction but providing no corroborating details.

## FINAL AGENCY DECISION AND APPEAL

Following completion of the investigation, the Agency provided the Complainant with the investigative report and notice of hearing rights before an EEO Administrative Judge. Pursuant to the Complainant's request, the Agency issued a final decision under 29 C.F.R. § 1614.110(b), finding that the Complainant was not subjected to discrimination or retaliation as alleged. The Complainant subsequently appealed this determination.
