# CASE FILE SUMMARY

**Complainant Position:** Senior Special Agent, Polygraph Examiner, Forensic Services Division, Washington, D.C.

**Complaint Filed:** January 2, 2020 (Formal EEO complaint)

**Primary Allegations:**
1. Discriminatory Merit Promotion Plan (MPP) Supervisory Evaluation rating in 2019 compared to prior 2018 rating
2. Discriminatory hostile work environment based on retaliation/reprisal, including:
   - Obstruction of return to Quality Control (QC) and mentor duties (September 10, 2019)
   - Denial of reconsideration for QC and mentor duties (September 13, 2019)
   - Exclusion from direct communications regarding assignments since September 1, 2019

**Agency Final Decision:** No discrimination found. Agency determined: (1) legitimate nondiscriminatory reasons existed for MPP evaluation; (2) Complainant was restored to mentoring duties and remains under consideration for QC duties; (3) no exclusion from communications occurred.

**Appeal Arguments:**
- Complainant claims untimely final decision and requests default judgment
- Alleges inadequate investigation
- Claims 2019-2020 MPP SE continues the reprisal pattern despite receiving "Outstanding" annual performance rating three months prior
- Contends less qualified employees selected for collateral duties
- References prior final decision ordering reassignment to QC and EEO training

**Agency Response:** MPP evaluations are separate from annual performance reviews; delay in decision was minimal; no disparate treatment established.
