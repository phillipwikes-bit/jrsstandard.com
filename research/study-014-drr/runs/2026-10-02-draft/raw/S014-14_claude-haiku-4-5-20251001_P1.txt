# CASE FILE SUMMARY

**Complainant:** Supervisory Transportation Security Officer, SV-1802-G Band, Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta International Airport, Atlanta, Georgia

**Complaint Filed:** February 2, 2021

**Allegations:** Discrimination and hostile work environment based on sex (male) and retaliation for prior protected EEO activity

**Key Incidents:**
- November 24, 2020: Co-worker (CW1) placed a flower on Complainant's body while claiming to practice Voodoo; Complainant reported incident to management on November 24 and 28, 2020
- Post-November 24, 2020: Management allegedly failed to address concerns and prevented Complainant from attending certain meetings
- December 30, 2020: Manager allegedly intimidated Complainant during investigative questioning by blocking the door and refusing to show authorization

**Investigation Results:**
- Inspector's fact-finding report (January 22, 2021) could not substantiate claims; acknowledged "contentious conversation" but found most evidence "conjecture"
- CW1 disputed Complainant's account; coworkers confirmed conversation occurred but could not verify details
- Agency initially dismissed complaint; EEOC Commission reversed and remanded for further processing (Norman S. v. DHS, July 22, 2021)

**Final Agency Decision:** No discrimination or retaliation substantiated

**Complainant's Position:** Investigated findings were biased; alleged discriminatory pattern favoring women; contended security footage would corroborate his claims

**Current Status:** Appeal filed by Complainant
