**Case Summary**

**Parties and setting:** Complainant, an African American/Black, Guyanese female Biologist (GS-11, promoted to GS-12 in May 2013), worked in the Neuromuscular Disease Research Section, Laboratory of Neurogenetics, National Institute on Aging, in Bethesda, MD. Her first-line supervisor was the Section Chief (Caucasian male). Her second-line supervisor was the Lab Chief (British, Caucasian male).

**Complaint:** On November 8, 2017, Complainant filed an EEO complaint, later amended. She alleged discrimination based on race/color, national origin, sex, and disability (bipolar disorder diagnosed in 2009, PTSD re-diagnosed in 2017, generalized anxiety disorder, benign mitral valve prolapse, and fibroid tumors with surgery in December 2013). She also alleged reprisal for EEO activity (counseling initiated July 27, 2017).

**Claims (17 total):**
- **Claims 1–5 (2017):** The Section Chief missed a scheduled meeting. He reassigned her to making DNA plates, work she considered beneath a Ph.D. biologist. After she began EEO counseling, management excluded her from a major project, did not communicate with her about assignments, and assigned her no projects.
- **Claims 6–10 (2009–2017):** Hostile work environment allegations against the Section Chief. These include inappropriate comments (e.g., calling her an "idiot" and "Diva," joking about Africa, and commenting on her mental illness and ancestry), unfair treatment, and exclusion from emails, projects, and top-tier publications. They also include requiring her to work on Christmas Day, placing her name last on an article, and yelling at her on May 12, 2017. Other allegations include disclosing her medical information to a colleague, telling her to avoid a colleague because of EEO filings, giving her a lower-model computer, delaying her promotion paperwork until a hiring freeze, and pressing her about her political affiliation.
- **Claim 11:** Failure to promote on February 23, 2018.
- **Claims 12–17 (Feb.–Apr. 2018):** She was reassigned to report to the Lab Chief because of her EEO complaint. Her photograph was moved in the breakroom. The Section Chief did not communicate with her about a collaborative project on March 14, 2018. He told staff on April 5, 2018 that she no longer reported to him. Her name was removed from the Institute's website staff listing. Colleagues copied the Lab Chief on all her emails.

**Procedural history:** The Agency investigated the complaint. After an Administrative Judge remanded it for a final decision, the Agency dismissed claims 6–11 for failure to timely initiate EEO counseling. It found no discrimination on the remaining claims.

**Appeal:** Complainant filed her appeal before the Final Agency Decision (FAD) issued. She argues that more than 15 months had passed since the AJ's 60-day deadline without a FAD. She submitted documentation that she asked the EEO office about the FAD's status on May 20, 2020. Citing caselaw, she asks for default judgment against the Agency as a sanction for the delay. The Agency did not respond to the appeal.
