# CASE FILE SUMMARY

**Case:** EEO Discrimination Complaint No. 520-2022-00326X

**Complainant:** Full-time Mail Handler Equipment Operator, Morgan Processing and Distribution Center, New York, NY

**Filing Date:** November 18, 2021

**Allegations:** Discrimination based on sex (female), disability (physical), and reprisal for prior protected EEO activity under Title VII and the Rehabilitation Act, including:
- Denial of overtime work within limitations (June 25, 2021, ongoing)
- Verbal abuse by coworker with no corrective action (July 19, 2021)
- Micromanagement and reassignment to specific sections (August 1, 2021, ongoing)
- Denial of temporary schedule change request (August 31, 2021)

**Investigation Summary:** Agency completed investigation and issued ROI on April 5, 2022. Investigation documents, including supplemental affidavits from Acting Supervisor (March 28, 2022, and June 7, 2022 update), were transmitted to Complainant and her representative.

**Key Issue:** Question of whether Complainant timely received notice of ROI and opportunity to request hearing within required timeframe. Complainant received email notifications on April 5 and May 17, 2022, with download periods of 30 days each; however, delivery notifications were not confirmed.

**Agency Final Decision:** Issued June 2, 2022, finding Complainant failed to prove discrimination allegations. Decision based on Complainant's alleged failure to request hearing within required timeframe under 29 C.F.R. § 1614.108(f).

**Complainant's Hearing Request:** Filed May 31, 2022 (received June 6, 2022), with letter refuting Acting Supervisor's statement and requesting access to Employee Everything Report.

**Appeal:** Filed June 15, 2022; dismissed November 29, 2022.
