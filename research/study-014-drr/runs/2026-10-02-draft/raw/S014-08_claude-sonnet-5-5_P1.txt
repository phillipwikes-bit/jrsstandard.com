**Case Summary**

**Parties and Background:** Complainant was a Counseling Psychologist/Licensed Marriage and Family Therapist at the Agency's Health Clinic in Baumholder, Germany. She filed an EEO complaint alleging discrimination, reprisal, and a hostile work environment.

**Allegations (September 2014 to January 2017):**
- Ongoing harassment: denial of training, derogatory comments, nitpicking, undermined authority, and isolation.
- Clients rescheduled or reassigned to other providers, including two on December 21, 2016.
- The Captain made decisions about her clients without her input at the October 5, 2016 meeting.
- She felt attacked and disrespected at the October 12, 2016 meeting with the Lieutenant Colonel and the Lieutenant.
- Her November 12, 2016 performance evaluation allegedly did not reflect her work accurately.
- She was excluded from coverage of cases during a holiday provider shortage.
- The NCOIC did not introduce her to a new staff member.
- The IG (Sergeant First Class) failed to follow up on a meeting with her counsel.
- She received developmental counseling on January 27, 2017 about her temporary reassignment.

**Key Record Facts:**
- **Prior grievance:** A November 2014 administrative grievance was resolved in January 2015 with the requested remedy of mediation.
- **Training:** Management said it lacked funds to pay for non-military or personal-research training, offered time off without pay, and noted that one training was cancelled.
- **Client transfers:** Staff said some transfers resulted from her statement that she saw only marriage and family counseling patients. The Lieutenant Colonel investigated, found at least one transfer unintentional, and reversed it.
- **October 5, 2016 meeting:** This was a routine High-Risk care coordination meeting. The Captain denied making changes to her clients and said Complainant was aggressive and recorded her without consent.
- **October 12, 2016 meeting:** Management denied hostility, offered a neutral third party, and said Complainant gave no specific examples of a hostile environment.
- **Evaluation:** She received 11 "Exceed" and 4 "Met" ratings, with an overall "Excellent." The "Met" ratings concerned staff collaboration, and the rater gave supporting examples. The second-line rater concurred.
- **IG report (November 15, 2016):** It found no evidence of harassment or discrimination. It found that she created a disruptive environment and recommended moving her out of the clinic. It also found she unlawfully recorded a conversation in violation of German law.
- **IG contact:** She said her counsel had left Germany by the time the Sergeant First Class responded. The IG office told the EEO Investigator it abstains when the Agency is taking action.
- **NCOIC:** He was reassigned and did not respond to the investigator. Complainant admitted she did not introduce herself or report the matter.
- **December 21, 2016 transfers:** Management officials said they were unaware of any issues that day.
- **Temporary reassignment:** She was assigned to Landstuhl Regional Medical Center on January 4, 2017. The Chief described the January 27 counseling as standard and not negative.

**Procedural History:** After the investigation, Complainant requested an EEOC hearing. On October 18, 2021, the Agency moved for a decision without a hearing. On January 31, 2022, the AJ granted the motion. The AJ found:
- the record was adequately developed and no material facts were in dispute;
- the Agency gave legitimate, non-discriminatory reasons, and Complainant did not show pretext;
- the conduct was not severe or pervasive enough for a hostile work environment;
- the complaint reflected her dissatisfaction and personal grievances against coworkers and management.

**Outcome:** The Agency did not issue a final action, so the AJ's decision became the Agency's final action by operation of law (29 C.F.R. § 1614.110). The complaint was resolved in the Agency's favor.
