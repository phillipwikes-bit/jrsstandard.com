# CASE FILE SUMMARY

**COMPLAINANT:** Registered Nurse, 4 East Medical-Surgical Unit, VAMC West Los Angeles

**CLAIMS INVESTIGATED:**
1. Supervisor demanded complainant work 12-hour shifts
2. Supervisor disclosed complainant's confidential medical information (surgery cancellation) in violation of Rehabilitation Act
3. Complainant required to work with coworkers who improperly accessed her medical records without authorization
4. Management failed to timely resolve sick leave issues
5. Coworker referred to complainant as "a 50-year-old mental patient"
6. Supervisor allegedly supported coworker who was bullying complainant
7. Complainant accused of intimidating, aggressive, and harassing behavior
8. Complainant accused of sending email from another employee's account
9. Supervisor requested misconduct form signature
10. Supervisor's intimidating behavior (pacing near work area, accusations)
11. Unnamed employee interfered with privacy violation investigation
12. Article about nurses with mental disabilities posted where complainant could see it
13. Supervisor mismanaged staff relationships

**KEY FINDINGS:**

- **Claim 1 (12-hour shifts):** Not substantiated; complainant remained on 8-hour schedule
- **Claim 2 (Medical disclosure):** Not substantiated; six interviewed coworkers confirmed supervisor did not disclose information; complainant herself disclosed surgery to coworkers
- **Claim 3 (Improper record access):** Two coworkers found to have improperly accessed complainant's records; both disciplined; no discriminatory animus found
- **Claim 12 (Privacy investigation interference):** No evidence of discriminatory resistance
- **Hostile work environment:** Not established; no evidence complainant was singled out based on disability

**FINAL DECISION:** All claims dismissed. Agency found no unlawful discrimination or harassment based on disability.
