# CASE FILE DOCUMENT

## Investigation Summary and Disposition

**Complainant Position:** Carrier Technician, Garland-South Station, Garland, Texas

**Complaint Filing Date:** April 17, 2021

**Basis of Complaint:** Disability Discrimination (Physical – Neck and Shoulder Injury) under the Rehabilitation Act

---

## Allegations and Timeline

The complainant alleged that her supervisor, the Manager of Customer Service, subjected her to discriminatory treatment based on her disability throughout a period spanning November 2020 through April 2021. The specific allegations include:

Beginning in November 2020, the complainant was continuously questioned regarding her work restrictions and denied work assistance. Despite documented medical restrictions, she was required to complete the walking portion of her mail route beginning November 9, 2020, and continuing thereafter.

On December 23, 2020, the complainant was reprimanded in a manner she characterized as yelling and public embarrassment in the presence of coworkers. On the same date, she was directed to submit a new CA-17 form (employee injury documentation) without compensation for time spent obtaining completion of this document. Additionally, on this date, she was reassigned from Route 74 to Route 23, requiring her to perform casing and carrying duties.

On February 1, 2021, the complainant was summoned to supervisory desk to discuss her medical documentation. On March 23, 2021, she reported harassment and was subsequently referred to the District Reasonable Accommodation Committee (DRAC). Following denial of a leave request on April 2, 2021, the complainant was charged with Absent Without Leave (AWOL), despite her assertion that the leave request had been previously approved by the Supervisor of Customer Service. Finally, on or about April 15, 2021, management assigned her modified duties with a 10:00 a.m. start time but failed to provide a mail cart to assist with her work duties.

---

## Collateral Attack Dismissal

The complainant filed an additional claim alleging that she was attacked by a dog while performing her duties and was not properly compensated for missed work time. The Agency dismissed this claim under 29 C.F.R. §1614.107(a)(1) as a collateral attack on the Office of Workers' Compensation Programs (OWCP), which maintains exclusive jurisdiction over work-related injury compensation matters. This dismissal was affirmed consistent with established Commission precedent prohibiting use of the EEO complaint process to challenge OWCP adjudicatory proceedings.

---

## Central Allegations

The core allegations constitute claims of Rehabilitation Act violations, specifically that the Manager subjected the complainant to harassment based on disability and compelled her to work outside medically prescribed restrictions. The complainant further contended that the denial of a mail cart—despite prior leave approval—constituted discriminatory treatment.

---

## Adjudication and Final Disposition

Following investigation completion, the complainant was provided a copy of the investigation report and notice of her right to request an Administrative Judge hearing. Although the complainant requested a hearing, the Administrative Judge denied the request as a sanction for failure to submit required Preliminary Case Information and failure to respond to the Order to Show Cause. The matter was remanded to the Agency, which issued a final decision pursuant to 29 C.F.R. § 1614.110(b), concluding that the complainant failed to establish discriminatory conduct by the Agency.
