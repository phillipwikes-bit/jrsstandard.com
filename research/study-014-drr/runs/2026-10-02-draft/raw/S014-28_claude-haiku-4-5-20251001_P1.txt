# CASE FILE SUMMARY

**Complainant:** GS-0343-13 Management Analyst, OSPE, Office of the Secretary

**Complaint Filed:** October 2, 2018

**Allegations:** Reprisal for prior EEO protected activity (2016 complaint), specifically:
- Refusal to correct position description inaccuracies (February 2017)
- Unauthorized posting of EEO affidavit to shared drive (June 2017)
- Inappropriate reference to prior EEO complaint during work meeting (June 29, 2018)
- Excessive micromanagement and telework restrictions (July 2018)
- Denial of FAC-P/PM-IT certification application (July 2018)
- Denial of supervisory reassignment request (July-August 2018)
- Denial of request to participate in Agile panel (September 2018)

**Key Findings:**
- Position description deemed accurate by management and HR review
- Shared drive incident attributed to coworker's inadvertent error; document promptly deleted
- Telework restrictions claimed by complainant were not applied; grandfathered schedule maintained
- FAC-P/PM-IT certification denied due to position requirements and missing documentation
- Supervisory reassignment request reviewed through appropriate channels and reasonably denied
- Panel participation denied due to OSPE's limited Agile implementation

**Agency Decision:** Complainant failed to prove discrimination as alleged

**Appeal Status:** Complainant contends investigation was incomplete; Agency opposes appeal and requests affirmation of final decision
