# CASE FILE SUMMARY

**EMPLOYEE:** Mail Handler (Modified Assignment - Limited Duty), PS 4/P, Church Street Station, New York

**PERIOD AT ISSUE:** July 5 – July 31, 2019

**KEY FACTS:**
- Complainant performed Clerk Craft duties under her modified assignment, prompting a union grievance (June 15, 2019) alleging violation of the collective bargaining agreement regarding cross-craft work.
- On June 11 and June 21, 2019, the Agency's Health & Resource Management (HRM) requested updated medical documentation showing current medical restrictions to create a compliant modified assignment.
- Complainant refused to submit updated medical documentation, believing it unnecessary.
- Complainant was instructed not to return to work pending submission of medical restrictions.
- On July 5, 2019, Complainant submitted a doctor's letter; HRM deemed it inadequate due to unclear limitations and requested a revised letter.
- Complainant was not allowed to work from July 5–31, 2019, and returned on August 1, 2019 with a revised modified assignment at a different location.

**LEGAL DETERMINATION:**
The Agency's Final Agency Decision (FAD) found no discrimination. The Agency determined Complainant was not qualified for the unmodified position and failed to provide requested medical documentation necessary for accommodations. No comparative employees showing disparate treatment were identified.

**CURRENT STATUS:** Complainant appealed the FAD.
