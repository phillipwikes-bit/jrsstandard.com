# CASE FILE SUMMARY

**Complainants:** Two Emergency Management Specialists (Regional and National Tribal Government Liaisons) employed remotely in California since October 2015

**Complaint Date:** June 13, 2019

**Allegations:** Discrimination and harassment based on race (Native American), national origin (Native American), and retaliation for prior EEO activity

**Key Claims:**

1. **Pay Discrimination:** Complainants hired at Pay Band IV despite performing identical work as Pay Band V peers; subjected to unfavorable salary negotiations; management conducted April 2018 pay disparity review but took no action and later denied knowledge of it.

2. **Career Advancement Barriers:** Precluded from applying to senior leadership positions restricted to Pay Band V employees.

3. **Reorganization:** June 2018 reorganization allegedly designed to isolate complainants and discourage HR escalations.

4. **Unequal Work Requirements:** Required to create separate strategic plans, unlike non-tribal peers.

5. **Hostile Work Environment:** Senior leadership failed to address hostile and discriminatory treatment by contractor officials and repeatedly placed tribal team in adverse situations.

6. **Resource Denial:** Forced to coordinate activities through separate discriminatory processes; required to use "gatekeeper" system; restricted meeting participation; subjected to mandatory contractor oversight in daily engagements.

7. **Non-Recognition:** Excluded from agency social media recognition of worker contributions.

**Procedural Status:** Two separate Administrative Judge decisions issued summary judgments finding no discrimination or retaliation. Appeals followed.
