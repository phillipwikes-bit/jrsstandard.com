# CASE FILE SUMMARY

**Complainant:** Counseling Psychologist/Licensed Marriage and Family Therapist, Health Clinic, Baumholder, Germany (September 2014–present)

**Allegations:** Discrimination, harassment, hostile work environment, and retaliation, including:
- Lack of training and professional development opportunities
- Derogatory comments, unwarranted interrogations, and undermining of authority
- Client reassignments without her input
- Unfair performance evaluation
- Exclusion from team meetings and staff introductions
- Inadequate investigation of complaints

**Key Events:**
- November 2014: Administrative grievance filed; mediation remedy granted
- October 2016: IG complaints filed; Captain allegedly made decisions about her clients without input; meeting with Lieutenant Colonel and Lieutenant regarding reassignment request
- November 2016: Performance evaluation issued (11 "Exceed," 4 "Met" ratings; overall "Excellent"); IG report found no harassment/discrimination but recommended reassignment due to disruptive interactions
- December 2016: Two clients redirected after sick leave; new staff member not introduced to her
- January 2017: Temporarily reassigned to Landstuhl Regional Medical Center

**Investigation Findings:** No evidence supported allegations of harassment or discrimination. Agency provided non-discriminatory reasons for contested actions.

**Administrative Judge Decision (January 31, 2022):** Granted Agency's Motion for Decision Without a Hearing. Determined complaint constituted personal grievances rather than unlawful employment discrimination or hostile work environment.

**Status:** Final Agency action issued.
