# FINDINGS

## I. Jurisdiction and Procedural Background

The Complainant, an Aircraft Mechanic at the Agency's Aircraft Maintenance Squadron facility at Robins Air Force Base, Georgia, filed an Equal Employment Opportunity (EEO) complaint on May 25, 2021. The complaint alleged that the Agency subjected him to a hostile work environment based on race (African-American), color (Black), and age (52) stemming from an incident occurring on December 11, 2020. Following the investigation and issuance of the Report of Investigation (ROI), the Complainant timely requested a hearing before an Equal Employment Opportunity Commission Administrative Judge (AJ). The AJ issued a Notice of Proposed Summary Judgment on January 21, 2022, and subsequently issued a final decision on February 25, 2022, finding in favor of the Agency.

## II. Factual Findings

### A. The December 11, 2020 Incident

On December 11, 2020, the Complainant sustained a bleeding head wound while working on an aircraft. While seeking first aid supplies (specifically a band-aid), the Complainant's Aircraft Overhaul Supervisor observed him away from his work area and verbally confronted him. During this encounter, the Supervisor yelled at the Complainant, stating: "Get your ass back on the plane, you do not do shit every day, I want you off my fucking team immediately, and I am tired of your shit."

The Supervisor did not deny engaging in this verbal outburst. The Supervisor acknowledged yelling at the Complainant but asserted that he had numerous performance concerns with the Complainant that motivated his conduct.

### B. Complainant's Initial Characterization

Critically, the Complainant initially conceded that he did not believe the December 11, 2020 incident was based on his age or race. The language used by the Supervisor was facially neutral and not explicitly tied to any protected characteristic.

### C. Subsequent Allegations

Following the incident, the Complainant contacted his second-level supervisor (Team Lead) and another Agency official. 

Subsequently, the Complainant introduced additional allegations for the first time in his stipulations of fact submitted in response to the Notice of Proposed Summary Judgment. These newly raised allegations included claims that the Supervisor harbored a history of hatred toward the Complainant and mistreatment of other African-Americans, made comments about President Obama, and displayed images of the Confederate flag in the hangar. However, the Complainant provided no supporting evidence for these allegations.

### D. Timing and Documentation Issues

The following procedural deficiencies are noted:

1. The additional allegations were not included in the EEO Counselor Report
2. The additional allegations were not included in the formal EEO complaint
3. The additional allegations were not included in the Complainant's affidavit submitted during the investigation
4. The additional allegations were raised for the first time in response to the Notice of Proposed Summary Judgment
5. The Complainant identified a purported list of incidents and witnesses during the investigation; however, these details were not reflected in the formal complaint process

## III. Agency's Responsive Actions

The Agency took the following actions against the Supervisor:

- Suspended the Supervisor without pay for 10 days
- Reassigned the Supervisor to day shift
- Precluded the Supervisor from supervising any personnel for 90 days
- Immediately terminated the Supervisor's role as the Complainant's supervisor

## IV. Administrative Judge's Analysis and Determination

The AJ determined that while the Supervisor's behavior was unprofessional and unacceptable, it did not rise to the level of harassment or constitute a hostile work environment. The AJ made the following findings:

1. The incident was isolated and represented a single occurrence where the Supervisor lost his temper
2. The language used was facially neutral with no explicit reference to protected characteristics
3. The severity of the conduct, while inappropriate, did not constitute harassment sufficient to establish a hostile work environment
4. The Complainant's initial position that the incident was not race- or age-based was entitled to weight
5. The newly introduced background allegations, appearing for the first time post-investigation, lacked evidentiary support and were not properly documented in the investigative record
6. Consideration of the totality of circumstances did not reveal a work environment permeated with hostility

The AJ issued a decision on February 25, 2022, finding that the Complainant failed to prove he was subjected to discrimination as alleged.

## V. Agency's Final Determination

The Agency issued a final order adopting the AJ's findings and conclusion that the Complainant failed to establish discrimination by the Agency based on the alleged protected characteristics.
