# INVESTIGATION RECORD

## Case File Documentation

**Employee Status and Position:**
At the time of the events giving rise to this complaint, the Complainant was employed with the Customs and Border Protection (CBP) Agency as a CBP Officer, Grade GS-1895-12. She was stationed at the El Paso Port of Entry in El Paso, Texas, within the Agency's El Paso Field Office under the Office of Field Operations division.

**Complaint Filing:**
On April 13, 2020, the Complainant filed a formal Equal Employment Opportunity (EEO) complaint with the Agency. The complaint alleged hostile work environment and discrimination based on three protected characteristics: sex (female), disability (specifically cervical myelopathy and cervical fusion resulting in limited neck movement), and age (56 years old).

**Allegations and Timeline of Events:**

The complaint detailed a series of incidents spanning approximately four months, beginning in September 2019 and concluding in early February 2020. The Complainant alleged that Supervisory CBP Officer designated as Supervisor 1 initiated a pattern of discriminatory behavior.

Between September 8, 2019, and January 2020, Supervisor 1 repeatedly assigned the Complainant regular cashier duties in addition to issuing I-94 documentation, despite the fact that the Complainant was on official Light Duty status. In September 2019, Supervisor 1 expressed to the Program Manager an unwillingness to work with the Complainant in Passport Control Secondary, citing her Light Duty status and lack of weapon authorization as reasons.

On September 13, 2019, Supervisor 1 directly told the Complainant that she did not wish to work with her and characterized the Complainant as a liability due to her health conditions. From October 2019 through February 2020, on approximately 10 to 15 separate occasions, Supervisor 1 interrupted conversations between the Complainant and coworkers, instructing them to cease communicating with her.

On December 24, 2019, Supervisor 1 assigned the Complainant to passport control duties despite her Light Duty restriction. When the Complainant explained her limitations, Supervisor 1 responded dismissively with "it is what it is." Later that same day, in the presence of the public, Supervisor 1 yelled at the Complainant to begin issuing permits while loudly stating that the Complainant did not want to perform her job. Supervisor 1 then addressed members of the public and invited them to file complaints against the Complainant for inadequate service.

On January 1 and 3, 2020, Supervisor 1 yelled at the Complainant, making accusations regarding unauthorized pat downs and case processing while on Light Duty, and alleged abuse of her light duty schedule. On January 2, 2020, Supervisor 1 accused the Complainant of making false statements in a memorandum. The Complainant's Light Duty shift was subsequently changed from 10:00 p.m. to 6:00 a.m. to midnight to 8:00 a.m. for two days following a call from Supervisor 1 to the Chief.

On January 6, 2020, Supervisor 1 yelled at the Complainant by telephone, accusing her of working an incorrect schedule and refusing to perform her duties. Between January 15 and 16, 2020, Supervisor 1 raised her tone, claimed the Complainant was unwilling to work, and threatened both verbal and written reprimands for failure to assume another officer's duties, as well as threatened discipline for insubordination.

A second supervisory officer, designated as Supervisor 2, also engaged in alleged discriminatory conduct. On January 16, 2020, Supervisor 2 made derogatory comments and refused to initial an assignment, using profanity. When the Complainant declined to perform cashier duties, Supervisor 2 made disparaging remarks about her value as an employee and ordered her to relocate within the office.

On January 31, 2020, both supervisors collaborated in directing the Complainant to perform cashier duties. After Supervisor 1's involvement, Supervisor 2 reassigned the Complainant to detention log officer duties while raising his voice and demanding she vacate the work area. Eventually, Supervisor 2 ordered the Complainant to go home on administrative leave.

**Subsequent Proceedings and Agency Decision:**

On February 6, 2020, the Complainant received formal notification of investigations against her, including accusations of unwillingness to work, disobedience, neglect of duties, and failure to obey directives. She was afforded Weingarten rights and requested to provide a written statement.

Following investigation, the Agency issued a final decision pursuant to 29 C.F.R. § 1614.110(b), concluding that the Complainant failed to establish discrimination or disparate treatment as alleged in her complaint. The Agency provided the Complainant with the investigation report and notice of her right to request an administrative hearing before an Equal Employment Opportunity Commission Administrative Judge.
