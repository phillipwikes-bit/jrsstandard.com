# CASE FILE SUMMARY

**Complainant Position:** Carrier Technician at Agency's Garland-South Station, Garland, Texas

**Complaint Date:** April 17, 2021

**Basis of Complaint:** Discrimination on the basis of disability (physical—neck and shoulder)

---

## ALLEGATIONS

1. **Beginning on or about November 7, 2020 and ongoing:** Continual harassment (questioning) about work restrictions and no work assistance provided

2. **Beginning on or about November 9, 2020 and continuing:** Required to do the walking portion of her route

3. **December 23, 2020:** Yelled at and embarrassed in front of coworkers

4. **December 23, 2020:** Required to submit a new CA-17 and not paid for time missed from work to complete it

5. **December 23, 2020:** Moved off Route 74 and required to case and carry Route 23

6. **February 1, 2021:** Called to supervisor's desk to discuss medical documents

7. **March 23, 2021:** Harassed and referred to the District Reasonable Accommodation Committee (DRAC)

8. **April 2, 2021:** Leave request not granted and charged Absent Without Leave (AWOL)

9. **On or about April 15, 2021:** Given modified assignment with 10:00 am start time and no mail cart provided

---

## DISMISSED CLAIM

A claim alleging Complainant was attacked by a dog while on the job and not properly compensated for missed time was dismissed pursuant to 29 C.F.R. §1614.107(a)(1) as a collateral attack on the Office of Workers' Compensation (OWCP). (ROI at 71-75) Dismissal was affirmed based on precedent that EEO process cannot be used to challenge actions occurring during OWCP adjudicatory process. (Janiece H. v. U.S. Postal Serv., EEOC [number removed], Jan. 8, 2016)

---

## GRAVAMEN OF REMAINING CLAIMS

Violation of the Rehabilitation Act: Manager subjected Complainant to harassment based on disability and forced work outside medical restrictions, including:
- Marking Complainant AWOL despite prior approval of leave request by Supervisor of Customer Service
- Denial of mail cart to assist with duties

---

## PROCEDURAL HISTORY

- Agency provided Complainant with Report of Investigation and notice of right to hearing before Equal Employment Opportunity Commission Administrative Judge (AJ)
- Complainant requested hearing
- AJ denied hearing request as sanction for failure to submit required Preliminary Case Information and failure to respond to AJ's Order to Show Cause
- AJ remanded complaint to Agency
- Agency issued final decision pursuant to 29 C.F.R. § 1614.110(b)

---

## FINAL DECISION

**Decision-Maker:** Agency

**Result:** Complainant failed to prove Agency subjected her to discrimination as alleged
