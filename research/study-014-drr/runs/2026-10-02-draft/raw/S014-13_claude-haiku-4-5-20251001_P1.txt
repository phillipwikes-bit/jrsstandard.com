# CASE FILE SUMMARY

**Case:** EEO Discrimination and Retaliation Complaint

**Complainant:** Administrative Technician, GS-0303-05, Chicago Regional Office

**Complaint Date:** August 24, 2021

**Allegations:** Discrimination based on disability (diabetes and liver fibrosis) and retaliation for requesting reasonable accommodation

**Termination Date:** July 28, 2021

**Employment Duration:** January 17, 2021 – July 28, 2021 (probationary period, Career-Conditional Appointment)

**Agency's Final Decision:** Complaint dismissed; no discrimination or retaliation found

**Key Factual Findings:**
- Complainant received a progress review (June 15, 2021) indicating unsatisfactory performance
- Multiple supervisors documented performance deficiencies: incomplete/untimely payroll processing, errors causing delayed employee compensation and new hire resignations
- Third-level supervisor issued memorandum on June 17, 2021 outlining performance failures
- Termination letter cited unacceptable performance during probationary period, specifically failures in new hire onboarding and payroll processing

**Agency's Reasoning:** Management articulated legitimate, nondiscriminatory reasons for termination based on documented performance issues. Three supervisors confirmed these deficiencies. Although one supervisor (who supervised only the Missing Documents Project) stated she observed no problems, the Agency found her limited scope of supervision did not contradict the documented payroll/onboarding failures. No evidence showed opposition to reasonable accommodation or telework, as all Administrative Technicians received telework flexibility.

**Outcome:** Complainant failed to establish pretext; appeal followed.
