# FINDINGS

## Background

The Complainant worked as a Senior Special Agent, Polygraph Examiner in the Forensic Services Division at the Agency's Washington, D.C. office. On January 2, 2020, the Complainant filed a formal Equal Employment Opportunity (EEO) complaint alleging discrimination and retaliation for prior protected EEO activity. The Agency accepted the complaint for investigation on March 11, 2020, identifying three primary claims for investigation.

## Procedural Issues

### Timeliness of Final Decision

The Complainant contends that the Agency's final decision was issued untimely, having submitted his request for a final decision on July 10, 2020. The Complainant requests imposition of default judgment as a sanction for this alleged delay. The Agency asserts that any delay in issuing its final decision was minimal and does not warrant sanctions. Based on the record provided, the Agency did issue a final decision and provided the Complainant with notice of his right to an EEOC Administrative Judge hearing in accordance with 29 C.F.R. § 1614.110(b).

### Adequacy of the Report of Investigation

The Complainant challenges the adequacy of the Agency's Report of Investigation, asserting that the investigation was insufficient. The Complainant notes that one management official did not respond to a request to provide testimony. This allegation requires examination of whether the investigation was conducted thoroughly and in compliance with procedural requirements.

## Substantive Claims

### Claim 1: Merit Promotion Plan Supervisory Evaluation Discrimination

The Complainant contends that he received an unfavorable 2019 MPP Supervisory Evaluation rating compared to his prior 2018 rating. The Agency determined that legitimate, nondiscriminatory reasons supported the 2019 rating decision.

The Complainant asserts that this rating constitutes a continuation of prior retaliation, noting that he received an "Outstanding" rating on his 2019-2020 annual performance appraisal approximately three months prior to his MPP SE rating. The Complainant further asserts that the MPP SE represents retaliatory action based on his prior EEO complaints.

The Agency contends that the MPP SE is a separate and distinct process from the annual performance evaluation process and notes that the same supervisor (S2) did not complete the Complainant's 2018-2019 MPP SE.

### Claim 2(a) and 2(b): Quality Control and Mentoring Duties

The Complainant alleged that on or about September 10, 2019, the Agency obstructed his return to Quality Control and mentor duties, and that on September 13, 2019, his request for reconsideration was denied.

The Agency asserts that the Complainant was approved to resume mentoring duties and has been restored to such duties. Regarding Quality Control duties, the Agency states that the Complainant remains eligible for consideration but that various factors are evaluated when selecting employees for QC assignments. The Agency further asserts that no commitment regarding QC assignment was made in the prior EEO case referenced by the Complainant, though the record indicates that a prior final decision ordered the Agency to reassign the Complainant to Quality Control.

### Claim 2(c): Exclusion from Communications and Assignment Delegation

The Complainant alleged that since September 1, 2019, supervisory personnel have delegated assignments directly to Washington Field Office examiners while excluding him from these communications, thereby circumventing his role as the designated Senior Polygraph Examiner/Polygraph Backup.

The Agency asserts that S2 denied ever consulting exclusively with the Washington Field Office examiner or excluding the Complainant from communications, and further asserts that the Complainant has not been treated differently from other examiners in this regard.

## Prior EEO Case Compliance

The Complainant asserts that a prior final EEO decision ordered the Agency to reassign him to Quality Control duties and to provide EEO training to specified management officials, but that these remedies have not been implemented. This allegation raises questions regarding the Agency's compliance with prior Administrative Judge or final agency decisions.
