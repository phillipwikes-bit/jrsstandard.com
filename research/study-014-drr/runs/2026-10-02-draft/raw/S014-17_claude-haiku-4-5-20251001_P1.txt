# CASE FILE SUMMARY

**Complainant:** Customs and Border Protection Officer (CBPO), San Ysidro Port of Entry, California (DOB: 1954)

**Claims:** Discrimination and reprisal based on age and prior protected EEO activity under Title VII and the Age Discrimination in Employment Act (ADEA)

**Key Allegations (May 2018 – March 2020):**
- Harassment including refusal to rescind a Memorandum of Counseling (May 31, 2018)
- Supervisor bullying and harassment with no corrective action (February-June 2019)
- Various reprimands and schedule changes
- Removal from Intelligence Targeting Unit detail (March 8, 2020)

**Agency's Defenses:**
- Memorandum of Counseling issued for TECS policy violations after repeated non-compliance
- Birthday potluck was optional with voluntary $10 donations
- Removal from detail due to job performance failure and inability to complete assigned tasks

**Investigation Outcome:**
Agency dismissed several claims for insufficient evidence. Final decision found: (1) no prima facie case of discrimination; (2) Agency articulated legitimate, nondiscriminatory reasons; and (3) no evidence of discriminatory animus. Complainant did not submit an affidavit despite being given extension and administrative leave.

**Current Status:**
Complainant appealed, contending the investigation was inadequate and requesting referral to an Administrative Judge. Complainant also disputes Agency's stated reasons. Agency maintains Complainant had adequate opportunity to present evidence but failed to do so.
