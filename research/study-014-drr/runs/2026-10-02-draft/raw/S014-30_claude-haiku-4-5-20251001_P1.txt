# CASE FILE SUMMARY

**Complainant:** Legal Assistant (Court), GS-0986-08, Orlando Immigration Court

**Allegations:** Discrimination and hostile work environment based on race (African-American), sex (male), age (49), and reprisal for prior protected EEO activity under Title VII, ADEA, and the Rehabilitation Act

**Incident Date:** October 22, 2015

**Key Events:**
- Complainant received a mid-year review with critical feedback on customer service, record management, and workplace conduct
- Review conducted in a conference room (rather than supervisor's office) with second-line supervisor present
- Complainant attempted to submit a complimentary letter from a defendant but was told it lacked credibility

**Administrative Judge Finding:**
- Granted Agency's motion for summary judgment without hearing
- Found no discrimination, determining:
  - Mid-year reviews do not result in ratings of record, so criticism could not affect formal evaluation
  - Conference room location was for privacy purposes
  - Second-line supervisor's presence was justified due to documented prior misconduct issues
  - Complimentary letter would not refute feedback regarding attorney communication

**Appeal Arguments:**
- Complainant contends he received disparate treatment (different meeting location and supervisor presence)
- Questions impartiality based on race of Agency counsel and AJ
- Asserts genuine factual disputes warrant a hearing

**Status:** Appeal pending
