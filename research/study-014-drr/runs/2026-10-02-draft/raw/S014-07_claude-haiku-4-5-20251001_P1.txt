# CASE FILE SUMMARY

**COMPLAINANT:** Taxpayer Advocate, Laguna Niguel, California

**COMPLAINT DATE:** November 4, 2021

**PROTECTED CLASSES:** Race (Hispanic) and National Origin (Mexican)

**ALLEGATIONS:**
Complainant alleged harassment by a co-worker serving as On-the-Job Instructor and Lead, including:
1. Mocking of accent and expressed dislike of speaking with Complainant (June 2020 – July 2021)
2. Sabotage of work through misinformation and inventory lists
3. Overloading calendar with unrealistic appointments
4. Advising manager to process fraudulent tax return

**AGENCY INVESTIGATION FINDINGS:**
- Acknowledged accent-related comments as unwelcome verbal conduct based on protected categories
- Found insufficient evidence that incidents 2, 3, and 4 constituted harassment based on protected classes
- Determined complaints were uncorroborated and lacked proof of discriminatory motivation
- Internal anti-harassment inquiry did not substantiate claims

**AGENCY FINAL DECISION:** No discrimination found

**COMPLAINANT'S APPEAL ARGUMENTS:**
- Agency failed to acknowledge harasser's initial role as her manager
- Comments about accent began on first day of employment
- Prior supervisor (S2) ignored complaints and allowed harassment to continue despite requests for supervisor change

**AGENCY'S RESPONSE:**
- Comments reflected communication difficulty, not mockery based on national origin
- Allegations lack severity and pervasiveness required for hostile work environment
- Management promptly separated parties in late July 2021
