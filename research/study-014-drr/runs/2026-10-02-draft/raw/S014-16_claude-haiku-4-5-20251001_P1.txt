# CASE FILE SUMMARY

**Case:** EEO Discrimination and Hostile Work Environment Complaint

**Complainant:** Deportation Officer (DO), GS-12, San Bernardino, California
- National Origin: Romanian
- Age: 48 (DOB: 1971)
- Prior EEO Activity: Yes (Cases HS-ICE-21417-2012 and HS-ICE-22663-2012)

**Respondent:** Supervisory Detention and Deportation Officer
- DOB: 1977
- National Origin: Vietnamese

---

## ALLEGATIONS

Complainant filed complaint on June 6, 2019, alleging discrimination and hostile work environment based on national origin, age, and reprisal for prior protected EEO activity under Title VII and ADEA. Four discrete incidents alleged:

1. **July 26, 2018**: Supervisor denied schedule adjustment request to attend concert with daughter
2. **July 27, 2018**: Supervisor recalled Complainant to office after he left for the day
3. **October 1, 2018**: Complainant discovered pay discrepancies in AUO calculations; claims supervisor informed another DO (DO 2) of pay errors before correcting them
4. **October 12, 2018**: Supervisor admonished Complainant in Performance Work Plan and rated him on areas not in official PWP

---

## INVESTIGATION FINDINGS

Agency investigation concluded Supervisor provided legitimate nondiscriminatory reasons for all challenged actions. No evidence demonstrated management's actions were motivated by discriminatory intent or based on Complainant's protected classes. Alleged conduct was not sufficiently severe or pervasive to constitute unlawful harassment.

---

## AGENCY DECISION

**Final Agency Decision (August 10, 2021)**: Complaint DISMISSED. Complainant failed to prove discrimination by preponderance of evidence.

*Note: Dismissal imposed as sanction for Complainant's failure to submit required investigative affidavit despite multiple orders.*

---

## CURRENT STATUS

Appeal pending. Neither party submitted appeal statement.
