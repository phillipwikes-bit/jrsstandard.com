# CASE FILE SUMMARY

**Complainant Status:** Full-time Mail Handler Equipment Operator at Agency's Morgan Processing and Distribution Center, New York, New York

**Complaint Filing Date:** November 18, 2021

**Bases of Complaint:** Sex (female), disability (physical), and reprisal for prior protected EEO activity under Title VII and the Rehabilitation Act

**Allegations:**
1. On June 25, 2021, and ongoing: Denied overtime work within her limitations
2. On July 19, 2021: Verbally abused by a coworker; no action taken (CF at 205-08)
3. Beginning August 1, 2021, and ongoing: Micromanaged and assigned to rewrap section or city manual section and/or confronted about job duties
4. On August 31, 2021: Request for temporary change of schedule denied

**Initial Allegation as Accepted:** "On July 20, 2021, you were verbally abused by a supervisor and no action was taken." (CF at 60)

**Clarification During Investigation:** Complainant clarified that the incident occurred on July 19, 2021, that it was a coworker (not a supervisor) who verbally abused her, and that supervisors took no action when she reported it. (CF at 205-08)

**Investigation Completion and ROI Transmission:**
- **April 5, 2022:** Agency transmitted ROI and Notice of right to hearing to Complainant and her non-attorney representative (CF at 98-103). Email stated ROI available for download for 30 days and requested confirmation of receipt; destination server did not send delivery notification. (CF at 97)

**Post-Investigation Documents Submitted:**
- **April 5, 2022:** Agency provided Acting Supervisor's investigative affidavit (completed March 28, 2022) and Litigation Hold Notice (signed March 31, 2022) (CF at 85-96, 86-95). Acting Supervisor stated he had no organizational relationship to Complainant during relevant time and was not at facility on July 20, 2021. (CF at 86-93)

- **May 17, 2022:** Agency again emailed Complainant and her non-attorney representative notifying them ROI available for download for 30 days; destination server did not send delivery notification. (CF at 82-84)

Documents appear to have been provided via first class mail. (CF at 85)

**Hearing Request:**
- **May 31, 2022:** Complainant mailed hearing request to Agency and Commission's New York District Office (CF at 40-42)
- **June 6, 2022:** Agency received hearing request (stamped on mailing envelope and form) (CF at 40, 42)
- **May 28, 2022 Letter:** Included with hearing request, Complainant refuted Acting Supervisor's affidavit statement that he was not present on date of incident and requested Employee Everything Report from Agency (CF at 41)
- **May 18, 2022 Phone Call:** According to Complainant, she called EEO Services Analyst to refute Acting Supervisor's statement; EEO Services Analyst told her case was closed on May 1, 2022 (CF at 41). Complainant asked how case could be closed if she and representative had not been notified or received ROI (CF at 41). EEO Services Analyst told her she could request hearing but should attach letter stating she found out case was closed on May 18, 2022 (CF at 41)

**Agency Final Decision:**
- **June 2, 2022:** Agency issued final decision pursuant to 29 C.F.R. § 1614.110(b), citing failure to request hearing within timeframe provided in 29 C.F.R. § 1614.108(f). Agency concluded Complainant failed to prove Agency subjected her to discrimination as alleged.

**Post-Decision Documents:**
- **July 6, 2022:** Agency provided updated June 7, 2022, affidavit from Acting Supervisor (CF at 1-10). Acting Supervisor stated his organizational relationship with Complainant during complaint timeframe was Acting Supervisor (CF at 2). Acting Supervisor stated he was not present when coworker verbally abused Complainant on July 19, 2021, and did not know if Complainant or anyone acting on her behalf reported any harassment (CF at 4-9)

**Commission Action:**
- **June 10, 2022:** EEOC's New York District Office received Complainant's hearing request; docketed as Hearing No. 520-2022-00326X
- **June 15, 2022:** Complainant filed instant appeal
- **November 29, 2022:** EEOC AJ issued order dismissing Complainant's hearing request, noting Agency had already issued final decision, which Complainant had appealed
