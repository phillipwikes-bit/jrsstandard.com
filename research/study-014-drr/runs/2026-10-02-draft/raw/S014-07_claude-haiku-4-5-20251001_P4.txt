# INVESTIGATION RECORD SUMMARY

**Complainant's Employment and Complaint Filing:**
Complainant worked for the Agency as a Taxpayer Advocate in Laguna Niguel, California. On November 4, 2021, Complainant filed a formal EEO complaint alleging discrimination based on race (Hispanic) and national origin (Mexican).

**Complaint Acceptance and Framing:**
By letter dated November 16, 2021, the Agency accepted the formal complaint for investigation and framed the claims as follows: Whether a co-worker who was an On-the-Job Instructor (OJI) and Lead subjected Complainant to harassment based on race/national origin (Hispanic/Mexican) on multiple occasions beginning in June 2020 and continuing through July 2021 in the following manner:
1. Made fun of Complainant's accent and said she disliked speaking to Complainant
2. Tried to sabotage Complainant's work by creating a list of inventory late actions and expired casework, and purposely gave Complainant misinformation regarding case processing
3. Filled Complainant's calendar with unrealistic appointments that experienced employees could not complete
4. Advised Complainant's manager to have Complainant process a fraudulent tax return

**Investigation and Agency Final Decision:**
After investigation, the Agency provided Complainant with a copy of the investigation report and notice of the right to request a hearing before an EEOC Administrative Judge pursuant to 29 C.F.R. § 1614.108(f). When Complainant did not request a hearing within the provided timeframe, the Agency issued a final decision pursuant to 29 C.F.R. § 1614.110(b), finding no discrimination.

**Agency's Findings on Incident (1):**
The Agency determined: "assuming [Complainant's initial supervisor, S1] made these and other similar comments [as set forth in incident (1)], the statements would be unwelcome verbal conduct based on Complainant's protected categories of race/national origin/ethnicity." (FAD at 12)

**Agency's Findings on Incidents (2), (3), and (4):**
The Agency stated: "Complainant has produced no persuasive evidence that management's actions described in [alleged incidents] 2, 3, and 4 were harassment based on her statutorily protected classes. Complainant's uncorroborated statements that management was motivated by race/ethnicity and/or national origin to take the actions complained of are not sufficient to sustain her burden of showing harassment…Finally, we note that Complainant's allegations of harassment were addressed through an internal anti-harassment inquiry, which did not substantiate Complainant's claims." (FAD at 13)

**Complainant's Appeal Arguments:**
Complainant asserts that:
- The Agency's final decision did not properly set forth that the alleged harasser (S1) was initially her former manager prior to serving as her Lead and OJI
- S1 made derogatory comments regarding her accent since her first day of employment
- She provided notice to S2 regarding S1's behavior and requested a change of supervisor
- S2 ignored the harassment and allowed the behavior to continue

**Agency's Response:**
The Agency requests affirmation of its final decision finding no discrimination. The Agency reiterates that Complainant failed to establish she was subjected to harassment.

Regarding S1's comments about Complainant's accent, the Agency states: "[a]ssuming the truth of Complainant's allegations, nothing in S1's comments indicate she mocked Complainant's accent or that the comments were based on her being of Mexican descent. At best, [S1] indicated she expressed difficulty in communicating with Complainant. At worst, S1's comments were the type of insensitive, hurtful comments that the Commission has declined to find as discriminatory." (Agency Response Brief at 11)

The Agency asserts that Complainant's claims fall short of the severity and pervasiveness required for a hostile work environment. The Agency states that management promptly separated Complainant and S1—specifically, after Complainant raised the issue of harassment by S1 in late July 2021, S4 separated them, and Complainant had no further contact with S1 after this separation.

Regarding Complainant's raising the matter with S2, the Agency states it "appears" that Complainant only expressed a "lack of support." (Agency Response Brief at 17-18)
