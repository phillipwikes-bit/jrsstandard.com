**CASE FILE SUMMARY**

**Complainant:** Probationary Credentials Assistant, GS-0303-07, Naval Medical Center Portsmouth, Virginia

**Complaint Filed:** November 8, 2018

**Allegation:** Race discrimination (African-American) in termination on August 2, 2018

**Theory at Hearing:** Complainant alleged that her second-level supervisor (S2), Department Head, terminated her based on race after learning she was in an interracial relationship with a White man in May 2018. She claimed S1 (first-level supervisor) had warned her not to disclose this relationship to S2.

**Key Evidentiary Issues:**
- Complainant's interracial relationship theory was absent from her initial EEO complaint, counseling records, and investigation affidavit—only first raised at the hearing approximately three years post-termination
- Complainant had previously attributed termination to unrelated incidents (Leesburg conference and alleged grievance filing)
- Corroborating witness's affidavit similarly lacked any mention of the interracial relationship theory despite referencing other discrimination claims
- S1 denied making prejudicial comments at the hearing

**Administrative Judge Findings:**
- Found Complainant and witness testimony not credible due to the suspicious late introduction of the interracial relationship theory
- Found the Agency articulated a legitimate, nondiscriminatory reason: Complainant misrepresented facts to leadership regarding a provider's credentialing privileges request and provided the provider with inaccurate guidance, causing application delays
- Found Complainant not credible regarding pre-submission communications with the provider
- Found the secondary termination reason (failure to timely initiate renewal process) unsupported by evidence, but found it would not constitute pretext given the primary valid reason

**Outcome:** AJ Decision favored the Agency. Complainant failed to prove discrimination as alleged. Decision became final when Agency did not issue final order within forty days.
