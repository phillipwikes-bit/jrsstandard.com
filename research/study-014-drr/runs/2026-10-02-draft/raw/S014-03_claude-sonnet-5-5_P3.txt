# Investigation Record Summary

## Background and Accepted Claims

At the time of the events giving rise to this complaint, Complainant worked as a full-time Mail Handler Equipment Operator at the Agency's Morgan Processing and Distribution Center in New York, New York. On November 18, 2021, she filed an EEO complaint alleging that the Agency discriminated against her on the bases of sex (female), disability (physical), and reprisal for prior protected EEO activity, in violation of Title VII and the Rehabilitation Act. She alleged that:

1. On June 25, 2021, and ongoing, she was denied overtime work within her limitations.
2. On July 19, 2021, a coworker verbally abused her and no action was taken.
3. Beginning August 1, 2021, and ongoing, she was micromanaged, assigned to either the rewrap section or the city manual section, and/or confronted about her job duties.
4. On August 31, 2021, her request for a temporary change of schedule was denied.

## Clarification of Claim 2

The Agency initially accepted claim 2 for investigation as follows: "On July 20, 2021, you were verbally abused by a supervisor and no action was taken." CF at 60. During the investigation, Complainant clarified that the incident occurred on July 19, 2021, and that the person who verbally abused her was a coworker, not a supervisor. She also stated that she reported the incident to supervisors and nothing was done. CF at 205-08.

## Transmission of the Report of Investigation

After completing its investigation, the Agency stated that on April 5, 2022, it sent Complainant and her non-attorney representative a copy of the report of investigation (ROI) and notice of her right to request a hearing before an EEOC Administrative Judge (Notice). That same day, the Agency emailed both of them stating that the final ROI was available for download for 30 days and asking them to confirm receipt. CF at 98-103. The record contains an April 5, 2022, message indicating that the email was relayed to Complainant and her representative but that the destination server did not send a delivery notification. CF at 97.

Also on April 5, 2022, the Agency provided Complainant and her representative with documents submitted after the investigative file was completed, asking that they be added to Complainant's copy of the file. CF at 85-96. The documents were an Acting Supervisor's investigative affidavit, completed March 28, 2022, and the Acting Supervisor's Litigation Hold Notice, signed March 31, 2022. CF at 86-95. In the affidavit, the Acting Supervisor stated that he had no organizational relationship to Complainant during the relevant period and that he was not at the facility on July 20, 2021. CF at 86-93. It appears these documents were sent by first class mail. CF at 85.

On May 17, 2022, the Agency again emailed Complainant and her representative, advising that an electronic copy of the ROI would be available for download for 30 days. CF at 82-83. As with the earlier email, the record contains a May 17, 2022, message showing that it was relayed to Complainant and her representative but that the destination server did not send a delivery notification. CF at 84.

## Complainant's Hearing Request

On May 31, 2022, Complainant mailed a hearing request to the Agency and to the Commission's New York District Office. CF at 40-42. The Agency stamped the mailing envelope and the hearing request form as received on June 6, 2022. CF at 40, 42.

With her request, Complainant enclosed a letter dated May 28, 2022. In it, she stated that after receiving the Acting Supervisor's affidavit in April 2022, she wanted to refute his statement that he was not present on the date of the incident, and she requested a copy of his Employee Everything Report from the Agency. CF at 41.

Complainant also described a May 18, 2022, telephone call to an EEO Services Analyst, made to dispute the Acting Supervisor's statement. According to Complainant, the Analyst told her that her case had been closed on May 1, 2022. She stated that she asked how the case could be closed when neither she nor her representative had been notified or received a copy of the ROI. She further stated that the Analyst told her she could request a hearing but should attach a letter explaining that she learned on May 18, 2022, that her case was closed. CF at 41.

## Agency Final Decision

On June 2, 2022, the Agency issued a final decision. It stated that it was acting under 29 C.F.R. § 1614.110(b) because Complainant had not requested a hearing within the time provided in 29 C.F.R. § 1614.108(f). The decision concluded that Complainant had not proven that the Agency subjected her to discrimination as alleged.

## Subsequent Events

On June 10, 2022, the EEOC's New York District Office received Complainant's hearing request and docketed it as Hearing No. 520-2022-00326X. On June 15, 2022, Complainant filed the instant appeal.

On July 6, 2022, the Agency again provided Complainant and her representative with documents submitted after the investigative file was completed, asking that they be added to her copy of the file. CF at 1-10. The document was an updated affidavit from the Acting Supervisor, dated June 7, 2022. CF at 2-10. In it, he stated that during the relevant period his organizational relationship with Complainant was that of Acting Supervisor. CF at 2. He also stated that he was not present when the coworker verbally abused Complainant on July 19, 2021, and that he did not know whether Complainant or anyone acting for her had reported any harassment. CF at 4-9.

On November 29, 2022, an EEOC Administrative Judge issued an order dismissing Complainant's hearing request. The order noted that the Agency had already issued a final decision, which Complainant had appealed.
