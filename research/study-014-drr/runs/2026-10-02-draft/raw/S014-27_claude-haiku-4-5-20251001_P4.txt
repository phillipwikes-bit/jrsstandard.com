# INVESTIGATION SUMMARY

## Case Identification and Timeline
- **Complaint Filed:** January 2, 2020
- **Formal Complaint Accepted:** March 11, 2020 (Agency letter)
- **Request for Final Decision:** July 10, 2020 (Complainant)
- **Final Decision Issued:** Pursuant to 29 C.F.R. § 1614.110(b) (date not specified in record)

## Complainant Information
- **Position:** Senior Special Agent, Polygraph Examiner
- **Division:** Forensic Services Division
- **Location:** Washington, D.C.

## Claimed Discrimination - Basis and Claims

**Alleged Basis:** Reprisal for prior protected EEO activity

**Claims Accepted for Investigation (per Agency letter, March 11, 2020):**

1. **Merit Promotion Plan (MPP) Supervisory Evaluation (SE):** Whether Complainant was discriminatorily rated lower in his 2019 MPP SE compared to his 2018 rating by his prior supervisor.

2. **Hostile Work Environment - Retaliation/Reprisal:**
   - a. On or about September 10, 2019: [S2] obstructed his return to Quality Control (QC) and mentor duties
   - b. On or about September 13, 2019: [S2] denied his request for reconsideration of the denial of QC and mentor duties
   - c. Since September 1, 2019, to present: [S2] and another Agency official delegated assignments directly to the Washington Field Office examiner and excluded him from communications, circumventing him as the designated Senior Polygraph Examiner/Polygraph Backup

## Agency's Final Decision Findings

**Regarding Claim (1) - MPP SE Rating:**
- Agency articulated legitimate, nondiscriminatory reasons for Complainant's MPP SE
- Decision: No discrimination found
- Agency Note: MPP SE is a separate and distinct process from the annual performance evaluation process
- Agency Note: S2 did not complete Complainant's 2018-2019 MPP SE

**Regarding Claims 2(a) and 2(b) - QC and Mentor Duties:**
- Agency found Complainant was restored to mentoring duties and given approval to resume mentoring duties
- Agency stated Complainant remains eligible for QC duties
- Agency noted various factors are taken into consideration when selecting an employee for QC duties
- Decision: No discrimination found

**Regarding Claim 2(c) - Communications and Assignment Delegation:**
- [S2] asserted he never consulted with Complainant or any other WFO examiner regarding assignments
- [S2] asserted he had not excluded Complainant from communications
- Agency asserted Complainant has not been treated differently from other examiners
- Decision: No discrimination found

## Appeal Arguments

**Complainant's Arguments:**
- Final decision was untimely issued (requested July 10, 2020)
- Requested sanction of default judgment against Agency
- Report of Investigation (ROI) was inadequate
- 2019-2020 MPP SE is a continuation of reprisal
- Rated "Outstanding" on 2019-2020 annual performance appraisal three months prior to MPP SE rating
- Less qualified employees being selected for mentoring and quality control collateral duties
- One management official did not respond to request for testimony
- Prior EEO case final decision ordered Agency to reassign Complainant to Quality Control and provide EEO training to specified management officials, but Agency has not complied

**Agency's Response:**
- Delay in issuing final decision was minimal; sanctions not warranted
- MPP SE is separate and distinct from annual performance evaluation
