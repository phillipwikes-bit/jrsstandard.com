# CASE FILE SUMMARY

**Case:** EEO Discrimination Complaint - Reasonable Accommodation and AWOL Charges

**Complainant:** GS-15 Primary Care Physician, East Orange VA Medical Center, East Orange, NJ

**Period at Issue:** April 21, 2020 – January 22, 2021

---

## ALLEGATIONS

1. **Denial of Reasonable Accommodation** (April 21, 2020 – January 22, 2021): Complainant requested 100% telework, 20-hour work weeks, and modified assignments due to right shoulder injury.

2. **Improper AWOL Charges** (August 10, 2020 – January 22, 2021): Complainant charged with AWOL despite pending reasonable accommodation request.

---

## FACTUAL BACKGROUND

Complainant sustained a shoulder injury in July 2019 and aggravated it in December 2019. She submitted formal accommodation requests in February 2020 requesting light duty, part-time work (20 hours/week), no lifting, computer assistance, and telework. The Agency approved LWOP (Leave Without Pay) for rehabilitation through August 7, 2020.

The Agency requested medical documentation supporting Complainant's ability to perform essential functions (patient examinations, computer use, documentation). Despite multiple requests, Complainant failed to provide complete medical records or authorize physician contact. Medical statements from treating physicians recommended telework and light duty but were dated at intervals suggesting temporary disability.

When Complainant refused to sign a telework agreement for 1-3 months and failed to provide requested medical documentation, the Reasonable Accommodation Coordinator closed the request on August 7, 2020. Management subsequently charged Complainant AWOL beginning August 10, 2020. Complainant was terminated January 22, 2021.

---

## ADMINISTRATIVE JUDGE'S DECISION

The AJ found **no discrimination**, determining Complainant failed to establish the Agency denied reasonable accommodation because:
- Insufficient medical documentation was provided
- No medical diagnosis or duration established
- Complainant could not perform essential duties (patient examinations) and refused to authorize medical information disclosure
- Requested permanent telework was unsuitable for her position requiring physical patient examinations

---

## STATUS

Complainant appealed the Agency's final decision. Matter is pending before the Commission.
