**Case Summary: EEO Reprisal Complaint, Senior Special Agent, Polygraph Examiner**

**Parties/Background:** Complainant is a Senior Special Agent, Polygraph Examiner in the Agency's Forensic Services Division, Washington, D.C. He filed a formal EEO complaint on January 2, 2020, alleging reprisal for prior protected EEO activity. The Agency accepted the complaint on March 11, 2020.

**Accepted Claims:**
1. Complainant received a lower 2019 Merit Promotion Plan (MPP) Supervisory Evaluation than he had received from his prior supervisor in 2018. (The record text for this claim is incomplete.)
2. He was subjected to a retaliatory hostile work environment when S2:
   - a. On or about Sept. 10, 2019, obstructed his return to Quality Control (QC) and mentor duties.
   - b. On or about Sept. 13, 2019, denied his request for reconsideration of that denial.
   - c. From Sept. 1, 2019, to the present, S2 and another official assigned work directly to the Washington Field Office (WFO) examiner and excluded Complainant, bypassing him as the designated Senior Polygraph Examiner/Backup.

**Procedural History:** After the investigation, the Agency issued the report of investigation (ROI) and notified Complainant of his right to request a hearing before an EEOC Administrative Judge. At Complainant's request, the Agency issued a final decision under 29 C.F.R. § 1614.110(b) finding no discrimination.

**Agency Findings:**
- Claim 1: The Agency articulated legitimate, nondiscriminatory reasons for the MPP rating.
- Claims 2(a)–(b): Complainant was restored to mentoring duties and was under consideration for QC duties.
- Claim 2(c): S2 stated he never consulted Complainant or other WFO examiners about assignments and did not exclude Complainant from communications.

**Complainant's Appeal Arguments:**
- The Agency's final decision was untimely (he requested it July 10, 2020), and he seeks default judgment as a sanction.
- The ROI was inadequate, and one management official did not respond to a request for testimony.
- The 2019–2020 MPP rating continues the reprisal, and it conflicts with his "Outstanding" annual appraisal for 2019–2020, issued three months earlier.
- Less qualified employees are being selected for mentoring and QC duties.
- The Agency has not complied with a prior EEO final decision ordering his reassignment to QC and EEO training for specified managers.

**Agency's Response:**
- Claim 1: The MPP evaluation is separate from the annual performance appraisal process, and S2 did not complete Complainant's 2018–2019 MPP evaluation.
- Claims 2(a)–(b): Complainant was approved to resume mentoring. He remains eligible for QC, though various factors govern selection.
- Claim 2(c): Complainant was not treated differently from other examiners.
- The delay in issuing the final decision was minimal, so sanctions are unwarranted.

**Status:** The matter is on appeal. Complainant asks that the Agency's no-discrimination finding be reversed. No appellate determination appears in the record provided.
