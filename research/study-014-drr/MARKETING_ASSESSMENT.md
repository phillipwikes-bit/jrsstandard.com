# Study 014: marketing advantages and cautions (*Judgment*, 2026-10-02)

Every advantage below is **conditional on the pre-registered results** (PROTOCOL_DRAFT, "What each result would allow us to say"). Nothing here may be used before Study 014 runs. CLAUDE.md section 24 applies: no "prevents", "eliminates" or "guaranteed", and no compliance claim.

## Advantages, if the results support them
1. **A demonstrable before-and-after.** Show a public EEOC record, the AI summary of it, and the dates, sources and citations that disappeared. It is concrete, verifiable and timely, as organizations adopt AI drafting for reports of investigation.
2. **A public, rerunnable benchmark.** Built from public federal decisions with open scripts. "Run it on your own model" sets JRS apart from vendor claims nobody can check.
3. **A vendor-neutral standard-setter, not a tool seller.** It fits the trade venues (vendor-neutral rule) and the B2B licensing aim: JRS as the specification that AI drafting and review tools can be measured against.
4. **Prevention as the product** (if the keep-sources prompt works). A JRS-derived drafting instruction set that preserves anchors becomes a licensable standard for drafting-tool vendors. This may be a larger market than detection.
5. **A coherent research programme.** Study 001: humans detect DRR (83.9%, constructed corpus). Study 014 Part 1: AI drafting introduces it (if H1 holds). Part 2: JRS-structured review catches it (if H2 holds).
6. **Candour as credibility.** Pre-registration and publishing whatever Study 014 finds, including a negative result, is rare in this market, and diligence-minded buyers and editors value it.
7. **Free practitioner tools.** An "anchor checklist" (dates, sources, record citations, who said what) suits the free training (D8), the investigator guides, and the Dewey, SCCE, HCCA and IAOHRA pieces.
8. **Fabricated citations, if found.** Highly newsworthy. It must be reported soberly, with counts and examples, and without naming a vendor as at fault.

## Cautions
- "AI creates DRR" stays a measured, scoped statement ("in this test, these drafters, this prompt"). It is not a slogan; the estate's own manuscript is author-blind.
- The Engine: market the method, the standard and the benchmark, unless Study 014's H3 shows an Engine advantage.
- Small samples, one domain, one vendor: say so in every piece.
- If H1 or H2 fails, the planned narrative is not used. The negative result is published with the same prominence.
