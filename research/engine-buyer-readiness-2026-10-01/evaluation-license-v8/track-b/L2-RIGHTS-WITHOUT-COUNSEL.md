   - AI-assisted development disclosed;
   - provided as is;
   - liability capped at the fee paid;
   - no indemnity from you;
   - protection resting on confidentiality and use restrictions, not on a claim of copyright.

   I can draft this next.
3. **Get one paid review at the point it matters most:** the final agreement, before the first signature, once a real buyer exists. It is one document reviewed once, not ongoing counsel. Low-cost routes:
   - your state or county bar association's lawyer referral service, many of which offer a reduced-fee first consultation;
   - a law school intellectual property or small-business clinic, which may be free if you qualify;
   - a fixed-fee contract review from a solo practitioner.

   I have not checked prices or eligibility. Ask before booking.
4. **Not acceptable without a lawyer:** a full IP sale, an exclusive licence, or anything that transfers ownership (Track C). Those convey title, and the contributor issues and the missing signed documents become central.
