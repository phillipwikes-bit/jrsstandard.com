SUPPLIER ACCESS EXCEPTION APPROVAL (FICTIONAL RECORD FOR ENGINE TESTING)
Record ID: SAE-2026-0412
Organization: Northwind Assembly Ltd (fictional)
Supplier: Cobalt Calibration Services (fictional)
Prepared by: R. Okafor, Access Governance Analyst
Approved by: M. Lindqvist, Head of Information Security

1. Request. On 3 March 2026 Cobalt Calibration Services asked for remote access to the line-4 torque-test controller for 10 working days, 9 to 20 March 2026, to install firmware release 7.2. Standard policy ACC-07 section 4.1 allows supplier remote access only through the vendor jump host, which does not support the controller's serial-over-IP protocol. An exception to ACC-07 section 4.1 was therefore requested.

2. Evidence reviewed. (a) Cobalt's statement of work dated 2 March 2026, listing the firmware tasks. (b) Cobalt's ISO 27001 certificate, valid to 30 November 2027, checked against the issuing body's register on 4 March 2026. (c) Engineering confirmation from P. Duarte, dated 4 March 2026, that release 7.2 fixes defect TT-118 affecting test accuracy. (d) Network design note ND-22 describing a temporary isolated VLAN.

3. Decision and basis. Exception approved on 5 March 2026 for 9 to 20 March 2026 only. Basis: the firmware fix is needed to correct defect TT-118 (evidence c); the supplier holds a current certification (evidence b); and the risk of direct access is reduced by three controls: access limited to the isolated VLAN in ND-22 (evidence d), session recording on the firewall, and a named Cobalt engineer with a time-boxed account that expires automatically on 20 March 2026 at 18:00.

4. Alternatives considered. On-site installation was considered and rejected because Cobalt's only qualified engineer is based abroad and the earliest on-site date was 28 April 2026, which would leave defect TT-118 open for seven further weeks.

5. Accountability. R. Okafor prepared this record on 5 March 2026. M. Lindqvist approved it on 5 March 2026. Review of the session recordings is assigned to R. Okafor, due 27 March 2026.

6. Chronology. 2 Mar statement of work received; 3 Mar request; 4 Mar certificate checked and engineering confirmation; 5 Mar decision; 9 Mar access opens; 20 Mar access expires; 27 Mar recording review due.
