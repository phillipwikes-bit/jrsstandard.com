## What I applied, and what I did not

**Applied, because it is sound and the facts support it:**

- **Authority first.** Your MCCR credential now opens the block instead of sitting in a footer. It is the strongest trust signal you have and it was buried. Per your instruction the year range and the "thirteen years reading records after the fact" phrasing are gone: the line is the title and the institution, nothing more.
- **Real social proof, not registry counts.** The honest, impressive number counts every study, not just the two that issue 24-record sets: 52 distinct people have graded records across the three studies, of whom 32 independent experts each completed a full 24-record set, unpaid. All 32 are credentialed specialists. The "trained reviewer" category in the reliability analysis is the separate bench pool (R- codes), not these people, so calling all 32 experts is accurate. 52 is a distinct-human count with cross-study duplicate codes removed: see research/count_participants.py. That is verifiable and it is what a compliance reader respects.
- **The discouraging ratio is gone.** Three named Registry members out of forty backers is true, and publishing it tells a reader that most professionals declined. It never appears again in public copy.
- **Privacy before the ask.** "Never stored" now sits above the link rather than after it.
- **Expectation set before the click.** The block says plainly that it takes one short form. A reader who expects a form and finds one converts. A reader promised zero friction who then meets a form leaves and does not come back. That is the same bait-and-switch mechanic Dr. Cialdini identifies, applied to your actual site.
- **Individual capacity stated.** The line that removes the corporate liability fear: supporting as an individual practitioner, not on behalf of an employer.

**Not applied, and why:**

- **"Join 150+ Certified Governance Advocates."** You have three named Registry members. Any number of that kind would be fabricated, and your audience is the one population professionally trained to check.
- **"Master of Science in Negotiation and Conflict Management."** That credential appears nowhere in your study record. If you hold it, tell me and it goes in the authority line. I will not add a degree I cannot verify.
- **"No sign-up required" and "zero form fields."** Your site requires a short form and one box on every route, by your own instruction, repeated three times. Copy promising frictionless access would be false and would produce exactly the bounce it is meant to prevent. If you want the copy to say no sign-up, the site has to change first, and that is your call, not mine.
