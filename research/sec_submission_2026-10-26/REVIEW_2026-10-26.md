## 4. Items for the owner

1. **The SSRN preprint is public (abstract 7591521) and this repository had no record of it.** It is now recorded. To fix the Makhlouf Shabou author error, upload a revised version to SSRN.
2. **Ethics statement wording (SEC).** It says the study "did not involve human subjects research", and the next sentence describes a consenting human second reader. A reviewer may notice the tension. Suggested replacement: *"Ethical approval was not sought because the study analysed only publicly available documents. Its one participant, the independent second reader, was an adult volunteer who gave informed consent."*
3. **Second-reader credit (optional).** Jeffrey Billups consented to be named (tracker 2026-08-28). The acknowledgement is anonymous, which suits anonymous review. He can be named in the accepted version.
4. **Data promised in the data availability statement.** The SEC version promises the dataset, data dictionary and reproduction script "with this submission", and a Zenodo DOI before publication. The files exist in `research/JCI_SUBMISSION_2026-08-28/02_DATA/`. The Zenodo deposit has not been made.
5. **Not established from the repository:** whether the RMJ version (R9, the submission candidate of 2026-09-01) was ever submitted, and its outcome; and whether the first author has agreed to SocioEconomic Challenges as the venue. The tracker (2026-09-01) records that the choice of venue is hers as much as the owner's.
