🔍 I spent my career reviewing records after the fact as a Lead Civil Rights Officer with the Maryland Commission on Civil Rights. The same pattern kept appearing: the decision was defensible, the record was not.

⚠️ I call this Decision Reconstruction Risk (DRR): when a consequential record can no longer explain, on its own terms, how or why a decision was reached.

⚡ Generative AI makes DRR harder to detect. Polished prose can conceal missing evidence, unsupported statements, and untraceable reasoning. The document looks complete. Its justification may not be.

🛡️ THE JUSTIFICATION REVIEW STANDARD (JRS)
A practical framework helping HR, compliance, legal, audit, AI governance, and investigative teams test whether AI-assisted records will withstand scrutiny, before they become permanent.

Five core questions:
🔎 Can the conclusion be reconstructed?
❓ Is the evidence identifiable?
⏳ Can the chronology be followed?
🧩 Can the reasoning be traced?
⚖️ Is the evidence sufficient?

📊 GROUNDING & VALIDATION
Independently evaluated by experts across 16 countries, 5 continents, and multiple legal traditions.
