Thank you for your thoughtful and encouraging response. I sincerely appreciate the time you have taken to consider our research and, particularly, your recognition of its relevance to the mission of SocioEconomic Challenges.

My central objective in developing this research has been to address a consequential but often overlooked problem: whether organizations can demonstrate the evidentiary basis for decisions that materially affect people's employment, opportunities, and lives.

As AI-assisted documentation becomes increasingly common, I believe this question takes on greater importance for institutional accountability, transparency, and public trust.

The two manuscripts approach this problem from complementary perspectives. Together, they examine documentation integrity, the reconstructability of consequential decisions, and the extent to which structured professional review can identify weaknesses in the supporting record. Importantly, the studies also acknowledge the methodological limitations of their findings and the need for further independent validation.

I am particularly encouraged by your observation that these issues align with the journal's socioeconomic focus. It is precisely this connection, between institutional decision-making and its consequences for individuals, that I hope the research can bring into the scholarly discussion.

My co-authors, Stacyann Young and Ubayet Hossain, and I would welcome any editorial recommendations that could strengthen either manuscript's clarity, contribution, or suitability for publication. We are committed to ensuring that both studies meet the journal's scholarly and methodological expectations.

Thank you again for your consideration and for the opportunity to contribute to SocioEconomic Challenges. I look forward to your assessment and hope these studies can make a meaningful contribution to the journal's readership.
