# Regression Scenario Register

**LOCAL ENGINEERING CANDIDATE ONLY. NOT INDEPENDENTLY EVALUATED. NO RELEASE OR DEPLOYMENT AUTHORIZATION.**

Fifty synthetic scenarios, authored by `scenarios/author-scenarios.mjs` and hashed into `SEAL.json` before candidate logic existed. Each carries a synthetic marker, provenance note, expected safe behaviour, limitation, expected limitation kinds, expected route and a sealed differential expectation. Result counts: `as_expected` 50.
