## 1. What changed in two days

The registration gate went live on 2 August. Here is what the endpoints report today.

| Measure | Before the gate (17 Jul to 1 Aug) | Since the gate (2 to 4 Aug) |
|---|---|---|
| Field guide downloads | 56, across 6 countries | **0** |
| Initiative supporters | 39, across 7 countries | **0** |
| Named registrations captured | 0, none were possible | **0** |
| Organizations running records | 0 | **0** |
| Contributor confirmations | not built yet | **0 of 20** |
| Training enrollments | 7 | 7, unchanged |
| Training completions | 7 | 7, unchanged |

Your read is correct. Everything measurable stopped.

Pre-gate the site produced roughly 3.3 guide downloads and 2.3 support clicks a day. At that rate two days should have produced about seven downloads and about five supporters. It produced none of either.

## 2. What that does and does not prove

The honest position is that we cannot yet tell these two situations apart:

1. **Traffic arrived and the form turned it away.** People clicked, saw a form asking for name, organization, title, work email and three consent questions, and left.
2. **Traffic did not arrive.** Nothing was pushed on LinkedIn or by email in that window, so there was little to convert.

Nothing in the system was recording the difference, because the only thing being logged was a completed registration. A visitor who opened the form and abandoned it left no trace at all.

**That is fixed as of today.** The form now records an anonymous view ping when it opens, with no personal data. From now on the dashboard shows form opens, registrations, and the conversion rate between them. Within a few days of any real traffic you will know which of the two problems you have, and they need opposite responses: a traffic problem needs distribution, a conversion problem needs a shorter form.

Two other measurement facts worth holding on to:

- Pre-gate numbers were never contactable. 56 downloads and 39 supporters produced **2 named people**, both from the old supporters page. The gate did not destroy a valuable stream; it stopped an anonymous one and has not yet replaced it.
- The 7 training enrollments remain the only genuinely conveyable records on the whole dashboard: named, employed, contactable, all 7 consented to transfer, and 7 of 7 completed.

## 3. Updated probability

The 2 August table assumed the gates would move. Two of the three have not.

| Scenario | 2 Aug estimate | 4 Aug estimate | Why it moved |
|---|---|---|---|
| As-is, marks unregistered, zero organizations using it | 10 to 15% | **8 to 12%** | Two more days elapsed with no marks filed and no user acquired, against a self-imposed end-of-August horizon |
| Priced deliberately low, content and brand deal | 20 to 25% | **18 to 22%** | Same reason. The buyer pool for a content deal cares about the corpus, which is intact, so this moves least |
| Marks filed, one organization using it, active outreach | 35 to 50% | **35 to 50%** | Unchanged. This is still available and still the only combination that moves the number materially |

The gate itself is close to neutral on sale probability. It stopped a metric that had no diligence value and started one that does, but it has not yet produced a single record, so nothing has been banked.

**What actually moved the estimate down is the calendar.** You have roughly four weeks to end of August. The trademarks have been outstanding since July. Registration takes 8 to 12 months from filing, which is exactly why a pending application filed now is worth far more in a buyer conversation than one filed after the conversation starts.

## 4. The three gates, current status

**Gate 1, trademarks: still not filed.** Unchanged since July. Cheapest fix available, largest single effect on how a buyer's counsel reads the asset, entirely within your control, and it does not depend on anyone else responding to anything.

**Gate 2, no organization using it: still zero.** The org-pilot workspace exists and works, records are never stored, and it is reachable from every gateway. It has had zero sessions because nobody has been asked to use it. The tool is not the constraint; the ask is.

**Gate 3, contributor rights: infrastructure now built, zero returns.** Twenty personal confirmation links exist, each capturing named credit, continuing use, and transfer permission as forced choices. None have been opened. These were generated yesterday, so the pipeline has simply not been used yet. This gate can go from open to substantially closed in a week, entirely through messages you already have drafted.

## 5. What is genuinely worth something today

Stated plainly, because a buyer will state it plainly:

- The **written corpus**: three field guide editions, the training with certificates, the standard, the reference card, the papers.
- The **validation dataset**: 24 constructed records with a fixed, independently verified key, and 32 completed reviewer sets across two arms.
- The **brand**: JRS and Decision Reconstruction Risk, unregistered.
- The **code and domain**.
- **Seven trained and certified professionals** across five countries and five organizations, all transfer-consented, all completed.

What is not worth anything in diligence: 56 anonymous downloads, 39 anonymous support clicks, and a page view count.

## 6. The order I would work in, for the next four weeks

1. **File the two trademark applications this week.** Few hundred dollars per class, self-filable, no dependency on anyone.
2. **Send the twenty contributor links.** The messages are written. This closes most of Gate 3 in days and it is the only gate that gets harder every week as people disperse.
3. **Change the ask in outreach from endorsement to pilot.** Detailed in the companion conversion plan.
4. **Keep submitting the articles.** They set the price you can ask, not the odds someone asks.
5. **Decide your floor now**, before any conversation starts.

## 7. Bottom line

At a deliberately low price with the gates as they stand: roughly **one in five** within a year, and the single most likely outcome is still no sale.

The gate did not cause the slowdown in value, because the thing it stopped was not producing value. What it did was make the absence of demand visible, which was always true and previously hidden behind a download counter. The response to that is distribution and a direct ask, not removing the gate.
