# Cognitive Controls: Graph Boundary

Local ID: EGC-DOC-06 (NON-CANONICAL). Code: `lib/engine-evidence-graph/controls.mjs`.

## Claim
Each graph defect produces one object with exactly detect, reflect, correct and learn. "Correct" is always a neutral record-completion question. Objects failing the v0.2 neutrality check (reused unchanged) are withheld and reported, never rewritten.

## Local engineering evidence
`DEFECT_TEMPLATES`; validator rule GV-13; probes `e.*`; workbench section "Cognitive Controls".

## Boundary
Controls never tell a reviewer what to decide, whether access should be granted, whether anyone is credible, or what employment, disciplinary, legal or medical action to take.

## Interpretation
Controls ask for missing record content; they do not judge it.

## Limitation
Neutrality is checked lexically. A question can still frame a reviewer's attention.

## External evidence still required
Reviewer-experience study under an authorised protocol.
