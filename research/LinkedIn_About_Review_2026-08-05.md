## 1. Fix today: the podcast line

Live text:

> Listen: The Dewey Publications Podcast (23:45)

Two problems in one line.

**The timestamp cannot be correct.** The published episode runs 18 minutes. Your own study record already flagged this and removed the same "23:45" reference from the prospectus in July.

**"Listen" implies you are on it.** You are not a guest. The episode is "July 6th, 2026," hosted by Peter Broida. JRS is discussed and jrsstandard.com is listed in the episode notes under resources for investigators. That is a third-party mention, which is worth having and worth describing accurately.

Replacement:

> Mentioned on: The Dewey Publications Podcast, episode "July 6th, 2026" (jrsstandard.com listed in the episode notes)
> https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/the-dewey-publications-podcast/id888037481?i=1000775802123

## 2. "Across multiple institutions" says something you did not do

Live text:

> JRS has been rigorously evaluated across multiple institutions

Reviewers took part **in a personal capacity, unpaid, and not on behalf of their employers**. That is what your consent terms say. "Across multiple institutions" tells a reader that organizations evaluated JRS as organizations, which implies approvals nobody gave. It is also the first line a diligence reader will test, because it is the strongest claim in the section.

Replacement:

> JRS has been independently evaluated by experienced professionals across a range of countries, sectors, and legal traditions:

## 3. The country count is out of date

Live text says 10 countries across 5 continents. With the panel now at 16 completers, it is **11 countries across 5 continents**: Australia, Germany, India, Nigeria, Poland, Singapore, South Korea, Spain, the United Arab Emirates, the United Kingdom, and the United States.

## 4. Your strongest number is missing entirely

The About section reports reliability, AI consistency, and the CEP feature. It does not report the detection result, which is the primary finding of the study and the only one that clears a threshold set in advance.

Add this as the first evidence bullet, above reliability:

> Detection: In a blind study against a verified answer key, an international panel of 16 experienced professionals identified unreconstructable records with 83.9 percent accuracy, clearing the threshold set before any data were examined.

## 5. Two small corrections

**Trained-reviewer AC1 is 0.62, not 0.63** on the current data. Match the paper.

**"High Reliability" as a label is doing more work than the data supports.** The point estimates clear the pre-registered floor of 0.61, on 10 records against a target of about 26. Your body text already says "interim" and "substantial," which is exactly right. Just drop the word "High" from the label so the heading and the sentence agree.

**Add the never-stored line to the pilot.** "Your record text is never stored" is the single objection that stops senior people from clicking, and it is currently absent from the About even though it is on the pilot page itself.
