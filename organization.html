Organizational method information

Understand what is available for organizations.

Decision Reconstruction Risk (DRR) concerns the independent reviewability of retained records. JRS is a practical review instrument that practitioners can use within existing workflows, not a software platform they must acquire. Organizations do not need to purchase or replace a software platform to begin examining whether their decision records preserve sufficient evidence, chronology and reasoning for later independent review. Practitioners can start with the free JRS method, guides and training within their existing systems, subject to the published use terms. This site does not offer organizational software, licensing, certification or validated operational effectiveness.

Scope boundaries

Information-only organizational route

  1. Identify the records, data restrictions, organizational workflow, retention rules and authorized reviewers.
  2. Use the publication-edition comparison and record exactly which document was consulted.
  3. Practice with constructed training exercises and the non-scoring browser check. No automated conclusion is supplied.
  4. Preserve evidence, chronology, reviewer reasoning and escalation decisions in your own controlled system.
  5. For a general question, use the existing contact form. That is not research enrollment, contractual acceptance or an operational pilot application.

Confidentiality: Do not upload, paste or email case files, employee details, privileged records, credentials or identifying third-party information. Public contact is not a secure records-intake system.
