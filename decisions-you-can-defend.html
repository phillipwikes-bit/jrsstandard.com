The professional documentation responsibility

Decision Reconstruction Risk (DRR) is the risk that a later reviewer cannot independently reconstruct the evidence, sequence of events or reasoning that led to a recorded conclusion. It can arise whether a record was written manually or with AI assistance.

JRS provides structured questions for human reviewers to identify matters requiring source verification, correction within authorized procedures or escalation. It is not an automated case evaluator and does not replace investigative, legal, audit or organizational controls.

Scope and evidence boundary. A reviewable record does not by itself establish that the underlying decision was lawful, correct, fair or legally defensible. JRS remains in operational validation. Its use does not certify compliance or demonstrate validated field effectiveness.
