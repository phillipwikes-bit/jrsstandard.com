# Remaining serious flags on good drafts: sample review (developer judgement)

2026-11-25, before the last two fixes (abbreviation aliases, whole-sentence negation). 259 critical findings remained on the 60 P4 development drafts. 24 were drawn by sorting on a SHA-256 of drafter, decision and finding id (fixed order, not chosen by hand) and each was read against the public decision.

| Judgement | Count |
|---|---|
| Wrong: the draft is supported by the decision (paraphrase, clause narrowing, added pin-cite, "Administrative Judge (AJ)") | about 15 |
| A note by the drafter about the record, not a statement about the case | about 7 |
| Possibly a real error in the draft | 0 to 2 |

Judged by the developer, not an independent reviewer. It shows that most remaining serious flags on good drafts are matching errors, which is why criterion 2 of this hold-out (half of good drafts free of serious flags) is far from met on development data. The hold-out (Sonnet P3) has not been run.
