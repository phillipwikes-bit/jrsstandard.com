At the time of events giving rise to this complaint, Complainant worked as a Rating Veterans Service Representative at the Agency’s Regional Office facility in North Little Rock, Arkansas. 1. On May 16, 2020, he became aware of his non-selection/failure to promote to the Supervisory Veterans Service Representative, GS-13 position under Vacancy announcement 350-20-DLM-10787513-NBU. 2 an individual asserted that he was subject to discrimination based on race, age, sex, and reprisal when he was not selected for an interview for a position for which he was well-qualified and the selected candidates, who were younger, white women, were less qualified than he was. See Report of Investigation (ROI) at 67-69. He stated that his prior protected activity consists of being interviewed by the Administrative Review Board in a complaint of discriminatory and unfair labor practices and filing a complaint with the Office of Special Counsel. See ROI at 63. The Assistant Veterans Service Center Manager (Assistant Manager), who determined the best qualified candidates from the applicant list and served on the interview panel, stated that the criteria to be deemed best qualified was whether the applicant was meeting or exceeding all elements of their performance standard from October 1, 2019 to March 31, 2020. See ROI at 81. If an applicant was not meeting or exceeding all elements of their performance standards, they were deemed to be not best qualified. See ROI at 81. She stated that she found Complainant not to be best qualified because he was not meeting all elements of his performance standards during the relevant time. See ROI at 83. Complainant’s recent performance appraisal rated Complainant as not meeting the goal of Fully Successful on the Quality element of his evaluation. See ROI at 204-207. The Assistant an individual explained that the best qualified candidates were then scored and ranked based on their resume, previous supervisory experience, interview questions, and a written exercise. See ROI at 83. She further stated that of the two selectees, one had extensive supervisory experience within the Agency and while the other did not have previous supervisory experience, both selectees demonstrated through the interview and the written exercise a high likelihood of success in the position. See ROI at 84. At the conclusion of the investigation, the Agency provided Complainant with a copy of the report of investigation and notice of his right to request a hearing before an Equal Employment Opportunity Commission Administrative Judge (AJ). In accordance with Complainant’s request, the Agency issued a final decision pursuant to 29 C.F.R. § 1614.110(b). the decision found that the Agency articulated legitimate, nondiscriminatory reasons for not selecting Complainant for the position and that Complainant did not submit evidence to support his assertions of discriminatory animus. The decision concluded that Complainant failed to prove that an individual subjected him to discrimination as alleged. Complainant appealed, arguing that the Agency’s explanations of its actions was untrue and should not have been accepted. In response, the Agency urges affirmance of its final decision. The Agency dismissed Complainant’s allegation that the Agency subjected him to a hostile work environment between March 8, 2019 and October 2019 for untimely counselor contact. See Report of Investigation (ROI) at 35-37.
