At the time of events giving rise to this complaint, Complainant worked as a Staff Assistant, GS- 14, at the Agency’s VA Office of Construction and Facilities Management (CFM) in Washington, D.C. period of June 2017 to April 25, 2018, she was denied a reasonable accommodation and subjected to a hostile work environment. Specifically, an individual alleged that: 1. Beginning in June of 2017 and continuing, Complainant’s Program Manager was “badmouthing [Complainant’s] work performance” to her co-workers. 2. During January of 2018, the Program Manager told Complainant that she did not know that Complainant was going on leave, when Complainant had requested leave in October of 2017. 3. On February 21, 2018, the Program an individual denied Complainant’s reasonable accommodation request. 4. During February of 2018, the Program Manager questioned Complainant about her use of the computer system but did not question other employees. 5. In March of 2018, after Complainant requested telework to accommodate her surgery, the Program Manager incorrectly sent Complainant to others, who told Complainant that the approval needed to come from the Program Manager. 6. Despite Complainant’s raising harassment concerns to management, the Associate Executive Director took no action for months to address the alleged harassment. 2 At the conclusion of the investigation, the Agency provided Complainant with a copy of the report of investigation and notice of her right to request a hearing before an Equal Employment Opportunity Commission Administrative Judge (AJ). When, according to the Agency, Complainant did not request a hearing within the time frame provided in 29 C.F.R. § 1614.108(f), the Agency issued a final decision pursuant to 29 C.F.R. § 1614.110(b). The decision concluded that Complainant failed to prove that an individual subjected her to discrimination as alleged. Upon receipt of the Agency’s final decision on the merits, Complainant filed the instant appeal. On appeal, an individual contends that the Agency improperly issued a Final Agency Decision although Complainant had submitted a timely hearing request. Complainant provided a receipt showing the delivery of her hearing request. She asks that the FAD be reversed and that she be allowed to have a hearing. The record documents that, on or around February 17, 2018, Complainant contacted the Harassment Prevention Program to initiate a sex harassment claim. The an individual acknowledged, in a statement dated February 26, 2019, that Complainant initiated a harassment inquiry. A fact- finding was conducted and concluded that harassment occurred, but Complainant was not told the result of the investigation. See Record (Report of Investigation) at page 361 and 364.
