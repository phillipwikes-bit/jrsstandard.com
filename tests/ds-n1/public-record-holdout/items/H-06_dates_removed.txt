The record indicates that, the AJ issued a Notice of Intent to Sanction for each complaint because the Agency failed to submit the complaint file and/or the report of investigation., the Agency filed a separate response to each Notice of Intent to Sanction, indicating that sanctions against the Agency were not warranted because it was not served with a notice that Complainant filed a hearing request, and it was not on notice of any order or other requirement. Specifically, the Agency stated that Complainant did not use Request for a Hearing Form that was provided to her when the report of investigation was transmitted to her. In that form, Complainant was informed to choose the Agency, in EEOC Public Portal, as “DoD Office of Secretary/Washington Headquarters Services.” Instead, Complainant used an outdated version of Request for a Hearing Form which did not indicate an option to request a hearing in the EEOC Public Portal and thus no instruction choosing the Agency therein. The Agency stated that Complainant’s hearings were docketed, via EEOC Public Portal, with the Agency as “DoD Armed Forces Retirement Home”. The Agency indicated that, the EEOP Director requested and received access to the “DoD Armed Forces Retirement Home” FedSEP box as a stopgap measure while discussing with individuals at the EEOC (Office of Federal Operations and Washington Field Office) other ways to ensure more effective usage of FedSEP, given the large population, consisting of multiple agencies, serviced by “DoD Office of Secretary/Washington Headquarters Services.”, the EEOP Director promptly uploaded a copy of the final Agency decision in FedSEP, and, the Agency counsel requested an extension to respond to the AJ’s Notice of Intent to Sanction.
