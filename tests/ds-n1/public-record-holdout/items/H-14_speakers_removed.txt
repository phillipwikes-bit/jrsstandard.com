At the time of events giving rise to this complaint, Complainant worked as a Food Service Worker, WG-7408-02, at the Agency’s William S. Middleton Memorial VA Hospital in Madison, Wisconsin. On May 29, 2019, Complainant filed an EEO complaint alleging that the Agency discriminated against him on the basis of age (78) when, on April 19, 2019, Complainant was terminated from his probationary position. Complainant entered duty on December 26, 2018, on a one-year probationary period. Report of Investigation (ROI), p. 148. His immediate supervisor was the Food Service Supervisor, WS-4 (age 33) (S1). an individual averred he did not know Complainant’s specific age, but he guessed he was in his early 70’s. Tab 7-2, p. 86. an individual testified that, on April 11, 2019, another coworker reported that he observed Complainant eating the Department’s food (a hamburger) and reported it to an individual, who stated he observed Complainant eating something. ROI, p. 71. an individual denied eating the Department food, which is considered theft. an individual claimed that he was falsely accused of eating food from a patient’s tray. an individual stated the food was described as a hamburger, but there were no hamburgers on the menu that day, and he stated, “I don’t eat meat.” ROI, p. 38. an individual acknowledged that hamburgers were not on the menu on the day in question, but an individual averred there may have been an extra one on the cart because it was easier to have the sandwich assembled on the cart when requested by a veteran. an individual stated this is not the first time that employees reported Complainant eating the Department’s food. an individual later acknowledged that he ate an apple turnover. ROI, p. 71. Complainant had previously received a letter of written counseling for failing to complete his assigned duties and to follow the nutrition and food service policies. Complainant’s third level supervisor, the Chief, Nutrition and Food Services, GS-14 (age 36), (S3) recommended Complainant’s termination. ROI, pp. 70-71, 76-77, and 190. an individual averred she was not aware of Complainant’s age. ROI, pp.71-72. She stated she had terminated another younger person for eating the Department food. The Chief, Human Resources (S2) was the signatory to the decision, which was issued to Complainant on April 19, 2019. At the conclusion of the investigation, the Agency provided Complainant with a copy of the report of investigation and notice of his right to request a hearing before an Equal Employment Opportunity Commission Administrative Judge (AJ). In accordance with Complainant’s request, the Agency issued a final decision pursuant to 29 C.F.R. § 1614.110(b). The decision concluded that Complainant failed to prove that an individual subjected him to discrimination as alleged.
