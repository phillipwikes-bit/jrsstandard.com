Record labelling packet

All records, names, organisations and documents in this packet are fictional. They were constructed for testing a documentation-review tool. Please do not search for them.

For each case, read the record and its sources as a careful reviewer would before the record is finalised. Then, in the label sheet, give:

  1. status: one of READY (nothing needs fixing before a human finalises it), REVIEW_REQUIRED (something should be fixed or noted, but nothing material is unsupported or contradicted), HOLD (a material statement is unsupported, contradicted, attributed to the wrong person or wrongly dated), or INCOMPLETE (the case cannot be reviewed as given).
  2. claims: for each listed claim, supported, not_supported or contradicted by its cited sources.
  3. note: a short reason for anything other than READY.

Judge only from what is on the page. Whether evidence is sufficient is your judgment; there is no answer key in this packet. Please work independently and do not consult the developer.
