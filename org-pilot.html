Run JRS on your own records

The Organizational Pilot Package. Paste up to twenty five of your own records, one at a time. Each is assessed against the five review conditions and you get the read immediately. Free, no obligation, and you can stop after one.

Running an organizational pilot is confidential. Your records are processed in memory and never stored or retained, and running one carries no public endorsement obligation. After you have your results, you will have the option, only if you want it, to add your organization's name to the public International Registry of Supporters.

Built by Phillip Wikes, former Lead Civil Rights Officer, Maryland Commission on Civil Rights. The five conditions have been applied record by record by 53 international reviewers across three studies, including an expert panel of 32 independent experts across 16 countries, each completing a full 24-record set.

One of the five questions your records will be asked

Could a reviewer with no access to the author, no institutional memory, and no supplementary explanation identify the basis for each conclusion from the file alone?

If the answer is no, the record is reconstructable only by the person who wrote it. That is the gap the diagnostic finds.

Your record text is never stored. Each record is assessed in memory and discarded. Nothing you paste is written to any database, and it is not used to train anything. Only the count of records you ran and the pattern of results is kept, so the programme can report that an organization used the standard without ever holding what it looked at.

Even so, please remove names and other identifying details before pasting. A record works for this purpose with the specifics redacted.
Ticking the box means three things and no more: contact me about the JRS project, store my details securely, and allow that record to travel with the project if the JRS assets are ever transferred to a successor. It does not publish your name or your organization. Your record text is never stored: each record is assessed in memory and discarded. Full detail in the Privacy Policy.
