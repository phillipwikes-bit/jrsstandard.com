Enterprise Evaluation · Integration · Transactions

A review layer for consequential records, designed to fit existing systems.

JRS examines whether a decision record preserves the basis, evidence, reasoning, and chronology required for later independent review. Organizations can evaluate the public method. The Review Engine is a controlled technical implementation that platform providers can separately evaluate with its API and Decision Reconstruction Manifest.

1 · EvaluateTest the current implementation, limitations, data handling, and workflow fit.
2 · IntegrateMap outputs to records, evidence stores, review queues, and human routing.
3 · LicenseDefine only the methodology, software, brand, implementation, and field rights actually granted.
4 · AcquireEvaluate a defined asset transfer after rights, diligence, liabilities, consideration, and transition terms are established.

What can be examined now

Technical materials

The current Review Engine API contract, OpenAPI specification, Manifest schema, constructed example, data-flow disclosures, integrity controls, and a completed constructed integration evaluation are available for inspection.

Method and evidence

The Standard, Codebook, research program, preliminary findings, negative results, limitations, reviewer resources, and implementation materials are publicly reviewable.

Controlled evaluation

A written package defines scope, test rights, confidentiality, expected outputs, success measures, support, retention, incident handling, and end-of-evaluation disposition.

Transaction diligence

A qualified party can request a controlled rights and provenance review. Public materials do not represent that every potential asset is presently cleared for exclusive licensing or transfer.

Current status. JRS is available for organizational evaluation, controlled platform evaluation, integration discussion, licensing discussion, and acquisition discussion.
