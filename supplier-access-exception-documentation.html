Reviewing a Supplier-Access Exception Before Finalization

When documenting a supplier-access exception, a reviewer should be able to locate the request, authorization, applicable policy, dates and reasons supporting the approval. Use these questions within the organization’s existing authorized workflow. No case record is submitted to JRS.

Identify the retained source material

Locate the written exception request, requester identity and role, business need, cited policy provision, documented approving authority, access scope and stated duration. If an item is absent or held in another system, document that limitation rather than treating the approval summary as sufficient evidence.

Human review using the five JRS conditions

  1. Reconstructability: Can a later reviewer determine what exception was requested and approved?
  2. Identifiable basis: Can the supporting request, approval and policy be located?
  3. Chronology: Can the sequence of request, authorization, access and expiry be rebuilt?
  4. Reasoning trace: Does the record show why the specific exception was authorized under the applicable process?
  5. Sufficiency: Does the retained material adequately support the documented conclusion, or are gaps unresolved?

Verify the approval trail

Confirm that the exception record identifies the dated request, the actual approver and authority, the policy basis, permitted access, effective and expiration dates, and where supporting logs or approvals are retained. Ensure that a later reviewer could distinguish what was known at approval from later explanations. Do not insert a fact, citation or approval that the source record does not support.

Document the disposition

Record whether the evidence was located, what remains unresolved, who reviewed the exception, and whether the matter was corrected or escalated according to local policy. JRS does not approve access or replace the organization's authorization process.

For broader use, read how the review fits an existing workflow and the operational boundaries.

Review boundary. These are general documentation-review questions, not an approval of supplier access, legal advice or a compliance determination. Use authorized organizational procedures and retain all source material in existing controlled systems. Do not upload records to JRS.
