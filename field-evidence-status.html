JRS Field Evidence: Observations, Limits, and Validation Status

The published research concerns particular constructed-record samples. Practical relevance and operational effectiveness require separate evidence from authorized organizational use.

What is currently supported

Evidence streamCurrent interpretationWhat it does not prove
Detection study16 reviewers examined 24 constructed records, producing 384 graded judgments. Mean detection accuracy was reported as 83.9% with a 95% interval of 72.7% to 95.1% across reviewer-level scores.Individual reviewer dependability, effectiveness in real records or causal improvement from JRS use.
Separate agreement sample22 analyzed raters, 104 determinations and 10 records with multiple raters. AC1 point estimates were 0.739 and 0.623; the pre-registered lower-bound criterion was not met.Validated reliability or agreement under all operating conditions.
Technical engineering checksLocal and constructed evaluations may establish bounded properties of a named development configuration.Deployed capability, real-world classification accuracy, independence or production authorization.
Operational field applicationStill requires independently reviewable workflow evidence, documented human decisions and reproducible measures.No live operational effectiveness claim is established by the studies above.

What an inspectable field observation should include

  1. Define the record type and workflow, including exclusions and use permission.
  2. Identify the specific JRS publication, review instrument and effective version.
  3. Preserve a pre-review record state and source identifiers in the authorized system, without uploading personal case files to the public website.
  4. Record each identified gap, source evidence, reviewer interpretation and escalation or correction decision.
  5. Compare the post-review record with the pre-review state using predeclared criteria, including elapsed time and additional reviewer burden where available.
  6. Report denominators, disagreement, unresolved cases and limitations rather than only successful examples.

Evidence separation

A practitioner noticing a useful gap is a field observation. A completed pilot with a defined sample and outcome criteria is stronger evidence. An independent evaluation would require an appropriate design and external review or adjudication. These states must not be presented as equivalent.

Current assessment: Operational validation is ongoing. This page does not establish completed independent field validation, certification, commercial availability, or permission to submit confidential records.
