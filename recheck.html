Ten public-records cases

You are being asked to read ten records and answer one question about each. You are not checking anyone's work, and you are not being shown anyone else's answers. There is no right total: some sets are mostly one category and some are mixed.

What you are deciding

For each case, read the source and answer this:

Could a reviewer with no access to the author, no institutional memory, and no supplementary explanation identify the basis for each conclusion from the record alone?

ReadyThe basis for each conclusion is present in the record and can be rebuilt from it without asking anyone.
Needs workThe record states conclusions whose basis is partly present, so a cold reader could reconstruct some of it and would have to assume the rest.
GapThe record does not carry a reconstructable basis at all. A cold reader could not say why the conclusion was reached.
Ground rules. Read the source before deciding; the summary is orientation, not the record. Do not look up how a case came out. If you already know one, tick the box on that case and answer anyway: it will be noted rather than dropped. Do not discuss the cases with anyone else on the study until your answers are in. If a source is unreachable, leave that case blank and say so in the reason box.

It takes about two hours. Your answers save in this browser as you go, so you can stop and come back.
