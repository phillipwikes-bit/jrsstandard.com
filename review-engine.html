Review Engine status

The JRS Review Engine is a constrained documentation-review candidate. It is not a public record-processing service, production system, or autonomous decision-maker.

Current public status. This website does not accept record text. Public review routes refuse submissions. The local-development profile is limited to completed, non-HR supplier-access exception drafts. Findings are evidence-linked and require human review.

Available

Public method, Standard, Codebook, field guides, simulations, research materials, and Manifest schema.

Not authorized

Public record submission, production processing, or employment, housing, lending, insurance, medical, or legal-outcome decisions.

Release conditions

External controlled use is not verified. A future release would require independent labeled and adjudicated holdout evaluation, operator-control evidence, counsel review of real data flows and claims, and recorded owner authorization.
