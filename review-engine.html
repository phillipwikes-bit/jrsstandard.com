Controlled implementation · Local development only

Review Engine status

The JRS Review Engine is a constrained documentation-review candidate. It is not a public record-processing service, production system, or autonomous decision-maker.

Current public status.

This website does not accept record text. Public review routes refuse submissions. The local-development profile is limited to completed, non-HR supplier-access exception drafts. Findings are evidence-linked and always require human review.

Scope boundary

Clear separation between the public method and controlled implementation.

The published Standard, Codebook, field tools, training, simulations, research materials, and Manifest schema can be inspected and used as described. The Engine remains separate from these public materials.

Publicly available

Method and learning materials

Public resources explain the JRS methodology and its research record. They do not provide a public Engine workflow or generated evaluation output.

Not authorized

Public operational use

No public record submission, production processing, or use for employment, housing, lending, insurance, medical, or legal-outcome decisions is authorized.

Release conditions

External controlled use is not verified.

A future release would require all four conditions below. This list is a status disclosure, not a public testing pathway or offer of access.

Gate 01Independent holdout evaluationLabeled, adjudicated results from unused evaluation material.
Gate 02Operator-control evidenceVerified access, retention, recovery, and execution controls.
Gate 03Counsel reviewReview of real data flows, privacy, evaluation, and claim boundaries.
Gate 04Recorded owner authorizationA dated release decision after the preceding gates are complete.

Human authority remains essential. JRS can identify record-level documentation conditions requiring review. It does not determine whether the underlying decision is legally correct, compliant, or substantively justified.
