JRS | PRACTITIONER EVALUATION WORKSHEET
Version 0.1 | 3 November 2026
Status: Blank evaluation aid. Not research results, certification, or proof of operational effectiveness.

PURPOSE
Document whether application of the Justification Review Standard (JRS) identifies record-quality issues not identified by an organization's ordinary review. Use within an authorized workflow and keep the completed worksheet in the organization's own controlled records system.

BOUNDARIES
- This is a local working template, not an online research enrollment or upload mechanism.
- Do not transmit confidential records, names, protected information, credentials, privileged content, or source documents to the public JRS website or public contact form.
- Human reviewers retain authority for findings, correction, escalation and finalization.
- A record-quality finding does not determine the legality or merits of the underlying decision.
- Record the exact publication used. The Standard PDF v2.0 (2026) and online methodology v1.0 (May 2026) are distinct publications and are not established as equivalent.
- Use a workflow approved by the organization. General-purpose real-record effectiveness and software-engine performance have not been established.
