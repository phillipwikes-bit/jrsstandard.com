Suppressed & Inactive Cohorts

A zero on this page can mean three different things and you cannot tell them apart by looking: nothing happened, nothing was sent, or something is deliberately withheld. Every cohort below states which of the three it is. None of them is counted in an active total: a roster size is not an audience, an unsent link is not a non-response, and a withheld breakdown is not an absence of data.
