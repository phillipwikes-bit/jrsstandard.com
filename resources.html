Publication version control

The downloadable JRS Standard is labeled Version 2.0 (2026). The online methodology identifies v1.0 (May 2026). The current Manifest schema 1.0.1 accepts Standard v1.0 identifiers and has not been established as compatible with the separately versioned PDF. Do not cite these as an identical edition. Scope

JRS identifies documentation conditions requiring review. It does not decide whether the underlying decision is legally correct, compliant, or substantively justified, and it does not replace professional judgment or applicable procedures.
