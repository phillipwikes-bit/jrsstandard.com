Public resources · Free and ungated

Free JRS training and tools for practitioners.

Decision Reconstruction Risk (DRR) arises when a retained decision record cannot independently show the evidence, chronology and reasoning behind its conclusion. These free resources help practitioners check that risk using the workflows they already have.

JRS is a practical review instrument that practitioners can use within existing workflows, not a software platform they must acquire. Organizations do not need to purchase or replace a software platform to begin examining whether their decision records preserve sufficient evidence, chronology and reasoning for later independent review.

Use within an existing workflow

Start with a record already on your desk.

JRS is a human-applied documentation review method, not a case-management system. Apply it at an existing approval or review step. Keep the case file, source documents and personal information in your organization's authorized systems.

  1. Identify the draft and its sources

    Select the record due for review. Identify the source material, relevant dates and prior decisions supporting its statements. Do not upload or send case-record content to this website.

  2. Examine the five review conditions

    Check reconstructability, identifiable basis, chronology, reasoning trace and sufficiency. Verify AI-assisted wording against the original evidence. The seven-point record check is a free, non-scoring browser aid.

  3. Record the disposition in your system

    Document any missing evidence, chronology or reasoning in the existing case or approval record. Return the draft for correction or escalate under local procedures when needed. An authorized person, not JRS, decides whether it advances.

Further guidance and publication details

Free resources you can use today: Open the seven-point record check, investigator field guides, six self-paced training modules, simulations, or supporting review references. No software purchase is required. Keep all case files in your existing authorized system.

Different publication versions? Compare the published Standard, online methodology and investigator supplements. Organizational information describes the current public access boundaries.

First time using JRS? See a short fictional record review, then choose a free tool.

Scope and next step: JRS does not certify legal correctness or compliance, and this site does not process confidential case files. Read the online methodology, see investigator guidance, or examine the research evidence and limitations. If a gap is discovered after filing, local retention and amendment procedures still govern any correction.
