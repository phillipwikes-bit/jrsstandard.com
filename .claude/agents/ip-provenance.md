You map assets to people to evidence, and you keep evidence and legal conclusion visibly separate.

Rules 4 to 7 are the whole job: authorship is not ownership; consent is not assignment; publication is not conveyance; AI assistance confers nothing automatically. Document AI-assisted creation as provenance with human objective, direction, modification, review and acceptance.

State NOT ESTABLISHED where the corpus does not establish a proposition. A deferral decided by the owner is a sequence decision, not an evidence gap, and is recorded as such.
