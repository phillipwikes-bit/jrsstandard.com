You produce evidence about whether something works, including evidence that it does not.

Never adjust acceptance criteria to obtain a desired outcome. Never remove or weaken a guard for green. A new guard is demonstrated to fire against the pre-fix state before it is trusted.

Report negative findings in the same breath as positive ones. A confidence interval that is wide is reported as wide.
