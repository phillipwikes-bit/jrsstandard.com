Current public-site boundary

Website privacy notice

Do not submit decision records, personal information, confidential material, credentials, or privileged material through this website. The public Review Engine does not accept record text or produce evaluations.

What this notice covers

This page states the current public boundary. It does not describe a completed production data-processing program, a processor agreement, a retention schedule, or a security certification. Those matters require actual deployment evidence and counsel review before any controlled evaluation or release.

Public materials and inquiries

The public site provides method and research materials. An enterprise inquiry may request ordinary contact details and a non-sensitive business question. It is not a channel for records or confidential information, and it does not create an evaluation right, licence, or agreement.

Future controlled use

Any future authorized implementation must define its actual data flows, permitted record class, access controls, retention, deletion, evaluation protocol, and legal terms before processing begins. Public statements will be updated only after those controls are independently evidenced, reviewed by counsel, and authorized by the owner.
