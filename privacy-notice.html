Current public-site boundary

Website privacy notice

Do not submit decision records, third-party personal information, confidential material, credentials, or privileged material through this website. Contact forms request only the sender's own basic contact details and a general, non-sensitive inquiry. The public Review Engine does not accept record text or produce evaluations.

What this notice covers

This notice describes the current limited public contact route. It is not a statement that a processor agreement, retention schedule, independent privacy audit or security certification has been completed. Controlled evaluation involving decision records remains unavailable pending separate evidence, review and authorization.

Public materials and inquiries

Public resources are available without providing contact details. The Contact page requests a name, reply email, inquiry category, optional organization and a general non-sensitive message. Contact consent is limited to responding to that inquiry. It is not consent for marketing, research enrollment, publication or transfer.

Current contact processing. The public Contact form sends a visitor's name, reply email, optional organization, selected topic and message through the existing third-party Formspree form service. Formspree handles the submission in its account inbox and sends the configured notification to the JRS Gmail inbox. JRS uses this information only for inquiry response; it is not entered into the JRS research participant ledger or treated as permission for commercial marketing, licensing or transfer.

The contact form uses an existing Formspree account previously employed by JRS for pilot inquiries. Submissions are identified as public professional contact requests by distinct source and type fields. The provider's detailed retention and deletion settings have not been independently verified; visitors should submit only routine, non-sensitive inquiries. Provider acceptance confirms submission receipt by Formspree, not guaranteed inbox delivery. The sender may alternatively use the direct email link.

Future controlled use

Any future authorized implementation must define its actual data flows, permitted record class, access controls, retention, deletion, evaluation protocol, and legal terms before processing begins. Public statements will be updated only after those controls are independently evidenced, reviewed by counsel, and authorized by the owner.
