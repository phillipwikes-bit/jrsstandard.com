JRS public website · Data and consent boundaries

Website privacy notice

Public review resources and self-paced training may be read without registration or submission of personal information. This notice covers the site's public contact form and optional named training registration. It does not authorize submission or automated processing of organizational decision records.

Do not submit case records, third-party personal information, confidential material, credentials, or privileged information. This site provides manual reference materials, not a public automated record-assessment service.

General professional contact

The public Contact form requests a visitor's name and reply email, with optional organization, inquiry category and a general non-sensitive message. It sends the inquiry to the existing third-party Formspree service, whose account inbox receives the submission and may forward an email notification to the JRS mailbox. The information is used for responding to that inquiry, not as authorization for marketing, research enrollment or transfer.

Provider acceptance confirms the form service received a submission; it is not evidence that an email notification reached the intended mailbox. Formspree retention and deletion settings have not been independently verified.

Optional reviewer training registration

The training modules are open without registration. A visitor choosing optional named enrollment supplies a name and email address, and may provide an organization, professional title and an answer to an optional software-related question. The server also records a limited source tag, country code derived from the request, consent values and a timestamp.

The website sends these fields to the existing JRS Supabase-backed pilot_contacts record store with the source tag training-enroll. This uses the site's server-side enrollment endpoint, not the Formspree contact form. The endpoint does not return the registration details publicly. The privacy and access design depends on actual deployed credentials, database permissions and administrator controls; independent production assurance of these controls and the provider's retention/deletion configuration has not been completed.

Enrollment is voluntary. The required contact checkbox covers communication about the requested named completion record and JRS project updates. A separate, optional checkbox concerns whether the enrollment record could be transferred with the JRS project to a future successor. That checkbox is not an authorization for commercial sale, public disclosure or release of protected records. No name is published through enrollment. A training completion record is not a professional certification or accreditation.

Resource access and site measurement

Public guide and PDF downloads may be counted through the site's existing interaction-event system, including document identifiers and limited technical metadata such as country and user agent. Selected public pages use Google Analytics. Download counts are activity indicators, not proof that the documents were read, adopted or used in actual professional review. Research datasets, reviewer records and owner-private dashboards are governed separately.

Access, correction and deletion requests

For a question about your own submitted information, or to request access, correction or deletion, contact info@jrsstandard.com. Requests require appropriate verification and review. No universal retention period or guaranteed immediate deletion is asserted while provider settings and governing obligations remain under review.

Limitations and future changes

This notice describes observed website source behavior and current public uses, not an independent privacy audit, formal certification, processor agreement or legal compliance determination. Future controlled evaluations involving decision records require a separately authorized data-flow, retention, deletion and legal review before any such processing begins.
