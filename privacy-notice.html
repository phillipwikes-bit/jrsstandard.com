JRS Public Website

Website privacy notice

Issue: October 31, 2026. This notice covers public JRS information resources and ordinary professional inquiries. Decision Spellchecker is a separately developed, controlled software candidate. There is no public record-evaluation or document-upload service.

Do not submit case records, employment files, sensitive or confidential material, privileged documents, account credentials, or personal information about third parties. Name and contact email are requested only when you choose to send an ordinary professional inquiry.

Information collected through inquiries

The planned Contact & Inquiries form asks for your name, email address, optional organization, inquiry category, a short non-sensitive message and an affirmative permission to respond. Submitted inquiries are designed to be saved in the existing JRS contact-record system with the category, contact-consent version, date and limited location information supplied by the hosting platform. The form does not ask for a case record, upload or an underlying decision.

The contact permission authorizes retention and follow-up for the stated inquiry. It does not authorize publishing your name or transferring your contact record to an acquirer. Existing research, enrollment and supporter consent records follow their own separately documented consent terms.

Technical service providers

Vercel hosts the site and processes ordinary website requests. Supabase is the existing contact and program database. The planned inquiry form uses Cloudflare Turnstile to verify submissions, which can involve technical visitor information being processed by Cloudflare. The form is configured to remain unavailable until that verification service and storage have been configured.

If a transactional email service is activated, a sending provider such as Resend or SendGrid may receive the inquiry category, contact details and submitted message to deliver a notification to the owner. Email delivery is secondary to the stored inquiry; it is not yet verified as operational.

Research, downloads and website measurements

JRS maintains records of resource downloads, training activity, research participation and other existing program measures. Public-page Google Analytics usage and download event counters are separate from the owner-private Program Status dashboard, which is intentionally not tagged for Google Analytics. Historical study and consent records are not transformed into commercial software validation by inclusion in site reporting.

Optional public-initiative support actions

The Right to Know Why and Decisions You Can Defend pages offer an optional action that does not require a name, email address, payment or subscription. The action is submitted only when you select it. A successfully recorded action is stored in the existing support-event database with the initiative code, the source label, limited country information where available, and a bounded browser user-agent string. A first-party campaign-specific cookie records that this browser has already acted to reduce repeated counts; the cookie is not a person ID. Technical website and provider logs may separately process network information. These records should not be described as fully anonymous in every jurisdiction or as verified unique supporters or organizational endorsements.

If the database does not acknowledge the new initiative-page submission, the site displays that the action could not be confirmed and does not set a new campaign-counted cookie. Existing historical campaign links and older interaction records remain separate and are not retrospectively reclassified or independently verified by this change. Public resources remain accessible without taking a support action.

Access, retention and corrections

Named contacts and inquiry responses are intended for authorized owner access, not public release. The authentication and database access controls for the redesigned dashboard remain subject to deployment-specific verification. A noindex tag or hard-to-guess URL is not an access control.

Inquiry records are retained for follow-up and disposition rather than for a fixed period represented here. A documented records-retention and deletion schedule remains a prerequisite for the redesigned inquiry system's production release. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of ordinary contact details, subject to applicable obligations.

Contact and updates

For privacy or data-subject requests, write to info@jrsstandard.com. This notice may be revised if the actual services, data flows or approved policies change. It is not a claim of external compliance certification, legal approval or independent security validation.
