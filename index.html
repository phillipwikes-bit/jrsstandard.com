JRS™ · Justification Review Standard

Can this record still explain the decision it documents?

JRS is a pre-finalization documentation-review standard. It examines whether a consequential record preserves the basis, evidence, reasoning, and chronology needed for later independent review.

Explore free resources → Read the standard → Enterprise inquiries →

Asset boundary. JRS is the public methodology. The Review Engine is a controlled technical implementation maintained separately for evaluation and possible integration. The Decision Reconstruction Manifest is the versioned evidence-artifact specification. Publication permits inspection. It does not grant commercial, implementation, transfer, or trademark rights.

The operating question

The record must survive the decision-maker.

If the decision is challenged two years from now, an independent reviewer should be able to determine what happened, what evidence was considered, how the reasoning developed, and why the recorded conclusion followed.

Risk

Decision Reconstruction Risk

The record survives, but the basis for the decision cannot be reconstructed from the file itself.

Current state

Present capability and target architecture remain distinct.

Public methodology
Published and usable without purchasing software.
Review Engine
Operationally implemented, single-model, and empirically unvalidated for autonomous consequential-decision use.
Manifest v1.0
Public schema and example available. A controlled generator and integrity tests exist.
Current API output
The current endpoint does not emit a conforming Manifest. Customer-controlled Manifest delivery remains target licensing architecture.
Human authority
JRS identifies documentation conditions requiring review. It does not make the underlying professional decision.
Transaction status
Licensing and acquisition conversations may begin. A binding transfer remains subject to established rights and an executed agreement.

Current commercial status. JRS is ready for licensing, integration, and acquisition conversations. It is not represented as ready for execution of an exclusive licence or asset purchase agreement until the conveyable rights and transaction terms have been established.
