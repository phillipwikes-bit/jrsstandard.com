Three questions, and nothing is assumed

1. Print me as shown above.

2. May this work be used commercially?

The paper and the underlying study may at some point be used beyond academic publication: in training material, in a licensed product, or in materials shown to an organisation paying for the work. That would mean the work earns money and you would not receive a share of it. This asks whether you are willing for your contribution to be used that way. You can say no and remain a full author.
