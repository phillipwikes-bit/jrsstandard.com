What did you test your detection claim against?

A tool that claims it can spot documentation which will not survive review needs something independent to be measured against. You run a held-out record set; scoring is returned by the holder, and you receive a calibration report against credentialed human raters.

What is readYour tool or your team runs a held-out record set; scoring is returned by the holder
TurnaroundAgreed at scoping; depends on your run schedule
Pricestated below, fixed. No hourly rate and no scope creep
Written forAI assurance vendors, model evaluation teams, audit firms

What you receive

  • Licensed access to the record set for one run
  • Scoring returned by the holder. The answer key is never released
  • A calibration report against the human rater distribution
  • A written statement of what the result does and does not support
Data Isolation Guarantee All customer files and sample records submitted for diagnostic evaluation are processed in ephemeral working memory and deleted upon delivery of the diagnostic report. Customer records are never stored, logged to public research sets, or used for model training.
