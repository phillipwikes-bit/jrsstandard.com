Contact JRS

Questions about the Justification Review Standard, corrections to published materials, accessibility concerns and voluntary professional feedback are welcome. The investigator guides, training and reviewer references remain free without registration or contact.

Send an inquiry

Complete the fields and select Submit inquiry. JRS uses its existing Formspree submission service to receive the message and send a notification to the designated JRS Gmail inbox. No email application is required. Do not include case records, confidential documents or personal information about other people.

The submission is processed by Formspree, which applies its own spam filtering. This is a contact channel, not a secure record-submission system.

After submission, a receipt will appear when the inquiry service accepts the message. A receipt does not establish that a particular email arrived in the inbox.

Prefer email? You can also write directly to phillipwikes@gmail.com.

Privacy and scope. Do not email case files, confidential employment or housing records, credentials, privileged material or identifying information about third parties. An inquiry does not create research enrollment, a software license, a commercial commitment or consent to marketing.
