Evidence Development Program

What the research shows, and what remains unproven.

The research examines whether reviewers can identify Decision Reconstruction Risk in documentation. It concerns the published JRS method, not the performance or availability of the separate Review Engine.

Read this first. The principal detection results come from constructed, AI-generated records with known answers. They do not establish effectiveness on real workplace, investigation or administrative records. The human-reviewer reliability criterion and the cross-vendor reproducibility criterion have not been established.

Evidence at a glance

83.9%Mean expert detection accuracy against a verified key on constructed records. Detection subset: 16 experts, 384 graded reads; 95% participant-level CI 72.7% to 95.1%.
36 expertsCompleted a full 24-record set across 16 countries. This broader completion group must not be confused with the 16-expert detection subset.
58 reviewersGraded at least one record across three studies. A participation count is not an accuracy or validation estimate.

The detection threshold was met on its pre-specified analysis. A separate reviewer-agreement criterion was not met because the confidence-interval lower bounds did not clear the required threshold. Cross-vendor raw agreement does not establish reproducibility. See the study-specific documents for estimators, denominators and limitations.
