Investigation report review

How to Check Evidence Support in an Investigation Report

An investigation report may accurately state a finding but still leave a later reviewer unable to identify the evidence supporting it. Check the connection before finalization.

Start with the finding, not the prose

  1. Identify each material factual finding and evaluative conclusion. Separate what the record reports from what the investigator infers.
  2. For each factual proposition, identify the source: interview, document, log, contemporaneous note, policy or other authorized record. Record how the reader can locate it.
  3. Check whether the source actually supports the finding as stated. An exact quotation may establish text presence without establishing the inference drawn from it.
  4. Record conflicting information and the reason for crediting or discounting it where the applicable process requires that explanation.
  5. Confirm that event dates, interview dates and decision dates can be distinguished and placed in sequence.
  6. Route unresolved gaps to the authorized human reviewer. Do not supply missing facts by drafting more confidently.

Fictional example

Before: “The investigation confirmed repeated failures to comply with the procedure.”

Review question: Which events, dates, procedure version and identified sources support “repeated” and “confirmed”?

Proper response: Check the underlying materials. If the evidence supports a narrower conclusion, narrow it. If sources are unavailable, mark the gap for review. Do not invent interviews or records.

Review handoff

In the authorized case system, document the finding, source identifier, exact supporting passage where appropriate, unresolved conflict, reviewer, and disposition. Preserve underlying evidence rather than substituting an AI-generated summary.

Read the relevant investigator field guides or use the seven-point record check. The field guides are separate, discipline-specific publications.

Review boundary. These are educational review questions and fictional illustrations, not determinations about a live record, legal merits, regulatory compliance or the performance of the JRS Review Engine. Apply organizational procedures and retain all source material in authorized systems. No record upload is requested.
