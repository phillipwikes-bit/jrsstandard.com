When citing a record

Record the exact title, edition number, publication year, URL or retained copy, and any organizational adaptation. Identify the source actually used.

When wording differs

Do not silently replace an older supplement with newer wording. Identify discrepancies and document the responsible reviewer's resolution.

Accessibility

Published PDF tagging and reading order have not been independently certified. Request an accessible format, retaining the original edition label.

Boundaries

Publication does not establish legal validity or real-world effectiveness. Review the research evidence and limitations.

Minimum version-control procedure

  1. Select the document that matches the review purpose.
  2. Read the chosen publication's scope and limitations.
  3. Record its exact title, edition, date and source.
  4. Resolve conflicts between references explicitly before adopting a procedure.
